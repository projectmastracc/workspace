# Example: Compound profile — Creatine monohydrate (calibration)

**Why this is a quality target:** Clean high-evidence performance/nutrition compound. Tests Executive Card, Practical Guidance ceilings, and FAQ adjudication — not anecdotal edge cases.

---

## Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound / class** | Creatine monohydrate — amino acid derivative / ergogenic (nutrition + performance) |
| **Overall certainty** | **Established** (strength, lean mass, repeated-bout performance) |
| **Verdict** | Best-evidenced strength/power supplement; 3–5 g/day monohydrate is sufficient for most users. |
| **Key practical note** | 3–5 g/day daily; optional load 20 g/day × 5–7 days; no cycling required. |
| **Top caveats** | Caution in significant renal disease; hair-loss claim remains unreplicated; hydration matters. |

---

## 1. Bottom-line / Overview

Creatine monohydrate increases intramuscular phosphocreatine and improves high-intensity performance and lean mass gains when training is present (**Established**). Maintenance dosing of 3–5 g/day matches the bulk of trial protocols. Loading is optional and only accelerates saturation (**Probable**). Safety in healthy adults is well characterized for standard doses (**Established**); long-term data at extreme doses are thinner.

## 2. Chemistry & Classification

Guanidino compound; monohydrate is the reference form. Close analogs (HCl, ethyl ester, buffered) lack evidence of superiority for outcomes that matter (**Established** for monohydrate as standard).

## 3. Mechanism of Action

Increases PCr stores → faster ATP regeneration in short, intense efforts (**Established**). Secondary cell-volumizing and possible cognitive effects under stress are less certain (**Probable** / **Speculative** by outcome).

## 4. Pharmacokinetics

Oral absorption adequate; muscle saturation over days to weeks depending on load vs maintenance. No meaningful CNS “stimulant” PK profile.

## 5. Human Evidence

Meta-analyses and many RCTs support strength and lean-mass outcomes with resistance training (**Established**). Vegetarian/vegan responders often start from lower baseline stores (**Probable**). Cognitive endpoints are mixed and context-dependent (**Speculative**–**Probable** depending on endpoint).

## 6. Preclinical / Mechanistic Support

Mechanistic work on PCr and bioenergetics aligns with human outcomes (translation risk: low for performance endpoints).

## 7. Subjective / Experiential Profile

| Theme | Consistency | Concordance |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Faster recovery between sets / better volume | High | **strong** vs performance literature |
| Water weight in first weeks | High | **strong** (intracellular water) |
| “Hair loss on creatine” | Moderate discussion | **literature-silent** to weak for causation — single DHT association not replicated |

**Weighing:** Controlled evidence strongly supports performance reports. Hair-loss fear is common in forums; literature does not establish causation (**Speculative** / **Unknown** for causal claim). Do not assert “creatine does not cause hair loss” as absolute; assert “causal evidence is inadequate.”

## 8. Practical Guidance

| Parameter | Recommendation | Certainty |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| Dose (maintenance) | 3–5 g/day | **Established** |
| Loading (optional) | 20 g/day × 5–7 days (split), then maintenance | **Probable** |
| Timing | Daily consistency > workout timing | **Probable** |
| Form | Monohydrate | **Established** |
| Hydration | Maintain adequate fluid intake | **Probable** |

**Not recommended when:** significant renal disease without medical oversight (**Established** caution).

## 9. Safety, Side Effects, Contraindications & Monitoring

GI upset at large single boluses (**Probable**). Transient creatinine rise can confuse labs — interpret in context (**Established**). No routine cycling for safety in healthy adults (**Probable**).

## 10. Interactions

Caffeine interaction literature is mixed; not a reason to avoid standard use (**Speculative** concern). Stacks with protein/resistance training are complementary (**Established** context).

## 11. History & Development

Decades of sports-nutrition use; one of the most studied supplements.

## 12. Reputation & Comparative Context

| Alternative | vs creatine | Note |
|-------------|-------------|------|
| Creatine HCl / esters | Inferior evidence base | No outcome superiority |
| Beta-alanine | Different mechanism (H+ buffering) | Complementary, not substitute |

## 13. Open Questions

Large RCTs on hair outcomes; cognitive benefits in sleep-deprived vs healthy rested adults.

## 14. Evidence Matrix + Key Sources

Overall: **Established** for primary performance use. Prefer meta-analyses of RCTs (High trust).

## 15. FAQ / Common Claims

| Claim | Verdict | Certainty |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Must load forever at 20 g | Not supported | **Unknown** / contradicted by maintenance data |
| Must cycle off | Not required for safety in healthy adults | **Probable** |
| Causes hair loss | Unproven causation | **Speculative** / **Unknown** |

### Guidance & Application Notice

Evidence-graded synthesis; user assumes responsibility; **Unknown** = no recommendation.
