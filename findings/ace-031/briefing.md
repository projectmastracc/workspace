# ACE-031 (Ramatercept) — Full Compound Profile

**Class:** Soluble activin receptor type IIB–IgG1 Fc fusion (ActRIIB-Fc) · multi-ligand TGF-β superfamily trap · investigational myostatin-pathway anabolic  
**Developer:** Acceleron Pharma · **Status:** Clinical development **discontinued** (2011–2013 safety stop) · **Not** FDA/EMA approved for any indication  
**PED / research-chem market:** Sold as “ACE-031” / ramatercept on black market; identity and purity **Unknown** per vial  

**Evidence frame:** Human PD for lean mass after single SC dose in healthy postmenopausal women is **Established** (RCT). Program termination for vascular signals (epistaxis, telangiectasia) is **Established**. Multi-ligand (including BMP9) mechanism for vascular risk is **Probable**. Athletic performance / contest-prep use of UGL product is **Speculative** and carries **Established** class safety flags. Isolation: standalone document; do not import other findings packages as evidence.

## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | ACE-031 (ramatercept): recombinant fusion of the extracellular domain of human **activin receptor type IIB (ActRIIB / ACVR2B)** to human **IgG1 Fc**. Acts as a circulating **decoy receptor** (“ligand trap”) for myostatin and related TGF-β family ligands. |
| **Primary use cases** | Intended: muscle wasting / Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD). Preclinical/clinical: systemic lean-mass increase. PED market: claimed myostatin inhibition for hypertrophy. |
| **Bottom line** | ACE-031 is one of the **best-documented human proofs** that broad ActRIIB ligand trapping increases lean mass and thigh muscle volume in weeks—even after a **single** SC dose in non-training postmenopausal women (+3.3% LBM, +5.1% thigh volume at 3 mg/kg by day 29). **Established.** It is also a textbook case of **efficacy limited by off-target biology**: the same decoy binds ligands beyond myostatin (activins, GDF11, **BMP9/10**, others), and the DMD program was **stopped** for non-muscle vascular events (epistaxis, telangiectasias, gum bleeding). Development was discontinued. Black-market “ACE-031” is not pharmaceutical ACE-031 and does not erase those safety signals. |
| **Biggest upsides** | Rapid lean mass / muscle volume PD in humans — **Established**. Multi-ligand muscle efficacy often exceeds pure anti-myostatin Abs in animals — **Probable**. Fat mass ↓ and bone biomarker shifts in human PD data — **Probable**. Long t½ (~10–15 days) allows infrequent SC dosing in trials — **Established**. |
| **Biggest downsides** | Vascular fragility signals (bleeding, telangiectasia) leading to program kill — **Established**. BMP9 (and related) sequestration as leading mechanistic explanation — **Probable**. Unknown long-term cancer/repro/metabolic profile at PED abuse doses — **Unknown/Speculative**. UGL authenticity — **Unknown**. Mass ≠ strength/function (class lesson) — **Probable**. |
| **Stack role** | Experimental **systemic multi-ligand trap**, not a foundational PED. Historically more “powerful” on mass PD than selective GDF8 Abs in early human data—and more systemically risky than myostatin-selective approaches. Inferior risk/evidence profile vs progressive training + proven AAS for gym goals; superior **mechanism documentation** vs UGL “peptides” with no human PK. |
| **Certainty frame** | Human single-dose mass PD **Established**. DMD multi-dose trends **Probable** but confounded by early stop. PED “safe cycle” **not Established**—contraindicated by development history at therapeutic doses. |

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### 2.1 Structure and classification

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **INN / code** | Ramatercept (ACE-031) |
| **Molecular class** | Fc fusion protein (biologic), **not** a short peptide |
| **Design** | Soluble ActRIIB extracellular domain + IgG1 Fc → long circulating half-life, ligand trap without intracellular signaling |
| **Mechanism class** | Decoy receptor for ActRIIB ligands; prevents endogenous receptor activation on muscle (and other tissues) |
| **Related Acceleron assets** | **ACE-083** (follistatin-based local trap; volume↑, function often fails); **ACE-536 / luspatercept** (modified ActRIIB trap optimized for erythropoiesis—**approved** for anemia, not muscle); **ACE-011 / sotatercept** (ActRIIA-Fc lineage—different receptor, different indications) |
| **Legal / status** | Never marketed as approved drug; clinical development halted; controlled research-only / black-market presence |

**Important naming note:** “ACE-031 peptide” in research-chem marketing is a category error. ACE-031 is a **large Fc-fusion glycoprotein**, not a 20–40 aa research peptide.

### 2.2 Ligand binding profile (why it works—and why it hurt)

ActRIIB is a promiscuous type II receptor. Soluble ActRIIB-Fc therefore sequesters multiple ligands:

| Ligand | Muscle / body role | Consequence of trapping | Certainty |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| **Myostatin (GDF8)** | Primary negative regulator of skeletal muscle mass | Hypertrophy / lean mass ↑ | **Established** |
| **Activin A/B** | Muscle-negative Smad2/3; inflammation/fibrosis contexts; FSH physiology | Extra muscle gain vs pure GDF8 block | **Established** contribution in animals; **Probable** in humans |
| **GDF11** | Related TGF-β ligand; aging/muscle debates | Muscle ± systemic effects | **Established** binding; phenotype **Speculative** |
| **BMP9 / BMP10** | Vascular integrity / endothelial signaling (ALK1–endoglin axis) | **Bleeding, telangiectasia** risk | **Probable** (mechanistic consensus after trials) |
| Other BMPs / TGF-β family members | Bone, hematopoiesis, metabolic tissues | Bone biomarkers, spleen/erythroid signals in animals | **Probable** off-target |

**Core insight:** ACE-031 is **not** a selective myostatin inhibitor. Selectivity failure is the product’s defining risk/benefit feature. **Established** (pharmacology + clinical outcome).

### 2.3 Pharmacokinetics (human clinical)

From Attie et al. 2013 (single SC dose, postmenopausal women):

| Parameter | Finding | Certainty |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Route** | Subcutaneous | **Established** (trial) |
| **Dose range studied** | 0.02–3.0 mg/kg single dose | **Established** |
| **Exposure** | AUC and Cmax increase approximately linearly with dose | **Established** |
| **Elimination half-life** | Mean **~10–15 days** | **Established** |
| **Implication** | Weeks-long exposure after single injection; multi-dose every 2–4 weeks in DMD design | **Probable** accumulation with q2wk |

**DMD multi-dose (Campbell 2017):** SC every 2–4 weeks (e.g., 0.5 mg/kg q4wk; 1.0 mg/kg q2wk cohorts before stop). **Established** trial design; full steady-state PD incomplete due to termination.

**UGL PK:** Not characterized. Black-market material studied by anti-doping labs (WADA-linked administration studies in rats at high mg/kg for detection)—confirms black-market presence, not pharmaceutical quality. **Established** that counterfeit/black-market product exists; **Unknown** lot identity.

- Market research chemicals: vials labeled ACE-031—**no** chain of custody to Acceleron drug product after program kill.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Evidence map

| Tier | Study | Population | Key result | Certainty for claim |
|------|-------|------------|------------|---------------------|
| Phase 1 RCT | Attie 2013 PMID 23169607 | Healthy postmenopausal women N=48 | Single 3 mg/kg: LBM +3.3%, thigh +5.1% at d29; t½ 10–15 d | **Established** short-term lean mass PD |
| Phase 2 RCT (terminated) | Campbell 2017 PMID 27462804 NCT01099761 | Ambulatory DMD boys | Stopped for epistaxis/telangiectasia; lean mass/BMD/fat trends; 6MWT NS | Safety stop **Established**; PD trends **Probable** |
| Preclinical | Multiple ActRIIB-Fc / ACE-031 | Mice, nonhuman models | Large muscle mass increases; multi-ligand | **Established** (animals) |
| Anti-doping | WADA black-market ACE-031 study | Rats + market product | Detectable after high-dose admin | Black market **Established** |
| Athlete RCTs | — | Trained men/women | **None** | Athletic performance **Unknown** |

### 4.2 Attie et al. 2013 — healthy postmenopausal women (primary human PD study)

- **Design:** Double-blind, placebo-controlled, single ascending SC dose; 0.02–3 mg/kg vs placebo (3:1); N=48.  
- **Safety:** Generally well tolerated at single dose; injection-site erythema noted.  
- **PK:** Linear AUC/Cmax; mean t½ **10–15 days**.  
- **PD (3 mg/kg, day 29):**  
  - Total body lean mass **+3.3%** (DXA, P=0.03)  
  - Thigh muscle volume **+5.1%** (MRI, P=0.03)  
  - Serum biomarkers consistent with effects on **bone and fat** metabolism (statistically significant changes reported)  
- **Population limits:** Older women, not resistance-trained young men; **single** dose; short follow-up; no strength/power primary endpoints of athletic relevance.  
- **Funding:** Acceleron (sponsor).  
- **Certainty:** Lean mass and muscle volume increase after one high SC dose — **Established**. Transfer to young male athletes at black-market doses — **Speculative**.

### 4.3 Campbell et al. 2017 — ambulatory DMD boys (termination study)

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multiple ascending-dose SC every 2–4 weeks. Primary: safety. Secondary: PK/PD, function.  
- **Dosing (examples):** 0.5 mg/kg q4wk; 1.0 mg/kg q2wk (study stopped after second dosing regimen).  
- **Safety outcome:** Not associated with serious or severe AEs in the published framing, but study **stopped** due to **potential safety concerns: epistaxis and telangiectasias**. Gum bleeding also reported in program communications (MDA). Non-muscle AEs drove discontinuation.  
- **PD/efficacy trends:**  
  - Lean body mass ↑ trends  
  - Fat mass ↓ trends  
  - BMD ↑ trends  
  - 6MWT: trend to maintain distance vs placebo decline — **not statistically significant**  
- **Limits:** Pediatric dystrophic population; early stop truncates efficacy read; open questions on dose–AE relationship; small effective exposure window.  
- **Program fate:** DMD trial + extension suspended; Acceleron discontinued ACE-031 development after FDA/Health Canada safety review (MDA 2013 communications).  
- **Certainty:** Vascular safety signal sufficient to kill program — **Established**. Muscle PD in DMD — **Probable** incomplete. Functional benefit — **not demonstrated**.

### 4.4 Preclinical (why mass effect is large)

- ActRIIB-Fc increases muscle in rodents beyond GDF8 knockout alone because **activin** (and other ligands) also restrain mass (**Established** genetics/pharmacology).  
- Latres et al. 2017: selective GDF8 + activin A antibodies can mimic muscle hypertrophy of ActRIIB.hFc while **avoiding** some multi-ligand toxicities (bleeding attributed largely to **BMP9**; also glucose/pancreas and splenic erythropoiesis with broad trap in mice). **Probable** mechanistic map for ACE-031 AEs.  
- Cadena and related work: ACE-031 associated with greater LBM and fiber CSA in non–fiber-type-specific manner in models. **Established** preclinical hypertrophic efficacy.

### 4.5 What clinical data do **NOT** show

1. **No** approved therapeutic use.  
2. **No** RCT in healthy resistance-trained athletes for strength, hypertrophy under training, or bodybuilding outcomes.  
3. **No** safe long-term multi-dose regimen established—development stopped at the multi-dose pediatric stage for vascular signals.  
4. **No** proof that 6MWT/function improves in DMD (trend only, NS).  
5. **No** validated “gym dose” from pharmaceutical product (only trial mg/kg bands).  
6. **No** assurance that black-market ACE-031 equals clinical material.  
7. **No** long-term cancer, fertility, or CV outcomes at PED abuse durations.  
8. Mass PD does **not** equal athletic performance or injury resilience.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Lean mass / muscle volume hypertrophy

- **Phenotype:** Measurable LBM and MRI muscle volume increase within ~4 weeks (Attie).  
- **Pathways:** P1–P5.  
- **Chain:** SC ActRIIB-Fc → long exposure → sequester GDF8 + activins (+ others) → ↓ Smad2/3 restraint → fiber CSA / lean mass ↑.  
- **Clinical:** **Established** at 3 mg/kg single dose in postmenopausal women.  
- **Forum:** Overgeneralized to young males.  
- **Practical:** Effect size ~3–5% lean/volume after one high dose is large for a non-training study—but achieved in a discontinued drug with vascular risk.  
- **Certainty:** Human PD **Established**; athletic optimization **Speculative**.

### 6.2 Fat mass reduction / recomp

- **Pathways:** P7; secondary to muscle and ligand effects.  
- **Clinical:** Biomarker and DMD fat trends **Probable**. Class (bimagrumab) stronger fat-loss data.  
- **Certainty:** **Probable** body-comp shift direction; magnitude in athletes **Speculative**.

### 6.3 Bone

- **Pathways:** P8.  
- **Clinical:** Biomarker (Attie) and BMD trends (DMD) **Probable**.  
- **Certainty:** **Probable** co-effect; not a primary bone drug.

### 6.4 Strength / function / performance

- **Phenotype desired:** More force, better 6MWT, better lifts.  
- **Clinical:** DMD 6MWT **not significant**; Attie did not establish athletic strength endpoints.  
- **Class lesson:** Bimagrumab and other myostatin-pathway agents often increase mass more reliably than function.  
- **Certainty:** Functional athletic benefit of ACE-031 **Speculative / unproven**.

### 6.5 Disease modification in dystrophy

- **Intent:** More muscle, less decline in DMD.  
- **Outcome:** PD trends; program killed on safety before robust efficacy.  
- **Certainty:** Incomplete — **not** an available therapy.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism + counters

*Ester-style note does not apply; this is a multi-week biologic. Risk scales with dose, frequency, duration, and true product identity.*

### 7.1 Epistaxis (nosebleeds) and gum bleeding

1. **Phenotype:** Recurrent or spontaneous mucosal bleeding reported in multi-dose DMD program and noted in adult development.  
2. **Mechanism:** **Probable** sequestration of **BMP9** (and related vascular ligands) → impaired endothelial / ALK1 pathway vascular integrity. Not a myostatin-specific effect.  
3. **Counters**

| Pathway node | Counter | How | Clinical | Forum | Notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|-----|----------|-------|-------|-----------|
| Exposure | Do not use / stop drug | Removes trap | Program stop | — | Primary risk control | **Established** |
| Dose frequency | Lower/less frequent dosing | Less cumulative ligand trap | Trial design logic | — | Did not save program | **Probable** incomplete fix |
| BMP9 pathway | Selective GDF8±activin inhibitors instead of ActRIIB-Fc | Avoid BMP9 trap | Latres logic | — | Different molecules | **Probable** class strategy |
| Acute bleeding | Local care; medical eval if recurrent | Supportive | Standard | — | | **Established** first aid |

### 7.2 Telangiectasia (dilated skin vessels)

1. **Phenotype:** Visible small dilated vessels in skin.  
2. **Mechanism:** Same vascular/BMP-axis disruption **Probable**.  
3. **Counters:** Discontinuation; medical dermatology if persistent. No proven supplement reverse. **Probable** only stop-exposure.

### 7.3 Injection-site reactions

1. **Phenotype:** Erythema, local irritation (Attie).  
2. **Mechanism:** SC biologic local inflammation. **Established** mild AE.  
3. **Counters:** Rotate sites, proper SC technique, sterile product. **Probable**.

### 7.4 Mass without function / connective tissue risk

1. **Phenotype:** Larger muscle on imaging without proportional performance; theoretical injury if load jumps.  
2. **Mechanism:** Class pattern (myostatin pathway); tendon adaptation lag **Speculative**.  
3. **Counters:** Progressive loading, not ego lifts; patience on strength. **Probable** training practice.

### 7.5 Metabolic / glucose (class preclinical)

1. **Phenotype:** Impaired glucose tolerance with broad ActRIIB-Fc in mice (Latres)—not fully explained by GDF8+activin A alone.  
2. **Certainty for human ACE-031:** **Speculative**.  
3. **Counters:** Monitor fasting glucose if experimental exposure; do not assume metabolic free lunch.

### 7.6 Reproductive / FSH (theoretical)

1. **Mechanism:** Activin sequestration can lower FSH drive. **Established** physiology; **Speculative** clinical magnitude for ACE-031 trial doses (not the leading AE that killed the program).  
2. **Counters:** Hormone panels if exposed; avoid if fertility priority.

### 7.7 Immunogenicity / neutralizing antibodies

1. **Risk:** Fc fusion proteins can elicit ADA. **Probable** class risk; detailed ADA rates for ACE-031 less publicized than vascular AEs.  
2. **Counters:** Medical monitoring if true clinical product (unavailable); UGL unknown immunogen risk.

### 7.8 Contaminated / mislabeled black-market product

1. **Phenotype:** Infection, wrong dose, no active protein, or unexpected toxins.  
2. **Certainty:** Market risk **Established** (WADA documents black-market ACE-031).  
3. **Counters:** Do not use non-pharma biologics; analytical verification if research-only context.

### 7.9 Theoretical long-term oncologic risk

1. **Mechanism:** Chronic multi-ligand TGF-β family modulation is context-dependent in cancer biology.  
2. **Certainty:** **Speculative** for ACE-031; no long-term athlete cohorts.  
3. **Counters:** Avoid with active malignancy; no proven “cancer stack” protection.
