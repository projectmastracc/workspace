## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and classification

- **Scaffold:** 3,9-diazabicyclo[3.3.1]nonane (bispidine-related) with N9-methyl and N3-(pyridin-3-yl). Compact tertiary amine + pyridine — classic nAChR ligand architecture.
- **Identity lock:** CAS 946074-35-7; MW 217.31; formula C₁₃H₁₉N₃. Vendor InChIKey (cross-check with COA): KHQNUZUXDBHVNC-UHFFFAOYSA-N. XLogP3 ≈ 1.8 (computational).
- **Pharmacologic class:** **Functionally selective α6β2\* partial agonist**. Binding affinity is high at both α6-containing constructs **and** α4β2; differentiation is **functional** (efficacy / EC50 bias; limited agonism at non-α6 subtypes), **not** pure binding selectivity. Do not describe AN317 as an “α6-selective binder.”
- **Origin:** Characterized by Sandager-Nielsen, Ahring, Dyhring and collaborators (Saniona / Aniona). Companion same-wave paper: **AN6001** (α6β2\* PAM, PMID 31887290) — different molecule, same target program. AN317 is **not** listed on Saniona’s current clinical pipeline (epilepsy-focused SAN\* assets); treat as research tool / non-prioritized preclinical.
- **Physical form (suppliers):** Oil; purity claims ≥98–99% (research-grade). Catalog example: MCE **HY-160959**. **Research use only** — not GMP clinical material.

## 3. Complete pathway map (known + hypothesized)

α6β2\* nAChRs are heteropentameric ligand-gated cation channels. Native nigrostriatal/mesolimbic forms commonly include **α6β2β3** and **α4α6β2β3**; recombinant characterization often uses **α6/α3β2β3 chimeras** (α6 N-terminal domain on α3 backbone) with optional gain-of-function **V9′S** mutations for measurable currents. Absolute EC50 on engineered reporters is not identical to native human pentamers.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Striatal / terminal dopamine release

- **Phenotype:** Increased [³H]DA overflow from striatal terminals; theoretically improved motor drive and motivation via nigrostriatal / mesolimbic DA.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 → P2 → P4 → P5.
- **Mechanism chain:** AN317 binds α6β2\* orthosteric site → partial channel open → cation (esp. Ca²⁺) influx at terminal → Ca²⁺-triggered vesicle fusion → DA into cleft → postsynaptic D1/D2. Partial agonism may **cap** maximal release vs full agonists (nicotine) and can **antagonize** co-present full agonists at the same site. Endogenous CIN ACh tone (P4) sets the competition backdrop.
- **Clinical support:** **None** (human).
- **Forum support:** **None**.
- **Practical notes:** Preclinical only. Synaptosome assay is bath concentration–response around nM–µM, **not** a human mg dose. Certainty: **Probable** in rat tissue (paper); **Unknown** human.

### 6.2 SNc dopaminergic neuron activity

- **Phenotype:** Higher activity / Ca²⁺ in SNc DA neurons → support of nigrostriatal tone; dendritic DA can modulate local SN microcircuits.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 → P3 → P2/P5.
- **Mechanism chain:** Somatodendritic and/or network α6β2\* activation depolarizes or increases Ca²⁺ in SNc cells → increased firing or burst propensity → axonal DA release.
- **Clinical:** **None**.
- **Forum:** **None**.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** (slice, paper); **Speculative** translation to human SNc under disease.

### 6.3 Tremor reduction (DA-depletion model)

- **Phenotype:** Alleviation of high-frequency tremor after reserpine.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1–P5 → P14.
- **Mechanism chain:** Reserpine depletes monoamine vesicles → tremor phenotype; α6β2\*-driven residual DA signaling and basal ganglia rebalancing reduce tremor measures. Dose signal from secondary sources: **~1 mg/kg s.c.** in rat.
- **Clinical:** **None**.
- **Forum:** **None**.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** rodent; **Speculative** human PD tremor. Reserpine tremor ≠ full human PD spectrum.

### 6.4 Neuroprotection (MPP⁺ / class PD rationale)

- **Phenotype:** Improved survival of cultured mesencephalic DA neurons vs MPP⁺; hypothesized slowing of nigral loss.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 → P6 (± P16).
- **Mechanism chain:** Activity- and Ca²⁺-dependent survival programs (ERK/CREB, PI3K/Akt class — largely **hypothesized** molecular detail for AN317 specifically); reduced toxin-triggered apoptosis. Field: α6\* densely expressed on vulnerable DA neurons and **preferentially lost** in PD striatum (P18); nicotine epidemiology supports nAChR neuroprotection hypothesis (**class**).
- **Clinical:** **None** for AN317; nicotine PD disease-modification trials mixed/not established.
- **Forum:** **None**.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** in MPP⁺ culture; **Speculative** human disease modification. Model survival ≠ idiopathic PD progression.

### 6.5 L-DOPA-induced dyskinesia reduction (pipeline/conference)

- **Phenotype:** Fewer AIMs when co-administered in 6-OHDA + L-DOPA models.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P2, P5, P13.
- **Mechanism chain:** Class: chronic/acute α6/α4 nAChR stimulation alters DA release patterning and striatal plasticity that drives LID (not simply “more DA = more LID”). AN317 doses cited **0.1–3 mg/kg** in rats (conference).
- **Clinical:** **None**.
- **Forum:** **None**.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** rodent if conference data hold; **Speculative** human LID.

### 6.6 Cognition / motivation / “nootropic DA” (not a labeled claim)

- **Phenotype:** Theoretical PFC DA and attention/motivation boost.
- **Pathway IDs:** P2 (PFC hypothesized), P7 residual α4β2.
- **Mechanism:** Pipeline notes claim in vivo PFC DA microdialysis; field α6\* is more NAc/nigrostriatal-biased — cognitive claim is weaker than motor/PD positioning. Healthy users lack PD motor deficit that makes DA-terminal modulation clinically legible.
- **Clinical / forum:** **None**.
- **Certainty:** **Speculative**.

1. **Phenotype:** Queasiness, vomiting risk, appetite change — dominant AE for varenicline (~30% range class) and nicotine.
