## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and receptor pharmacology

- **Parent scaffold:** DHT (5α-androstan) with **17α-methyl** (oral first-pass survival) and **2-hydroxymethylene** (enolizable C2 formyl-equivalent). Fully named **17β-hydroxy-2-(hydroxymethylene)-17α-methyl-5α-androstan-3-one**.
- **Already 5α-reduced** — not a substrate for 5α-reductase; no local T→DHT amplification. Intrinsic androgenicity still drives skin/hair/prostate via AR. **5ARI (finasteride/dutasteride) does not block parent oxymetholone** (**Established** chemistry); only the test-base DHT component is 5ARI-sensitive.
- **AR affinity paradox:** Saartok et al. (1984) reported oxymetholone **relative binding affinity too low to determine** in muscle/prostate AR competitive assays — yet the compound is a strong clinical/anabolic agonist. NCI EVS paraphrases **low affinity but strong AR signaling** (definitional, not primary assay). Resolution candidates (**Probable–Speculative** mix): (i) active metabolite mestanolone, (ii) non-equilibrium or coactivator-biased signaling not captured by RBA, (iii) high free fraction from **very low SHBG affinity**, (iv) non-genomic AR. **Do not** read Saartok as “Anadrol has no AR activity.”
- **SHBG:** Very low affinity — **<5% of testosterone, <1% of DHT** (Saartok). High free fraction may partially offset low RBA. Binding data **Established**; potency link **Speculative–Probable**.
- **Aromatase:** **Not a substrate** — non-aromatizable DHT derivative (**Established** structural enzymology). Estrogenic phenotype therefore cannot be explained by E2 formation from oxymetholone itself.
- **Progestin receptor:** **No significant progestogenic activity** (class synthesis / secondary monographs). Gyno is **not** framed as classic 19-nor PR path; cabergoline default is usually misapplied lore.
- **Estrogen receptor (hypothesis):** Direct ER binding/activation proposed to explain water retention and rare gyno despite non-aromatization. **Speculative** molecular; wet phenotype **Established–Probable** (largely fluid/Na nodes).
- **3α-HSD:** **Poor substrate** — reduced inactivation in muscle relative to DHT; contributes to anabolic:androgenic skew in animal models. **Probable**.
- **Animal anabolic:androgenic ratios:** Roughly **1:2 to 1:9** (androgenic:anabolic convention in rodent assays). **Established** as historical animal tables; human tissue selectivity **Unknown** / **Speculative** for dosing.

**Design ceiling:** Ethical/legal barriers prevent modern recreational oxymetholone RCTs. Hengge 2003 is near-ceiling controlled human physiology for weight/BCM and liver signals at **medical** doses (100–150 mg/d) — not gym 50 mg isolation, not eugonadal athletes. Industry sponsorship (Unimed) on Hengge 2003: efficacy framing may lean optimistic; **toxicity findings still reported transparently** and remain the best human tox anchor.

### Key studies — measures, effects, limits

**FDA Anadrol-50 label (2004)**  
Indication: anemias from deficient red-cell production. Dose: **1–5 mg/kg/day** (usual effective **1–2 mg/kg/day**); medical courses often **months**, not weeks. Major warnings: **peliosis hepatis**, **hepatic tumors** (benign often androgen-dependent; malignant reported), **lipid changes (↓HDL ↑LDL)** increasing atherosclerosis risk, cholestatic hepatitis/jaundice, edema / electrolyte retention (Na, Cl, K, phosphate, Ca), HPTA suppression (oligospermia, testicular atrophy, libido Δ), virilization, premature epiphyseal closure, clotting factor **II/V/VII/X** suppression (warfarin potentiation / ↑PT), glucose intolerance, gynecomastia, CNS excitation/insomnia, polycythemia, prostate stimulation, iron deficiency observed on therapy (monitor iron/TIBC). Certainty for labeled risks: **Established** as regulatory warning set (incidence often unquantified at gym doses).

**Hengge et al. 2003 (*AIDS*; PMID 12646793)** — Phase III double-blind RCT, HIV wasting (n=89): oxymetholone **50 mg BID (100 mg/day)** vs **50 mg TID (150 mg/day)** vs placebo, **16 weeks**; open-label extension 50 mg BID weeks 17–32. Unimed Pharmaceuticals sponsorship.

| Endpoint | Approximate finding | Certainty for claim |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Body weight | Active arms ~**+3.0–3.5 kg** vs ~**+1 kg** placebo | **Established** direction in wasting |
| Body cell mass / LBM (BIA) | Clinically meaningful gains vs placebo (active arms) | **Established** in this population (composition method imperfect) |
| Hepatotoxicity | Enzyme elevations **dose-dependent**; ALT >5× range classically cited ~**27% BID (100 mg)** / ~**35% TID (150 mg)** active arms; jaundice/hepatomegaly confined to active arms; higher interruptions at TID | **Established** dose-related enzyme injury signal at 100–150 mg/d × 16 wk |
| Clinical context | Advanced HIV wasting on HAART; high prior hepatitis B/C serology | **Not** clean gym-user rates — do not paste 27–35% as universal gym incidence. Direction of liver risk **Probable** transferable; absolute rates **Unknown** at 50 mg × 4 wk |

Strengths: randomized, placebo-controlled, hard weight/BCM and safety labs. Limits: disease population, concurrent illness/meds/viral hepatitis background, not resistance-trained eugonadal men, 16-week medical duration ≠ 2–6 week gym use, BIA not DXA, no strength 1RM endpoints, no modern ApoB/echo package. **100 mg milligram dose overlaps “high gym”** — liver risk at 100 mg is more transferable than hypertrophy magnitude.

**Hengge et al. 1996 (*Br J Nutr*)** — Pilot advanced HIV: weight/appetite/Karnofsky signals; smaller, open to bias. Supports anabolic phenotype direction. Certainty: **Probable** historical signal only; do not pool with 2003 for effect sizes.

**Pavlatos et al. 2001 (*Clin Ther*; PMID 11440282)** — Comprehensive clinical review: sparse formal PK, anemia efficacy, HIV wasting signals, hepatotoxicity emphasis. Best single narrative package pre-Hengge phase III. **Not** a primary PK trial; do not over-cite for quantitative half-life. Certainty of synthesis: **Probable** inventory.

**Saartok et al. 1984 (*Endocrinology*; PMID 6539197)** — AR RBA for oxymetholone **too low to determine**; SHBG affinity **very low** (<5% T, <1% DHT). Foundational for affinity paradox. Certainty: **Established** assay result; physiologic interpretation open.

**Bi et al. 1992 (*J Steroid Biochem Mol Biol*; PMID 1567786)** — Human urinary metabolic map: C2 processing → **mestanolone** via 2-COOH decarboxylation; seco-diacids. Certainty: **Established** metabolite chemistry; contribution to clinical anabolism **Speculative**.

**LiverTox — Androgenic Steroids (NIDDK/NLM, ~2020)**  
- Transient enzyme elevations linked among orals particularly to **danazol and oxymetholone** (often transient).  
- **Bland cholestasis** class phenotype ~**1%** of patients on methyltestosterone/danazol/stanozolol/**oxymetholone** (dose-related; estimate imprecise).  
- Modest enzyme rise with deep jaundice classic for AAS cholestasis; prolonged pruritus/jaundice possible after stop.  
- **Peliosis** and **hepatic tumors** (adenoma, HCC) associated with prolonged high-dose 17α-alkyl use; oxymetholone featured in classic case series (Fanconi anemia multi-year exposures, etc.).  
Certainty: class hepatology **Established**; gym short-cycle absolute rates **Unknown**.

**Bond, Smit, de Ronde 2022 (*Front Endocrinol*)** — Modern AAS mechanism/side synthesis; notes oral 17α-alkyl lipid worsening; oxymetholone HIV liver context; already-5α-reduced structure. Certainty: **Probable** as class map (not replacement for Hengge/FDA/NTP).

**NTP TR-485 (1999) + RoC** — Female F344/N rats: **clear evidence** of carcinogenic activity (hepatocellular neoplasms). Male rats: equivocal evidence (subcutaneous fibroma/fibrosarcoma, adrenal pheochromocytoma, renal tubule adenoma signals). RoC: oxymetholone **reasonably anticipated to be a human carcinogen** (limited human evidence + animal data). Certainty: **Established** animal liver cancer signal; quantitative gym short-cycle cancer % **Unknown**.

**NCI EVS / secondary pharmacologic notes** — Low AR affinity yet strong signaling — aligns Saartok + clinical potency. **Probable** summary statement; not primary pharmacology.

**Class CV observational (transport, not oxy-only)**  
HAARLEM-type prospective multi-AAS cycles: BP rise, LDL↑ ApoB↑ HDL↓, HCT↑, reversible LV mass/LVEF signals. Oral 17α agents typically **worsen lipids more** than injectables. Oxymetholone-specific deltas **Unknown**; expect **at least** oral-class lipid injury (**Probable**). Never write “HAARLEM showed Anadrol does Y.”

### What clinical data do **NOT** show

1. No modern RCTs of oxymetholone for healthy athlete hypertrophy, strength, or hard CV outcomes.  
2. No validated human elimination half-life / modern plasma PK package.  
3. No quantified ER binding Ki for oxymetholone at physiologic free concentrations.  
4. No clean isolation of mestanolone’s fraction of total anabolic effect.  
5. No head-to-head athlete trials vs methandienone, oxandrolone, or stanozolol with matched monitoring.  
6. No incidence rates for peliosis/HCC at 50 mg × 6 weeks in healthy users (case literature dominated by prolonged medical/anemia exposures).  
7. No randomized evidence that TUDCA, NAC, milk thistle, or AI prevent oxymetholone hepatotoxicity or bloat.  
8. No HAARLEM-style isolation of oxymetholone monotherapy labs in large n.  
9. No long-term CV outcome trials (MI, stroke, HF) powered for Anadrol exposure.  
10. No proof that 2–6 week courses eliminate peliosis/tumor risk — only that absolute risk is lower with less cumulative exposure (**Probable**).  
11. HAARLEM-class CV reversibility after short cycles does **not** prove long-term plaque risk is zero if repeated orals are cycled chronically.  
12. Animal anabolic:androgenic ratios are **not** validated human tissue-selectivity percentages.

### Weighing rules

Forum data are high-density and high-bias (selection, UGL identity, stack confounding, expectancy, survivorship). Concordance among (1) near-universal rapid mass/strength/water reports, (2) Hengge weight/BCM and enzyme rates, and (3) label hepatology/edema/lipids is strong enough to treat **rapid anabolism, edema/BP, enzyme risk, and lipid injury** as **Probable–Established** human phenotypes without athlete RCTs. **Direct-ER gyno** remains a coherent **Speculative** mechanism for a **Probable** uncommon phenotype. “50 mg is safe” is a **relative** community risk-ranking, not a safety theorem. Never convert forum frequency into incidence %.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Skeletal muscle hypertrophy and nitrogen retention

- **Phenotype:** Rapid LBM-associated gains; strength out of proportion to training age when diet/training support; medical analog = BCM restoration in wasting.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P3, P10, P11, P18 (± P2).  
- **Mechanism chain:** Despite low measured RBA, effective AR pathway activation (free fraction high + possible mestanolone) → ARE-driven myocellular protein synthesis and nitrogen retention → net protein accrual. Poor 3α-HSD substrate status may prolong local active androgen in muscle relative to DHT.  
- **Clinical support:** Hengge weight/BCM **Established** in HIV wasting (~+3.0–3.5 kg vs ~+1 kg PBO over 16 wk at 100–150 mg); athlete DEXA RCTs absent.  
- **Forum support:** Very high.  
- **Practical notes:** Scale gains include substantial water; true myofibrillar fraction smaller than scale delta. Certainty wanted effect: **Probable** gym; **Established** wasting.

### 6.2 Strength

- **Phenotype:** Rapid 1RM / working-set jumps within 1–3 weeks common in lore.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P3, P19, P2 (speculative neural).  
- **Mechanism:** Myocellular force capacity + intracellular water/glycogen improving leverage and pump tolerance + possible CNS AR effects.  
- **Support:** Class oral AAS strength **Probable**; oxy-specific RCTs none. Forum very high. Certainty: **Probable**. Label regulatory language historically states AAS not shown to enhance athletic ability — that is legal framing, not a negative RCT of Anadrol strength in trained men.

### 6.3 Fullness, pumps, glycogen/water phenotype (wanted facet)

- **Phenotype:** “Blown up,” vascular pumps in some, soft look in others; glycogen supercompensation appearance.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P8, P9, P19.  
- **Mechanism:** Na/water retention + hypothesized ER tone + intramuscular glycogen/water. Same nodes drive BP and soft look — double-edged.  
- **Certainty:** Phenotype **Probable**; clean separation of “good pump” vs “bad bloat” **Speculative**.

### 6.4 Appetite / recovery (secondary)

- **Phenotype:** Some report increased appetite and training recovery; medical HIV data showed nutrition-score improvements in subsets.  
- **Pathway:** Multi-node (central AR, well-being, anabolism).  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative–Probable** heterogeneous.

### 6.5 Erythropoiesis (medical desired; gym side-adjacent)

- **Phenotype:** ↑ RBC production — therapeutic in labeled anemias; unwanted polycythemia in eugonadal athletes.  
- **Pathway:** P4 (± P16 iron).  
- **Certainty:** **Established** medical; gym HCT rise **Probable** (magnitude vs injectables **Unknown** oxymetholone-specific).

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

For each major side: phenotype → mechanism → pathway-matched counters. **Dose reduction and hard stop are first-line.** No counter map eliminates residual absolute risk. Speculative pathway-matched counters allowed; not sold as Established.

### 7.1 Hepatotoxicity (enzymes, cholestasis, peliosis, tumors)

1. **Phenotype:** Asymptomatic ALT/AST rise common at gym doses; fatigue, RUQ discomfort, dark urine, jaundice in more severe injury; rare **bland cholestasis** (~1% class estimate LiverTox); very rare **peliosis** / hepatic adenoma / HCC — literature weighted to **prolonged high-dose medical** use. Hengge: ALT >5× range ~**27% (BID ~100 mg)** and ~**35% (TID ~150 mg)** over 16 weeks in HIV wasting (trial-specific; high viral hepatitis background). NTP: clear female-rat hepatocellular neoplasm evidence.  
2. **Mechanism:** 17α-alkylation enables oral bioavailability but burdens hepatocytes (P6a–d): oxidative/mitochondrial stress → enzyme leak; impaired canalicular bile salt export → bland cholestasis; chronic proliferative/vascular lesions (peliosis, tumors) with long exposure. Concurrent alcohol, other orals, viral hepatitis amplify. Certainty: phenotype **Established**; molecular detail **Probable**. Keep sub-nodes separate: enzyme ≠ cholestasis ≠ peliosis ≠ tumor.

Short-cycle “reversible labs” ≠ zero long-term plaque contribution if orals are repeated for years. UGL purity uncontrolled. **No stack substitutes for exposure control.**

## 8. Practical use

**Honesty frame:** There is **no Established safe recreational protocol** for oxymetholone. Rows below are **harm-reduction patterns** from community practice + label/Hengge dose context + mechanism — labeled **Speculative** as protocol, not medical endorsement. Medical anemia dosing (1–5 mg/kg/day for months) is a different risk envelope and is **not** a gym template.

### Order of operations (engineering sequence)

1. **Exposure budget first** — dose (50 vs 100 mg), duration (weeks, not months), no stacked methylated orals.  
2. **Test base design** — enough for function under HPG shutdown; avoid oxy-only.  
3. **Baseline phenotype & labs** — BP series, CBC, CMP (ALT/AST/GGT/bili), lipids/**ApoB**, fasting glucose/HbA1c, total/free T, E2, LH/FSH; iron studies if relevant; CV/liver history; semen bank if fertility matters.  
4. **Lifestyle layer (non-negotiable)** — alcohol≈0, cardio, sodium awareness, sleep, no smoking.  
5. **On-cycle monitoring** — home BP; LFTs ~2–3 weeks; CBC/lipids mid/end; body weight vs waist (water vs tissue).  
6. **Side-triggered pharma** — BP meds if HTN; SERM if gyno; lipid drugs if ApoB high; stop oral if liver/BP break. Prefer node-matched over shotgun AI/caber.  
7. **Exit** — stop oxy (clearance days–weeks; half-life **Unknown**); continue test cruise or PCT (SERM ± hCG per goals); recheck LFTs/lipids/hormones 4–12 wk off.

### Contraindications (strong practical)

- Severe hepatic disease / active liver injury  
- Uncontrolled hypertension, known decompensated HF, high-risk fluid-retention states  
- Known hormone-sensitive prostate carcinoma (label androgen caution); male breast carcinoma (label)  
- Pregnancy (Category X class androgens)  
- Active desire for near-term fertility without banking  
- Concurrent therapeutic anticoagulation without specialist oversight  
- Adolescents with open epiphyses (non-medical)  
- Unwillingness to monitor labs/BP or stop

## 12. Open questions

1. **Human PK study** with modern LC-MS — elimination half-life, free fraction, steady-state, food effect — to retire **8–9 h folklore**.  
2. **ER binding / transcriptional assays** for oxymetholone and major metabolites at physiologic free concentrations — tests direct-ER hypothesis.  
3. **Mestanolone contribution fraction** — parent vs metabolite AR occupancy in human tissue.  
4. **Athlete RCT** (50 vs 100 mg vs placebo × 6 weeks) with DEXA, strength, LFTs, ApoB, BP, HCT — ethically hard but would lock dose–response.  
5. **Head-to-head vs methandienone** on mass vs water vs liver enzymes.  
6. **Cholestasis prevention RCT** of TUDCA/NAC vs placebo on 17α-alkyl orals — currently **Speculative** protection.  
7. **Short-cycle peliosis/tumor absolute risk** in healthy adults — currently **Unknown** (case literature dominated by prolonged medical use).  
8. **Echo/plaque trajectory** specific to repeated Anadrol cycles vs injectable-only matched users.  
9. **Glycogen/intramuscular water quantification** (MRS) — separates P19 from extracellular edema.  
10. **Female microdose safety** — likely remains unfavorable; formal data thin.  
11. **Resolution of Saartok affinity paradox** — coactivator bias, non-genomic AR, or metabolite-only efficacy.  
12. **Warfarin/DOAC interaction magnitude** at 50 mg gym doses vs medical multi-month dosing.  
13. **MR binding / renal epithelial transport** effects explaining extreme water phenotype.  
14. Exact canalicular transporter IC50 profile (BSEP/MRP2/etc.) for bland cholestasis.

Until these exist: potent oral 17α-alkyl DHT derivative with **Established** medical anabolism and **Established** hepatic/lipid/edema warnings, a **Probable** gym mass/strength phenotype, a **Speculative** direct-ER explanation for non-aromatizing estrogenicity, scientifically **Unknown** half-life (community 8–9 h = folklore), and **no defined safe recreational dose**.

## 14. Short boundary

Oxymetholone (Anadrol) is a Schedule III, FDA-labeled oral 17α-alkylated DHT-derivative AAS with exceptional short-horizon mass and strength pharmacology and a side-effect surface dominated by hepatotoxicity, edema/BP, dyslipidemia, HPG shutdown, and class cardiovascular risk — plus a non-aromatizing wet phenotype that remains mechanistically incompletely solved (fluid nodes **Probable**; direct ER **Speculative**). This document maps known and hypothesized pathways and harm-reduction counters; it is not a prescription, not evidence of a safe recreational regimen, and not a substitute for physician-led care. Laws and anti-doping rules apply.
