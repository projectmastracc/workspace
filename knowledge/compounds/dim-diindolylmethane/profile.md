# DIM (3,3'-Diindolylmethane) — Knowledge Profile

**Slug:** dim-diindolylmethane  
**Source briefing:** findings/dim-diindolylmethane/briefing.md  
**Research ID:** 2e6baadd  
**Updated:** 2026-07-31  

## Class
Dietary indole phytochemical; major acid-condensation product of I3C from cruciferous glucobrassicin. Forms: crystalline DIM, BR-DIM (clinical form), Infemin.

## Core mechanisms
- AhR selective modulator (SAhRM) → CYP1A1/1A2 induction → estrogen 2-hydroxylation preference
- ↑ 2-OHE1/16α-OHE1 ratio (**Established** human PD)
- Parent/total urinary estrogens often lower (**Probable**, large observational + trial direction)
- ↑ SHBG (**Probable**, Thomson women on tamoxifen)
- Competitive AR antagonist + blocks AR nuclear translocation (**Established** in vitro μM; **Probable** tissue PD at high BID BR-DIM)
- Aromatase inhibition: cell-level only — **not** pharmaceutical AI (**Speculative** clinical AI effect)
- Significant phase 1/2 metabolites; some ox-metabolites more potent AhR agonists than parent

## Practical band
- Start **50–100 mg/day** with food; common **100–200 mg/day**; trials **BR-DIM 150 mg BID**
- Cmax plateaus ~**200 mg** single dose BR-DIM
- Prefer BR-DIM to match literature; crystalline not mg-equivalent

## Hard stops / interactions
- **Do not** unsupervised stack with **tamoxifen** (↓ endoxifen **Established**)
- Not a default stack with pharmaceutical AIs
- Stop for severe headache, rash, clear hypo-E phenotype, libido collapse

## Evidence ceiling
- Biomarker/chemoprevention interest **≠** proven cancer treatment (Amare 2020 systematic review of 22 trials)
- HPV clearance: large null trial
- Forum AE: dark urine, headache, GI, low-E symptoms — high concordance with trials
