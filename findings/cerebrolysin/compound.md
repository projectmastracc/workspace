## 3. Mechanism & Pharmacology

### 3.1 Observed / claimed mechanism

Cerebrolysin is framed as a **pleiotropic neurotrophic-factor–mimetic** preparation rather than a single receptor ligand.

- **NTF modulation (preclinical + limited clinical narrative):** Influences expression/signaling related to BDNF, NGF, IGF-1, VEGF, and TNF-α pathways; stimulates neurons/glia to produce endogenous NTFs; may facilitate proNGF → active NGF conversion (manufacturer and review literature). (Rejdak & Neglur 2023, *Med Res Rev*, DOI [10.1002/med.21960](https://doi.org/10.1002/med.21960)) — **Probable** for multimodal NTF pathway engagement in models; **Speculative** for precise human receptor occupancy.
- **Downstream themes:** Neuroprotection (anti-excitotoxic / anti-apoptotic signaling narratives), neuroplasticity, neurogenesis support, angiogenesis facilitation, and anti-inflammatory modulation in injured CNS tissue — **Speculative-to-Probable** depending on endpoint (animal/mechanistic stronger than human molecular readouts).
- **Trk-adjacent framing:** Reviews and secondary sources discuss TrkA/TrkB–MAPK/ERK and PI3K/Akt cascade support; Cerebrolysin does **not** equal recombinant BDNF or NGF as a drug substance — **Speculative** for direct Trk agonism as sole MoA.

### 3.2 Pharmacokinetics / administration science

| Parameter | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Route | **IV infusion** (label primary for therapeutic volumes); IV slow push ≤10 mL undiluted; **IM** ≤5 mL undiluted. **Not oral.** |
| Dilution (IV infusion) | 10–50 mL diluted to ≥100 mL total with saline, Ringer’s, or 5% glucose; infuse over ~15–60 min (product materials; trials often ~20–30+ min) |
| Half-life / metabolites | Mixture of many small peptides/amino acids — classical single-entity t½ not meaningful; rapid systemic peptide clearance expected; CNS effects attributed to multimodal signaling during/after dosing window — **Speculative** for precise CNS PK |
| BBB | Low-MW peptides claimed to cross or act at injured BBB; human quantitative BBB PK sparse — **Speculative** |
| Onset framing | Acute neuroprotective intent (hours–days post injury) + neurorestorative cycles (weeks–months) |

**Analyst inference:** Clinical effects, if real, are more consistent with **acute injury/neurorecovery pharmacology** than chronic daily oral nootropic kinetics.

## 4. Documented Effects

Certainty labels follow project standards. Industry sponsorship is common across positive stroke/TBI/AD packages (Bornstein, Muresanu, Vester, Guekht networks; EVER Neuro Pharma). Independent/critical syntheses (Cochrane stroke, Cochrane VaD, AHA silence, AlzDiscovery) are more cautious.

### 4.1 Acute ischemic stroke (AIS)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Early neurological deficit (NIHSS ~day 21–30) | Bornstein et al. 2018 MA of 9 RCTs (N=1879): MW 0.60 favoring Cerebrolysin; NNT ~7.7 for clinically relevant early NIHSS change; 30–50 mL × 10–21 d, start within 72 h. PMID [29248999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29248999); DOI 10.1007/s10072-017-3214-0 | **Probable** (consistent early NIHSS signal; industry-linked analyses; CASTA overall primary less clear) |
| Early NIHSS (updated MA) | Patel et al. 2025 SR/MA 14 RCTs (N=2884): ΔNIHSS MD +1.39 (0.53–2.25); authors declared no industry funding. PMID [41018475](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41018475); DOI 10.7759/cureus.91054 | **Probable** |
| Day-90 functional independence (mRS 0–2) | Patel 2025: RR 1.31 (0.90–1.91), **NS**; Bornstein ordinal mRS day 90 positive mainly in moderate–severe subgroup (NIHSS >12, N=314, MW 0.61) | Unselected: **Unknown**. Moderate–severe day-90 enrichment: **Speculative** (post-hoc/subset) |
| All-cause death / hard outcomes | Cochrane 2023 (Ziganshina et al.): probably **no reduction** in death; insufficient for robust disability benefit claim. DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD007026.pub7 | **Established** that mortality benefit is **not** demonstrated |
| Landmark CASTA (Heiss 2012) | Large Asian double-blind RCT; overall primary outcome not a clear win for routine use; contributes to “mixed functional” narrative. Stroke 2012; DOI 10.1161/STROKEAHA.111.628537 | Context: limits overclaim of universal benefit |
| Motor recovery (CARS package) | Positive motor/recovery signals in dedicated rehab-oriented trials (e.g., Muresanu CARS 2016) — industry-linked | **Probable** for early motor/neurorecovery surrogates in selected programs |
| Hemorrhagic transformation | Patel 2025 pooled HT RR 0.55 (0.32–0.92) — exploratory favorable signal | **Speculative** (needs replication; not a label claim for HT prevention) |

**Stroke bottom line:** Early NIHSS improvement is the best-supported efficacy signal (**Probable**). Hard day-90 independence and mortality benefits are **not Established**. Cochrane concludes no clear clinical benefit on death and raises non-fatal SAE concerns in some analyses — **material tension** with industry MAs.

### 4.2 Traumatic brain injury (TBI)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Multidimensional recovery (moderate–severe TBI) | CAPTAIN II (Muresanu et al. 2020): 50 mL/d × 10 d then two cycles 10 mL/d × 10 d; multidimensional ensemble day 90 MW_combined 0.59 (0.52–0.66), P=0.0119; “small-to-medium” effect; safety ≈ placebo. PMID [31897941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31897941) | **Probable** (positive RCT; single-center phase IIIb/IV; industry-linked investigators) |
| CAPTAIN series synthesis | Vester et al. 2021 prospective MA of CAPTAIN series supports multidimensional benefit. PMID [33620612](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33620612) | **Probable** with COI caveat |
| Broader TBI SR | Jarosz et al. 2023: heterogeneous doses 10–50 mL; variable initiation and outcomes; directionally favorable but design quality uneven. PMC10046100 | **Probable** for short-term recovery signals; not Established standard of care |

### 4.3 Alzheimer’s disease (mild–moderate)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Cognition + global clinical change | Gauthier et al. 2015 MA of RCTs at **30 mL/day**: cognition SMD −0.40 at 4 wk (−0.66 to −0.13); global clinical change OR 3.32 at 4 wk and OR 4.98 at 6 mo; global benefit MW 0.57; safety ≈ placebo. PMID [25832905](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25832905); DOI 10.1159/000377672 | **Probable** for short–medium-term CGI/cognition benefit in mild–moderate AD |
| Earlier MA | Wei et al. 2007 CGI-focused support | Consistent direction |
| Disease modification / long-term slowing | No high-quality long-term disease-modification package comparable to modern AD drug standards | **Unknown** for disease modification |
| US standard of care | Not FDA-approved; not first-line vs AChEIs/memantine in US/UK pathways | Access/practice gap |

### 4.4 Vascular dementia

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Cognition / global function | Cochrane 2019 update (Cui et al.): 6 RCTs, N=597; modest benefit on cognition and daily function suggested; effect size may not be clinically important; methodological limitations; **not definitive**. DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD008900.pub3; PMID related [31710397](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31710397) | **Speculative-to-Probable** small benefit; **insufficient for routine recommendation** (Cochrane stance) |

### 4.5 Prevention of cognitive decline in healthy people / “anti-aging”

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Prevention in asymptomatic adults | No robust primary-prevention RCTs | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |
| “Reverse aging,” IQ boost, biohacking cognition | Forum/marketing claims without adequate controlled evidence | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |

### 4.6 Safety summary (effects profile)

- SAE and mortality generally **comparable to placebo** in stroke safety MAs (Strilciuc et al. 2021: 12 RCTs, N=2202; SAE RR ~0.99; DOI 10.3390/ph14121297; PMID [34959697](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34959697)) — **Probable** overall short-course tolerability.
- Cochrane stroke 2023: overall SAE may not differ, but **non-fatal SAEs** may increase with Cerebrolysin in some analyses — **Probable** signal of residual uncertainty; do not treat as “risk-free.”
- Common non-serious AEs: headache, dizziness, injection-site reactions, flushing, nausea, agitation/insomnia reports (label/trial narratives).

## 5. Documented Risks & Contraindications

### 5.1 Label contraindications (EVER product materials / SmPC-class info)

| Contraindication | Rationale / note |
|------------------|------------------|
| **Hypersensitivity** to Cerebrolysin or components | Porcine protein/peptide origin — anaphylactoid risk |
| **Epilepsy** / status epilepticus / major seizure disorders | Seizure facilitation concern; label contraindication |
| **Severe renal impairment** | Accumulation / safety concern for peptide–amino acid load |

### 5.2 Precautions & interactions

- **Additive CNS effects** possible with antidepressants / MAO inhibitors — special attention per product warnings (**Probable** caution; sparse dedicated DDI RCTs).
- **Allergic diathesis** — caution.
- **Pregnancy / lactation** — inadequate human data → avoid unless clear clinical necessity (**Unknown** safety → no elective use).
- **Porcine origin** — religious, cultural, and allergy considerations.
- **Infusion technique** — open-ampoule sterility, single-use, flush lines with NaCl; do not freeze; store ≤25°C protected from light (product materials).

### 5.3 Risk tiers

| Risk | Evidence | Certainty |
|------|----------|-----------|
| SAE/death ≈ placebo in short stroke courses | Strilciuc 2021; Patel 2025 | **Probable** |
| Non-fatal SAE increase (Cochrane) | Ziganshina 2023 | **Probable** residual harm signal in some analyses |
| Seizure provocation in predisposed | Label contraindication; occasional trial seizures in TBI series | **Probable** enough to honor absolute CI |
| Long-term repeated cycle safety (years) | Limited high-quality long-term surveillance outside marketed-country use | **Unknown** for multi-year cycling |
| Prion / zoonotic theoretical risk | Theoretical for brain-derived biologics; manufacturing controls claimed | **Speculative** residual; no modern outbreak signal in literature reviewed |
| Self-injection infection / dosing error (bro-science) | Parenteral DIY risk | **Established** procedural hazard independent of drug MoA |

### 5.4 Not recommended populations

- Healthy “optimization” users seeking cognitive enhancement — **Unknown** benefit.
- Active epilepsy / uncontrolled seizures.
- Severe CKD / renal failure.
- Known hypersensitivity to product.
- US patients expecting FDA-labeled access — product is **not** FDA-approved (import/personal-use legal gray areas vary by jurisdiction; not medical advice).

## 8. Practical Guidance

**Boundary:** Evidence-graded educational synthesis for contextualizing published regimens. Individual medical decisions require a qualified clinician and lawful access. **Unknown = no recommendation.**

### 8.1 Route (Established for pharmaceutical use)

- **Primary:** **IV infusion** of labeled concentrate diluted in ≥100 mL saline / Ringer’s / 5% glucose; infuse within ~15–60 minutes (product dosage materials).
- **IV injection:** up to **10 mL undiluted**, slow over ~3 minutes.
- **IM:** up to **5 mL undiluted**, slow over ~3 minutes — only for low-volume regimens.
- **Not oral.** Oral “Cerebrolysin” products are not equivalent to trial-grade parenteral therapy — **Unknown** efficacy → do not recommend as substitute.
- Ampoules: use immediately after opening; sterile technique; flush catheter with NaCl before/after; do not freeze.

### 8.2 Trial / label dosing by indication

| Indication | Daily dose | Schedule | Source trail | Certainty for *using this regimen when indicated* |
|------------|------------|----------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Acute ischemic stroke** | **20–50 mL IV** (trials often **30–50 mL**) | Start ASAP (trials within 12–72 h of onset); **10–21 days** once daily | EVER dosage sheet; Bornstein 2018 (30–50 mL × 10–21 d); CASTA/CARS-type protocols | **Probable** for early NIHSS-oriented adjunct dosing where registered; **not Established** for reducing death or guaranteeing day-90 independence |
| **TBI (moderate–severe)** | Acute: **20–50 mL IV** (CAPTAIN **50 mL** × 10 d); then cycles **10 mL** × 10 d × 2 (with off intervals) | Label: 7–30 days acute window; CAPTAIN: 50 mL d1–10 → off → 10 mL cycles d31–40 & d61–70 | CAPTAIN II PMID 31897941; EVER dosage (TBI 20–50 mL, 7–30 d) | **Probable** multidimensional recovery adjunct in moderate–severe TBI; confirm local protocol |
| **Alzheimer’s disease** | **10–30 mL IV** (many AD RCTs / Gauthier MA fixed **30 mL/day**) | **1 cycle:** 5 days/week × 4 weeks; **2–4 cycles/year** | EVER dosage recommendation PDF; Gauthier 2015 (30 mL/d focus) | **Probable** short–medium-term cognitive/global benefit in mild–moderate AD where accessible; not US SOC |
| **Vascular dementia** | **10–30 mL IV** | Same cyclic pattern as AD: 5 d/wk × 4 wk; 2–4 cycles/year | EVER dosage; Cochrane VaD doses 10–30 mL | **Speculative-to-Probable** small effect; **recommend against routine use** (Cochrane: insufficient) — specialist shared decision only |
| **Healthy cognitive enhancement / prevention of decline** | Any | Any | No adequate RCTs | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |

**Strength:** Product concentration **215.2 mg/mL** — dosing is conventionally expressed in **mL**, not mg, in clinical literature.

### 8.3 Timing & logistics

- **Stroke/TBI:** Earlier initiation (hours–days) matches trial design intent — **Probable** time-sensitive window for acute regimens.
- **Dementia cycles:** Intermittent IV courses (not lifelong daily oral) — plan outpatient infusion capacity.
- **With rehab:** Often studied as add-on to standard care ± rehabilitation (CARS, stroke recovery packages) — **Probable** that rehab context matters; drug is not a rehab substitute.

| **AChEIs** (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) | Different MoA (cholinergic vs multimodal NTF-mimetic); oral daily SOC in AD where approved; some literature explores Cerebrolysin ± ChEI | Prefer guideline AChEIs as foundational AD Rx where available (**Established** role). Cerebrolysin is **optional adjunct** in registered markets (**Probable** short-term add-on signal in selected trials) — not a first-line AChEI replacement. |

### 8.5 Monitoring & harm reduction

| Domain | Action | Certainty |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Pre-treatment** | Screen epilepsy/seizure history, renal function (eGFR/Cr), allergy/porcine product sensitivity, concurrent MAOI/antidepressants | **Established** good practice from label CIs |
| **During infusion** | Observe for hypersensitivity, BP changes, agitation, headache; sterile IV technique | **Established** parenteral safety practice |
| **Neurologic** | Track indication-specific scales (NIHSS, mRS, GCS/GOSE, ADAS-cog/CGI as appropriate); stop if new/worsening seizures | **Probable** |
| **Renal** | Avoid in severe impairment; reassess if AKI develops mid-course | **Established** from CI |
| **SAE watch** | Pneumonia, PE, cardiac events, hemorrhagic complications common in stroke/TBI populations — attribute carefully; Cerebrolysin not proven to increase death but Cochrane flags non-fatal SAE imbalance in some data | **Probable** need for standard acute-care monitoring |
| **Stop criteria** | Anaphylaxis/hypersensitivity; recurrent seizures; severe renal failure; patient withdrawal; lack of meaningful clinical trajectory after completed cycle (shared decision) | **Established** clinical judgment |

### 8.6 Access & legal caveats

- Registered widely outside the US; **not FDA-approved**.
- US personal import / “research peptide” gray markets are **not** equivalent to GMP hospital product and may be unlawful — **do not recommend illegal acquisition**.
- Where prescribed lawfully: use licensed product, medical supervision, and local SmPC.

### 8.7 Explicit “not recommended when”

| Scenario | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Prevention of cognitive decline in healthy people | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |
| “Reverse aging” / IQ biohacking protocols | **Unknown** → **no recommendation**; **recommend against** unsupervised parenteral DIY |
| Active epilepsy / severe renal failure / hypersensitivity | Contraindicated — **do not use** |
| As sole therapy instead of reperfusion, BP control, or approved dementia SOC | **Recommend against** |
| Oral substitution for IV trial regimens | **Unknown** equivalence → **no recommendation** |

### 8.8 Bottom-line regimen map (actionable)

1. **AIS (registered markets, specialist care):** Consider **30 mL IV daily × 10–21 days** early after onset as **adjunct** to standard stroke care if logistics allow — aim for early neurological recovery (**Probable**), not proven mortality reduction.  
2. **Moderate–severe TBI:** CAPTAIN-style **50 mL × 10 d + two 10 mL cycles** is the best-specified recovery package (**Probable** multidimensional benefit).  
3. **Mild–moderate AD (where registered):** **10–30 mL** (evidence densest at **30 mL**) **5 days/week × 4 weeks**, **2–4 cycles/year** — short-term cognitive/global support (**Probable**); maintain AChEI/memantine per local guidelines when indicated.  
4. **VaD:** Prefer **not routine** (**Speculative**/insufficient).  
5. **Healthy / anti-aging:** **No recommendation.**

## 9. Compound Context Summary

Cerebrolysin is a **porcine brain-derived multimodal neuropeptide infusion** (EVER Neuro Pharma), registered in many countries for stroke, TBI, and dementia but **not FDA-approved**. Mechanistically it is an **NTF-mimetic mixture** (BDNF/NGF/IGF-1/VEGF–related narratives), not pure recombinant BDNF. Best human signal: **early post-stroke NIHSS** and **selected TBI multidimensional recovery** (**Probable**); AD short-term cognition/global change (**Probable** at ~30 mL cyclic IV); VaD modest/uncertain. Mortality and universal day-90 independence are **not Established**; Cochrane is skeptical on hard AIS outcomes. Label dosing: stroke/TBI **20–50 mL IV** courses; dementia **10–30 mL** cycles 5 d/wk × 4 wk, 2–4×/year. Contraindications: **epilepsy, severe renal impairment, hypersensitivity**. **Prevention in healthy people and anti-aging self-injection = Unknown → no recommendation.** Use only as supervised IV adjunct where legal and indicated.
