# ART27.13 (AZD1940 / NEO-1940) — Research Briefing

**Research ID:** dd436905  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Status:** Investigational dual CB1/CB2 agonist — **not approved** (FDA/EMA/MHRA)  
**Primary developmental context:** Cancer anorexia-cachexia syndrome (CACS) supportive care  
**Historical context:** Failed acute/experimental pain program (AstraZeneca)  
**Audience:** Oncology supportive care, body-composition interest, compound/performance readers  

**Dual narrative (non-negotiable):**

| Era | Owner | Indication | Outcome | Trust |
|-----|--------|------------|---------|-------|
| **Pain — FAILED** | AstraZeneca | Acute dental postop + capsaicin hyperalgesia | **Null analgesia** at 400–800 μg; hard AE rates | **High** (peer-reviewed RCTs; sponsor null = anti-marketing) |
| **Cachexia — interim POSITIVE** | Artelo (via NEOMED/adMare) | CACS (CAReS Ph1/Ph2) | Weight / LBM / activity **signal** at top dose | **Low–moderate** (interim, n=5 top dose, sponsor press, not confirmatory) |

Pain nulls do **not** kill cachexia plausibility (different endpoints/biology). Cachexia interim does **not** create recomp, PED, or nootropic indications.

**Hard framing:** Not a nootropic. Not a validated PED. Investigational CACS agent only. Clinical doses are **micrograms (μg)** — never confuse with mg (10³ dosing error).

**Practical PK implications**

1. Long t½ → missed single day is not catastrophic; **washout and titration are slow**.

3. Microgram potency → compounding/scale errors dominate non-clinical handling risk.  
4. Tmax early–mid post-dose aligns with timing of acute postural symptoms.

**Effect-size credibility:** ~12 percentage-point weight separation is **clinically meaningful if confirmed**. With n=5 vs n=6 and SD≈9, estimates are **unstable** (winner’s curse, titrator/completer selection, interim cut, sponsor packaging). Do **not** use +6% vs −5% as a planning effect size for healthy users or as transferable recomp data.

**Methodologist phrase:** *“Very small blinded interim suggests large weight/LBM separation at top dose but is statistically and operationally fragile; await full primary analysis.”*

## 8. Adverse Effects — Mechanisms, Pathways, Counters

**Principle:** Pain-era single-dose **800 μg** in relatively healthy dental subjects produced **very high** orthostatic rates. Cancer Ph1/2 reports **better overall tolerability** at titrated doses ≤1300 μg in a different population — **not** proof that orthostatic risk is gone. Treat orthostasis as **molecule-established liability**.

Do **not** swap dental AE % with CAReS “related AE” % — different population, regimen, duration, and reporting frames.

## 14. Bottom Line & Boundaries

### Integrated causal model

ART27.13 is a **microgram-potent dual CB1/CB2 full agonist** engineered for **peripheral predominance**. Peripheral **CB1 (P1)** drives the intended **orexigenic / energy-storage** program and the **vascular-autonomic** program that produces **orthostatic dizziness and hypotension (P4)** — the dominant historical AE cluster at **800 μg** single dose. Peripheral **CB2 (P2/P6)** supplies a **preclinical muscle-protection** hypothesis that may help explain interim **lean mass** gains beyond pure refeeding. **CNS restriction is incomplete (P3):** humans show mild “high/sedated” VAMS effects. **GI CB1 (P8)** creates a nausea paradox. **Analgesia (P9) is closed against** in two human acute pain models. **Long half-life (P10)** means daily dosing accumulates over days — AE risk is not “day-1 only.”

### Honest dual-status summary

**Pain (high trust):** AZD1940 was evaluated by AstraZeneca in peer-reviewed trials for acute and experimental pain and **did not demonstrate analgesic efficacy**, while producing high rates of postural dizziness and other AEs at **800 μg**. Naproxen assay sensitivity confirms the dental null is credible, not underpowered hope.

**Cachexia (interim, low n, sponsor):** The same chemical entity, as ART27.13, is under Artelo development for cancer anorexia-cachexia. **Interim Phase 2 company disclosures (Sep 2025)** report weight and lean-mass gains versus placebo at the highest dose (**1300 μg n=5** for the headline weight contrast: **+6.38%** vs PBO **−5.42%**; LBM **+4.23%** vs **−3.15%**), with wearable activity trends and sponsor-claimed mild–moderate related AEs. These data are **interim, small-n, selection-prone (titrators), high-variance, sponsor-controlled, and not yet a complete peer-reviewed primary clinical paper.** Phase 1 open signal (64% stabilize/gain) is hypothesis-generating only.

None of that makes ART27.13 approved, benign, a smart drug, or a physique PED. The pain program remains the clearest map of acute AE liability; the cachexia program remains **unfinished science**.

### Open gaps (research debt)

- Full CAReS Ph2 final n, ITT, dose–response, fat vs LBM partition, anorexia scale effect sizes  
- Formal multi-dose PK at 1300 μg; CYP/transporter DDI matrix  
- Standing BP under chronic CAReS dosing vs AZ single-dose 80% dizziness  
- Tumor outcome / checkpoint interaction data  
- Head-to-head vs anamorelin or megestrol  
- Independent replication of myotube CB2 protection + clinical biomarkers  
- Abuse liability beyond mild VAMS  

### Short boundary

- **Not approved** anywhere for any use.  
- **Not** a consumer supplement, **not** a nootropic, **not** a validated PED.  
- **Investigational CACS agent** only — trial/oncologist context.  
- Clinical doses are in **micrograms (μg)** — unit errors are dangerous.
