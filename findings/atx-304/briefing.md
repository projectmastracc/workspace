# ATX-304 (O304) — Compound Profile

**One maximum-depth research document.** Audience: metabolic / longevity / performance users evaluating AMPK network activators.

---

## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | **ATX-304** (also **O304**, **O-304**, CAS **1261289-04-6**); formerly Betagenon / Baltic Bio; now **Amplifier Therapeutics** (Cambrian Bio pipeline). Class: oral small-molecule **pan-AMPK activator** + **mitochondrial activator** (“AMPK Network Activator”). |
| **Primary use cases** | Obesity / visceral + liver fat; prediabetes–T2D glucose and insulin resistance; cardiometabolic risk (BP, microvascular perfusion); experimental exercise-mimetic / energy-expenditure support; MASLD/MASH interest (preclinical). |
| **Bottom line** | ATX-304 keeps AMPK phosphorylated (suppresses dephosphorylation at T172) and also increases mitochondrial proton conductance / respiration, so both **energy supply** (glucose/FA uptake) and **demand** (mito activity) rise. In humans: **TELLUS** Phase IIa (28 d, 1000 mg/day on metformin) lowered FPG (~0.6 vs 0.1 mM placebo in post-hoc FPG band), HOMA-IR, BP (~−5.8/−3.8 mmHg), and improved calf microvascular indices; **Phase 1b** (2026, 400 mg QD × 8 wk, obesity + prediabetes) raised **RMR ~8%**, cut liver fat / VAT / TGs, raised adiponectin, with **placebo-like TEAE profile** and **no core-temp or HR uncoupling signals**. Weight loss in that Phase 1b was minimal at that exposure—expected per company. Strong preclinical fat-loss, muscle-sparing, endurance, and MASLD signals. **Not approved; not a consumer supplement.** Grey-market “research” product quality and dose are **Unknown**. |
| **Biggest upsides** | ↑ RMR / EE (**Probable** human Phase 1b; **Established** rodent); ↓ liver fat / VAT / TGs (**Probable** Phase 1b); insulin sensitivity / FPG on metformin (**Probable** Phase IIa); muscle-sparing fat loss + endurance (**Probable** animals; human weight-loss PoC pending REWIRE-2); no cardiac hypertrophy unlike some prior AMPK allosteric drugs (**Probable** preclinical) |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Investigational only — long-term human safety thin beyond ~4–16 wk exposures (**Established** gap); grey-market purity risk (**Unknown**); mild early food-intake drop in some mouse regimens then rebound hyperphagia (**Probable** animal); elevated bile acids / BUN in MASLD mice (**Probable** animal, human translation **Unknown**); theoretical mito-uncoupling heat/tachycardia (not seen clinically at tested exposures—**Probable** safety at those doses); hypoglycemia risk if stacked with strong antihyperglycemics (**Speculative**); eGFR hemodynamic drop (post-hoc clinical signal)—can be useful for hyperfiltration but needs monitoring (**Probable**) |
| **Stack role** | Peripheral oral **exercise-mimetic metabolic lever**: fat oxidation + EE + insulin sensitivity without CNS appetite agonism. Complementary (not substitute) to GLP-1RAs in animals; potential co-agent for body-recomp / MASLD / T2D stacks once pharmaceutical-grade access exists. |

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

| Item | Detail | Certainty |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Identity** | Small heterocyclic oral molecule; CAS 1261289-04-6. Synonyms: O304, O-304, ATX-304, “formerly 0304.” | **Established** |
| **Class** | Pan-AMPK activator (β1- and β2-containing heterotrimers); dual mitochondrial activator via increased proton conductance / mild uncoupling phenotype in cells | **Established** (AMPK); **Probable** (mito) |
| **MoA class distinction** | Does **not** primarily allosterically activate AMPK via ADaM site (unlike MK-8722 / PF-739 class). **Suppresses PP2C-mediated dephosphorylation of p-T172** on AMPKα—ADP-like protection of the active phospho form. Companion ADA 2026 data: AMPK activation **not secondary to ATP depletion**; ATP preserved up to solubility limit (~20 µM) | **Established** |
| **CNS** | **Peripherally restricted**; does **not** cross BBB in rodents | **Established** (rodent) |
| **Route** | Oral (tablet/suspension in trials) | **Established** |
| **Half-life / steady state** | Long plasma t½; human steady state ~**day 14** at clinical dosing | **Probable** |
| **Clinical doses used** | TELLUS: **1000 mg/day** × 28 d (T2D + metformin). Phase 1b: **400 mg once daily** × 8 wk (+ optional OLE). REWIRE Phase 2 plans: higher exposures | **Established** (sponsor-reported) |
| **Rodent diet doses** | Common research range ~0.25–2 mg/g chow; gavage often ~20–100 mg/kg/day depending on endpoint | **Established** (methods papers) |
| **Food / formulation** | Clinical reformulation bridging studies reported historically (Betagenon); food effect details sparse in public domain | **Unknown** detail |

**Practical PK takeaway:** Expect delayed full effect (weeks), not same-day stimulant-like energy. Long t½ favors QD dosing and slow washout.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Phase IIa — TELLUS (O304, Betagenon, 2017–2018)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Design** | Exploratory randomized, parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled, **28 days** |
| **Population** | **n ≈ 65** T2D on **metformin ≥3 months**; HbA1c inclusion 6.5–9.0%; MRI-capable |
| **Dose** | **O304 1000 mg/day** vs placebo |
| **Primary framing** | Safety + FPG exploration at single dose level |
| **Glucose (post-hoc FPG band >7 to <13.3 mmol/L)** | ΔFPG day 28: **−0.60 mM** O304 (n=25) vs **−0.10 mM** placebo (n=24); between-group significant (Wilcoxon absolute *P*≈0.01). Effect emerged **days 21–28** (matches late steady state) |
| **HOMA-IR** | Significant within-O304 reduction at day 28 (absolute *P*≈0.01); not in placebo |
| **Blood pressure** | O304: **SBP −5.8 mmHg**, **DBP −3.8 mmHg**; placebo slight increases (+1.2 / +0.9). Within-O304 and some between-group relative tests significant. HR unchanged |
| **Microvascular (calf MRI T2\*)** | ↑ hyperemic perfusion indices (e.g., Δ-T2\* between-group *P*≈0.026); benefit clearest in low baseline perfusion subgroup (longer TTP) |
| **Safety** | Sponsor/paper: well tolerated; no SAEs attributed in CORDIS summary of study completion (59/60 completed per EU report variants) |
| **Limits** | Short duration; single dose; FPG analysis partly post-hoc; sponsor-linked investigators; small n; not modern obesity-primary trial |
| **Certainty** | Metabolic/vascular signals **Probable**; magnitude modest over 28 d |

**Source:** Steneberg et al., *JCI Insight* 2018;3(12):e99114. DOI 10.1172/jci.insight.99114. EU AMPK-DIAB / CORDIS 754268 reporting.

### 4.2 Phase 1b — ATX-304 obesity + prediabetes (Cambrian / Amplifier, ADA 2026)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Design** | Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **8 weeks** + optional open-label extension |
| **Population** | **n = 23** adults with **obesity + prediabetes** |
| **Dose** | **ATX-304 400 mg QD** |
| **Key outcomes (sponsor ADA #1782-P / press)** | **RMR +8%** (*P*<0.01); **↓ plasma TGs** (*P*<0.01); **↑ adiponectin** (*P*<0.01); **↓ liver fat (MRI-PDFF)** (*P*<0.05); **↓ VAT** (*P*<0.05); **minimal weight loss** at this exposure (expected); hepatic DNL reduction noted in high-exposure subgroup (n=6); **no** clear MMTT glucose / ISI / leptin effects reported in poster summary |
| **Safety** | TEAEs mostly mild, frequency similar to placebo; **no** AEs of mitochondrial failure phenotype; **no** ↑ continuously monitored core body temperature; **no** ↑ 24-h HR; OLE without new signals |
| **Mechanistic companion (#1788-P)** | AMPK activation **not** driven by ATP drop; ATP preserved |
| **Limits** | Small n; company-presented (poster/press—not yet large peer-reviewed full paper as of this briefing); exposure below planned weight-loss Phase 2; short duration |
| **Certainty** | RMR + ectopic fat / lipid signals **Probable**; weight-loss efficacy at higher dose **Unknown** (await REWIRE-2) |

**Source:** Cambrian Bio ADA 2026 Scientific Sessions communications (June 2026); company Phase 1b poster abstract materials.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Increased energy expenditure / RMR

- **Phenotype:** Higher resting burn; potential recomp advantage; animals lose fat while eating same or more.
- **Pathways:** P1–P3, P6–P7 (AMPK network + mito proton leak + adipose/BAT FA oxidation).
- **Chain:** Sustained pAMPK → ACC off → FA ox on; mito conductance ↑ → substrate oxidation without proportional ATP gain → whole-body EE ↑ → human RMR +8% at 400 mg.
- **Clinical:** Phase 1b RMR **Probable**. TELLUS not RMR-primary.
- **Forum:** Endurance/energy anecdotes **Speculative**.
- **Practice:** Effects lag steady state (~2 wk). Not a stimulant.

### 6.2 Liver fat / VAT / TG reduction

- **Phenotype:** Better hepatic and visceral adiposity markers.
- **Pathways:** P5, P6, P11, P12.
- **Chain:** ↓ DNL + ↑ β-oxidation + FA uptake remodeling; oxidized lipid burden ↓ in mice; human MRI-PDFF / VAT / TG moved in Phase 1b.
- **Clinical:** **Probable** (small Phase 1b).
- **Certainty:** Direction **Probable**; dose for clinical MASLD indication **Unknown**.

### 6.3 Glucose / insulin sensitivity

- **Phenotype:** Lower FPG, lower HOMA-IR, less β-cell strain.
- **Pathways:** P4, P10 + indirect fat loss.
- **Chain:** Muscle glucose uptake (insulin-independent component) → less insulin demand → β-cell rest; metformin combo synergistic in mice.
- **Clinical:** TELLUS **Probable** modest FPG/HOMA; Phase 1b MMTT/ISI null per summary—exposure/population dependent.
- **Practice:** Expect multi-week lag; monitor if on insulin/sulfonylureas.

### 6.4 Blood pressure / microvascular function

- **Phenotype:** Lower BP; better calf hyperemia.
- **Pathways:** P9 (+ cardiac P8 in animals).
- **Clinical:** TELLUS SBP/DBP and MRI perfusion **Probable**.
- **Performance angle:** Better muscle perfusion may support endurance (**Speculative** human performance).

### 6.5 Muscle-sparing fat loss / exercise capacity

- **Phenotype:** Animals: fat ↓, lean largely preserved; ↑ treadmill distance, ↓ lactate; cardiac stroke volume ↑ without hypertrophy.
- **Pathways:** P4, P6–P8, P3.
- **Human:** Phase 1b not powered for body-recomp success; REWIRE-1 (muscle function / lipid) and REWIRE-2 (weight loss PoC) are the decisive trials.
- **Certainty:** Preclinical **Probable**; human performance **Speculative** until Phase 2.

### 6.6 Longevity / “aging metabolism”

- **Phenotype:** Marketing claim—restore age-declined AMPK network.
- **Pathways:** P1, P3, P16, P17.
- **Evidence:** Age-related AMPK decline literature is real; ATX-304 as human geroprotector is **Speculative**. Worm lifespan ≠ human lifespan.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

Clinical TEAEs at pharmaceutical doses: predominantly **mild**, **placebo-like** (Phase 1b). Below: material or theoretical sides for users who will still want node-level counters.

### 7.1 Gastrointestinal discomfort / early anorexia then hyperphagia (mainly animal)

1. **Phenotype:** Mouse MASLD model: transient intake drop with weight loss, then **20–30% higher** food intake while fat remains low.
2. **Mechanism:** Possible taste/diet formulation + rapid EE shift + adipose mobilization; not classic GLP-1 nausea pathway.
3. **Counters**

| Pathway node | Counter | How | Clinical | Forum | Practice | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|-----|----------|-------|----------|-----------|
| GI motility / nausea nonspecific | Dose titration, take with food if formulated for it | Reduces peak GI load | Sparse | Common sense | Start low if grey-market | **Speculative** |
| Energy deficit drive | Adequate protein + structured meals | Matches higher EE | — | — | Don’t under-eat protein if recomp goal | **Probable** lifestyle |
| Bile acid / liver remodeling | Avoid stacking heavy hepatotoxins; monitor LFTs | P11-related stress | Animal bile acids ↑ | — | Labs if long run | **Speculative** |

### 7.2 Mitochondrial uncoupling phenotype (heat, HR, malaise) — **theoretical**

1. **Phenotype:** Classical uncouplers (DNP): hyperthermia, sweating, tachycardia—**dangerous**.
2. **Mechanism:** Excess proton leak → heat, ATP crisis at high dose.
3. **ATX-304 data:** Company stresses **balanced** supply+demand; Phase 1b **no** core temp or 24-h HR AEs; ATP preserved in cells. Still, dose/quality matters outside trials.
4. **Counters**

| Pathway node | Counter | How | Clinical | Forum | Practice | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|-----|----------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Excess thermogenesis | Stop drug; cooling; medical care if severe | Removes driver | Trial monitoring clean at 400 mg | — | Hard stop if fever/HR surge | **Established** first aid |
| ATP stress | Avoid stacking other uncouplers (DNP, high-dose usnic acid, aggressive 2,4-DNP analogs) | Prevents additive leak | — | — | **Do not stack uncouplers** | **Probable** harm avoidance |
| Hydration / electrolytes | Fluids, Na/K as appropriate | Supports CV strain | — | — | Basic | **Speculative** for this drug |

### 7.3 Hypoglycemia (stacked antihyperglycemics)

1. **Phenotype:** Low glucose if insulin/SU + improved uptake.
2. **Mechanism:** P4 + improved HOMA-IR.
3. **Counters**

| Node | Counter | Notes | Certainty |
|------|---------|-------|-----------|
| Excess insulin action | Reduce insulin/SU under clinician | Standard | **Probable** |
| Glucose monitoring | CGM / fingersticks early weeks | Detect lag-onset FPG drop | **Probable** |
| Carb availability around hard training | Timed carbs | Performance safety | **Speculative** |

### 7.4 eGFR dip / renal hemodynamics

1. **Phenotype:** Post-hoc: reduced eGFR, rapid/reversible—framed as anti-hyperfiltration.
2. **Mechanism:** **Speculative** detail (afferent/efferent tone, tubular AMPK, BP drop contribution).
3. **Counters / monitoring**

| Node | Action | Certainty |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Hemodynamic GFR | Baseline + follow-up creatinine/eGFR; don’t panic at small acute dip if stable | **Probable** monitoring |
| Volume depletion | Avoid NSAID + aggressive diuretic stacks without oversight | **Probable** |
| True injury | Stop if rising creatinine progressive, oliguria, or AKI symptoms | **Established** medicine |

### 7.5 Lean mass / anabolic interference (gym context)

1. **Phenotype:** Concern that chronic AMPK ↔ mTOR opposition could blunt hypertrophy.
2. **Mechanism:** P15 class biology; animals often **preserve lean** during fat loss; slight lower lean *gain* in some long MASLD cohorts.
3. **Counters**

| Node | Counter | Certainty |
|------|---------|-----------|
| mTOR training signal | Keep progressive resistance training + protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg | **Probable** lifestyle |
| Energy availability | Don’t combine deep deficit + ATX + overtraining | **Probable** |
| Periodization | Emphasize ATX in cut/recomp blocks more than pure bulk (**Speculative** strategy) | **Speculative** |

### 7.6 Cardiac theoretical risks (context: failed AMPK drugs)

1. **Phenotype:** MK-8722-class: cardiac hypertrophy / glycogen accumulation in animals.
2. **ATX-304:** Opposite preclinical pattern—↓ cardiac glycogen, ↑ stroke volume, **no** heart weight ↑ in mice/rats to 6 months high dose.
3. **Human:** No Phase 1b temp/HR uncoupling signals; dedicated cardiac imaging sparse publicly.
4. **Counters:** Avoid stacking with known cardiotoxic agents; investigate chest pain/palpitations; future REWIRE data will matter. Certainty of human cardiac safety long-term: **Unknown**.

| Node | Counter | Certainty |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Wrong identity / dose | Pharmaceutical trial only is rational default; third-party HPLC if still using RC | **Unknown** quality |
| Solvent residues | Avoid oils from unknown synthesis | **Speculative** risk |

## 10. Monitoring, contraindications, stop criteria

### Monitoring (if someone is on pharmaceutical trial or supervised access)

| Marker | Why | Frequency suggestion |
|--------|-----|----------------------|
| Weight, waist, DEXA/BIA if available | Fat vs lean | Every 4 wk |
| FPG / HbA1c / fasting insulin | Glycemic efficacy | Baseline, 4–8 wk |
| Lipid panel + ALT/AST/GGT | Liver/lipid program | Baseline, 4–8 wk |
| Creatinine / eGFR | Hemodynamic signal | Baseline, 2–4 wk, then periodic |
| BP and resting HR | Known effects | Weekly early |
| Optional: MRI-PDFF, RMR (research) | Matches Phase 1b endpoints | Trial context |
| Core temp if uncoupling feared | Safety | If symptoms |

### Relative caution populations

- Pregnancy / lactation: **no data** — avoid (**Established** gap).
- Advanced CKD: eGFR effects need specialist framing.
- Active eating disorder / extreme deficit: EE↑ may worsen underfueling.
- Concurrent strong uncouplers: avoid.

### Stop criteria

- Fever, inappropriate tachycardia, severe diaphoresis (uncoupling alarm).
- Progressive creatinine rise / AKI picture.
- Severe hypoglycemia.
- Hepatitis-range LFT spikes.
- Allergic / systemic reaction.

| **Exercise** | True AMPK + multi-organ | Gold standard | Adherence; ATX aims to mimic pieces pharmacologically |
| **GLP-1RAs** | Weight, glucose, CV | Large WL % proven | Lean loss risk, cost, GI; less direct EE |
| **SGLT2i** | Cardiorenal, mild EE/calorie loss | Outcome trials | GU infections; not strong RMR drug |
| **MK-8722-class pan-AMPK allosterics** | Direct AMPK | Potent glucose uptake | Cardiac hypertrophy/glycogen liability in animals |
| **AICAR** | AMPK | Research history | Poor oral PK, practicality |
| **MOTS-c / exercise peptides** | Metabolic exercise mimic narrative | Peptide fashion in forums | Injectable, different target set, thinner human drug development than ATX’s Phase 2 path |
| **SLU-PP-332 (ERR agonist)** | EE / exercise-mimetic hype | Oral research interest | Much earlier / less human data than ATX-304 |
| **Classical uncouplers (DNP)** | EE via proton leak | Strong WL | **High toxicity**—not comparable safety class |

**Positioning:** ATX-304 is the furthest-along **clinical oral dual AMPK + mito network activator** with **human RMR and ectopic-fat signals** and a cleaner **cardiac preclinical profile** than some earlier direct AMPK drugs—but it is still pre-Phase-3 and not available as an approved medicine.

## 14. Short boundary

ATX-304/O304 is an **investigational drug**, not a dietary supplement. This briefing maps mechanisms, trials, and practical nodes for research-literate users; it is **not** a prescription, purchase recommendation, or assurance of grey-market safety. Human data through Phase 1b/IIa are encouraging for metabolic rate, ectopic fat, glucose, and vascular markers at pharmaceutical doses, but **weight-loss Phase 2 and long-term safety remain open**. For medical use, the only appropriate path is regulated clinical trials or future approved prescribing.
