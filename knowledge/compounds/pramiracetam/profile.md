# Pramiracetam — Knowledge Profile

**Slug:** pramiracetam  
**Source briefing:** findings/pramiracetam/briefing.md  
**Research ID:** 90cbd1ef  
**Updated:** 2026-07-31  
**Isolation note:** Do not import as evidence for other compounds; same-topic memory only.

## Identity
- Fat-soluble racetam; CI-879; Pramistar (IT/EE); C14H27N3O2; MW ~269.4
- Parke-Davis 1970s; not FDA-approved

## PK (Established)
- Linear oral 400–1600 mg; Tmax 2–3 h; t½ ~4.5–6.5 h (Chang 1985)
- Auteri 1992: high inter-subject t½ variance (~2–8 h)
- Practical: BID–TID; take with fat (Probable)

## Pathways
- **P1 HACU/CHT1 ↑ hippocampus** — Established preclinical; Probable dominant MOA candidate
- P2 ACh network under demand; P3 scopolamine reverse (animal)
- P4 nNOS cortex ↑ rat high i.p. dose — Speculative human
- P12 no direct monoamine/GABA/mAChR agonist profile — Established negative
- P14/P15 choline deficit headache vs relative ACh excess blunting

## Clinical certainty ceilings
- PK: Established
- AD symptomatic benefit: Probable null (Claus 1991, up to 4 g)
- TBI cognition: Speculative (McLean n=4, CNI COI)
- Healthy young nootropic RCT: none
- Elderly + training pilot: Speculative–Probable modest

## Forum signature (Probable phenotypes)
- Laser focus / cold logic; memory sponge under learning
- Emotional blunting / zombie (signature vs ani/pira)
- Headache/fog choline mismatch; stim vs sedate split; tolerance

## Practical
- 250–600 mg 1–3×/d with fat
- Choline Alpha-GPC or CDP titrate both directions
- Aniracetam for affect offset (Speculative stack)
- Cycle if tolerance; stop if mood crash

## Stack role
Cold cognitive drive / cholinergic capacity tool — not mood base.
