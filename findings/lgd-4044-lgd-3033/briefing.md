# LGD-4044 & LGD-3033 — Dual Compound Research Briefing

**Research ID:** bb3b7b11  
**Type:** Dual / comparative SARM profile + mechanism-matched side counters  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  

---

## 0. Nomenclature first (read this before any dose table)

| Label user may see | What it actually is | Evidence status |
|--------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **LGD-4044** | **Not a real Ligand Pharmaceuticals clinical code.** Black-market brand/typo. When a “real” SARM is present, it is almost always **LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211)**. One narrative review even printed “LGD-4044” while describing the Basaria LGD-4033 trial — a literature mix-up that helped the label stick. | **Established** that LGD-4044 is not an authentic IND molecule; **Probable** that retail “LGD-4044” ≈ mislabeled LGD-4033 or unknown powder |
| **LGD-3033** | **Not a real published clinical code.** Closest authentic molecule is **LGD-3303** (quinoline SARM from Ligand). | **LGD-4033** | Ligandrol, VK5211, Anabolicum. Nonsteroidal SARM. Human Phase 1 (Basaria 2013) + Phase 2 hip fracture (VK5211). | Authentic; best-studied of the set |
| **LGD-3303** | 9-chloro-2-ethyl-1-methyl-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethyl)-3H-pyrrolo[3,2-f]quinolin-7(6H)-one. Full agonist anabolic / partial agonist prostate in rats. **No published human RCTs.** | Authentic preclinical only |

**This document therefore maps:**

1. **“LGD-4044” → LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211)** as the only evidence-bearing identity.  
2. **“LGD-3033” → LGD-3303** as the only evidence-bearing identity, with explicit forum-label caveats.  
3. Side-effect counters that apply to tissue-selective AR agonism generally (and specifically to LGD-4033 human data).

Product identity risk is not a side note: Van Wagoner et al. (JAMA 2017) found only ~52% of internet “SARM” products contained a known SARM, and only ~41% matched labeled dose. **Any practical protocol inherits that uncertainty.**

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### 2.1 LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211) — the real molecule behind most “LGD-4044”

| Parameter | Data | Certainty |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Structure class | Nonsteroidal; 4-[(2R)-2-[(1R)-2,2,2-trifluoro-1-hydroxyethyl]pyrrolidin-1-yl]-2-(trifluoromethyl)benzonitrile; MW ~338 | Established |
| AR affinity | Ki / Kd ≈ 0.9 nM; EC50 ~3.6–4.4 nM; Emax ~132–133% (transcriptional assays) | Established (preclinical/clinical source papers) |
| Off-target NR | PR, GR, MR Ki > 4000 nM; ERα not fully characterized in public summaries | Probable |
| Route | Oral | Established |
| t½ human | **24–36 h** (single-dose ~31 h); supports once-daily dosing | Established |
| Accumulation | ~3× plasma levels day 21 vs day 1 at 0.1–1 mg | Established (Basaria 2013) |
| Linearity | Dose-proportional 0.1–1 mg multi-dose; single doses studied up to 22 mg | Established |
| Aromatization / 5α-reduction | Nonsteroidal — **does not aromatize** to estrogen or reduce to DHT-like steroidal metabolites | Established (chemistry); residual estrogenic phenotype can still fall via HPG suppression |
| CNS | AR expressed in brain; behavioral CNS selectivity not fully mapped in humans | Speculative for cognition/mood phenotypes |
| Food effect | Not well published | Unknown |

**Clinical exposure context:** Phase 1 multi-dose used **0.1 / 0.3 / 1.0 mg** for 21 days. Phase 2 VK5211 used **0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 mg** for 12 weeks. Gym black-market use is typically **5–10 mg/day** (sometimes 15–20) for 6–12 weeks — i.e. often **5–10× Phase 2 high dose**.

Shared core: **tissue-selective AR agonism**. Downstream forks differ by tissue cofactors, coactivators, chromatin state, and (for steroidal androgens) metabolic enzymes SARMs largely skip.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 LGD-4033 — human trials

#### A. Single-dose Phase 1 (Meglasson et al., ICE 2010 abstract)

- Healthy males; ascending single oral doses **0.1–22 mg**.  
- Well tolerated; no SAEs reported in abstract.  
- Dose-proportional exposure; t½ ~31 h.  
- Preclinical ORDX rat exposure modeling suggested human efficacious repeated dose ~0.25 mg.  
- **Certainty:** Probable (abstract-level; not full paper depth).

#### B. Multi-dose Phase 1 — Basaria et al., *J Gerontol A* 2013 (PMID 22459616)

| Design | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| N | 76 healthy men, 21–50 y |
| Arms | Placebo, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0 mg daily × **21 days** |
| Follow-up | 5 weeks post-drug |

**Key findings (directionally established in this RCT):**

| Endpoint | Result | Certainty |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Safety / SAEs | No drug-related SAEs; AE frequency similar to placebo | Established (this trial, short) |
| PSA, AST/ALT, QT, Hb | No significant adverse shifts at these doses/duration | Established (this trial only) |
| Lean body mass | Dose-dependent ↑; ~**1.21 kg** at 1 mg over 3 weeks (commonly cited) | Established |
| Fat mass | No significant change | Established (short window) |
| Strength / stair climb | Trends; not the standout vs LBM | Probable weak signal |
| Total T, SHBG, HDL, TG | Dose-dependent **suppression** | Established |
| Free T, FSH | Significant suppression at **1.0 mg** | Established |
| Recovery | Hormones/lipids returned toward baseline after discontinuation (within follow-up) | Established (this short exposure) |

**What this trial does *not* show:** Safety of 5–20 mg/day; 8–12 week cycles; older multi-morbid patients; hepatotoxicity incidence; CV hard outcomes; female safety; fertility endpoints.

#### C. Phase 2 — VK5211 hip fracture (Ristic et al., ASBMR 2018; Viking disclosures)

| Design | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| N | 108 (83 F / 25 M), mean age ~77 |
| Arms | Placebo, **0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 mg** daily × **12 weeks** |
| Primary | Change in LBM (less head) |

**Key results:**

| Endpoint | Result | Certainty |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Placebo-adj LBM | **+4.8% (0.5 mg), +7.2% (1 mg), +9.1% (2 mg)**; all p significant | Established (as reported) |
| ≥2 kg LBM gain proportions | Placebo 14% vs 57 / 65 / 81% across doses | Established |
| Fat mass | Dose-dependent ↓; ~−6.2% placebo-adj at 2 mg (p=0.01) | Probable |
| 6MWT / SPPB | Numerical improvement (e.g. ~+22 m at 2 mg); **not powered** | Speculative–Probable |
| Safety | AE rates similar to placebo; no drug-related SAEs claimed | Probable (older, rehab population; abstract/company report depth) |

**Development status:** Viking VK5211 still described as mid-stage / not approved; not a marketed drug.

#### D. Hepatotoxicity case literature (non-RCT)

- **Barbara et al. 2020** (ACG Case Rep): 32M, ~**10 mg/day × ~2 weeks**, severe **cholestatic** DILI (bili peaking ~35 mg/dL; biopsy: cholestatic hepatitis).  
- Additional LGD-4033 ± multi-SARM cases (Koller, Flores, Labban, etc.): mixed/cholestatic patterns, sometimes after PCT polypharmacy.  
- Pattern: rare but **severe** when it hits; often **supra-clinical dose** + unregulated product.  
- **Certainty of causal class risk:** Probable. **Incidence:** Unknown.

#### E. Product integrity — Van Wagoner et al., JAMA 2017

- 44 internet SARM products: only **52%** contained a known SARM; **41%** dose-matched label.  
- **Certainty:** Established for sampled market; generalizes as high product-risk prior.

#### F. Cardaci et al. 2022 — LGD-4033 + MK-677 (healthy men, research context)

- Co-administration increased body mass / lean / fat in studied context; adverse signals on lipids, liver enzymes, bone markers reported in that experiment’s framing.  
- Reinforces that **stacking** with GH secretagogues is not free.  
- **Certainty:** Probable for that specific stack pattern; small-N limits.

### 4.2 LGD-3303 — preclinical only

#### A. Vajda et al., *JPET* 2009 (PMID 19017848) — PK/PD castrate male rats

- Potent levator ani anabolism; prostate partial agonist with **plateau ≤ eugonadal**.  
- Selectivity **independent** of oral vs continuous infusion PK shape.  
- Higher tissue levels in prostate than muscle → molecular AR interaction explains SARM profile.  
- Oral bioavailability described as good in class summaries.  
- **Certainty:** Established (rodent).

#### B. Vajda et al., *JBMR* 2009 (PMID 18847323) — OVX female rats ± alendronate

- ↑ muscle weight; ↑ BMD/BMC cortical + cancellous; periosteal anabolic component.  
- Combo with alendronate additive at multiple sites.  
- Little/no cross-reactivity with related nuclear receptors in vitro.  
- **Certainty:** Established (rodent osteoporosis model).

#### C. Kudwa et al. 2010 (PMID 20392832) — sexual preference

- LGD-3303 enhanced female preference for males **only if sexually experienced**.  
- Supports CNS-accessible AR activity relevant to sexual motivation circuits.  
- **Certainty:** Probable (specific rodent paradigm).

#### D. Human clinical

- **None published** for efficacy, hormones, lipids, or liver.  
- Designer-drug detections exist (anti-doping / equine).  
- **Certainty of human risk/benefit:** Unknown from trials; **Speculative** from class analogy.

### 4.3 What clinical data do **not** support

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| “SARMs have no suppression” | **False** for LGD-4033 even at 0.1–1 mg |
| “LGD-4044 is a distinct milder analog” | **Unsupported** |
| “LGD-3033 is clinically proven shorter half-life cousin of 4033” | **Unsupported** |
| “No liver risk because Basaria ALT was fine” | **Overread** — different dose, duration, product purity, N |
| “Prostate-safe = androgen-side free” | **False dichotomy** — HPG, lipids, CNS, skin still active |

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Lean mass hypertrophy (both; proven only for 4033)

1. **Phenotype:** Faster LBM accretion; fuller muscle; scale weight up if calories allow.  
2. **Pathways:** P1 (muscle AR) ± P11 (glycogen/water).  
3. **Chain:** Drug → AR–HSP complex nuclear translocation → ARE-driven transcription → net protein balance positive under training stimulus.  
4. **Clinical:** LGD-4033 LBM dose-response **Established**. LGD-3303 rat muscle **Established**; human **Unknown**.  
5. **Forum:** High consistency for 4033 at 5–10 mg.  
6. **Practice:** Caloric surplus + progressive overload still dominate; SARM is multiplier not magic. Expect some LBM to be glycogen/water (especially 4033). **Certainty of usable LBM at gym doses:** Probable.

### 6.2 Strength

1. **Phenotype:** Faster load progression, better pumps.  
2. **Pathways:** P1 + neural AR effects (P10, hypothesized).  
3. **Clinical:** Modest/variable in short trials; **Probable** overall.  
4. **Forum:** Common but not universal; some “disappointed on strength” logs.

### 6.3 Bone support

1. **Phenotype:** Not usually “felt”; structural.  
2. **Pathways:** P2.  
3. **Clinical/preclinical:** LGD-3303 bone package **Established** in rats; human hip-fracture program used **4033/VK5211** with LBM focus — bone endpoints secondary/less public.  
4. **Certainty human bone outcomes for either at gym use:** Speculative–Probable class effect.

### 6.4 Recomp / fat decrease

1. **Phenotype:** Harder look if diet tight; fat mass ↓ in VK5211 elderly trial.  
2. **Pathways:** LBM ↑ + possible AR metabolic effects; not a fat-burner.  
3. **Certainty:** Probable mild fat ↓ secondary; Speculative as cutting primary agent.

### 6.5 “Dry” look (claimed for 3303/3033)

1. **Phenotype:** Less subcutaneous water than 4033.  
2. **Pathways:** Speculative differential cofactor/glycogen signaling; **or** just lower true dose / different active.  
3. **Certainty:** Speculative.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 Hypothalamic–pituitary–gonadal suppression

1. **Phenotype:** Falling morning wood, libido, mood, testicular volume; post-cycle crash; low total/free T, low/normal LH/FSH, low SHBG.  
2. **Mechanism:**  
   - Exogenous AR agonism → hypothalamic/pituitary negative feedback (P4).  
   - SHBG ↓ amplifies free-fraction dynamics but total T still collapses.  
   - Spermatogenesis risk via FSH/LH drop + local testicular androgen disruption (**Probable** with deep/prolonged suppression).  
3. **Clinical:** Basaria dose-dependent; free T/FSH hit at 1 mg. Forum: near-universal at 5–10 mg by mid-cycle.

| Pathway node | Counter | How it hits the node | Clinical | Forum | Practice notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|----------------------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Feedback / low LH-FSH | **Enclomiphene** 6.25–12.5 mg ED (on-cycle or PCT) | SERM → blocks E2 feedback at pituitary → ↑ LH/FSH | Strong for hypogonadal/T recovery contexts; not SARM-specific RCTs | Very common | Start when libido/labs drop or last 2 wk + 2–4 wk PCT | Probable (class) |
| Same | **Tamoxifen** 10–20 mg ED PCT | SERM; classic AAS PCT | Established in AAS culture literature imperfectly | Common backup | Less “clean” feel than enclo for some | Probable |
| Low LH drive | **hCG** 250–500 IU 2–3×/wk | Mimics LH at Leydig cell | Established hypogonadism use | Used under SARM by advanced | On-cycle testicular support; not a full axis fix alone | Probable for testis volume |
| Absolute androgen deficit on-cycle | **Testosterone base** (TRT dose) | Replaces systemic T while SARM provides extra AR tone | Established TRT | Common advanced | Changes risk profile (E2, Hct, BP); not “SARM-only” anymore | Established replacement logic |

### 7.2 HDL / lipid atherogenic shift

1. **Phenotype:** HDL crash on bloodwork; TG often down with 4033; LDL variable.  
2. **Mechanism:** Hepatic AR (P5) + SHBG-linked metabolic shifts; similar to oral androgens class.  
3. **Clinical:** Established in Basaria.

| Low HDL | **Cardio + high fiber / Mediterranean pattern** | ↑ HDL via lifestyle | Established lifestyle lipid science | Underused vs pills | Non-negotiable base | Established |
| Low HDL | **High-dose omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** | TG↓, modest HDL/particle effects | Established for TG | Common | 2–4 g EPA+DHA | Probable adjunct |

### 7.3 Hepatotoxicity (DILI)

1. **Phenotype:** Fatigue, pruritus, dark urine, jaundice, acholic stool; labs: ALT/AST ± ALP/bili; can be **cholestatic-dominant** weeks after stop.  
2. **Mechanism:**  
   - **Idiosyncratic** immune/metabolic injury with dose component (Barbara discussion).  
   - Possible BSEP/MRP2 transporter stress (**Hypothesized**).  
   - Contaminants / wrong APIs in research liquids (**Probable** contributor).  
3. **Clinical:** Not seen as mean ALT rise in Basaria ≤1 mg/21d; multiple case reports at ~10 mg and multi-SARM use.

| Pathway node | Counter | How it hits the node | Clinical | Forum | Practice notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|----------------------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Exposure | **Dose discipline ≤ clinical range if possible; short cycles** | Reduce intrinsic + idiosyncratic dose risk | Case doses often 10 mg | Community still uses 5–10 | Biggest lever | Probable |
| Product risk | **Avoid multi-compound liquids; third-party test** | Removes mislabel hepatotoxins | Van Wagoner | Sparse true testing | Assume dirty until proven | Probable |
| Oxidative / support (adjunct only) | **TUDCA** 250–500 mg, **NAC** 600–1200 mg | Bile acid / GSH support | TUDCA cholestasis literature; not SARM-proven | Very common stack | **Does not make DILI impossible** | Speculative for SARM DILI prevention |
| Early detection | **ALT/AST/ALP/bili baseline + mid + end** | Stop rule | Standard DILI practice | Growing | Stop if ALT >3× ULN or bili rises | Established monitoring logic |
| Active DILI | **Immediate cessation; medical care** | Remove toxin | Standard | — | UDCA sometimes used in cholestatic DILI — clinician | Established |

### 7.4 Libido / erectile dysfunction (mid-cycle crash)

1. **Phenotype:** Early libido bump → week 3–6 flatline; softer erections.  
2. **Mechanism:** P4 (low T/E2) + P10 (CNS AR context) + SHBG collapse dynamics; psychological fatigue.  
3. **Counters:** Enclomiphene/hCG as in 7.1; **tadalafil** 2.5–5 mg ED for PDE5 support (**Probable** symptomatic); sleep/stress; avoid AI without labs. Dopaminergic “libido stacks” (e.g. caber) are **usually wrong tool** without prolactin issue — **Speculative/harmful if misused**.

### 7.5 Lethargy / flat mood

1. **Phenotype:** Low drive outside gym or even in gym.  
2. **Mechanism:** Low T/E2 (P4/P6), sleep fragmentation (**Speculative**), training load, possible CNS AR tone changes (P10).  
3. **Counters:** Axis support (7.1); fix sleep; dose reduction; rule out low E2 if on test+AI; short-term caffeine as symptomatic only.

### 7.6 Water retention / “bloat” (4033 > claimed 3303)

1. **Phenotype:** Puffy look, glycogen fullness, scale spikes.  
2. **Mechanism:** P11 glycogen; possible mild mineralocorticoid-adjacent fluid (**Speculative** — LGD-4033 MR affinity low in vitro). Not classic estrogen bloat.  
3. **Counters:** Sodium/potassium balance; lower carbs around photos; **not** high-dose AI; dandelion/OTC diuretics weak. For true edema — medical eval.

### 7.7 Androgenic skin / hair (acne, MPB)

1. **Phenotype:** Acne, accelerated hair loss in predisposed.  
2. **Mechanism:** P7 partial androgenic AR in follicle/sebaceous gland; 3303 partial agonist data makes androgenic sides **plausible**.  
3. **Counters:** Topical **minoxidil**; **ketoconazole** shampoo; oral **finasteride/dutasteride** (blocks DHT — **limited** if sides are direct SARM-AR; still helps native DHT component) — **Speculative** for pure SARM hair loss; **tretinoin**/benzoyl peroxide for acne; dose cut.

### 7.8 Tendon / joint niggles

1. **Phenotype:** Strength outpaces connective tissue.  
2. **Mechanism:** P12 hypothesized.  
3. **Counters:** Slow progression; collagen + vit C (**Speculative**); avoid max singles early cycle.

### 7.9 Cardiovascular risk (long-horizon)

1. **Phenotype:** Silent until event; driven by lipids ± BP/Hct.  
2. **Mechanism:** P5 + P9.  
3. **Counters:** Lipid stack (7.2); BP monitoring; don’t stack with harsh orals; time-limited exposure.

### 7.10 Virilization (female users)

1. **Phenotype:** Voice, hair, clitoromegaly risk — **Unknown** quantified for these SARMs; forum anecdotes for SARM class exist.  
2. **Mechanism:** AR agonism in androgen-responsive female tissues.  
3. **Counters:** Avoid; if exposure, **immediate stop** at first irreversible-leaning signs (voice). No reliable “protection stack” for virilization — only prevention/cessation. **Certainty:** Established AR biology; **Unknown** dose threshold for LGD-4033/3303.

## 10. Monitoring, contraindications, stop criteria

### Labs (minimum intelligent set)

| Time | Panel |
|------|-------|
| Baseline | Total T, free T, LH, FSH, E2, SHBG, CBC, CMP (ALT/AST/ALP/bili), fasting lipids, PSA (age-appropriate), BP |
| Mid-cycle (week 3–5) | Hormones + CMP + lipids |
| End-cycle | Same |
| Post-PCT 4–6 weeks | Hormones to confirm recovery |

### Stop criteria (practical)

- ALT/AST **>3× ULN** or rising bili/ALP with symptoms → **stop immediately**, medical evaluation.  
- Jaundice, severe pruritus, acholic stool → emergency path.  
- Psychiatric decompensation, severe ED with distress, suspected PE/chest pain → stop + care.  
- Female virilization signs → stop.

### Contraindications / high-risk contexts

- Active liver disease; unexplained elevated baseline LFTs.  
- Established ASCVD with uncontrolled lipids (relative).  
- Prostate cancer history (AR agonist class concern — clinician).  
- Pregnancy / trying to conceive without fertility plan.  
- Drug-tested sport (WADA S1.2 — **Established** ban).

## 15. Short boundary
