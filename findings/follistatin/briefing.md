# Follistatin — Compound Profile (Muscle Growth / PED Context)

**One document. Maximum depth.** Audience: performance users, research-engineer framing. Certainty labels: **Established** / **Probable** / **Speculative** / **Unknown**.

**Evidence frame:** Preclinical muscle hypertrophy from FST overexpression/AAV is **Established** in models. Human athletic hypertrophy from UGL “FST-344” is **Speculative**. Clinical gene-therapy functional gains in selected BMD/sIBM patients are **Probable** (open-label, small N; sIBM claims contested). Do not chain Mendell gene therapy → athlete PED protocols → UGL vial doses.

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### What it is

- **Gene:** *FST* encodes a single-chain cysteine-rich glycoprotein with an N-terminal domain, three follistatin domains (FSD1–3; FSD1 contains a heparin-binding motif critical for cell-surface association), and isoform-dependent C-terminal tails.
- **Mature isoforms (after signal-peptide cleavage):**
  - **FST288** — lacks the acidic C-terminal tail; highest heparin/surface affinity; predominantly tissue-bound; strongest local neutralization of activins. **Established.**
  - **FST315** — full acidic C-tail; predominant circulating plasma form (~majority of plasma FST); lower surface/heparin affinity → more systemic distribution, less gonadal trapping by design intent. **Established.**
  - **FST303** — intermediate (proteolytic processing of FST315); intermediate surface binding; often described as gonad-enriched. **Established.**
- **Affinity rank for surface/heparin association (typical):** FST288 > FST303 > FST315. **Established** (Sidis et al., binding/surface studies).
- **“FS344” / “FST-344” naming trap:** Human prepro-FST is 344 aa including signal sequence. Clinical construct **AAV1.CMV.FS344** encodes the prepro form that is processed to **circulating FS315**. Market “Follistatin-344” is often marketed as if it were a discrete short peptide or a special super-isoform; it is construct nomenclature for full-length prepro coding sequence, **not** a 344-aa small peptide drug. **Established chemistry; Speculative market identity.**

### Classification

| Label | Accurate? |
|-------|-----------|
| Glycoprotein / myokine / ligand trap | Yes |
| “Peptide” (short aa chain like CJC/Ipamorelin class) | No — wrong size class |
| Myostatin-selective inhibitor | Incomplete — also binds activin A/B, GDF11, other TGF-β family ligands with high affinity |
| SARM / AAS | No |

**Molecular weight:** ~31–39 kDa depending on isoform and N-glycosylation. **Established.** Not absorbed orally; not a typical SC “research peptide” PK profile.

### Binding pharmacology (core)

FST forms high-affinity complexes with:

| Ligand | Role in muscle / body | FST neutralization | Certainty |
|--------|----------------------|--------------------|-----------|
| Myostatin (GDF8) | Negative regulator of muscle mass | High-affinity binding; blocks ActRIIB engagement | **Established** |
| Activin A / B | Muscle-negative via Smad2/3; FSH regulation; inflammation/fibrosis contexts | High-affinity; often critical for “FST > Mstn-KO” phenotype | **Established** |
| GDF11 | Related ligand; aging/muscle debates | Bound and neutralized | **Established** binding; phenotype contribution **Probable/Speculative** by context |
| Some BMPs / other TGF-β family | Developmental, bone, vascular contexts | Isoform- and context-dependent partial sequestration | **Probable** off-target relevance for broad traps |

Crystal and biochemical work (e.g., myostatin:FST288 complex) shows shared but differentially used contact surfaces vs activin; FST is a **multi-ligand trap**, not a pure GDF8 antibody. **Established.**

### Delivery forms discussed

| Form | What it actually is | Human muscle evidence | PED market reality |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| **AAV-FST gene therapy** (AAV1.CMV.FS344 IM) | Viral vector → local muscle expression of circulating-type FST | Phase 1/2a BMD + sIBM (small N) | Not UGL; medical/research only; AAV Ab issues |
| **Recombinant full-length FST protein** | Glycoprotein biotherapeutic | Sparse as systemic drug for athletes; research use | Expensive; short t½ expected; not “peptide kit” |
| **UGL “FST-344” vials** | Claimed recombinant protein or fragment | **None** controlled | Identity, purity, dose, activity unverified |
| **ActRIIB-Fc (ACE-031)** / anti-ActRII (bimagrumab) / anti-GDF8 Abs | Different molecules, same pathway neighborhood | Human RCTs exist | Class lessons for sides and mass vs function |

### PK / ADME (sparse human data for free FST protein)

- **Oral:** effectively none for intact glycoprotein. **Established** class expectation.
- **IM gene therapy:** expression is local with potential for secreted circulating isoform (FS315 design). Mendell BMD/sIBM: bilateral quad IM AAV; systemic reproductive hormones monitored (see Clinical).
- **Recombinant protein half-life:** literature and class analogs suggest hours-range for free FST; glycosylation and Fc-fusions (research constructs like FST288-Fc) extend exposure. **Probable** short free-protein t½; **Unknown** for any specific UGL lot.
- **CNS penetration:** not a primary CNS agent; large glycoprotein. Negligible relevant CNS drug-like exposure expected. **Probable.**
- **Heparin/ECM binding:** FST288 localizes aggressively to cell surfaces; FS315 less so—this is the intentional safety design for gene therapy (less gonadal sequestration). **Established.**

**Flag:** Human PK for systemic recombinant FST in healthy athletes is essentially **Unknown**. Any UGL “dose chart” that treats FST like a 100–500 µg/day peptide is inventing protocol from forum culture, not from established human PK.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Stratification overview

| Tier | What exists for FST itself | Quality |
|------|---------------------------|---------|
| Systematic review / meta of FST therapy in athletes | None | — |
| Large RCT FST in healthy adults for hypertrophy | **None** | — |
| Phase 1/2a open-label gene therapy (disease) | BMD + sIBM, N≈6 each program | Small, no placebo, diseased muscle |
| Related pathway RCTs (not FST molecule) | ACE-031, bimagrumab, MYO-029, other GDF8 Abs | Higher quality for **class**, different molecules |
| Observational / training endogenous FST | Meta of resistance training | Indirect |
| Preclinical | Extremely strong genetic and AAV data | High for animals |

### 4.2 Mendell et al. 2015 — AAV1.CMV.FS344 in Becker muscular dystrophy

- **PMID:** 25322757 | Mol Ther | PMC4426808 | NCT01519349  
- **Design:** Phase 1/2a, open-label, N=6 BMD; bilateral IM quadriceps AAV1.CMV.FS344.  
  - Cohort 1: 3×10¹¹ vg/kg/leg (n=3)  
  - Cohort 2: 6×10¹¹ vg/kg/leg (n=3)  
- **Construct rationale:** FS344 → processed circulating **FS315** to reduce surface/gonadal off-target vs FST288. **Established** design intent.  
- **Measured:** 6MWT primary functional; histology (fibrosis, central nucleation, fiber size); safety labs including reproductive hormones.  
- **Effect direction/size:**  
  - 6MWT: improvements reported in several subjects (examples: +58 m, +125 m cohort 1; +108 m, +29 m cohort 2); some non-responders.  
  - Histology: reduced endomysial fibrosis, reduced central nucleation, more normal fiber-size distribution, hypertrophy especially high dose.  
  - Safety: no SAEs attributed; FSH/LH/T/E described as normal in report framing.  
- **Population:** dystrophinopathy with fibrotic, diseased muscle—not healthy trained athletes.  
- **Limits:** tiny N; no placebo; natural history/placebo effects on 6MWT possible; fibrosis may limit delivery (“fibrosis barrier”); short follow-up relative to lifelong risk; industry/academic gene-therapy context.  
- **Certainty for “FST gene therapy can remodel dystrophic muscle locally”:** **Probable.**  
- **Certainty for “FST grows healthy athlete muscle systemically”:** **Not supported** by this trial (wrong population, wrong delivery, wrong claim).

### 4.3 Mendell et al. 2017 — FST gene therapy in sporadic inclusion body myositis (sIBM)

- **PMID:** 28279643 | Mol Ther  
- **Design:** open-label gene therapy, N=6 treated; comparison to untreated matched natural-history style framing (not a randomized concurrent placebo RCT).  
- **Reported:** ~+56 m/year 6MWT trajectory vs ~−26 m/year untreated matched narrative; histology consistent with regeneration/fibrosis changes.  
- **Critique — Greenberg 2017, PMID 28927986:** argues **unfounded claims** of improved functional outcomes given study design cannot support causal functional attribution. **Important methodological limit.**  
- **Certainty for functional benefit in sIBM:** **Speculative to Probable histology; functional claims contested.**  
- **Certainty for healthy PED use:** still **not shown.**

### 4.4 What related pathway drugs show (class contrast — not FST)

#### ACE-031 (ActRIIB-Fc multi-ligand trap)

- **Attie et al. 2013, PMID 23169607:** single ascending dose in healthy **postmenopausal women** (n=48). At 3 mg/kg SC: lean mass **+3.3%** (DXA), thigh muscle volume **+5.1%** (MRI) at day 29. **Established** short-term lean mass PD for this molecule.  
- **DMD program (Campbell / published trial):** trends for lean mass; study **stopped** for vascular signals—**epistaxis, telangiectasia**, gum bleeding. **Established** class safety lesson: broad ActRIIB ligand trapping is not free.  
- **Implication:** systemic multi-ligand traps can move lean mass in humans but carry off-target vascular risk when ligand set is broad. FS315 gene therapy is **not identical** to ACE-031 (different PK, different ligand coverage, local expression)—do not equate AEs 1:1, but do not ignore multi-ligand biology.

#### Bimagrumab (anti-ActRII monoclonal)

- Increases LBM (~6–8% range reported in sarcopenia/obesity programs depending on study); fat mass reduction in obesity/T2D contexts.  
- sIBM phase 3: **failed** primary functional endpoints despite mass pharmacology in earlier work.  
- **Lesson:** **mass ≠ function.** **Established** pattern across myostatin-pathway drugs.

#### MYO-029 / stamulumab (anti-myostatin Ab)

- Small lean-mass signals; limited functional benefit (Wagner et al. lineage).  
- Pure GDF8 blockade historically underwhelmed vs animal genetics expectations.

### 4.5 Preclinical pillars (why the hype exists)

| Line | Finding | Certainty |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Lee & McPherron genetics | Myostatin loss → hypermuscularity; FST transgenic muscle increases comparable or greater; FST + Mstn-null animals approach ~**4×** wild-type muscle mass in classic reports | **Established** (mice) |
| Haidet et al. 2008 PNAS | Long-term AAV1-FS344 in mdx and aged models; strength gains; reproduction reported normal in treated animals | **Established** (animals) |
| Kota et al. 2009 | NHP AAV-FST quad mass/strength | **Established** (NHP) |
| Gilson 2009 PMID 19435857 | FST hypertrophy via satellite cells + Mstn + activin inhibition | **Established** (rodent) |
| Winbanks 2012 PMID 22711699 | FST-mediated hypertrophy Smad3-linked; can be myostatin-independent; mTOR involvement | **Established** (rodent) |

### 4.6 Endogenous training data

- **Khalafi et al. 2023, PMID 37328021:** meta-analysis — resistance training **↓ myostatin** (SMD ~−1.31, 26 studies) and **↑ follistatin** (SMD ~+2.04, 14 studies). **Established** that training already engages this axis.

### 4.7 What clinical data do **NOT** show

1. **No** randomized evidence that UGL FST-344 injections produce meaningful hypertrophy in healthy resistance-trained men.  
2. **No** established “PED dose” (µg or mg) with PK/PD for recombinant FST in athletes.  
3. **No** demonstration that systemic free FST protein matches AAV local gene-expression exposure.  
4. **No** clean separation of mass vs strength/power outcomes in healthy athletes for FST itself.  
5. **No** long-term cancer-safety RCTs for exogenous FST in healthy people.  
6. Gene therapy trials do **not** validate research-chemical vials; different molecule state, dose, duration, and regulation.  
7. Functional gains in tiny open-label disease trials do **not** transfer to contest prep or bulk cycles.  
8. “Local injection grows that muscle only” for recombinant protein is **not** clinically established (AAV local expression is a different mechanism).

**Certainty summary for primary claim “FST is an effective muscle PED for healthy athletes”:** pathway **Established** in animals; human disease gene therapy **limited Probable**; systemic UGL peptide use **Speculative / evidence-weak**.

### Weighing

Forum silence on dramatic unassisted transformations, plus frequent “expensive disappointment” reports, fits the evidence map: **if** many vials are under-dosed, degraded glycoprotein, or wrong identity, null anecdotes are expected; **if** some vials are real full-length protein at low µg doses, exposure is still orders of magnitude different from multi-week local AAV expression. Literature silence on athlete RCTs does **not** prove zero biology—it proves **claim unsupported**. Anecdotes cannot raise certainty above **Speculative** for systemic UGL FST hypertrophy.

Confounders always present: concurrent AAS, GH/IGF-1, insulin, high-volume training, creatine, selective reporting, vendor incentives.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Skeletal muscle hypertrophy / lean mass

- **Phenotype:** ↑ fiber CSA, ↑ muscle weight (animals extreme); modest % lean mass in human ActRII-trap trials (different drug).  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P2, P4, P5, P6, P7.  
- **Chain:** FST binds GDF8 + activins → prevents ActRII–ALK–Smad2/3 activation → releases transcriptional brake on muscle growth programs → parallel ↑ protein synthesis capacity (mTOR-capable) + satellite cell contribution under strong overexpression → net hypertrophy (± hyperplasia in animals).  
- **Clinical support:** disease gene therapy histology **Probable**; healthy athlete FST protein **not shown**. Class (ACE-031 +3.3% LBM single dose; bimagrumab larger LBM programs) shows pathway can move human mass.  
- **Forum support:** mixed/weak after cost adjustment.  
- **Practical notes:** Any real effect requires **authentic active protein or gene expression**, resistance training, and surplus protein/energy. Invented µg charts are not **Established**. Certainty for UGL: **Speculative**.

### 6.2 Strength / function

- **Phenotype:** animal strength ↑; human 6MWT mixed/open-label.  
- **Pathways:** P1–P6, P11 (disease fibrosis).  
- **Chain:** larger CSA can raise force capacity, but neural drive, tendon, disease inclusion pathology, and fibrosis dominate function. Pathway drugs often **decouple mass and function** (bimagrumab sIBM P3 failure).  
- **Clinical:** BMD some 6MWT responders; sIBM claims contested (PMID 28927986).  
- **Forum:** little clean strength evidence.  
- **Certainty athlete strength:** **Speculative**.

### 6.3 Body composition (fat)

- **Phenotype:** class traps sometimes ↓ fat mass (bimagrumab obesity; ACE-031 biomarker/fat signals).  
- **Pathways:** P2, P9.  
- **Chain:** activin/myostatin axis intersects adipose and insulin sensitivity; not primary “fat burner.”  
- **Certainty for FST-as-PED fat loss:** **Speculative**.

### 6.4 Muscle quality / fibrosis (disease context)

- **Phenotype:** ↓ fibrosis, more normal fiber-size distribution in BMD gene therapy.  
- **Pathways:** P11, P2.  
- **Certainty disease remodeling:** **Probable** (small N). Irrelevant to most gym use.

### 6.5 Hyperplasia

- **Phenotype:** fiber number ↑ in some FST overexpression models.  
- **Pathways:** P5.  
- **Certainty humans:** **Unknown / Speculative**. Do not market UGL FS as “hyperplasia peptide.”

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 FSH suppression / reproductive axis impairment

1. **Phenotype:** ↓ FSH → impaired spermatogenesis, menstrual disruption, fertility risk; possible secondary sex-steroid effects if prolonged. May be silent on total T short-term if LH intact.  
2. **Mechanism:** Activins stimulate pituitary FSH; FST sequesters activin → ↓ FSH drive (**Established** physiology). High surface-binding isoforms (FST288) raise gonadal/local concern; FS315 design intentionally lowers heparin/surface trapping (**Established** design logic). Mendell gene therapy reports normal FSH/LH/T/E in small N (**Probable** short-term safety for local FS315 expression; not proof for high-dose systemic protein).  
3. **Pathway → counter table**

| Pathway node | Counter | How it hits the node | Clinical | Forum | Practice notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|----------------------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Excess systemic activin sequestration | Prefer circulating-type FS315 exposure design; avoid high-dose FST288 systemic | Isoform PK reduces gonadal surface trap | Gene therapy construct choice | Rare discussion | UGL rarely specifies real isoform | **Probable** (design) |
| ↓ FSH | Monitor FSH, LH, inhibin B, semen analysis | Detect early | Standard endocrine | Sparse | Baseline + on-treatment | **Established** monitoring |
| Fertility desire | Hold agent; time-limited exposure | Remove driver | Logic | — | Do not combine with other HPTA hits casually | **Probable** |

### 7.2 Vascular AEs (class: multi-ligand ActRII traps)

1. **Phenotype:** epistaxis, telangiectasia, gum bleeding (ACE-031).  
2. **Mechanism:** Broad sequestration of BMP/activin-family ligands that maintain vascular integrity (**Probable**); ACE-031 is ActRIIB-Fc—not identical to FST—but shares multi-ligand philosophy. Mendell FST gene therapy small N did not report this pattern (**does not prove** absence at higher systemic exposure).

2. **Mechanism:** Activin can be anti- or pro-proliferative by context; chronic ligand sequestration reshapes microenvironment (**Speculative** for athlete exogenous FST).

### 7.6 Off-target endocrine / bone / hematologic (theoretical multi-ligand)

1. **Phenotype:** bone turnover shifts, anemia/hematopoiesis signals in some TGF-β pathway drugs (variable).  
2. **Mechanism:** BMP/activin cross-talk (**Speculative** magnitude for FS315).  
3. **Counters:** monitor CBC, bone markers if chronic experimental exposure; correct deficiencies (D3/K2/Ca only if deficient—not magic shields). Certainty: **Speculative**.

### 7.7 Opportunity cost / underwhelming anabolic (practical “side”)

1. **Phenotype:** spent money, delayed use of higher-EV tools, psychological frustration.  
2. **Mechanism:** weak delivery + weak evidence.  
3. **Counter:** prioritize training, diet, sleep, proven agents if user already accepts PED risk. Certainty: **Probable** as practical outcome.

## 8. Practical use

**Honest framing:** There is **no Established safe/effective PED dose** of recombinant FST for healthy athletes. Below separates **modalities**. Inventing precision protocols for UGL is epistemology theater.

| Parameter | Guidance | Certainty |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Dose — AAV gene therapy** | Clinical: on order of 3–6×10¹¹ vg/kg/leg IM quads (Mendell BMD cohorts). Medical protocol only. | **Established** as trial doses; not gym protocol |
| **Dose — recombinant protein (research)** | No athlete PK/PD dose. Any µg/day chart from peptide vendors is **not** evidence-based. Fc-fusion research constructs differ entirely. | **Unknown** effective systemic dose |
| **Dose — UGL FST-344** | **No Established dose.** Common forum scripts (e.g. ~100–300 µg/d × 10–20 d) are marketing/culture, not clinical. | **Speculative** (and often inert if fake) |
| **Timing** | If protein had short t½, frequent dosing would be logical—but without PK this is guesswork. Gene therapy is one-time expression course. | **Speculative** (protein) |
| **Duration / cycling** | Gene therapy: durable expression window, not a “cycle.” Protein: short experimental windows only if ever used; fertility goals → avoid. | **Speculative** |
| **Form** | Glycoprotein ≠ lyophilized short peptide class. Demand identity analytics (HPLC/MS) if research use; assume UGL unlabeled until proven. FS344 name ≠ special PED magic. | **Established** chemistry |
| **Stacks (beneficial, theoretical)** | Resistance training (already ↑ endogenous FST); adequate protein/energy; sleep. Progressive overload remains primary. | Training **Established**; synergy with exogenous FST **Speculative** |
| **Stacks (risky)** | Other multi-ligand traps; high-dose experimental growth cocktails if cancer risk concerns; fertility-toxic stacks when FSH risk matters; dirty UGL polyinjectables. | **Probable** |
| **vs “just train”** | Training meta already raises FST / lowers myostatin—highest evidence “FST intervention” available to healthy people. | **Established** |

**Do not label any UGL schedule as Established.** If a user insists on experimental protein research: treat as uncontrolled experiment—baseline labs, time-limited, fertility plan, no medical claims.

## 10. Monitoring, contraindications, stop criteria

### Monitoring (if any experimental or clinical exposure)

| Domain | Tests / checks | When |
|--------|----------------|------|
| Reproductive | FSH, LH, total/free T, estradiol; semen analysis if male fertility relevant; menstrual charting | Baseline, on-exposure, recovery |
| Muscle/efficacy | DXA or MRI lean mass if serious research; strength logs; girth (confounded) | Baseline + endpoint |
| Safety labs | CBC, CMP (ALT/AST especially if AAV), CK context | Baseline + interval |
| Vascular (if trap-class or high systemic multi-ligand) | Mucosal bleeding, skin telangiectasias, BP | Ongoing symptom watch |
| AAV-specific | Anti-AAV NAb titers, vector-appropriate immune labs | Pre-screen + protocol |
| Cancer risk baseline | Age-appropriate screening; do not use with active cancer outside oncology trials | Before elective experimental use |

### Contraindications / high-caution

- Active malignancy (outside controlled trials) — **Probable** prudence  
- Pregnancy / trying to conceive soon — activin/FSH axis — **Probable** avoid  
- Known bleeding disorder + broad ActRII trap drugs — **Probable** avoid  
- High-titer AAV Abs for planned same-serotype GT — **Established** barrier  
- Unwillingness to accept identity uncertainty of UGL proteins — practical contraindication  

### Stop criteria

- Persistent epistaxis, new telangiectasias, unexplained mucosal bleeding  
- Clear hypogonadal/fertility lab deterioration when fertility valued  
- Allergic / severe injection reaction  
- Hepatitis pattern on AAV therapy per medical protocol  
- No objective lean-mass/function signal after a predefined evaluation window (stop wasting risk/money)

## 11. Comparative context

| Agent | Mechanism | Human mass evidence | Function evidence | Sides highlight | Convenience | vs FST |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|--------|
| **FST gene therapy (AAV-FS344)** | Local expression multi-ligand trap | Small open-label disease | Mixed; contested sIBM | AAV immunity; theoretical FSH; long-term **Unknown** | Medical only, costly | Reference clinical FST form |
| **UGL “FST-344” protein** | Claimed recombinant trap | **None** controlled | **None** | Product risk; theoretical FSH | SC/IM vials, expensive | Weakest evidence form |
| **Anti-myostatin Abs** (e.g. MYO-029 class / newer GDF8 Abs) | Selective GDF8 | Modest lean mass | Often disappointing function | Generally cleaner than broad traps | IV/SC medical | Narrower than FST; less animal max mass |
| **ACE-031 (ActRIIB-Fc)** | Broad ligand trap | +3.3% LBM single dose (women) | DMD program limited | **Epistaxis/telangiectasia** — program stopped | SC | Shows trap efficacy **and** vascular cost |
| **Bimagrumab (anti-ActRII)** | Blocks receptor vs ligands | Strong LBM; fat ↓ in obesity | sIBM P3 function fail; sarcopenia mixed | Receptor-level AEs profile distinct | IV medical | Mass ≠ function lesson |

FST’s unique theoretical edge is multi-ligand Smad-brake release beyond pure AR—or beyond pure GDF8 Abs—but **delivery and human athlete data are the bottleneck**, not the PowerPoint pathway.

## 12. Open questions

Data that would most change this map:

1. **Randomized, placebo-controlled trial** of characterized recombinant FS315 (or defined isoform) in healthy resistance-trained adults with DXA/MRI, strength, FSH/semen, and bleeding surveillance.  
2. **Human PK/PD** of intact glycosylated FST after SC/IM dosing (t½, free vs bound, muscle interstitial levels).  
3. **Analytical survey** of UGL “FST-344” products (identity, quantity, bioactivity in GDF8/activin reporter assays).  
4. **Head-to-head** FS315 vs anti-GDF8 Ab vs anti-ActRII for mass **and** function in the same population.  
5. Long-term **oncologic and reproductive** follow-up after AAV-FST (decades, not months).  
6. Whether **satellite-cell / hyperplasia** contributions occur in adult human muscle under therapeutic FST levels.  
7. True incidence of **vascular AEs** if systemic FST exposure approaches ACE-031-like multi-ligand sink (may differ by ligand set).  
8. Interaction RCTs: FST pathway agent ± **testosterone** on mass vs fertility labs.  
9. Minimal **expression threshold** from gene therapy that separates histologic benefit from null.  
10. Clarity on **GDF11** contribution to adult human FST phenotype.
