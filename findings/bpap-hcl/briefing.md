# (−)-BPAP HCl — Full Research Briefing

**Compound:** (R)-(−)-1-(Benzofuran-2-yl)-2-propylaminopentane hydrochloride  
**Also known as:** (−)-BPAP, R-(−)-BPAP, FPFS-1169, BFPAP, benzofuranylpropylaminopentane HCl  
**Class:** Monoaminergic activity enhancer (MAE) — combined catecholaminergic + serotonergic activity enhancer (CAE/SAE)  
**Status:** Experimental research chemical; **no approved human indication; zero published human trials**  
**Query type:** Full compound profile + review  
**Certainty system:** **Established** / **Probable** / **Speculative** / **Unknown**

---

## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | (−)-BPAP HCl — tryptamine-lineage MAE; most potent known synthetic enhancer of impulse-evoked monoamine release (DA, NE, 5-HT) |
| **Primary use cases (research / nootropic interest)** | Cognitive/motivational “clean” monoamine amplification without amphetamine-style dump; experimental antidepressant / PD / AD / anti-aging stack hypotheses; alternative to low-dose selegiline’s CAE effect without MAO-B blockade |
| **Bottom line** | (−)-BPAP is a **preclinically deep, clinically empty** molecule from József Knoll’s lab. It multiplies the amount of dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin released **only when the neuron fires**, with peak *in vitro* effects in the **femtomolar–picomolar** range and *in vivo* activity at **microgram/kg** doses in rats. ~**130×** more potent than selegiline in a tetrabenazine shuttle-box model. Modern work (2022) ties the enhancer effect to **TAAR1** after DAT-dependent entry, with PKC and VMAT2 participation. Longevity and learning-preservation data in rats are striking but **single-lab, single-species**. **No human PK, safety, or efficacy data.** Forum/self-report base is thin; some users prefer it to PPAP for subtlety; rare reports of visual oddities; microgram dosing and bimodal dose–response make “more is better” dangerous. |
| **Biggest upsides** | Activity-dependent monoamine enhancement (**Established** preclinical); dual CAE+SAE vs PPAP’s CAE-only (**Established**); weak/non-significant MAO at MAE doses (**Established**); no classical monoamine *releasing* dump (**Established**); rodent longevity + learning preservation at enhancer doses (**Probable** within rat data; **Speculative** for humans); proposed human probe dose ~0.1 mg/day (**Speculative**) |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Zero human trials (**Established** gap); bimodal “wrong dose = flat or different mechanism” (**Established** preclinical); high-dose reuptake inhibition + weak MAO-A enter (**Established**); serotonergic enhancement → SSRI/MAOI interaction risk (**Probable**); arousal/insomnia/anxiety from NE/DA (**Probable** by mechanism); rare visual weirdness (**Speculative** forum); RC purity/dosing error risk at µg scale (**Probable**); lifespan claims not human (**Speculative** translation) |
| **Stack role** | Research-only “signal amplifier” for monoaminergic tone — theoretically pairs with **drive/task structure** and careful sleep hygiene; **not** a classical stimulant substitute, not a replacement for diagnosed depression/PD care. Compare to: low-dose selegiline (CAE + MAO-B), PPAP (CAE, no SAE), bromantane, or standard NDRI/stimulants depending on goal. |

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and lineage

- **Core:** Propylaminopentane side chain with **benzofuran-2-yl** ring (tryptamine-inspired), replacing the phenyl of **PPAP**.  
- **Active enantiomer:** **R-(−)** is more potent than S-(+) (FPFS-1170). Research and literature almost always mean **(−)-BPAP**.  
- **Salt:** Hydrochloride (standard experimental form). Free base MW ≈ **245.37 g·mol⁻¹** (C₁₆H₂₃NO).  
- **CAS (free base):** 260550-89-8.  
- **Developmental code:** FPFS-1169 (Fujimoto / Knoll collaboration).  
- **Discovery context:** Selected from ~65 synthetic tryptamine-related CAE candidates as a potential clinical follower of selegiline and as the **reference MAE** for mechanism work (first full characterization **1999**, Knoll et al., *Br J Pharmacol*).

**Classification (important distinction):**

| Action | Amphetamine / meth | Selegiline | PPAP | **(−)-BPAP** |
|--------|-------------------|------------|------|--------------|
| Impulse-evoked monoamine enhancement (MAE) | Yes (masked) | Yes (CAE) | Yes (CAE) | **Yes (CAE+SAE)** |
| Impulse-independent releasing agent | Dominant | No | No | **No** (at MAE doses) |
| MAO-B inhibition | Weak/variable | Strong selective | Negligible | **Negligible** |
| MAO-A inhibition | — | Weak at high dose | Negligible | **Weak only at high µM** |
| Serotonin enhancement | High-dose dump | Often *decreases* serotonergic tone at some regimens | Weak/minimal | **Strong SAE** |

### Pharmacokinetics (rats only — **Established** rodent; **Unknown** human)

From Magyar et al. 2002 (*Eur J Drug Metab Pharmacokinet*):

| Parameter | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Absorption | Well absorbed oral and parenteral |
| Oral bioavailability | Substantial (rodent) |
| Tmax | ~30–60 min; **second peak ~4 h** (enterohepatic recirculation) |
| CNS | Crosses BBB; distributes to multiple brain regions |
| Metabolism | **Not** a MAO substrate (unlike PEA/tryptamine) |
| Elimination t½ | **~5.5–5.8 h** |
| Excretion | Preferential **urine** > feces; >90% recovery by 72 h |

**Human PK:** **Unknown**. All practical dosing discussion is extrapolation or vendor/forum folklore.

### Solubility / handling notes

- HCl salt improves aqueous handling for lab work; still treat as potent low-dose compound.  
- At microgram human-interest scales, **volumetric dilution** is mandatory if anyone experiments (purity, weighing error, and biphasic curve compound risk).

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Human data for (−)-BPAP

| Tier | Finding |
|------|---------|
| Systematic reviews / meta-analyses of BPAP | **None** |
| RCTs | **None** |
| Observational / case series | **None** |
| Regulatory filings / approved uses | **None** (experimental) |

**What clinical data do *not* show:** Any human efficacy for depression, ADHD, PD, AD, cognition, longevity, or safety margins. Selegiline’s human PD/AD literature **cannot** be imported as BPAP evidence (different MAO profile, metabolites, potency, SAE component).

### 4.2 Landmark preclinical (mechanism & efficacy proxies)

#### Knoll et al., 1999 — *Br J Pharmacol* (PMID 10588928) — foundational

- Electrical stimulation–evoked [³H]-NA, DA, 5-HT release from rat brainstem enhanced at low concentrations (e.g. NA/DA ~0.18 µM stim paradigm; 5-HT more sensitive ~36 nM in abstract methods).  
- Discrete regions: significant monoamine release enhancement with **10⁻¹²–10⁻¹⁴ M** *ex vivo*.  
- *In vivo:* **0.1 µg·kg⁻¹ s.c.** enhanced catecholaminergic and serotonergic neuronal activity within 30 min.  
- Shuttle box: fully antagonized tetrabenazine depression at **0.05 mg·kg⁻¹** vs **5 mg·kg⁻¹** selegiline → ~**130×** potency (MW-adjusted).  
- β-amyloid hippocampal cultures: protection with **bimodal** peaks (**10⁻¹⁴ M** and **10⁻⁸ M**).  
- Weak selective MAO-A; no classical receptor agonist profile explaining MAE; blocks tyramine-induced contraction (interference with indirect amine binding, like deprenyl/PPAP).  

**Limits:** Single laboratory tradition; receptor target still unknown in 1999; femtomolar claims invite replication scrutiny (though later labs partially corroborated biphasic enhancer pharmacology).

#### Harsing, Knoll, Miklya, 2022 — *Int J Mol Sci* (PMID 35955676) — TAAR1 mechanism

- (−)-BPAP **potentiates electrical stimulation–evoked** [³H]DA release; **does not** raise resting non-vesicular release (unlike methamphetamine).  
- Biphasic S2/S1 peaks near **10⁻¹²–10⁻¹¹ M** and **10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵ M**.  
- **EPPTB** (TAAR1 antagonist) reverses BPAP’s enhancer effect.  
- **Nomifensine** blocks BPAP enhancement → DAT entry required.  
- **Ro31-8220** (PKC inhibitor) abolishes; **PMA** potentiates; **NEM** synergizes (readily releasable pool).  
- Hypothesis: **two TAAR1-related modes** — releaser site (meth) vs enhancer site (BPAP).  

**Certainty of TAAR1 as the MAE target:** **Probable**. Excellent functional pharmacology; still need human tissue / genetic validation / selective clinical TAAR1 tools.

#### Pharmacokinetics — Magyar et al., 2002 (PMID 12365195)

- Rodent oral activity, t½ ~5.5–5.8 h, enterohepatic second peak — supports once–twice daily theoretical schedules (**Speculative** human).

#### High-dose transporter actions — Shimazu et al., 2003 *Eur J Pharmacol* (PMID 14659999)

- Binding/reuptake IC₅₀s in nM range for DAT/NET >> SERT — **pharmacologically secondary** to fM–pM MAE if concentrations stay low (**Probable**).

#### Behavior / mood models

- Locomotion / D1 dependence (Shimazu 2001).  
- L-DOPA-sparing in reserpine model (Shimazu 2003 Life Sci).  
- Mood disorder models (Tsunekawa et al., 2008 *Behav Brain Res*) — antidepressant-like signals (**Probable** model validity only).  
- Methamphetamine reinstatement attenuation (Hiranita 2010) — **Speculative** addiction utility.

#### Neurotrophins — Shimazu et al., 2003 Life Sci (PMID 12679194)

- Astrocyte NGF/BDNF/GDNF up at **µM–sub-mM** (single bell curve; **not** the fM MAE peak).  
- **Implication:** Do not assume “enhancer micrograms = NGF storm.” Those pathways may require high local concentrations (**Speculative** *in vivo* relevance of neurotrophin data for low-dose BPAP).

#### Longevity — Knoll & Miklya, 2016 *Life Sci* (PMID 27777099)

- Male Wistar rats, s.c. 3×/week from week 10 of life to death (N=40/group).  
- BPAP **0.0001 mg·kg⁻¹** (“specific” enhancer) or **0.05 mg·kg⁻¹** (“non-specific” enhancer) → **significantly longer lifespan** vs saline (P<0.02); **more potent than low-dose selegiline** arms.  
- **0.0001 mg·kg⁻¹** BPAP: 18-month-old rats **learned as well as 3-month-old** saline rats.  

**Limits:** One lab, one strain/sex, subcutaneous chronic protocol, no multi-site replication of the BPAP arm; selegiline longevity meta-analytic literature is mixed/heterogeneous across species. Translation to human lifespan: **Speculative**.

### 4.3 Funding / provenance

Much of the corpus is **Semmelweis / Hungarian Academy / Fujimoto Pharmaceutical** collaborative work centered on Knoll’s enhancer theory. That does not invalidate careful release assays, but it means **independent multi-lab human development never happened**. Treat longevity and anti-aging rhetoric as **investigator-championed**, not consensus gerontology.

### 4.4 Negative / null space

- No human adverse event database.  
- No QT, abuse liability human labs, reproductive tox packages in public domain comparable to registered drugs.  
- Rasagiline is **not** an MAE and can antagonize selegiline’s enhancer effect in some models — class is **not** “all MAO-B drugs.”

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Motivation / drive / “get things done” under demand

- **Phenotype:** Higher willingness to act when a goal is present; less flatness; not forced stimulation at rest.  
- **Pathways:** P1, P2, P5, P6, P16 (DA in SN/striatum/OT; NE LC).  
- **Chain:** DAT entry → intracellular TAAR1 → PKC → larger impulse-evoked vesicular DA/NE → D1-linked locomotion readiness; D2 autoreceptor partially caps excess.  
- **Clinical:** None in humans. Rodent locomotion + learning under tetrabenazine load.  
- **Forum:** Sparse “functional” reports.  
- **Practice:** Effects should track **task engagement** more than couch stimulation (**Speculative** human).  
- **Certainty of desired effect in humans:** **Unknown** (mechanism **Probable**).

### 6.2 Mood / antidepressant-like lift

- **Pathways:** P1 (DA+NE+5-HT), P17.  
- **Chain:** Simultaneous CAE+SAE is theoretically closer to a “balanced monoamine” push than pure NDRI or pure SSRI — still not an SSRI.  
- **Evidence:** Strong model data; Knoll argued clinical antidepressant potential > selegiline; **no trial**.  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative** for human MDD.

### 6.3 Cognitive performance / aging cognition

- **Pathways:** P1, P12, P13, P14, P19.  
- **Evidence:** Learning preservation in aged BPAP rats (2016); β-amyloid protection *in vitro*; neurotrophins at high conc only.  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative** human cognition/anti-aging.

### 6.4 Parkinsonian / hypodopaminergic support (research interest)

- **Pathways:** P1 DA, P16, L-DOPA-sparing reserpine data.  
- **Contrast:** Unlike selegiline, **no MAO-B** contribution to DA spare. Pure enhancer strategy.  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative** human PD.

### 6.5 Longevity / healthspan (research interest)

- **Pathways:** P14, P19 + selegiline longevity literature as class analogy.  
- **Evidence:** BPAP rat lifespan extension at 0.0001 and 0.05 mg·kg⁻¹.  
- **Certainty:** **Probable** in that protocol; **Speculative** outside it / in humans.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

No human AE tables. Below: **mechanism-expected** sides + sparse anecdote, each with pathway→counter engineering.

### 7.1 Insomnia / hyperarousal / early waking

1. **Phenotype:** Difficulty initiating sleep, lighter sleep, early AM wake, residual wired feeling.  
2. **Mechanism:** Enhanced **LC-NE** (P1, P21) + DA mesocortical tone; possible 5-HT sleep architecture shifts (P20). Not orexin-proven.  
3. **Pathway → counter**

| Pathway node | Counter | How it hits the node | Clinical | Forum | Practice notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|----------------------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| LC-NE excess | Dose reduction / morning-only timing | Less enhancer occupancy at night | Class logic from stims | General nootropic practice | Respect t½ ~6 h rodent → avoid late doses | **Probable** |
| Catecholamine arousal | Sleep hygiene, dark/cool room | Behavioral brake on wake drive | Strong sleep med literature | High | Non-drug first | **Established** (hygiene) |
| Residual monoamines | Magnesium glycinate evening | Mild NMDA/GABAergic calm (indirect) | Mixed insomnia data | Common | Adjunct only | **Speculative** for BPAP |
| Hyperarousal | L-theanine 200 mg | α-wave / glutamate modulation | Modest human data | Common | Can stack daytime if jitter | **Speculative** |
| Severe insomnia | Temporary cessation | Remove driver | — | — | First-line if sleep collapses | **Probable** |
| Hypothesized 5-HT sleep disrupt | Avoid stacking SSRIs/5-HTP | Reduce 5-HT overload risk | Interaction principle | — | Critical if SAE engaged | **Probable** |

### 7.2 Anxiety / jitter / tachycardia

1. **Phenotype:** Edge, restlessness, palpitations.  
2. **Mechanism:** NE enhancement ± high-dose NET inhibition (P1, P8, P22).  
3. **Counters**

| Pathway node | Counter | How | Clinical | Forum | Notes | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|-----|----------|-------|-------|-----------|
| NE tone | Lower dose; confirm not in µM-reuptake regime | Leave secondary NET/DAT | — | — | Biphasic: higher ≠ better | **Probable** |
| Peripheral sympathetic | Avoid caffeine stack initially | Remove additive NE | Established combo risk | High | Common fail mode | **Probable** |
| Acute anxiety | Propranolol (if appropriate medically) | Peripheral β-block | Established situational anxiety | — | Medical supervision | **Probable** for phenotype not BPAP-specific |
| Central edge | Theanine / taut breathing | Partial anxiolysis | Modest | Common | — | **Speculative** |

### 7.3 Visual oddities / perceptual “weirdness”

1. **Phenotype:** Trails, color shift, mild hallucinogenic quality (rare report vs PPAP).  
2. **Mechanism (hypothesized):** SAE → cortical 5-HT tone (P20); impurity; or high-dose off-targets. **Not** established 5-HT2A agonism.  
3. **Counters**

| Node | Counter | How | Certainty |
|------|---------|-----|-----------|
| Excess 5-HT enhancement | Dose cut or stop | Reduce SAE drive | **Speculative** |
| Stacked serotonergics | Remove 5-HTP/SSRI/MDMA-class | Avoid synergy | **Probable** (safety principle) |
| Impurity | Analytical source only | Quality control | **Probable** risk control |

### 7.4 Flat / paradoxical non-response

1. **Phenotype:** “Nothing” or worse function after dose change.  
2. **Mechanism:** **Bimodal curve (P7)** — between peaks, effect collapses; or too high into non-specific range; or DAT blocked by other drugs.  
3. **Counters:** Re-titrate **down** before up; avoid chronic DAT inhibitors that block entry (P3); log dose carefully. **Probable** pharmacology.

### 7.5 Serotonin syndrome risk (theoretical)

1. **Phenotype:** Agitation, clonus, hyperthermia (medical emergency).  
2. **Mechanism:** SAE + MAOI/SSRI/SNRI/tramadol etc. High-dose weak MAO-A (P9) worsens if someone “pushes dose.”  
3. **Counters:** **Hard separation** from MAOIs and caution with serotonergic Rx; emergency care if syndrome suspected. **Probable** interaction class risk despite weak solo MAO-A.

### 7.6 Mood instability / irritability

1. **Mechanism:** DA/NE overshoot, sleep loss secondary, 5-HT imbalance.  
2. **Counters:** Sleep repair first; dose down; avoid poly-stim stacks. **Speculative** incidence.

### 7.7 Cardiovascular load

1. **Mechanism:** Catecholamine enhancement (P22).  
2. **Counters:** BP/HR self-check if experimenting; avoid in uncontrolled HTN / arrhythmia (**Probable** prudence). No BPAP-specific CV trial data (**Unknown** magnitude).

### 7.8 Dependence / rebound

1. **Knoll claim:** MAEs less prone to tolerance/abuse than releasers because release remains impulse-gated.  
2. **Evidence:** No human abuse liability study for BPAP. Rodent psychostimulant-like locomotion exists but differs from amphetamine dump.  
3. **Certainty of “no misuse potential”:** **Speculative**. Treat cessation irritability as possible (**Unknown**).

### 7.9 Product / dosing error (practical “side”)

- Microgram scale + research-chem supply chain → **overdose into reuptake/MAO range** is a real operational hazard.  
- **Counter:** Analytical COA, volumetric mg/mL dilution, conservative start. **Probable** risk.

## 11. Comparative context

| Agent | Mechanism vs BPAP | Evidence in humans | Sides / practical | When preferred |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Selegiline** | CAE + **MAO-B**; amphetamine metabolites; less SAE | Approved PD/depression (patch); longevity mixed | Cheese effect only if nonselective dose; known AE profile | Want approved drug + MAO-B |
| **PPAP** | CAE, little SAE; high-dose DNRI | None approved | Forum: push dose → DNRI feel | Want catecholamines without 5-HT |
| **IPAP** | SAE-leaning MAE | Preclinical | Niche | 5-HT-priority research |
| **Amphetamine / MPH** | Releaser / reuptake — **not** impulse-gated | Extensive | Abuse, crash, CV | Need reliable clinical ADHD efficacy |
| **Bromantane** | Adaptogen / DA synthesis enzyme upregulation (different) | Limited clinical | Milder | Prefer non-TAAR1 RC path |
| **Ulotaront / clinical TAAR1 agonists** | TAAR1 but often **antipsychotic** direction (reduce DA synthesis capacity in some models) | Clinical pipelines | Different phenotype | Not interchangeable with BPAP MAE story |

**Key conceptual point:** Modern **TAAR1 agonist antipsychotics** and Knoll **MAE/TAAR1 enhancer** narratives can look contradictory at the slogan level; they likely engage **different signaling contexts, partial vs full agonism, cell populations, and doses**. Do not assume BPAP = ulotaront.
