# Example: Mixed-evidence nootropic — Bacopa monnieri (calibration)

**Why this is a quality target:** Tests mixed human data + strong community use + anecdotal weighing without overselling.

---

## Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound / class** | Bacopa monnieri — botanical nootropic / adaptogen-adjacent (neuro + nutrition) |
| **Overall certainty** | **Probable** for modest memory endpoints with standardized extracts; lower for broad “smart drug” claims |
| **Verdict** | Reasonable evidence for small cognitive benefits with weeks of use; not a stimulant; expect delayed onset. |
| **Key practical note** | Often 300–600 mg/day standardized extract (e.g. 50% bacosides) for ≥8–12 weeks in trials — **Probable** where extract matches literature. |
| **Top caveats** | Extract standardization varies; GI side effects common; acute “feeling it” is not the trial model. |

---

## 7. Subjective / Experiential Profile (focus section)

| Theme | Consistency | Concordance |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Subtle memory / learning benefit after weeks | Moderate–high | **partial** — aligns directionally with RCTs; effect sizes modest |
| Anxiolysis / calm focus | Moderate | **partial** / **weak** depending on scale |
| Acute stimulant-like boost | Low–moderate claims | **contradictory** — not how trials are designed; often **Speculative** marketing |
| Brain fog on start / GI upset | Moderate | **partial** with AE tables |

**Weighing:** Controlled trials support modest memory-related benefits with chronic standardized use (**Probable**). Forums often overstate speed and magnitude of effects. Acute “limitless” framing is **not** literature-backed (**Speculative** / **Unknown**). Consistent reports of delayed, subtle benefit are directionally compatible with trials (concordance: **partial**).

## 8. Practical Guidance (excerpt)

- Prefer standardized extracts used in trials; 300–600 mg/day range is commonly studied (**Probable** when product matches).  
- Plan ≥8 weeks before judging cognitive endpoints (**Probable**).  
- Do not market as acute stimulant replacement (**Unknown** / contradicted for that use case).  
- With food if GI-sensitive (**Probable** practical note).  

## 15. FAQ (excerpt)

| Claim | Verdict | Certainty |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| “Works day one like modafinil” | Not supported | **Unknown** / contradicted for that framing |
| “All bacopa products are equal” | Standardization matters | **Probable** |

### Calibration note for reviewers

Must include concordance rating and must not upgrade pure forum hype to Established.
