## 3. Mechanism & Pharmacology

### What research says

Cerebrolysin is framed as a **pleiotropic neurotrophic-factor–mimetic** mixture, not a single receptor ligand.

- **NTF pathway engagement (models + limited human biomarkers):** Influences signaling related to BDNF, NGF, IGF-1, VEGF, and inflammatory pathways (e.g., TNF-α); preclinical themes include anti-excitotoxic / anti-apoptotic signaling, neuroplasticity, and BBB integrity narratives (Rejdak & Neglur 2023) — **Probable** as a multimodal pharmacology working model; **Speculative** for precise human receptor occupancy or single-peptide causal chain to clinical disability outcomes.
- **Not recombinant BDNF/NGF:** Do not equate the mixture to pure NTF biologics — **Speculative** identity claim if stated as equivalence.
- **PK:** Classical single-entity half-life is not meaningful; rapid peptide clearance expected. Therapeutic volumes require **IV infusion** (label: dilute 10–50 mL to ≥100 mL; infuse ~15–60 min). Low-volume IM/IV push only for small volumes per label. **Not oral.** BBB PK sparse — **Speculative**.

Clinical use, if any, fits **acute injury/neurorecovery + cyclic dementia courses**, not chronic daily oral nootropic kinetics.

## 4. Documented Effects

**Lead with what research truly supports**, then grades.

### 4.1 Acute ischemic stroke (AIS)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Early neurological deficit (NIHSS ~day 21–30) | Bornstein 2018 MA (9 RCTs, N=1879): MW 0.60; NNT ~7.7 for clinically relevant early NIHSS change; 30–50 mL × 10–21 d. Patel 2025 independent-facing SR/MA (14 RCTs, N=2884): ΔNIHSS MD +1.39 (0.53–2.25) | **Probable** |
| Day-90 functional independence (unselected mRS) | CASTA (Heiss 2012, N≈1070): **overall primary not met**. Patel 2025: mRS 0–2 RR 1.31 (0.90–1.91) **NS**, high heterogeneity | **Unknown** (residual subgroup hypotheses only are Speculative) |
| Day-90 function, moderate–severe only (higher baseline NIHSS) — **C3 final** | Bornstein secondary ordinal mRS MW 0.61 (N=314, NIHSS>12); CASTA post-hoc trends | **Speculative** (post-hoc / severity-restricted; not Probable day-90 proof). Early NIHSS remains separately **Probable** |
| Mortality | Cochrane AIS / Patel: **no demonstrated mortality reduction** | **Probable** null for death benefit (**Established** that mortality benefit is not shown) |
| Motor recovery (selected programs) | CARS 2016 ARAT day 90 positive with structured rehab (industry-linked) | **Probable** for early motor surrogates in that niche |
| Hemorrhagic transformation | Patel pooled HT RR 0.55 (0.32–0.92) | **Speculative** |
| Reperfusion adjunct (r-tPA / MT) | CEREHETIS: early deficit/sICH signals; day-90 function not clearly improved. Emerging MT cohorts (e.g., Staszewski 2025) | Early compatibility **Probable**; day-90 superiority **Speculative** |

**Stroke bottom line:** Best-supported claim is **modest early NIHSS improvement** (**Probable**). Day-90 independence in **unselected** AIS is **Unknown** / not Established (CASTA null primary; Patel mRS NS). Moderate–severe day-90 enrichment is **Speculative** (Bornstein subset N=314 / CASTA post-hoc) — hypothesis-generating only. Industry MAs emphasize early recovery; Cochrane/AHA emphasize hard outcomes and non-endorsement — **material tension**.

### 4.2 Traumatic brain injury (TBI)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Multidimensional recovery (moderate–severe) | CAPTAIN II (Muresanu 2020): MW_combined ~0.59 day 90; CAPTAIN-style 50 mL then cycles. Vester 2021 prospective MA of series supportive. CAPTAIN I (Poon) ITT primary miss undercuts robustness | **Probable** signal with **single-center + industry network caveats** (multi-center generalizability remains limited / not Established) |
| Broader TBI literature | Jarosz 2023 SR: heterogeneous 10–50 mL designs; directionally favorable | **Probable** short-term signals; not global SOC |

### 4.3 Alzheimer’s disease (mild–moderate)

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Short-term cognition / global change | Gauthier 2015 MA: cognition SMD −0.40 at 4 wk; global clinical change OR 3.32 (4 wk) / 4.98 (6 mo); global benefit MW 0.57; safety ≈ placebo | **Probable** short–medium-term CGI/cognition |
| 6-month cognition durability | Gauthier: 6-mo cognition SMD CI crosses null | **Unknown** for sustained cognitive benefit (bordering Speculative) |
| Disease modification | No modern biomarker-staged confirmatory package | **Unknown** |

### 4.4 Vascular dementia

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Short-term cognition / global | Cochrane (Chen 2013 / Cui 2019): modest MMSE/ADAS-cog/global signals (e.g., MMSE WMD ~+1); **insufficient for routine recommendation** | **Probable** small short-term effect; **not** routine-care **Established** |

### 4.5 Prevention / healthy cognition

| Outcome | Finding | Certainty |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Prevention of cognitive decline | No robust primary-prevention RCTs (AlzDiscovery: prevention **unknown**) | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |
| Anti-aging / IQ biohacking | Forum/marketing claims | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |

### 4.6 Safety (effects profile)

- SAE and mortality **generally comparable to placebo** in stroke safety MAs (Strilciuc 2021: 12 RCTs, N=2202; Patel 2025 SAE/mortality NS) — **Probable**.
- Cochrane AIS lineage: possible **non-fatal SAE increase** in some analyses — residual **Unresolved** discordance with industry-leaning safety MAs.
- Common non-serious AEs: headache, dizziness, flushing, nausea, injection-site reactions, agitation/insomnia reports.

## 5. Documented Risks & Contraindications

### Label contraindications (EVER SmPC-class materials — posology/CI only)

| Contraindication | Note |
|------------------|------|
| **Hypersensitivity** | Porcine peptide origin — anaphylactoid risk |
| **Epilepsy** / major seizure disorders | Label CI; seizure facilitation concern |
| **Severe renal impairment** | Peptide/amino-acid load safety concern |

### Precautions

- Additive CNS effects with antidepressants/MAOIs per product warnings — **Probable** caution (sparse dedicated DDI RCTs).
- Pregnancy/lactation: inadequate data → avoid elective use (**Unknown** safety).
- Porcine origin: allergy, cultural, religious considerations.
- **Established** procedural risk of unsupervised parenteral DIY (infection, dosing error).

### Risk tiers

| Risk | Certainty |
|------|-----------|
| SAE/death ≈ placebo (short stroke courses, modern MAs) | **Probable** |
| Non-fatal SAE imbalance (Cochrane subset analyses) | **Unresolved** / residual concern |
| Seizure provocation if predisposed | **Probable** enough to honor absolute CI |
| Multi-year cycling long-term safety | **Unknown** |
| Theoretical prion/zoonotic risk | **Speculative** residual; no modern outbreak signal in package reviewed |

## 9. Practical Guidance

**Boundary:** Educational, trial/label-aligned. **Unknown = no recommendation.** Lawful access + clinician supervision required.

### 9.1 Route (**Established** for pharmaceutical use)

- **Primary:** IV infusion of labeled concentrate diluted to ≥100 mL (saline / Ringer’s / 5% glucose); infuse ~15–60 min.
- IV slow push ≤10 mL undiluted; IM ≤5 mL undiluted (low-volume only).
- **Not oral.** **Recommend against DIY SQ / research-chem / gray-market “peptide” use** — efficacy **Unknown**; procedural harm **Established**.

### 9.2 Dosing by indication (trial/label-aligned)

| Indication | Daily dose | Schedule | Certainty for *regimen when indicated* |
|------------|------------|----------|----------------------------------------|
| **AIS** | **20–50 mL IV** (trials often **30–50 mL**) | Start ASAP (≤12–72 h in trials); **10–21 days** once daily | **Probable** only for early NIHSS-oriented adjunct where registered; day-90 unselected independence **Unknown**; moderate–severe day-90 enrichment **Speculative** (C3); **not Established** for death benefit |
| **TBI moderate–severe** | Acute **20–50 mL** (CAPTAIN **50 mL** × 10 d); then cycles (e.g., **10 mL** × 10 d × 2 with off intervals per CAPTAIN series) | Multi-week plan | **Probable** multidimensional adjunct (single-center/industry caveat) |
| **Mild–moderate AD** | **10–30 mL IV** (evidence densest at **30 mL**) | Cycles: e.g., **5 days/week × 4 weeks**; **2–4 cycles/year** | **Probable** short-term CGI/cognition; not US SOC |
| **VaD** | **10–30 mL** cyclic | Same cyclic pattern | Small effect **Probable**; **recommend against routine use** (Cochrane) |
| **Prevention / healthy enhancement** | — | — | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** |

Dosing conventionally expressed in **mL** (215.2 mg/mL concentrate).

### 9.3 Timing & co-interventions

- **Stroke/TBI:** Early initiation matches trial design — **Probable** time-sensitive window.
- **Dementia:** Intermittent IV courses, not lifelong daily oral.
- **With rehab / standard care:** Studied as add-on (CARS, CAPTAIN, stroke packages) — **Probable** that rehab/SOC context matters; never a substitute for reperfusion, BP/secondary prevention, or approved dementia Rx.

### 9.4 Comparisons to other neurotrophic / dementia agents

| Comparator | Relationship | Practical take |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| **AChEIs** (donepezil etc.) | Different MoA; oral SOC in AD where approved; limited combo RCTs (Alvarez lineage) | Prefer AChEIs as foundational AD Rx where available (**Established** role). Cerebrolysin optional IV adjunct in registered markets (**Probable** short-term add-on). Combo with donepezil **Probable** for additive/global signal in limited data — not US practice standard. |
| **Citicoline** | Separate mixed literature | **Unknown** comparative superiority (no large head-to-head MA) |
| **Recombinant NTFs** | Conceptual cousins; not systemic SOC drugs | Do not equate (**Speculative** equivalence) |
| **Actovegin / animal-derived peers** | Regional biologics | Judge each by own RCTs |
| **tPA / thrombectomy** | Reperfusion SOC | Cerebrolysin **adjunct only** (**Established** hierarchy) |
| **Anti-amyloid mAbs** | Different targets/risks | Not interchangeable |

### 9.5 Monitoring & harm reduction

| Domain | Action | Certainty |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Pre-treatment | Screen epilepsy, eGFR/Cr, allergy/porcine sensitivity, MAOI/antidepressants | **Established** good practice from label CIs |
| Infusion | Hypersensitivity, BP, agitation, headache; sterile technique | **Established** |
| Neurologic scales | NIHSS/mRS, GCS/GOSE, ADAS-cog/CGI as appropriate; stop for new seizures | **Probable** |
| SAE context | Stroke/TBI populations have high baseline SAE rates — standard acute monitoring | **Probable** |
| Unsupervised self-injection | **Recommend against** | **Established** harm-reduction stance |

### 9.6 Explicit “not recommended when”

| Scenario | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Healthy prevention / anti-aging / IQ biohacking | **Unknown** → **no recommendation**; **recommend against** DIY parenteral |
| Active epilepsy / severe renal failure / hypersensitivity | Contraindicated — **do not use** |
| Sole therapy instead of reperfusion, SOC stroke care, or approved dementia Rx | **Recommend against** |
| Oral or SQ research-chem substitution for IV pharmaceutical regimens | **Unknown** → **no recommendation** / **recommend against** |
| Framing as FDA-approved US SOC | **False** — do not frame as such |

### 9.7 Bottom-line regimen map (actionable)

1. **AIS (registered markets, specialist care, shared decision):** Consider **30 mL IV daily × 10–21 days** early after onset as **adjunct** to full standard stroke care — aim for **early neurological recovery** (**Probable**), not proven mortality reduction. Day-90 independence in **unselected** AIS is **Unknown**. Moderate–severe day-90 functional enrichment is **Speculative** (Bornstein subset N=314 / CASTA post-hoc), **not** Established universal day-90 benefit and **not** a Probable day-90 protocol claim — it may inform counseling only.
2. **Moderate–severe TBI:** CAPTAIN-style **50 mL × ~10 d + lower-dose cycles** (**Probable** multidimensional benefit; single-center/industry caveat; CAPTAIN I ITT miss).
3. **Mild–moderate AD (where registered):** **10–30 mL** (often **30 mL**) **5 d/wk × 4 wk**, **2–4 cycles/year** — short-term cognitive/global support (**Probable**); keep AChEI/memantine per local guidelines when indicated.
4. **VaD:** Prefer **not routine** (Cochrane insufficient).
5. **Healthy / prevention:** **No recommendation** (evidence **Unknown**).

## 11. Compound Context Summary

Cerebrolysin is a **porcine brain-derived multimodal neuropeptide IV infusion** (EVER Neuro Pharma), registered in many countries for stroke, TBI, and dementia but **not FDA-approved**. Mechanistically it is an **NTF-mimetic mixture**, not pure recombinant BDNF. Best human signals: **early post-stroke NIHSS** (**Probable**), **TBI multidimensional recovery** (**Probable**, single-center/industry caveats), and **AD short-term CGI/cognition** (**Probable**). Unselected AIS day-90 independence is **Unknown**; moderate–severe day-90 enrichment is **Speculative** (subset/post-hoc), not Established. Mortality benefit not shown. VaD: insufficient for routine use. Prevention: **Unknown**. Safety ≈ placebo **Probable**. Supervised pharmaceutical IV only; **against DIY SQ**. Adjunct only.
