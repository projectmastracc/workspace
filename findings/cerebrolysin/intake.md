## Sources acquired (pivotal)

### Stroke
1. **Bornstein et al. 2018** — Meta-analysis 9 RCTs early post-stroke NIHSS; MW 0.60, N=1879; NNT 7.7; day-90 mRS in moderate-severe MW 0.61 N=314; 30–50 mL × 10–21 d. PMID: [29248999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29248999); DOI 10.1007/s10072-017-3214-0. PMC available.
2. **Patel et al. 2025** — SR/MA 14 RCTs N=2884; ΔNIHSS MD +1.39 (0.53–2.25); mRS 0–2 RR 1.31 NS; SAE/mortality NS; hemorrhagic transformation RR 0.55. PMID: [41018475](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41018475); Cureus/PMC free. RoB2 + GRADE.
3. **Strilciuc et al. 2021** — Safety MA 12 RCTs N=2202; SAE/mortality NS vs placebo; trend SAE reduction high dose. DOI 10.3390/ph14121297; MDPI.
4. **Heiss et al. 2012 CASTA** — Large Asia double-blind RCT AIS (referenced in meta-analyses). PMID context Stroke 2012;43:630–636. DOI 10.1161/STROKEAHA.111.628537.
5. **Muresanu et al. 2016 CARS** — Motor recovery stroke RCT (in Bornstein/Guekht meta packages).
6. **Khasanova/Kalinin 2023 CEREHETIS** — Add-on to r-tPA; reduced sICH/early deficit; day-90 functional outcome not significantly improved (secondary reports).
7. **Staszewski et al. 2025** — Adjunct after mechanical thrombectomy (small core); functional outcomes at 90 d reported improved in that cohort. Transl Stroke Res. DOI 10.1007/s12975-025-01355-z.
8. **AHA/ASA 2019 AIS guidelines** — Cerebrolysin not a standard US guideline-endorsed therapy (Powers et al. Stroke 2019 PMID 30869305; DOI 10.1161/STR.0000000000000211) — use for Guidelines vs Literature.

### TBI
9. **Muresanu et al. 2020 CAPTAIN II** — RCT moderate-severe TBI; multidimensional outcomes; 50 mL then cycles. PMID: [31897941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31897941).
10. **Vester et al. 2021** — Prospective meta-analysis CAPTAIN series. PMID: [33620612](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33620612).
11. **Jarosz et al. 2023** — Systematic review TBI (Brain Sci); doses 10–50 mL; heterogeneous designs. PMC10046100 (recaptcha may block; abstract-level + secondary).

### Dementia / cognitive decline
12. **Gauthier et al. 2015** — Meta-analysis mild-moderate AD RCTs; global change, cognition, benefit-risk favorable. PMID: [25832905](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25832905); Dement Geriatr Cogn Disord. DOI 10.1159/000377672.
13. **Wei et al. 2007** — Earlier AD meta-analysis 6 RCTs CGI. J Neural Transm. DOI 10.1007/s00702-007-0630-y.
14. **Chen et al. 2013 Cochrane** — Cerebrolysin for vascular dementia; promising but insufficient for routine recommendation; small effect. CD008900. DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD008900.pub2.
15. **AlzDiscovery Cognitive Vitality** (2016 full report) — Independent-ish synthesis; safety generally good; dementia prevention **unknown**; AD meta-analyses positive but quality caveats; VaD small effect.

### Mechanism / pharmacology / dosing references
16. **Rejdak & Neglur 2023** (Med Res Rev) — NTF modulation review; Cerebrolysin multimodal BDNF/NGF/IGF-1/VEGF/TNF-α narrative. DOI 10.1002/med.21960.
17. **EVER Neuro Pharma product monograph / dosage sheets** (cerebrolysin.com PDFs) — Labeled indications (where registered): stroke, TBI, AD, VaD; typical dose tables 5–50 mL IV; cycles for dementia; contraindications epilepsy, severe renal impairment, hypersensitivity. **Industry primary** — use for posology labeling, not efficacy proof.
18. **Porcine brain protein origin** — mixture of low-MW peptides + amino acids; neurotrophic factor-mimetic pharmacology (multimodal).

### Comparative neurotrophic agents (context)
19. Literature framing vs: single NTFs (BDNF/NGF not systemic drugs), citicoline, actovegin, erythropoietin/neuroprotectants, AChEIs (donepezil combo trials in AD), stem-cell/rehab-only — comparisons are mostly **indirect** (no large head-to-head MAs).

## Key claims to adjudicate
| Domain | Claim space |
|--------|-------------|
| Stroke efficacy | Early NIHSS benefit **supported by MAs**; day-90 mRS/functional independence more mixed (CASTA neutral overall; subgroup/moderate-severe signals) |
| TBI | CAPTAIN series positive multidimensional outcomes; industry-linked investigators common |
| AD | Meta-analyses favor Cerebrolysin on CGI/cognition short-medium term; not FDA-approved; not US standard of care |
| VaD | Cochrane: small benefit, insufficient for routine use |
| Prevention of cognitive decline | **Unknown** / no robust prevention RCTs |
| Safety | Large safety MAs ≈ placebo for SAE/death |
| MoA | Multimodal NTF-mimetic; not a single receptor ligand |
| Dosing | Trial-based 10–50 mL IV; dementia cyclic regimens |
