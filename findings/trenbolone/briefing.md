# Trenbolone (Tren) — Full Compound Profile

**Research ID:** ecd74116  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Class:** Synthetic 19-nor anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS); Δ9,11-triene  
**Status:** Veterinary growth implant (TBA); human Parabolan (hexahydrobenzylcarbonate) historically marketed in limited jurisdictions; not approved for human medical use in most places; WADA banned; US Schedule III

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and receptor pharmacology

- **Parent:** 17β-hydroxyestra-4,9,11-trien-3-one — nandrolone backbone (C19 demethylation) plus conjugated double bonds at C9–C10 and C11–C12 → planar 3-oxotriene.
- **AR:** High affinity; binding assays and reviews place it at or near DHT potency (≈3× testosterone in some AR systems). **Established** molecular.
- **Not a substrate for aromatase** — no conversion of tren itself to estradiol. **Established**.
- **Not a substrate for 5α-reductase** — no local amplification to a DHT-like metabolite in skin/prostate; metabolites of 17β-trenbolone are generally less androgenic than parent. Underpins Yarrow’s tissue-selectivity argument (muscle/bone preserved; prostate less stimulated than equipotent TE in ORX models). Enzymology **Established**; gym-user prostate phenotype **Probable** modified but not “safe.”
- **PR:** High affinity (Bauer-class progestin receptor binding). Drives non-aromatizing gyno/lactation hypotheses. **Established** binding; **Probable** breast phenotype contribution; **Speculative** quantitative PRL drive from PR alone.
- **GR:** Binding reported; framed as partial antagonist / antiglucocorticoid contribution to anticatabolic effect (atrogin-1/MuRF1 suppression in rodent series). **Probable** binding; functional antiglucocorticoid contribution at gym doses **Speculative–Probable**.
- **SHBG:** Weaker interaction than many 17α-alkyl orals after de-esterification (injectable free alcohol).

### Esters and practical PK

Human PK of free trenbolone and esters is **sparse** (no modern labeled human ADME package for performance doses). Clinical duration = **ester release from oil depot**, not free-steroid half-life (free 17β-trenbolone is short — hours after de-esterification).

| Ester | Common name | Approx. practical depot behavior / cadence | Notes |
|-------|-------------|--------------------------------------------|-------|
| Acetate | TBA / “tren ace” | Depot EHL often cited ~1–3 d (depot-dependent); EOD–ED injections | Fast on/off; more cough reports (injection frequency); preferred for short cuts and abortability |
| Enanthate | Tren-E | ~7–11 d cited (depot-dependent); 2–3×/wk | Longer residual; smoother peak/trough for some |
| Hexahydrobenzylcarbonate | Parabolan / hex | ~8 d class citations | Historical human product (France); scarce UGL |
| Undecanoate | — | Longer depot | Rare |

- **Routes:** IM oil (primary); SC used by some; veterinary implants are SQ pellets (≠ gym pulse IM — do not extrapolate implant safety).
- **CNS penetration:** Lipophilic sterane; crosses BBB (and placental barrier in models). **Established** physicochemical; CNS phenotypes **Probable**.
- **Metabolism:** Hepatic A-ring and 17-position reduction/oxidation; urinary metabolites form WADA detection basis (Schänzer-class frameworks).
- **Food effects:** N/A for injectables.
- **Legal/source risk:** UGL contamination, mislabeling, solvent/oil quality → abscess, fibrosis, rare Nicolau-type necrosis beyond pure pharmacology. “Tren” on a log may be under-dosed, over-dosed, or not tren. **Unknown** product identity is a structural confound for all forum/case data.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### Stratification overview

| Tier | Tren-specific | AAS class (transportable by analogy) |
|------|---------------|--------------------------------------|
| Systematic review / meta (performance) | None | Global AAS prevalence; CV observational syntheses |
| RCT performance / hard outcomes | **None** modern | Sparse TRT-dose RCTs (not tren) |
| Prospective observational | Essentially none tren-only | **HAARLEM** (Smit et al.): n≈100 cycles — BP, lipids, HCT, echo n=31 |
| Cross-sectional CV imaging | Tren buried in “AAS user” cohorts | **Baggish 2010** LV dysfunction; **Baggish 2017** coronary plaque |
| Case reports / series | AMI, psychosis/suicide attempt, multi-organ compilations | Large |
| Qualitative social science | **Piatkowski 2023** tren-focused PIED interviews | Broader AAS ethnography |
| Preclinical / livestock | Dense — implants, ORX Yarrow/McCoy, CNS Ma/Zelleroth, Borecki 2024 | — |

**Design ceiling:** Controlled recreational tren RCTs are ethically/legally barred in most jurisdictions. HAARLEM is near-ceiling feasible design for **class** cycle physiology — not tren isolation.

### Key studies — measures, effects, limits

**Borecki et al. 2024 (*Endokrynol Pol*; PMID 38887114)** — Narrative organ review (nervous, muscle, adipose, reproductive, breast, immune). Best single tren-titled map. Strength: multi-organ pathway inventory. Limits: not PRISMA systematic; inherits animal/case weight; polypharmacy acknowledged. Use as **map**, not incidence. Certainty of synthesis: **Probable** as inventory; human causal isolation weak.

**Yarrow et al. 2010 (*Steroids*; PMID 20138077) + Yarrow/McCoy ORX rodent series** — Tissue selectivity: TREN preserves/augments muscle and bone with less prostate enlargement vs TE; GR/growth-factor framing; SARM-like clinical application hypothesis (cachexia/obesity). Strength: controlled ORX design, graded dose, TE comparator. Limits: **ORX hypogonadal rat ≠ eugonadal gym user on test base**; short duration; no human CV/sleep/psych endpoints; high-dose TREN still raises prostate/Hb — selectivity is dose-window-dependent. Certainty: **Probable** rodent selectivity; **Speculative** translation to recreational polypharmacy.

**HAARLEM (Smit et al.) — class AAS, not tren-only**  
Prospective Dutch amateur men, self-directed multi-AAS cycles (median ~4 compounds; crude ~900 mg/wk T-equivalents; GH/orals/recreational drugs common). Within-subject T0→T1→recovery.

| Endpoint | On-cycle change (approx.) | Recovery | Certainty for class |
|----------|---------------------------|----------|---------------------|
| SBP / DBP | **+6.87 / +3.17 mmHg** (≈+7/+3); HTN prevalence 41% vs 16% baseline (office) | Toward baseline ~3 mo off | **Established** direction/magnitude order |
| LDL-C | **+0.45 mmol/L** (~+17 mg/dL) | ~3 mo | **Established** |
| ApoB | **+18.2 mg/dL** | ~3 mo | **Established** (atherogenic signal) |
| HDL-C | **−0.40 mmol/L** (~−15 mg/dL) | ~3 mo | **Established** |
| Lp(a) | Mean **~−38 to −50%** | Reverses off | Does **not** cancel ApoB harm (**Probable** net adverse) |
| HCT | **+0.03 L/L** (3 pp); ~1/3 subjects HCT >50% on-cycle vs ~5% baseline | ~3 mo | **Established** |
| 3D LVEF (echo n=31) | **−4.9%** | Baseline median ~8 mo off | **Established** reversible subclinical remodeling |
| LV mass | **+28.3 g** (dose-correlated with weekly AAS) | Baseline | **Established** association |
| E/A; LA volume | **−0.45**; **+9.2 mL** | Baseline | **Established** diastolic/structural signals |

**Critical limits:** Not randomized; polypharmacy; 1:1 T-equivalence **understates tren potency**; black-market dose misclassification; office BP only; no MACE endpoint; follow-up ≤1 year. **Tren-specific deltas Unknown.** Use as **class quantitative spine** with transportability caveat.

**Baggish 2010 (*Circ Heart Fail*)** — Long-term AAS users (n=12) vs matched weightlifters (n=7): LVEF ~50.6% vs ~59.1%; impaired strain/diastolic indices. Small cross-sectional. Certainty: **Probable** class association long cumulative exposure with reduced LVEF/strain; tren fraction **Unknown**.

**Baggish 2017 (*Circulation*)** — CTA: long-term illicit AAS associated with greater coronary plaque volume; lifetime duration gradient. Certainty: **Probable** association; residual confounding; not compound-specific; poor model for single short cycle without lifetime frame.

**Case reports** — AMI on tren+test±nandrolone (e.g. Makarem 2025: smoker, FHx CAD); psych crises including suicide attempt on T+tren; multi-organ compilations. **Established** that severe events **can** occur in stacks including tren; **Unknown** incidence, dose threshold, tren-unique causality.

**Piatkowski et al. 2023 (*Drug Alcohol Rev*; PMID 36992616)** — Qualitative n≈16 tren narratives: mood/cognition “mush,” high psychosocial harm framing vs other AAS. Certainty: **Probable** lived-experience signal; zero incidence.

**Parkinson & Evans 2006** — Survey n=500 AAS users: high stacking/dose culture, near-universal subjective sides. Not tren-isolated. Context only.

**ACC 2024 expert analysis (athletes + AAS)** — Clinical risk frame (lipids, BP, CAD, remodeling); lists **community** tren acetate **150–300 mg/wk** and enanthate **200–300 mg/wk** as practice context — **not** medical endorsement.

**Ma & Liu 2015; Zelleroth 2019** — BBB/Aβ neurodegeneration models; cortical caspase toxicity at high in-vitro concentrations. Support **Speculative** human long-term CNS risk; do not equal clinical dementia diagnosis.

### What clinical data do **NOT** show

- No modern RCTs of trenbolone esters for human muscle, strength, recomp, or hard safety outcomes.  
- No established therapeutic index or “safe recreational dose.”  
- No tren-only prospective CV epidemiology separating tren from stacks.  
- No controlled human PSG/actigraphy of sleep architecture on tren.  
- No clean isolation that tren alone raises prolactin vs stack, PR activity, training stress, or sleep loss.  
- No human confirmation of cortical volume loss or Aβ accumulation at gym doses.  
- No head-to-head human trials tren vs nandrolone vs masteron vs SARMs.  
- HAARLEM reversibility of short-cycle surrogates does **not** prove long-term Baggish-type plaque risk resets.

### Weighing

Forum data are **high-density, high-bias** (selection, stack confounding, UGL identity, expectancy, survivorship). For tren, concordance among (1) near-universal sleep/sweat reports, (2) Piatkowski qualitative, and (3) dense preclinical CNS/thermoregulatory plausibility is strong enough to treat **trensomnia, sweats, and irritability** as **Probable** human phenotypes without PSG trials. Recomp/hardness sits in the same tier. **Prolactin-as-sole-villain** is weaker — multi-node **Speculative–Probable** (PR, sleep, E2 from test base, nandrolone co-use, dopamine). Literature silence on sleep mechanism ≠ absence of effect. “Low-dose tren is side-free” is a minority report of **heterogeneity**, not a safety theorem. Never convert forum frequency into incidence %.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Skeletal muscle hypertrophy and strength

- **Phenotype:** Rapid LBM accrual, myocellular density, strength out of proportion to water weight.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P3, P4, P5, P6 (± P2 speculative).  
- **Mechanism chain:** High-affinity AR occupancy → ARE-driven MPS genes → parallel Wnt/β-catenin stabilization and Notch restraint via Numb → satellite-cell activation/fusion (myonuclear addition) → IGF-1/FGF sensitization and elevated IGF-1 tone (livestock-clear) → GR-axis restraint of proteolysis (atrogin-1/MuRF1). Non-aromatizing profile avoids estrogenic extracellular water that can mask “hard” look.  
- **Clinical support:** No human tren RCT; class AAS hypertrophy **Established**; Yarrow ORX muscle preservation **Probable**; satellite/Wnt maps **Probable** preclinical.  
- **Forum support:** Very high consistency.  
- **Practical notes:** Effects visible within weeks on acetate; enanthate lags. Absolute gains confounded by test base, training, diet. Superiority vs equal-androgen-load TE: **Probable** subjective; **Speculative** quantified human.

### 6.2 Recomposition / lipolysis

- **Phenotype:** Simultaneous fat loss and muscle retention/gain; reduced subcutaneous smoothing.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P11, P1, P6, P22.  
- **Mechanism chain:** Adipocyte/pre-adipocyte AR → suppressed adipogenic transcription (PPARγ, C/EBPα) + Wnt bias away from fat lineage → rodent multi-depot fat loss; possible hepatic β-oxidation enzyme induction (**Speculative** human); anticatabolic GR effect preserves lean mass in deficit; appetite suppression can force deficit.  
- **Clinical support:** Rodent strong; human androgen–adipocyte data support direction; tren-specific DEXA trials absent.  
- **Forum support:** Signature claim — very high.  
- **Practical notes:** Best in mild deficit + high protein; appetite loss can wreck surplus bulks. Certainty: **Probable**.

### 6.3 “Hardness,” vascularity, dry look

- **Phenotype:** Dense, grainy appearance; less subcutaneous water than test/nandrolone.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P7, P11.  
- **Mechanism:** No E2-mediated water from tren; fat loss over muscle; glycogen/intramuscular shifts anecdotal.  
- **Support:** Mechanism + forum; not a lab endpoint. Certainty: **Probable** cosmetic.

### 6.4 Strength / training aggression (wanted facet)

- **Phenotype:** Heavier sets, competitive drive.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P14 (GABA_A/NMDA/AR-CNS — double-edged).  
- **Mechanism:** Myocellular force capacity + CNS AR/NMDAR/GABA tone.  
- **Risk:** Same nodes produce irritability. Wanted effect **Probable**; clean separation from sides **Speculative**.

### 6.5 Bone (secondary)

- **Phenotype:** Bone mass/strength in ORX models (Yarrow/McCoy).  
- **Human gym relevance low.** **Probable** rodent; **Speculative** performance relevance.

## 8. Practical use

**Honesty frame:** There is **no Established safe recreational protocol** for trenbolone. Rows below are **harm-reduction patterns** from community practice + ACC-listed dose context + mechanism — labeled **Speculative** as protocol, not medical endorsement.

### Order of operations (engineering sequence)

1. **Exposure budget first** — dose, duration, ester (acetate = more injection frequency/cough/peak sides; longer esters smoother PK but longer residual).

3. **Baseline phenotype & labs** — BP series, CBC, lipids/ApoB, CMP, total/free T, E2, LH/FSH, PRL if 19-nor history, CV and psych history; semen bank if fertility matters.

### Monitoring panel (harm-reduction intensity)

| Domain | Markers / tools | Cadence |

| Blood pressure | Home log | Daily on-cycle |
| Hematocrit / Hb | CBC | Baseline, mid (~4–8 wk), end |
| Lipids | LDL-C, HDL-C, TG, **ApoB** preferred | Baseline, mid, end, +6–12 wk off |
| Liver | ALT/AST/GGT; context with CK | Baseline, mid |
| Kidney | Creatinine, eGFR, cystatin C if available; urinalysis | Baseline, mid |
| Endocrine | Total T, E2 (sensitive), LH/FSH (off/PCT), PRL if breast/libido symptoms | As indicated |
| Metabolic | Fasting glucose/HbA1c | Baseline/end |
| Cardiac | ECG; echo if long AAS history, HTN, symptoms, or abnormal exam | Baseline high-risk; symptom-triggered |
| Subjective | Sleep diary, mood partner-check, libido, nipple exam | Continuous |
| Fertility | Semen analysis / bank pre if future kids matter | Pre-cycle |

### Contraindications (strong practical)

- Uncontrolled hypertension, known CAD/HF, high-risk VTE context  
- Active psychosis, uncontrolled bipolar, severe unipolar depression  
- Desire for near-term fertility without banking  
- Pregnancy (teratogenic androgen environment)  
- Unwillingness to monitor or stop  

### Stop criteria (hard)

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Sustained BP ≥160/100 or symptomatic HTN / uncontrolled on meds | Stop; treat |
| HCT above clinic threshold (often >52–54%) with symptoms or continued climb | Hold; phlebotomy plan |
| Chest pain, syncope, severe dyspnea, neurologic deficits | Emergency care; permanent re-eval |
| Paranoia, psychosis, suicidal ideation, violence ideation | Immediate cessation + psych emergency |
| Sleep collapse unresponsive to dose cut + hygiene | Abort tren |
| Rapid gyno / galactorrhea with distress | Hold; labs; targeted therapy |
| LVEF drop / new significant LVH on echo | Stop AAS; cardiology |
| Acute severe POME / anaphylaxis | Emergency; abandon oil route |
| Marked AKI, rhabdomyolysis suspicion | Emergency |

## 11. Comparative context

| Agent | Efficacy (recomp/hard look) | Side character | Evidence base | Convenience |
|-------|----------------------------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Trenbolone** | Top-tier dry recomp | Sleep, sweats, psych, CV class, PR | Preclinical dense; human RCT none | Pins; UGL risk |
| **Testosterone** | Best-characterized bulk/TRT; wetter at high dose | E2 management, HCT, CV class milder subjectively than tren for many | Human data rich | Esters flexible |
| **Nandrolone** | Joint comfort, mass; wetter | PR/PRL, deep suppression, less “tren psych” for many | More clinical history than tren | Weekly pins |
| **Masteron (drostanolone)** | Hardening, mild antiestrogenic reputation | Androgenic (hair/skin); milder CNS than tren | Sparse modern trials | Often stacked not solo |
| **SARMs (ostarine, RAD140, LGD)** | Oral convenience; milder gains | Suppression, lipids; less long-term data; not “mild tren” | Emerging clinical | Oral; detection varies |

**Takeaway:** Tren is chosen when hardness/recomp intensity outweighs sleep and CNS tax. Test remains the controllable backbone. Nandrolone trades dry look for joints and different PR burden. Masteron is a finishing androgen without tren’s folklore severity. SARMs are not mechanistic equivalents of a 19-nor triene injectable.

## 12. Open questions

1. **Human PSG + thermoregulation** study of tren acetate vs enanthate vs placebo — locks sleep/sweat mechanism and validates counters.  
2. **Tren-stratified prospective CV cohort** (plaque, LVEF, hard events) separate from mixed AAS.  
3. **Isolated prolactin RCTs** — tren alone vs tren+test vs nandrolone on serial PRL, sleep-controlled.  
4. **PR-selective strategies** for non-E2 gyno without shotgun caber.  
5. **Dose–response cognitive measures + structural MRI** in users (ethical/design hard).  
6. **Whether Yarrow-type prostate sparing survives** eugonadal high-dose human exposure with test base.  
7. **UGL identity analytics** linked to observed toxicity (vs cough/side myths).  
8. **Spermatogenesis recovery kinetics** after tren-containing vs test-only cycles of matched duration.  
9. **GR occupancy fraction in vivo** at gym serum levels — quantifies anticatabolic claim.  
10. **OSA treatment interaction** with tren sweats/HCT/BP — high clinical leverage if positive.  
11. **Non-genomic AR and orexin hypotheses** untested in humans.  
12. **Long-term neurodegeneration translation** from Ma & Liu — remains **Unknown**.

Until these exist: potent anabolic 19-nor with class AAS cardiometabolic liabilities (**Established–Probable**), plus a distinctive community adverse-effect signature centered on sleep, autonomic heat, and mood (**Probable** experience; **Speculative** mechanisms), without a defined safe dose (**Unknown** / not established).

## 14. Short boundary

Trenbolone is an unapproved-for-human, high-intensity AR/PR-active 19-nor with exceptional recomp pharmacology and a side-effect surface dominated by sleep, autonomic heat, neuropsych, HPG shutdown, and class cardiovascular risk. This document maps mechanisms and harm-reduction counters; it is not a prescription, not evidence of a safe recreational regimen, and not a substitute for physician-led care.
