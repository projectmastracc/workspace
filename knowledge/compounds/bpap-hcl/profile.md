# (−)-BPAP HCl — Compound Profile (Knowledge)

**Slug:** bpap-hcl  
**Updated:** 2026-07-31  
**Source briefing:** findings/bpap-hcl/briefing.md

## Identity
- (R)-(−)-1-(benzofuran-2-yl)-2-propylaminopentane HCl (FPFS-1169)
- Monoaminergic activity enhancer (CAE + SAE); not a classical releaser at enhancer doses

## Core mechanism
- Impulse-evoked vesicular release enhancement of DA, NE, 5-HT
- Probable intracellular TAAR1 agonism after DAT entry; PKC + VMAT2 involvement (Harsing 2022)
- Bimodal fM–pM and µM peaks; high-dose DAT/NET reuptake inhibition; weak MAO-A only at high concentration

## Evidence ceiling
- Preclinical: strong
- Human clinical: none
- Forum: sparse

## Practical anchors
- Suggested exploratory human dose in literature: ~0.1 mg/day (Speculative; untested)
- Rodent enhancer activity from ~0.0001 mg/kg
- t½ rat ~5.5–5.8 h; oral absorption with enterohepatic second peak

## Major risks
- No human safety data
- Serotonergic stacks / MAOIs
- Dose error at microgram scale; biphasic pharmacology
