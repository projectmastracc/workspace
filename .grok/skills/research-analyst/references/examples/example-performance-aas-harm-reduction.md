# Example: Performance compound harm reduction — Trenbolone class notes (calibration)

**Why this is a quality target:** Safety, monitoring, and refusal to invent “safe tren” protocols when evidence does not support them. Audience: gym/performance researchers — honest harm reduction, not encouragement.

> Abbreviated structural example focusing on Practical Guidance + Safety. Full monographs expand all 15 sections.

## Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound / class** | Trenbolone (and related esters) — anabolic-androgenic steroid (performance) |
| **Overall certainty** | **Established** class androgenicity/suppression; human controlled outcome data limited vs real-world harm signals **Probable** |
| **Verdict** | High-risk AAS; strong anabolic reputation in practice communities; medical harm potential is material — monitoring and risk reduction are the evidence-aligned practical focus, not optimization of abuse. |
| **Key practical note** | No “safe recreational protocol” is Established. If exposure occurs despite risks: aggressive monitoring, shortest effective duration mindset, exit/PCT planning, mental-health watch. |
| **Top caveats** | CV/lipids, HPTA suppression, neuropsychiatric/sleep sides, legality, source quality. |

## 8. Practical Guidance (harm-reduction oriented)

| Topic | Guidance | Certainty |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Recreational “recommended dose for gains” | **Do not recommend** a performance dose as healthy use | **Unknown** / not medically endorsed |
| Risk reduction if already using | Lower dose, shorter duration, avoid polypharmacy stack escalation | **Probable** (harm reduction logic) |
| Monitoring | BP, fasting lipids, HCT/hemoglobin, LFTs as indicated, symptoms (sleep, aggression, anxiety, libido), ECG if CV risk | **Probable** |
| PCT / recovery | Plan for profound suppression; SERM/HCG strategies are common in community practice — evidence quality variable; label clearly | **Speculative**–**Probable** by component |
| Protectives (e.g. BP agents, lipids) | Address specific side pathways with agents that have their own evidence (e.g. lifestyle, indicated antihypertensives) — not magic peptide covers | per agent |
| Contraindications | Prior psychosis, uncontrolled HTN/CVD, pregnancy (absolute for androgens), etc. | **Established**–**Probable** by item |

## 9. Safety, Side Effects, Contraindications & Monitoring

**Mandatory topics:** cardiovascular strain, adverse lipids, erythrocytosis, night sweats/insomnia, appetite changes, androgenic skin/hair effects, suppression of endogenous testosterone, potential renal stress signals, mental-health decompensation.

**Monitoring protocol example (Probable harm-reduction frame):**

- Baseline and on-cycle: BP, lipids, CBC/HCT  
- Symptom diary: sleep, mood, resting HR  
- Stop/escalate-care criteria: chest pain, severe dyspnea, psychiatric crisis, HCT above agreed threshold with clinician  

## 7. Subjective / Experiential Profile (abbrev.)

Forums consistently report high anabolic “bang,” poor sleep, night sweats, and mood volatility for many users (concordance with clinical concern: **partial**–**strong** for side-effect themes; efficacy claims are confounded).

**Weighing:** Community efficacy reputation is strong but not a substitute for controlled risk data. Side-effect themes are consistent enough to treat as **Probable** practical risk signals even where RCTs are sparse.

### Calibration note for reviewers

**FAIL** if missing monitoring/harm reduction.  
**FAIL** if Unknown “optimal tren cycle” is presented as Established.  
**PASS** if certainty ceilings are honest and monitoring is concrete.
