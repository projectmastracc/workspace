- Discovery MoA cluster (partial agonist, N/C, gene selectivity, Fst, osteoblast markers) is **overwhelmingly Kanno/Toho**.

- Independent **replication of N/C interaction and gene-selective transcriptome** by external labs: **not present** in this source set (**Unknown** if exists outside acquisition; **not Established**).

### 4.4 Primary vs secondary for “selectivity”

Source-critic judgment: **selectivity claims beyond gene-selective transcription in limited models are not traced to primary multi-tissue in vivo data in this set** (**Established** gap).

### 5.1 Tier definitions applied

- **High:** Independent, peer-reviewed, preferably multi-study or regulatory primary for its domain, full methods available, clean/transparent funding for the claim type.  
- **Moderate:** Legitimate peer-reviewed primary or good secondary; limitations in design tier, single lab, or incomplete audit.  
- **Low:** Secondary only, incomplete retrieval, or weak independence.  
- **Unreliable:** Marketing, anonymous blogs, or broken provenance.

**No source in the MoA set reaches High for clinical SARM claims** because the evidence tier is preclinical and partially single-lab (**Established**).

## 7. Practical guidance for analysts using these sources (source-use rules only)

Certainty labels apply to **how sources may be used**, not pharmacology conclusions:

1. **Cite Kanno 2011/2013 with DOI/PMID** for AR partial agonism and Fst induction — **Probable** as primary origin of those lab observations; treat as **Moderate** trust, single lab.  
2. **Do not cite Lee 2021 alone** to prove myostatin receptor antagonism — Lee **applies** a reframed label (**Speculative** as mechanism category if used that way).  
3. **Do not cite Park or Wikipedia as primary** for MoA — secondary/tertiary.  
4. **Health Canada** supports **regulatory: unauthorized, unreviewed products** and **class risk language** — **Established** as regulatory fact; not dose guidance.  
5. **Human efficacy, safety margins, oral gym dosing:** **Unknown** in peer-reviewed literature acquired — **no recommendation** may rest on these sources (**DR rule: Unknown = no recommendation**).  
6. **Independent replication gap** for N/C and Fst claims should be stated whenever certainty is graded above Probable for those mechanisms.
