## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and classification

- **Parent:** Boldenone = testosterone with dehydrogenation at C1–C2 (Δ1,4-diene-3-one, 17β-ol). Same skeleton as metandienone (Dianabol) **without** the 17α-methyl group.
- **Not 17α-alkylated** → does not share the classical oral C17α-alkyl cholestatic/hepatotoxic pattern of methandrostenolone, stanozolol, etc. **Established** (structure + AAS pharmacology; Kicman 2008).
- **Ester:** 17β-undecylenate (undecenoate; 11-carbon unsaturated fatty acid) is the veterinary/clinical standard. MW ester ~452.7 g/mol; free boldenone ~286.4 g/mol. Cypionate appears in UGL with shorter practical duration.
- **Prodrug logic:** Oil depot IM → slow release of ester → esterase hydrolysis → free boldenone → systemic AR agonism.

### Legal / regulatory

- **US:** Schedule III controlled substance; no FDA human approval; veterinary path only (Equipoise for horses).
- **WADA:** S1 Anabolic Agents, prohibited at all times; no legitimate human TUE (USADA).
- **Historical human brand:** Parenabol discontinued ~late 1970s.
- **IARC/USADA language:** Androgenic steroids as a class are Group 2A (probably carcinogenic to humans); USADA applies this framing to boldenone — treat as **class-level**, not a boldenone-only carcinogenicity trial. Individual risk magnitude at BB doses: **Unknown**.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### Evidence structure (honest ranking)

| Tier | What exists for boldenone specifically |
|------|----------------------------------------|
| Systematic reviews / meta of human bodybuilding EQ | **None** dedicated |
| RCTs healthy athletes (hypertrophy, Hct, lipids at 400–1000 mg/wk) | **None** modern |
| Historical human pharmaceutical use | Parenabol era (discontinued late 1970s); not a usable efficacy database for modern PED doses |
| Observational AAS / doping | Boldenone among detected AAS; multi-drug case series; USADA: meaningful positive counts (e.g. 87 boldenone cases in 2018 WADA testing figures ~6% of anabolic-agent positives that year) |
| Veterinary efficacy | Horse appetite/condition labels (~0.5 mg/lb IM q3 wk); growth/hematology in food animals |
| Controlled animal toxicology | Relatively rich (hepatorenal oxidative injury, reproductive, lipids) |

| Secondary pharmacology | Kicman 2008 (class); Llewellyn *Anabolics* (ratios/aromatization — not peer-reviewed primary) |

**Explicit statement:** Clinical data do **not** show dose–response curves for lean mass, strength, or “quality look” at community doses. They do **not** quantify human aromatization rate vs testosterone. They do **not** define evidence-based Hct cutoffs unique to EQ. **Absence of RCTs is not proof of safety or of null effect.**

### 4.1 Anabolic efficacy / physique

- **Measured:** Essentially not in modern controlled human trials.
- **Direction:** Expected positive from AR agonism; veterinary weight/condition positive.
- **Population:** Horses, food animals; human PED users uncontrolled.
- **Limits:** Cannot extract effect sizes vs test or nandrolone from RCTs.
- **Certainty for “builds muscle”:** **Established** as AR agonist pharmacology; **Probable** human utility at BB doses (forum + class); **Unknown** for quantified superiority vs peers.

### 4.2 Appetite

- **Veterinary:** Core Equipoise use case (appetite, general condition) — labeled path.
- **Human clinical:** Not systematically trialled.
- **Certainty:** Phenotype **Probable** (vet + high forum); mechanism node **Unknown**.

### 4.3 Erythropoiesis / hematocrit

- **Animal:** Multiple species show ↑RBC, Hb, Hct after boldenone (broilers, rabbits, calves).
- **Human:** AAS class erythrocytosis well documented; boldenone repeatedly flagged in secondary sources and forums as a **standout** Hct raiser among injectables. Controlled EQ-only human Hct curves lacking; EQ-vs-test delta at matched AR exposure **Unknown**.
- **Certainty:** Erythrocytosis risk **Probable** (class + animal + very high forum coherence); “EQ is among the worst injectables for Hct” **Probable** forum ranking, not RCT-proven rank order.

### 4.4 Endocrine (HPTA, estrogen)

- **HPTA:** AAS class suppresses LH/FSH/T; animal boldenone reproductive toxicity (↓ testes size, sperm parameters). **Established** class; **Probable** for BLD specifically. Recovery kinetics after long undecylenate: **Unknown–Speculative** individual.
- **Aromatization ~50% of T:** Handbook claim (Llewellyn lineage). **Not** confirmed by modern controlled human comparative aromatization PK. Quantitative claim = **Speculative**. Practical estrogen phenotype often mild/low when EQ is large stack fraction — **Probable** anecdotal concordance.
- **Progestogenic activity:** Minimal — **Probable**.

### 4.5 Cardiovascular (BP, lipids, thrombosis)

- **Lipids:** Human AAS literature: ↓HDL, often ↑LDL/TG. Behairy 2021 BLD rats: TC +56%, TG +45%, LDL +140%, HDL −41% vs control. **Probable** human direction for BLD; **Established** as AAS class pattern.
- **BP:** Class effect + viscosity from Hct. **Probable**.
- **Thrombosis / viscosity:** Composite of polycythemia, lipids, BP, possible endothelial stress. **Probable** risk elevation vs no AAS; magnitude **Unknown**.

### 4.6 Hepatic — Behairy et al. 2021 detail

- **Classical C17α-alkyl hepatotoxicity:** Low for non-alkyl injectable. **Established** structural principle.
- **Behairy et al. 2021 (*Front Pharmacol* 12:651497):** Male Wistar rats, BLD **5 mg/kg IM once weekly × 8 weeks** (Equigan®) ± vitamin C 120 mg/kg/day PO; n≈8/group; declared no commercial COI.
  - ↑ALT (~192%), ↑AST (~100%)
  - Dyslipidemia as above
  - ↑urea, creatinine, uric acid; electrolyte shifts (↓Na+ ↑K+)
  - Hepatic/renal ↑MDA, ↓GSH, GPx, GST, GSR
  - Histology: hydropic change, lipidosis, **peliosis-like** blood-filled spaces, biliary hyperplasia; renal glomerular/tubular injury
  - ↑AR and Hsp90 immunoexpression liver/kidney
  - VC partially reversed oxidative and functional markers; **did not** normalize AR overexpression
- **Allometric note:** Naive 5 mg/kg/wk × 80 kg ≈ 400 mg/wk is **invalid** 1:1 scaling. BSA-style HED lands roughly ~55–75 mg/wk free-weight equivalent — far below BB doses. Either framing: rodent injury at modest allometric exposure is a **hazard signal**, not a human lesion-rate estimate. Ester depot PK, species AR/SHBG differences, and single-strain limits further constrain translation.
- **Human:** Controlled BLD liver safety at 600–800 mg/wk **Unknown**. AAS liver disease case literature confounded (orals, multi-drug, alcohol).
- **Certainty:** Animal hepatorenal oxidative injury **Established** at studied regimen; human clinical hepatotoxicity lower than oral 17α-alkyls but **not zero** — monitoring **Probable** prudence.

### 4.7 Renal

- Animal: Behairy and related series show functional and structural renal injury under BLD.
- Human AAS: FSGS, elevated creatinine (muscle mass confound), case reports of kidney injury with heavy AAS — not EQ-specific RCTs.
- **Certainty:** Concern **Probable** at PED doses over long cycles with BP/Hct load; hard human incidence **Unknown**.

### 4.8 Neuropsychiatric

- No EQ-specific RCTs. Forum anxiety/restlessness signal is the main human evidence. Mechanism **Speculative**; phenotype **Probable** in a susceptible subset.

### 4.9 Carcinogenicity / reproductive

- USADA: reproductive harm in animals; classed with androgens as probable human carcinogen (IARC class language). Long-term human cancer epidemiology for EQ alone **Unknown**.

### 4.10 What clinical data do **not** show

1. Optimal human dose for hypertrophy vs Hct tradeoff.  
2. True % aromatization vs testosterone in men.  
3. Whether undecylenate vs cypionate changes side-effect profile beyond kinetics.  
4. That “not 17α-alkyl” means organ-safe at 800 mg/wk × 20 weeks.  
5. That blood donation fully normalizes thrombosis risk while still on cycle.  
6. Head-to-head RCT: EQ vs primo vs DHB vs anavar for lean look.  
7. Quantified incidence of “EQ anxiety” or human peliosis at BB doses.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Lean mass quality / “dry” look / vascularity

- **Phenotype:** Gradual muscle size increase with less subcutaneous water than high-aromatizing test-only blasts; visible veins when body fat allows.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 (AR), P4/P6 (low practical estrogen relative to high-test), P9 (RBC → perfusion appearance).
- **Mechanism chain:** AR-driven MPS and nitrogen retention → growth over many weeks; lower average E2 vs wet stacks → less extracellular fluid; ↑Hb/Hct and training volume → fuller muscle and vascular display.
- **Clinical:** AR agonism **Established**; aesthetic RCTs absent.
- **Forum:** High consistency on long EQ + test “quality” look.
- **Practice:** 12–16+ weeks; 600–800 mg/wk common for visible quality; 300–400 more “support/endurance.” Certainty: effect **Probable**; dose optimum **Speculative**.

### 6.2 Appetite increase

- **Phenotype:** Noticeable hunger, easier bulk.
- **Pathway IDs:** P11 (orexigenic — node unclear).
- **Mechanism chain:** Unknown central/peripheral sensor; veterinary condition scores improve with appetite.
- **Clinical/vet:** Strong vet use case.
- **Forum:** High.
- **Practice:** Budget calories; if fat gain is problem, dose down or tighten diet rather than assume “EQ is always dry.” Certainty: **Probable**.

### 6.3 Endurance / work capacity

- **Phenotype:** Better conditioning blocks, less “gassing,” higher session volume.
- **Pathway IDs:** P9 primarily; P1 recovery secondary.
- **Mechanism chain:** EPO/erythropoiesis → oxygen carrying capacity → aerobic work; AR recovery supports training frequency.
- **Clinical:** Animal hematology positive; human EQ VO2 trials lacking.
- **Forum:** High.
- **Practice:** Overlap with polycythemia risk — **same pathway**. Certainty: **Probable**; magnitude **Speculative**.

### 6.4 Strength (secondary)

- **Phenotype:** Strength rises with mass and recovery; rarely described as tren-like neural aggression.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** moderate; not the primary reason people pick EQ over other AAS.

### 6.5 Collagen/joint “feel” (context-dependent)

- **Phenotype:** Some report joints feel better than on dry DHT compounds **if** E2 is not crashed; others report aches if low-E2.
- **Pathway IDs:** P4/P6, P23, P1.
- **Certainty:** **Speculative**; manage via E2/test base, not via assuming EQ is joint-healing.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 Polycythemia / high hematocrit / elevated RBC mass

1. **Phenotype:** Rising Hct/Hb/RBC over weeks–months; ruddy complexion; headaches; dizziness; high BP; lab flags often mid-to-late cycle. Community concern thresholds commonly discussed around **Hct 50–54%** (some act earlier; some tolerate mid-50s with medical oversight — practice varies).
2. **Mechanism:** P9 EPO/erythropoiesis drive (androgen-stimulated renal EPO + direct marrow androgen effects classically invoked) → expanded red cell mass → ↑blood viscosity (P10) → ↑cardiac afterload and thrombotic propensity (P13, P18). Longer cycles and higher doses amplify because RBC production ramp is slow. Smoking, sleep apnea, high altitude, dehydration, and concurrent AAS stack risk.

2. **Mechanism:** ↑viscosity from Hct (P9→P10→P13); AAS-related fluid/electrolyte and vascular tone changes (P19, P2); dyslipidemia long-term vascular stiffness (P12); possible sleep disruption/anxiety sympathetic tone (P17).

### Order of operations (engineering sequence)

1. **Baseline labs** — CBC (Hct/Hb/RBC), CMP (ALT/AST/GGT, creatinine), cystatin-C if available, fasting lipids/apoB, eGFR, BP log 1–2 weeks, sensitive E2, total/free T, LH/FSH, A1c/glucose; hematology/VTE history; screen OSA risk.
2. **Testosterone base first** — establish stable test dose and subjective E2/libido before adding EQ.
3. **EQ add** — expect slow onset; community 600–1000 mg/wk × 12–16+ wk for primary “EQ look”; pin 2×/wk.
4. **No prophylactic AI.**
5. **On-cycle monitoring** — CBC q4–6 wk; home BP several×/week; E2+symptoms if mood/joints/libido shift; lipids mid/end; renal/hepatic mid/end.
6. **Phlebotomy plan pre-defined** — action lines often ~50–52% discuss; ≥52–54% strong reduce/phlebotomy pressure in forums; personalize with baseline, altitude, symptoms.
7. **Exit** — long ester washout (multiple weeks) before SERM-centric PCT if coming off; recheck CBC/lipids/hormones; do not re-blast long ester until Hct/BP/lipids recovered.

| Hct (approx.) | Common community response | Certainty |
|---------------|---------------------------|-----------|
| Rising but &lt;50% | Hydration, recan, watch trend | **Speculative** thresholds |

### Contraindications / high-caution contexts

- Competitive tested sport (detection months) — **Established** ban.  
- Uncontrolled hypertension, prior VTE/PE, polycythemia vera, significant CKD, active prostate cancer — high danger class (**Probable–Established**).  
- Pregnancy / women seeking to avoid irreversible virilization.  
- Inability to access labs or sterile gear.  
- Family/personal hypercoagulable states without specialist clearance.

### Stop criteria (practical)

Stop or immediately restructure if:

1. Hct beyond agreed threshold or symptomatic polycythemia.  
2. Sustained BP above personal red-line despite meds/dose cuts (e.g. ≥140/90 persistent — individualize with clinician).  
3. Acute coronary/neuro/VTE symptoms.  
4. Progressive renal decline or proteinuria.  
5. Unmanageable anxiety/insomnia.  
6. AI-crashed low-E2 not correcting with AI cessation and test adjustment.  
7. Infection at injection sites.  
8. Progressive unexplained enzyme rise with synthetic pattern (not training artifact alone).

## 12. Open questions

Data that would most change this map:

1. **Human multi-dose PK** of boldenone undecylenate at 300–800 mg/wk (AUC, free fraction, metabolites).  
2. **Head-to-head aromatization** study: boldenone vs testosterone isotopic or LC-MS estrogen production rates in men.  
3. **Prospective Hct curves** EQ-only vs test-only vs EQ+test at matched total AAS mg.  
4. **BP and apoB** randomized on EQ with/without ARB/statin co-therapy.  
5. **Human liver/kidney** serial cystatin-C/ALT with controlled designs at PED-relevant exposures (biopsy ethically barred).  
6. Mechanism of **appetite** (ghrelin/NPY/POMC).  
7. Mechanism of **EQ anxiety** (E2 interaction vs pure AR vs viscosity).  
8. Whether **front-loading** changes 16-week outcomes beyond early sides.  
9. True **DHB tissue conversion** fractions in human prostate/skin.  
10. Long-term **ASCVD event rates** in EQ-using cohorts with modern lipid therapy.  
11. Rigorous **E2 immunoassay vs LC-MS** comparison under boldenone metabolites.
