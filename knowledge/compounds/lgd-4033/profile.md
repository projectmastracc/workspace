# LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211)

**Aliases:** Ligandrol, Anabolicum, VK5211; market mislabel **LGD-4044**  
**Class:** Nonsteroidal SARM  
**Updated:** 2026-07-31  

## Essentials

| Item | Value | Certainty |
|------|-------|-----------|
| AR Ki | ~0.9 nM | Established |
| Human t½ | 24–36 h | Established |
| Clinical doses | 0.1–2 mg/day (trials) | Established |
| Gym doses | 5–10 mg/day common | Speculative community |
| LBM | Dose-dependent ↑ (Phase 1 ~1.2 kg/3 wk at 1 mg; Phase 2 up to ~9% placebo-adj at 2 mg/12 wk) | Established |
| Suppression | T, SHBG, HDL, TG dose-dependent; free T/FSH at 1 mg | Established |
| Liver | Clean mean enzymes in short low-dose RCT; cholestatic DILI case reports at ~10 mg unregulated | Probable class risk |
| Approval | Not FDA-approved; WADA S1.2 | Established |

## Pathways

Muscle/bone AR agonism; prostate partial; HPG feedback; hepatic HDL; no aromatization.

## Counters (short)

Enclomiphene/hCG/PCT for suppression; lipids lifestyle + omega-3; LFT stop rules; optional TUDCA/NAC (Speculative).
