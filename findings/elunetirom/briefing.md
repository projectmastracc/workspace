# Elunetirom (ABX-002) — Compound Profile

**Research ID:** 6d487b5d  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Audience:** Neuropsych / nootropic / performance-adjacent readers studying compounds  
**Status framing:** Investigational Phase 2 CNS thyromimetic — **not approved**, **not OTC**, **not a self-experimentation agent**. Full pathway and counter maps are for mechanism literacy and future clinical context.

| Field | Content |

| **Biggest upsides** | **Probable** (class): T3 psych-augmentation tradition supports CNS-TR as a live antidepressant node. **Speculative** (drug-specific): open-label BD HAMD signal + rs-fMRI connectivity change. **Probable** design thesis: TRβ preference + CNS-biased prodrug vs T3 cardiac/HPT/bone load. **Speculative**: psychoplastogen-like (neurogenesis, neurite, synaptogenesis) and atypical-depression energy phenotype match. |

| **Stack role** | **Not a stackable consumer agent.** If ever approved: adjunctive **CNS-TR booster** on mood stabilizer / atypical (BD) or antidepressant (MDD) — analogous niche to off-label T3, not a monoamine reuptake drug. ## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### 2.1 Identity and structure

| Parameter | Detail | Certainty |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| INN / codes | Elunetirom; ABX-002; MA-JD21 | **Established** |
| Active species | Prodrug → **LL-340001** (active TRβ-preferring agonist) | **Established** (identity); full public Ki/EC50 panels limited |
| IUPAC | 2-[3,5-dichloro-4-[(4-hydroxy-3-propan-2-ylphenyl)methyl]phenoxy]-N-methylacetamide | **Established** |
| Formula / MW | C19H21Cl2NO3; 382.28 g·mol⁻¹ | **Established** |
| Scaffold | Dichloro diarylmethane / phenoxy-N-methylacetamide thyromimetic-like scaffold (non-iodinated; not a T3 iodine congener) | **Established** chemistry |
| Selectivity claim | Potent, selective **TRβ** agonist; brain-enhanced vs therapeutic T3 peripheral exposures | **Probable** (sponsor + class design; in vivo selectivity often lower than in vitro — class lesson) |
| Activation platform | Autobahn **brain-targeting prodrug** platform: leverages **FAAH** (fatty-acid amide hydrolase) CNS-biased enzymatic cleavage; optionally tunable with peripherally restricted FAAH inhibitors | **Probable** platform MoA; quantitative brain:plasma free ratios for elunetirom **Unknown** publicly |

**Design thesis:** T3 works as antidepressant augmentation but is limited by **peripheral TR activation** (cardiac TRα-heavy tissue, bone, HPT suppression, monitoring burden). Elunetirom aims to uncouple CNS TR benefit from peripheral liability via (1) TRβ preference and (2) CNS-biased delivery of LL-340001 after prodrug conversion. **Design intent: Established** (company communications). **Full elimination of chronic peripheral risk: Speculative.**

### 2.2 Classification

- **Pharmacologic:** Thyromimetic; selective TRβ agonist; CNS-TR agonist.  
- **Regulatory:** Investigational new drug; **FDA Fast Track** for adjunctive bipolar depression (**May 2026**) — **Established** as process designation, **not** an efficacy finding, **not** approval.  
- **Not:** approved antidepressant, hormone replacement, OTC nootropic, anabolic, or liver-directed MASH drug (contrast **resmetirom**).

**Source rule:** Almost all clinical knowledge of elunetirom in humans is a **single-sponsor thread (Autobahn)**. Identity and parent-pathway (T3) science are multi-source. Efficacy in bipolar depression is currently a **one-company open-label press release**.

### 4.2 Phase 1 — healthy volunteers (NCT05528315 class; company Nov 7, 2023)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Design | Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; SAD + MAD; food effect; **n=48**, all completed |
| SAD | **0.0075–0.15 mg** single oral |
| MAD | **0.0028–0.0056 mg** QD × **14 days** |
| Safety | **No SAEs**; no safety discontinuations; generally well tolerated |
| PK | Dose-proportional, acceptable |
| HPT | Normal-range axis measures **except** transient **TSH reductions** at dose **54×** planned Phase 2 top |
| CNS PD | Top MAD **0.0056 mg**: **“mood alteration”** in **2 subjects** after ≥7 days, resolved by day 14–15; framed as analogous to T3 overmedication CNS effects; **not** seen at **0.0028 mg** |
| Funding | Sponsor Autobahn — high COI on interpretation; design (DBPC) better than open-label efficacy for safety claims |
| Limits | Healthy not depressed; short; company press not full peer tables; n underpowered for rare AEs (rule of three ≈ 6% upper CI for unseen events) |
| Certainty | Safety/PK short-term **Probable**; CNS TE via mood AE **Speculative** (soft AE, n=2, not PET/CSF biomarker); chronic peripheral selectivity **Unknown** |

**Mood-alteration as “target engagement” — validity:** Construct is **not** a validated engagement biomarker. Compatible with true PD, noise, expectancy under intense monitoring, or sleep disruption. Legitimate use: flag overstimulation/mania-watch nodes. Illegitimate use: proof of antidepressant MoA or dose-selection as “pharmacologically engaged.”

**What Ph1 does *not* show:** antidepressant efficacy, mania rates, chronic bone/cardiac safety, or zero HPT risk at all doses forever.

### 4.3 Phase 2 AMPLIFY-BD — bipolar depression (NCT06869187; company June 4, 2026)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Design | **Open-label, single-arm**, adjunctive; oral QD × **6 weeks** |
| Population | **n=21** adults, bipolar I or II, moderate–severe MDE; on mood stabilizer and/or atypical antipsychotic |
| Primary | Δ **HAMD-17** baseline → Week 6 |
| Efficacy topline (company-reported) | Mean HAMD-17 reduction **16.8** points W6 (**p<0.001** vs baseline); W2 **−9.7**; W4 **−13.7** |
| Response / remission (company-reported) | **75%** response (≥50% HAMD-17 ↓); **50%** remission (HAMD-17 ≤7) at W6 |
| Other scales | Company: significant improvements HAMD-6, HAMD-29, CGI-BP-S, PGI, SDS, SDQ |
| Atypical features | Significant improvements in atypical subgroup (company) — **exploratory** |
| Imaging | Subset **rs-fMRI**: connectivity changes in depression-relevant networks W0→W6 (company) |
| Safety | TRAEs **mild–moderate only**; **no severe or serious treatment-related AEs** (company) |
| Regulatory context | FDA **Fast Track** adjunctive bipolar depression (May 2026) — process status, not efficacy proof |
| Funding / COI | **Sole company source**; full peer presentation planned H2 2026; **maximum COI flag** for this package |

**Critical methodologic discount (binding):**

1. **No placebo / no blinded control** — bipolar depression placebo and nonspecific improvement rates historically substantial (~30–40% response range in many BD RCTs).  
2. **Small n=21** — unstable categorical rates; 75%/50% expected to shrink under blinding.  
3. **Open-label expectancy** inflates clinician-rated HAMD and patient globals in the same direction.  
4. **Regression to the mean** after moderate–severe MDE entry unmeasured.  
5. **Adjunctive** on active MS/AP — background treatment confounds attribution.  
6. **p-values vs baseline** test “did scores move?” — **not** “did drug beat placebo?” In open-label mood trials, large pre–post Δ with p<0.001 is **expected under nonspecific improvement**. Using these p-values as efficacy proof is a **category error**.  
7. Topline only — full AE tables, exact dose, labs (TSH, free T4, HR, weight, mania scales), missing-data handling not fully public.

**Certainty labeling for headline claims:**

| Claim | Certainty |
|-------|-----------|
| Participants improved on HAMD over 6 weeks (pre–post) | **Probable** as observation *if* CSR confirms; company-reported only |
| Improvement is **caused** by elunetirom beyond placebo/time/adjuncts | **Speculative** |
| 75% response / 50% remission will replicate in RCT | **Speculative** — treat as **promotional interim science** until PC peer-reviewed data |
| Rapid W2 signal is drug-specific | **Speculative** |
| rs-fMRI changes prove neuroplastic MoA | **Speculative** |
| Favorable short-term common-event tolerability in this sample | **Probable** (small n; rare events **Unknown**) |

**What AMPLIFY-BD does *not* show:** confirmatory efficacy, long-term maintenance, monotherapy effect, MDD efficacy, absence of switch risk, or superiority/non-inferiority vs quetiapine, lurasidone, cariprazine, lumateperone, lamotrigine, lithium optimization, or T3.

### 4.4 Phase 2 AMPLIFY — MDD (NCT06633016)

- Adjunctive MDD; **placebo-controlled** (first design that can actually test efficacy claims).  
- Topline expected **Q3 2026** (company).  
- **No primary efficacy readout** at research date (2026-07-31).  
- Certainty: **Unknown**. Do not forecast success from AMPLIFY-BD OL magnitudes. Positive MDD Ph2 does **not** automatically confirm BD efficacy (and vice versa).

### 4.5 Parent pathway clinical anchor — T3 / T4 (not elunetirom)

These support **why CNS-TR is a credible target**, not that elunetirom is proven.

| Body | Finding | Certainty (for T3/T4, **not** elunetirom) |
|------|---------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Aronson et al. meta** (T3 + TCA refractory depression) | Standardized ES ~**0.62**; roughly **~2×** odds of response vs control in pooled older trials; quality uneven | **Probable** class signal with limits |
| **Altshuler et al.** acceleration meta | T3 accelerates TCA response; effect larger with higher % women; ES ~**0.58** | **Probable** for acceleration question |
| **STAR\*D** Level 3 | T3 vs lithium after two failures — comparable efficacy signal; T3 often better tolerated than lithium in that context | **Probable** practical |
| **SSRI combinations** | Mixed RCTs; some enhancement positive; open series ~35–45% response | **Probable** modest adjunct |
| **Network meta augmentation (Zhou-type)** | Thyroid hormone among agents with OR for response vs placebo ~**1.8** range; sensitivity less robust than some atypicals | **Probable** with network caveats |
| **Bipolar depression T3** | Parmentier & Sienaert-type reviews: **6/7** studies suggestive; methodologically weak; guidelines sometimes mention | **Speculative→low Probable** (weaker than unipolar) |
| **Supraphysiologic T4** (Bauer et al. rapid-cycling / refractory bipolar) | Research use; PET brain metabolism context; not standard first-line | **Speculative** for broad practice; specialized |

**Transfer rule:** T3 efficacy **Probable** (heterogeneous) in unipolar augmentation → elunetirom efficacy **Speculative** until controlled data. Shared nuclear TR gene programs in brain: **Probable** class biology. “T3 works → elunetirom works”: **invalid** absolute transfer.

### 4.6 What clinical data do **not** show (explicit)

- Approved indication or consumer availability.  
- Placebo-subtracted effect size for bipolar depression.  
- Head-to-head vs T3, lithium, atypical antipsychotics, or lamotrigine.  
- Duration of effect after discontinuation; relapse prevention.  
- Suicidality outcomes (not powered/reported in topline).  
- Bone density, fracture, AF incidence, fertility.  
- Pediatric, geriatric-specific, pregnancy.  
- Remyelination clinical benefit in MS/AMN.  
- Nootropic / cognitive-enhancement efficacy in healthy users.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 HPT axis suppression (↓TSH ± free hormone shift)

1. **Phenotype:** Lab TSH drop; with significant suppression, risk of iatrogenic thyrotoxic tissue state or recovery lag after stop; monitoring anxiety. 2. **Mechanism:** Agonist at pituitary/hypothalamic TR → negative feedback (**P7**); may reduce endogenous T4/T3 production. Elunetirom Ph1: normal-range axis at planned doses; TSH dip at **54×** Ph2 top — suggests **wide PD margin** short-term (**Probable**), not zero chronic risk (**Unknown**).

## 14. Short boundary

Elunetirom (ABX-002) is an **investigational Phase 2** CNS-TRβ prodrug from Autobahn Therapeutics — **not FDA-approved**, **not available for self-experimentation**, and **not** a consumer nootropic or cutting agent. The AMPLIFY-BD HAMD −16.8 / 75% / 50% figures are **company-reported open-label pre–post numbers** and must not be read as confirmed drug–placebo efficacy. Placebo-controlled MDD data are still pending. This document maps mechanisms, class risks, and counters for literacy around thyromimetic psychiatry — it is **not** a dosing protocol.
