### Primary / pivotal peer-reviewed
1. **Kanno et al. 2011** — *(17α,20E)-17,20-[(1-methoxyethylidene)bis(oxy)]-3-oxo-19-norpregna-4,20-diene-21-carboxylic acid methyl ester (YK11) is a partial agonist of the androgen receptor.* Biol Pharm Bull. 34(3):318–323. DOI: [10.1248/bpb.34.318](https://doi.org/10.1248/bpb.34.318); PMID: [21372378](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21372378). Abstract + overview acquired; full HTML 404 on J-STAGE path tried. 2. **Kanno et al. 2013** — *Selective androgen receptor modulator, YK11, regulates myogenic differentiation of C2C12 myoblasts by follistatin expression.* Biol Pharm Bull. 36(9):1460–1465. DOI: [10.1248/bpb.b13-00231](https://doi.org/10.1248/bpb.b13-00231); PMID: [23995658](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23995658). **Full text acquired** (J-STAGE HTML). Key claims: YK11 induces myogenic differentiation (MyHC, MyoD, Myf5, myogenin); **Fst mRNA induced by YK11 not DHT**; AR-dependent (FLU, AR siRNA); anti-Fst Ab reverses Myf5 induction; funding MEXT/Open Research Center.
3. **Yatsu/Kanno et al. 2018** — *Selective Androgen Receptor Modulator, YK11, Up-Regulates Osteoblastic Proliferation and Differentiation in MC3T3-E1 Cells.* Biol Pharm Bull. 4. **Lee et al. 2021** — *Myostatin inhibitor YK11 as a preventative health supplement for bacterial sepsis.* Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 543:1–7. DOI: [10.1016/j.bbrc.2021.01.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2021.01.030); PMID: [33588136](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33588136). ## Limitations for analysts
- Evidence base is almost entirely **in vitro mouse cell lines** + **limited mouse/rat in vivo**; zero controlled human efficacy/safety trials.
- “Myostatin inhibitor” is **indirect** (follistatin upregulation), not demonstrated as direct MSTN ligand blockade in the pivotal C2C12 paper.
- Bro-science dosing (e.g., 5–15 mg/day oral) has **no published PK or human outcome basis** — mark Unknown for recommendations.
