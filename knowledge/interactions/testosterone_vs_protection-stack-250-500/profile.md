# Protection Stack: Injectable Testosterone 250–500 mg/week

**One document.** Full pathway inventory → per-side mechanisms → pathway-matched counters → assembled phased protocol. Research synthesis for informed adults evaluating or using performance-range injectable testosterone (enanthate, cypionate, propionate, blends) at **>250 and <500 mg/week** (typical community cruise / light blast: ~300–450 mg/wk). Not medical or legal advice.

**Dose band:** Above typical medical TRT for most men (~75–200 mg/wk injectable replacement). Serum total T commonly lands well above the upper reference range on mid-cycle draws; free T is often more elevated because SHBG falls. Esters assumed: TE, TC, TP, or short-ester blends — same active hormone after hydrolysis.

**Critical non-misapplication:** TRAVERSE (NEJM 2023) tested **transdermal gel** titrated to **physiologic** total T (~350–750 ng/dL) in older hypogonadal men with CV risk — **not** 300–450 mg/week IM esters. Do **not** cite TRAVERSE as safety proof for this dose band. **Established** mismatch.

**Certainty labels:** **Established** | **Probable** | **Speculative** | **Unknown**

### Non-negotiables (stack spine)

1. **This band is supra-TRT.** Bhasin-class 300 mg/wk TE already produces ~1,300+ ng/dL nadir total T in young men under GnRH clamp — a different pharmacodynamic plateau from replacement gel. **Established** dose–response.
2. **Gårevik et al. 2014:** single IM TE **250 mg and 500 mg** each ↓ **ApoA1 and HDL**; **125 mg did not**. Minimal acute lipid signal in that model = **250 mg**. Continuous weekly exposure is expected to maintain or deepen the lipid phenotype. **Established** (single-dose design); chronic magnitude **Probable**.
3. **Bloodwork-driven AI only.** Split dose → measure sensitive E2 + symptoms → microdose AI if needed → **never raise testosterone because an AI is “covering” aromatization**.
4. **Phlebotomy lowers a lab number; it is not proven risk-nullification.** Prefer primary controls (dose, frequency, OSA, smoking, BP) over becoming a lifelong blood bag at fixed high dose.
5. **Fertility is not preserved by testosterone alone.** Exogenous T suppresses LH/FSH; plan hCG ± bank before multi-month suppression.
6. **TRAVERSE is not a cruise safety certificate.**

## 4. Per-side deep dives

### 4.1 Dyslipidemia (↓HDL, ↓ApoA1, atherogenic shift)

#### Mechanism (full)

1. **Phenotype detail** — Fasting lipids: HDL-C drops; ApoA1 falls; LDL/ApoB may rise modestly or stay flat; TG variable. Users often feel fine. Damage is lab-silent until years of exposure + other risks stack.
2. **Known molecular drivers** — Androgens suppress hepatic ApoA1/HDL production; hepatic lipase upregulation precedes HDL2 fall (classic AAS lipid work). **Gårevik et al. 2014** (n=25 healthy men, single IM TE with 6–8 wk washouts; labs Day 0/4/14): **250 mg and 500 mg** decreased **ApoA1 and HDL**; **125 mg did not**. Max ApoA1 disturbance in that design was at Day 14 after 500 mg. Lp(a) fell after 500 mg. **This band sits on the wrong side of that acute threshold.** Bhasin 2001 weekly TE: HDL falls with rising dose through 300–600 mg/wk — chronic bridge inside the band. **Established** direction.
3. **Downstream** — Lower HDL/ApoA1 → reduced reverse-cholesterol-transport capacity (simplified but directionally correct) → higher long-horizon atherosclerotic risk with concurrent BP↑, HCT↑, smoking, visceral fat.
4. **Hypothesized** — Particle dysfunction beyond concentration numbers; E2 extremes (high *or* crashed AI) further distort lipids (**Speculative** magnitude).
5. **Cascade** — Supraphysiologic T (≥250 mg TE-equivalent exposure signal) → hepatic ApoA1/HDL↓ (± HL↑) → adverse lipid phenotype → additive CV risk with P8/P9.

**Method caveat:** Gårevik is **single-dose**, not multi-week cruise. Do not use Day-14 % as chronic prediction. Prefer Bhasin graded weekly for chronic direction/magnitude rank-order; use Gårevik as **acute dose-threshold lipid direction**. **Probable** chronic HDL stress at 250–500 mg/wk IM.

Base: training + diet + no orals. Bergamot optional (**Speculative** for this phenotype). Treat ApoB/LDL to risk, not ego. **Duration control beats any supplement.**

### 4.2 Erythrocytosis (↑ hematocrit / hemoglobin)

#### Mechanism (full)

1. **Phenotype detail** — HCT climbs over weeks–months (erythropoiesis signal ~3 mo, peak ~9–12 mo under TRT time-course literature — **Probable** timing skeleton; PED IM peaks may compress). Ruddy face, headaches, diastolic BP contribution, elevated resting HR in some.
2. **Known drivers** — T ↑ EPO set-point + ↓ hepcidin → ↑ iron availability → marrow erythropoiesis (Bachman/Bhasin-line). **Route matters:** IM injectable shows **higher erythrocytosis rates than gel** (Pastuszak-type cohorts: injectables highest %; Ohlander reviews). This band is **IM and supra** → expect **more** HCT pressure than TRAVERSE-range gel. Older men more sensitive at matched dose (Coviello). **Established.**
3. **Downstream** — Higher viscosity → shear stress, BP, theoretical arterial/venous thrombotic risk at HCT ≳52–54% (thresholds vary). Observational links between elevated HCT on T and MACE/VTE are **association-grade** (**Probable** concern, not proof).
4. **Hypothesized** — Peak-dependent EPO pulses with weekly large boluses worse than split (**Speculative**–**Probable**); OSA nocturnal hypoxia compounds EPO (**Probable** synergy).
5. **Cascade** — High IM T → ↑RBC mass → ↑HCT → viscosity/BP → event risk if extreme or clot-prone.

**Guideline anchors (TRT context, applied conservatively at supra-TRT):** Many societies: intervene / reduce / withhold around **HCT >54%**. AUA-style: investigate ≥50%; reduce/hold ≥54%. Performance practice often acts earlier when trending hard at 250–500 mg/wk.

**Bond et al. 2024 methods point (must absorb):** Guideline endorsement of phlebotomy for T-induced erythrocytosis **outruns outcome RCTs**. PV targets do not auto-transfer to secondary erythrocytosis. Periodic phlebotomy depletes iron and may stabilize HIF pathways; “correcting” HCT while holding high T dose may leave EPO drive elevated relative to the new set-point. Stack language: **lowers a number ≠ proven stroke/MI prevention**.

#### Integrated approach

Order: confirm true elevation → fix OSA/smoking/hydration → split dose → cut dose → phlebotomy as **bridge**, not lifestyle. CBC every 6–8 weeks early, then q3 months if stable. Never push through symptomatic hyperviscosity + extreme HCT without a plan. Prefer **not** to live on monthly phlebotomy at fixed high dose indefinitely.

### 4.3 Blood pressure elevation & fluid retention

#### Mechanism (full)

1. **Phenotype detail** — Home BP creeps; rings tight; evening ankle edema; facial puffiness if E2 high. Some stay normotensive.
2. **Known drivers** — Na⁺/water retention (androgen ± E2), blood viscosity from HCT, weight gain, sleep apnea, training-related acute spikes misread as baseline. FDA class ABPM programs: **BP increase** is a class effect of testosterone products (**Established** direction).
3. **Downstream** — Sustained HTN → LV strain, arterial risk; interacts with lipids/HCT for net CV surface. TRAVERSE PE/AF/AKI signals keep residual CV-adjacent risk live even at *gel TRT* — **not** a free pass at cruise IM.
4. **Hypothesized** — Endothelial NO impairment at supra + adverse lipids (**Speculative**); mineralocorticoid-adjacent tone (**Speculative** molecular map).
5. **Cascade** — T band → volume + HCT + OSA ± E2 water → BP↑ → CV risk.

### 4.4 Aromatization — high E2, gynecomastia, estrogenic water/mood

#### Mechanism (full)

1. **Phenotype detail** — Itchy/sensitive nipples, puffy areola, retro-areolar disc, emotional lability, soft water weight, elevated sensitive E2. Onset often weeks; faster with large infrequent pins or high body fat.
2. **Known drivers** — Aromatase converts T→E2 in adipose, gonads, brain, bone, vessel. More substrate + more adipose → more E2. ER activation in breast → ductal/glandular proliferation if unopposed relative to local androgens. **Established.**
3. **Downstream** — Glandular gyno (early reversible → later fibrotic), water retention, further HPTA feedback (already suppressed), libido changes.
4. **Hypothesized** — Local breast aromatase > serum E2 in some (**Speculative**).
5. **Cascade** — High T → ↑E2 → breast ER + systemic water/mood → gyno/edema phenotype.

**AI evidence ceiling (methods):** Aromatase inhibition lowers circulating E2 — **Established**. Blind prophylactic AI on 250–500 mg/wk improves net health outcomes — **Speculative**. Symptom- and lab-triggered low-dose AI reduces gyno incidence vs no AI — **Probable** (practice + pathway), not PED RCT-proven. Anastrozole 1 mg daily as default on-cycle — **Speculative** (often excessive).

### TRAVERSE external validity (scorecard)

| Domain | TRAVERSE | 250–500 mg/wk IM performance | Transfer |
|--------|----------|------------------------------|----------|
| Population | Older, hypogonadal, high CV risk | Often younger, eugonadal baseline | Poor for absolute rates |
| Formulation | Daily gel, flat-ish PK | IM ester peaks/troughs | Poor for HCT, E2 peaks, BP dynamics |
| Exposure | Physiologic target ~350–750 ng/dL | Clearly supraphysiologic | **Fails** for dose-dependent sides |
| Protocol | Down-titrate / stop on HCT >54%; baseline HCT >50% often excluded | Dose often held; HCT managed with donation while continuing | **Fails** for PED HCT handling |
| Endpoint | MACE noninferiority (HR ~0.96) under those constraints | User cares about lipids, HCT, BP, gyno, fertility | Partial conceptual only |

**Established (methods):** TRAVERSE supports CV **noninferiority of replacement-range gel T** in a **specific high-risk hypogonadal population under safety rules**. Secondary signals (PE, AF, AKI numerical excesses in some reports) are **not** a free pass.

**Invalid inference:** “TRAVERSE shows testosterone is CV-safe, therefore 400 mg/wk test + AI + BP meds is evidence-based safe.”

**FDA 2025 class labeling context:** TRAVERSE results added to labels; historical boxed CV-outcome warning language removed for products as a class; **BP increase warnings strengthened** from ABPM programs. Still **not** a cruise green light.

## 10. Certainty-tagged bottom line

1. **Pathways P1–P20** map exogenous T at this band: AR, E2, DHT, HPTA, EPO/HCT, Na⁺/water, lipids/HL, BP, OSA, SHBG, prostate, fertility, CNS, PK peaks, hypothesized wake-drive.
2. **Gårevik 2014:** single-dose TE **250 and 500 mg** ↓ **ApoA1 and HDL**; **125 mg did not** — lipid protection is mandatory thinking from the bottom of this band. **Established** (single-dose model); chronic weekly stress **Probable**.
3. **TRAVERSE:** high internal validity for physiologic gel replacement under safety rules; **low external validity** for 250–500 mg/wk IM. Not a protection-stack efficacy certificate.
4. **Primary stack levers:** injection frequency → bloodwork → micro AI only if needed → BP agents (telmisartan ± nebivolol) → HCT architecture (dose/frequency first; phlebotomy as bridge) → hCG for testis/fertility → SERM for gyno flares → lifestyle lipid/BP/OSA always on.
5. **Order of operations is the stack’s spine:** split before AI; **never raise test because AI is on**; phlebotomy thresholds respected without chronic donation-as-lifestyle; fertility planned explicitly.
6. **Speculative counters** (bergamot-as-AAS-HDL fix, RU58841, naringin-for-HCT, routine aspirin, prophylactic AI) are pathway-matched options only — not substitutes for dose architecture, BP meds, or HCT primary controls.
7. **No Level-1 evidence validates a full ancillary stack at this dose band.** Certainty tops out at **Probable** for most individual counters and **Speculative** for the stack-as-system risk neutralization.
