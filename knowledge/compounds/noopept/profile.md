## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | **Noopept** (brand, Russian Ноопепт); **Omberacetam** (INN); GVS-111; DVD-111; SGS-111; *N*-phenylacetyl-L-prolylglycine ethyl ester |
| **Class** | Synthetic dipeptide nootropic / neuroprotective; designed at Zakusov Institute (Gudasheva–Ostrovskaya line) as a **dipeptide analog of piracetam** — not a classical 2-oxo-pyrrolidine racetam |
| **Primary use cases** | Cognitive support in mild vascular / post-traumatic cognitive impairment (Russian clinical indication); grey-market nootropic for focus, memory, verbal fluency, mild anxiolysis in healthy users |
| **Bottom line** | Noopept is a short-lived parent that elevates **cycloprolylglycine (cPG)**, an endogenous cyclic dipeptide linked to AMPA / TrkB / BDNF signaling in rodents. Best human clinical signal is an open-label Russian comparative trial (Neznamov & Teleshova 2009): **10 mg BID × 56 days** in mild organic cognitive disorders vs piracetam, with MMSE-type gains and generally mild AEs (sleep, irritability, BP rise in a minority). Healthy-user cognitive enhancement has **no high-quality Western RCTs** — forum effects are real for some responders, null for others, and often inverted-U. Practical community band is **10–30 mg/day**, frequently sublingual, often stacked with choline. Human PK remains largely **Unknown**. Preclinical and clinical literature is heavily concentrated in the inventing institute — treat mechanism richness as **developer science**, not multi-lab consensus. |
| **Biggest upsides** | Low milligram potency vs classical racetams (**Established** dose scale); multi-pathway neuroprotective preclinical map (**Probable** in animals); clinical signal in MCI of vascular/traumatic origin at 20 mg/day (**Probable** for that population, thin by Western standards); community reports of focus / verbal fluency without classic stimulant crash (**Speculative** magnitude) |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Sleep disturbance, irritability, BP rise in clinical minority (**Probable** at trial dose); forum headache ± “choline need,” insomnia, overstimulation, high-dose fog / anhedonia (**Speculative** mechanism; high anecdote consistency for headache / sleep); long-term safety and healthy-user efficacy **Unknown**; US unapproved / import-alert grey market; Hungary controlled (2020) |
| **Stack role** | Low-dose daytime cognitive enhancer or neuroprotective adjunct — not a caffeine replacement, not a primary stimulant, not proven disease-modifying therapy. Pairs culturally with **alpha-GPC / CDP-choline**; competes with piracetam (higher gram doses), phenylpiracetam (more stimulant), aniracetam (more anxiolytic / AMPAkine feel), semax (peptide neurotrophic), coffee + L-theanine (acute, evidence-richer for healthy users) |

**Certainty priors (run-level)**

- **Established:** Chemical identity; rodent parent short t½; cPG as major active metabolite; rodent NGF/BDNF hippocampal increases with chronic use; Russian registered-use existence; AE pattern in Neznamov cohort  
- **Probable:** Clinical cognitive benefit in vascular / post-trauma MCI at ~20 mg/day (thin); multi-factor antioxidant / anti-excitotoxic / HIF neuroprotection in animals; cPG → AMPA / TrkB neuroprotection  
- **Speculative:** Healthy-user nootropic effect size; sublingual BA advantage; choline-depletion headache mechanism; optimal cycle length; long-term neuroprotection in humans  
- **Unknown:** Formal human PK/PD; large modern RCTs in healthy adults; dependence / withdrawal formal data; dementia prevention

**Classification note (critical):** Noopept is **not** a classical racetam (no free 2-oxo-pyrrolidine core like piracetam / oxiracetam). It is a **dipeptide ester** designed by peptide-mimetic imitation of piracetam’s pharmacophore (phenylacetyl-Pro-Gly-OEt). Marketing and FDA language often still bucket it with “racetam-family APIs.” Mechanistically and chemically, treat it as **dipeptide prodrug → cPG**, not as “microdose piracetam.”

### 2.2 Physicochemical notes

- Small lipophilic ester relative to piracetam → lower clinical mg doses.  
- Oral tablets 5–10 mg (Russian pharmaceutical presentation); powder / capsule grey-market forms common.  
- Water solubility limited relative to highly polar piracetam; community dissolves powder in water, alcohol, or propylene glycol for sublingual use (**Speculative** BA impact).  
- Stability: standard dry-powder storage; multi-year open-bottle potency without assay is **Unknown** (product-dependent).

### 2.3 Pharmacokinetics

| Parameter | Data | Certainty |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Parent t½ (rodent)** | ~5–10 min commonly cited (ADDF summary of Boiko et al. ~6.5 min); broader literature range sometimes ~5–26 min | **Established** (rodent) |
| **Parent brain residence** | Detectable early; largely gone by ~1 h in rodent reports | **Established** (rodent) |

| **cPG disposition** | Longer effective presence than parent; hippocampal enrichment reported for cPG in some work | **Probable** (rodent) |
| **Human PK** | Essentially uncharacterized in modern published ADME packages | **Unknown** |
| **Food effect** | Not formally characterized | **Unknown** |
| **Sublingual** | Community preference for “stronger” feel / first-pass avoidance | **Speculative** human BA; high cultural consistency |

**PK practical implication:** Subjective onset (often 15–60 min community) and multi-hour cognitive window are **not** explained by parent plasma t½. They map better onto **cPG generation + neurotrophic / gene-expression cascades** (hours–days for BDNF/NGF changes in chronic rodent dosing) than onto classic stimulant PK. Acute “stimulant” framing is mechanistically incomplete.

**Pathway certainty spine (PK → effect):**

```
Noopept (oral/SL)
  → parent short-lived (Established rodent; Unknown human)
  → cPG enrichment (Established rodent brain metabolite)
  → AMPA PAM-like action (Probable, developer electrophys)
  → TrkB / BDNF cascade (Probable cellular/behavioral)
  → human cognition (Speculative for healthy; Probable-thin for Russian MCI label)
```

**Implication:** Preclinical mechanism volume, metabolite papers, neurotrophin work, and the flagship human trial sit inside one **developer ecosystem**. That does not falsify results, but it: (1) inflates publication volume and pathway storytelling; (2) reduces independent replication density; (3) couples institutional incentives to favorable framing. ADDF’s “single laboratory” warning is correct. Independent Western RCTs are **absent**. Do not treat RF registration as multi-site ICH-grade efficacy.

### 4.2 Flagship clinical study — Neznamov & Teleshova 2009

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Citation** | Neznamov GG, Teleshova ES. *Neurosci Behav Physiol.* 2009;39(3):311–321. PMID **19234797** (Russian original *Zh Nevrol Psikhiatr* 2008 PMID **18697252**) |
| **Relation** | **One study, two languages** — do not double-count |
| **Design** | Comparative clinical: Noopept vs piracetam in mild cognitive disorders of **vascular and traumatic** organic origin. Per ADDF: **open-label, active-controlled** — **not** placebo DB-RCT. Randomization / allocation concealment poorly documented in English secondary literature |
| **Journal** | *Neuroscience and Behavioral Physiology* — Springer **translation journal** of selected Russian papers; peer-reviewed but low impact (Q3/Q4 band); not CONSORT-primary culture |
| **Dose** | Noopept **10 mg BID (20 mg/day)**; piracetam **400 mg TID**; duration **56 days** |
| **Population** | Patients with mild cognitive disorders (organic brain disease vascular / traumatic) — **not healthy young adults** |
| **Sample** | Noopept AE denominators imply **n≈31** on Noopept; total study modest (~53–60 across arms in secondary citations) |
| **Primary directional findings** | Both agents improved cognition and mood parameters; Noopept often narrated as faster onset / better tolerability vs piracetam in Russian clinical writing |
| **MMSE-type signal** | ADDF summary: Noopept group MMSE **~26 → ~29** over study (illustrative scale of gain in impaired cohort — practice effects / expectancy unseparated without placebo) |
| **AEs (Noopept arm, n≈31 per ADDF)** | Sleep disturbance **5/31 (~16%)**; irritability **3/31 (~10%)**; increased blood pressure **7/31 (~23%)**; headache / chest pain rare (~3%) |
| **Funding / origin** | Zakusov clinical pharmacology network / domestic pharmaceutical development — **not independent** of inventing institution |
| **Comparator problem** | Piracetam has weak / contested evidence for dementia / cognitive impairment in Cochrane-style reviews. Active-controlled trial vs a weak standard can show both improve (regression to mean, practice, care, expectancy) without proving absolute drug–placebo delta |
| **Certainty for “helps mild organic cognitive impairment at 20 mg/day”** | **Probable** (best available human clinical; thin; not FDA-grade) |
| **Certainty for “superior to piracetam”** | **Speculative–Probable** depending on endpoint narrative; design limits firm superiority claims |

### 4.5 What clinical data do **not** show

- Do **not** show robust, replicated, double-blind cognitive gains in **healthy** adults.  
- Do **not** show FDA-standard efficacy for Alzheimer’s disease or other dementias.  
- Do **not** define human half-life, AUC, metabolite kinetics, or dose–exposure–response.  
- Do **not** establish long-term (>months–years) safety, dependence liability, or withdrawal syndromes.  
- Do **not** validate marketing multipliers (“50,000× piracetam”) as equi-efficacy claims.  
- Do **not** prove sublingual superiority over oral tablets.  
- Do **not** separate absolute drug effect from practice / expectancy (no placebo arm).

**Weighing sentences**

1. Forum consensus that **10–20 mg/day** is the productive band and that **pushing dose often worsens** outcomes is more trustworthy than marketing high-dose protocols — it aligns with rodent inverted-U signals and clinical 20 mg/day anchor (**Probable** as practical guidance heuristic).  
2. **Healthy-user cognitive benefit** is real for a subset of users in narrative data but **cannot be upgraded past Speculative** without RCTs; nulls are common and under-shared less than positives (selection bias).  
3. **Headache + choline** is a **high-consistency cultural protocol** with only **Speculative** mechanistic support; it still earns a counter-table seat because it is the dominant community side-management tool and is low-cost to trial carefully.  
4. Sleep and irritability forum reports **concord well** with Neznamov AEs → treat as **Probable** class effects, not pure nocebo.  
5. Literature silence on healthy enhancement ≠ proof of null effect; it means **uncertainty**, not disproof. Conversely, dense forums ≠ clinical efficacy.  
6. Product quality variance (research chem vs Russian pharma tablets) confounds all anecdotal dose–response (**Unknown** purity / dose accuracy grey market).  
7. Do **not** use forums for effect-size estimation, long-term safety rates, or mechanism confirmation.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Cognitive enhancement / focus / mental clarity

- **Phenotype:** Easier sustained attention, less mental friction, subjective clarity (healthy users); improved cognitive battery scores (clinical MCI).  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1–P5, P13, P22–P23.  
- **Mechanism chain:** Parent → cPG (P1) → AMPA modulation + TrkB / BDNF tone (P2–P4) + functional cholinergic support (P5) + balanced inhibitory components (P13) → network signal-to-noise improvement; chronic BDNF / NGF shifts (P23) may lag days–weeks. Excess AMPA / arousal → inverted-U fog (P22).  
- **Clinical support:** **Probable** in mild organic cognitive disorders at 10 mg BID × 56 days; **Unknown / Speculative** in healthy adults.  
- **Forum support:** Moderate–high among responders; large null subgroup.  
- **Practical:** Start **10 mg morning** (± second 10 mg early afternoon); judge over **1–2 weeks**, not one dose. Certainty: **Speculative** (healthy) / **Probable** (clinical population).

### 6.2 Memory / learning / consolidation

- **Phenotype:** Better encoding / recall; anti-amnesic in animal models; MMSE-type gains clinically.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P2–P5, P10, P15, P23.  
- **Mechanism chain:** AMPA plasticity (P2) + BDNF / NGF (P3–P4) + cholinergic facilitation (P5) + optional anti-Aβ / neurite cellular support (P10) → hippocampal learning machinery; chronic dosing favors neurotrophin map.  
- **Clinical support:** **Probable** directional improvement in organic mild impairment; not proven as student “exam drug.”  
- **Forum support:** Moderate; often subtle and cumulative.  
- **Practical:** Prefer steady **10–20 mg/day** for multi-week blocks over mega-doses. Certainty: **Probable** (impaired) / **Speculative** (healthy students).

### 6.3 Verbal fluency / word-finding

- **Phenotype:** Faster lexical access, smoother speech — common forum signature.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P2, P5, P13 (network efficiency hypothesized).  
- **Mechanism chain:** **Hypothesized** cortical network efficiency via glutamatergic / cholinergic facilitation; no direct speech-trial literature.  
- **Clinical support:** Not specifically instrumented.  
- **Forum support:** Moderate consistency.  
- **Practical:** Often reported at **10–15 mg SL**. Certainty: **Speculative**.

### 6.4 Mild anxiolysis / social ease / “calm focus”

- **Phenotype:** Reduced social friction without heavy sedation (ideal case).  
- **Pathway IDs:** P13, P14, P3.  
- **Mechanism chain:** cPG anxiolytic-like rodent effects (P14) + hippocampal inhibitory component potentiation (P13) + BDNF mood tone (P3). Excess → irritability or flatness (not pure anxiolysis).  
- **Clinical support:** Mood improvement co-reported in Neznamov context (**Probable** mood co-benefit in patients); not a GAD trial.  
- **Forum support:** Moderate.  
- **Practical:** Keep dose low; do not combine liberally with other anxiolytics without care. Certainty: **Speculative–Probable**.

### 6.5 Neuroprotection / resilience under metabolic or vascular stress

- **Phenotype:** Desired by aging / nootropic users; indicated conceptually by Russian vascular cognitive use.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P6–P12, P3–P4, P16.  
- **Mechanism chain:** Anti-excitotoxic + antioxidant + HIF-1 + rheology + neurotrophins → multi-hit resilience in animals / cells.  
- **Clinical support:** Use in vascular / post-trauma cognitive impairment is the closest human context (**Probable** symptomatic cognitive help; **Unknown** disease modification).  
- **Forum support:** Often assumed; rarely measurable by users.  
- **Practical:** Do not treat as proven AD prevention. Certainty: **Probable** preclinical; **Unknown** human hard outcomes.

### 6.6 Sensory / perceptual sharpening (minor)

- **Phenotype:** Occasional reports of visual clarity / color.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P2 (hypothesized cortical gain).  
- **Clinical support:** None.  
- **Forum support:** Low–moderate.  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative**.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 Headache

1. **Phenotype:** Tension-type or “racetam headache”; frontal / pressure; sometimes with neck tension; onset hours after dose; more common forum than clinical tables (different populations / doses / products).  
2. **Mechanism:**  
   - **Hypothesized (Speculative):** Increased ACh turnover / precursor demand (P5 / P19) → relative choline deficit → headache (racetam-community model). **Critical distinction:** pramiracetam has Established preclinical HACU↑; Noopept’s cholinergic story is weaker and mostly anecdotal — do not assume identical choline dependency.  
   - **Hypothesized:** Vascular tone / mild BP or perfusion shifts (P12 / P21).  
   - **Hypothesized:** Glutamatergic overdrive / inverted-U network stress (P2 / P22) → cephalic discomfort.  
   - **Known confounders:** Dehydration, caffeine co-use, adulterated products, concurrent stimulants (P25).  
3. **Pathway → counter table**

|--------------|---------|----------------------|----------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| ACh precursor demand (P19) | **Alpha-GPC** 150–300 mg | Supplies choline for ACh synthesis | No formal trial with Noopept | High use / mixed success | Start low; excess choline can cause depression / fishy odor in some | **Speculative** mech; **Probable** practical try in subset |

### 10.1 Monitoring (practical)

Labs are **not** standardized for Noopept monitoring (**Unknown** biomarkers of effect). Human BDNF / CSF measures after oral Noopept: **Unknown**.

### 10.2 Caution / relative contraindications

- Uncontrolled **hypertension** or unstable cardiovascular disease (**Probable** caution given BP AEs).  
- Significant **anxiety disorders** or bipolar spectrum without clinical oversight (**Speculative** risk of irritability / activation).  
- **Pregnancy / lactation** — no adequate human data (**Unknown** → avoid).  
- **Pediatric** use outside medical context — not in this profile’s healthy-adult scope.  
- Concurrent complex unapproved nootropic polypharmacy.  
- Jurisdictions where controlled (e.g., **Hungary**) — legal non-use.  
- Expectation of treating progressive dementia without medical care — wrong tool framing.

### 10.3 Stop criteria

Stop and do not rechallenge at the same dose if:

- Sustained **insomnia** despite morning-only dosing  
- **BP** clearly elevated vs baseline  
- Escalating **irritability**, agitation, or mood collapse  
- Persistent **headache** unresponsive to dose cut ± choline  
- **Chest pain**, allergic symptoms, neurologic focal signs  
- Cognitive performance **below baseline** (fog / anhedonia) after dose reduction attempt  

Seek medical care for severe or systemic symptoms.

## 12. Open questions

Data that would most change this map:

1. **Human PK/PD study:** parent and cPG plasma / CSF time courses after oral vs sublingual 10–20 mg — resolves BA debates and dosing interval science (**Unknown** → could become **Established**).  
2. **Double-blind RCT in healthy adults** with pre-registered cognitive endpoints at 10 mg BID × 4–8 weeks — upgrades or kills healthy-user efficacy claims.  
3. **Replication of Neznamov** in multi-site modern design (vascular MCI) with active / placebo controls.  
4. **Choline interaction RCT:** Noopept ± alpha-GPC on headache incidence and cognition — tests P19.  
5. **BP mechanistic study** in hypertensives vs normotensives at clinical dose.  
6. **Independent multi-lab preclinical replication** outside primary Russian groups (addresses ADDF single-lab concern).  
7. **Long-term safety ≥6–12 months** with mood, sleep, BP, and cognitive durability.  
8. **Dependence / withdrawal structured assessment** after continuous use.  
9. **Genotype stratification** (e.g., APOE ε4 claims in Russian MCI lines) with modern methods.  
10. **Product quality survey** of grey-market omberacetam dose accuracy (extends Cohen-type work).  
11. Dose-equivalence and safety of **direct cPG** vs prodrug Noopept.
