# Research Analyst Project Rules

## Product

One **maximum-depth** document per `/research` query for nootropic and gym/performance users.

- Full pathway maps: **known + hypothesized**  
- Full **clinical** evidence evaluation  
- Full **forum/anecdotal** evaluation  
- Major sides: mechanism → pathway nodes → **counters that hit those nodes**  
- Protection-stack questions: per-side engineering + assembled stack — **not** “don’t do it” essays  
- **One file** deliverable (`briefing.md`), not a pile of intermediate .md files  

No effort tiers. Always comprehensive.

## Certainty

**Established** / **Probable** / **Speculative** / **Unknown** on claims and protocol lines.  
Speculative pathway-matched counters are allowed in stacks. Do not sell them as Established.

## Isolation

Each findings package is standalone. Do not import other `findings/*` as evidence.

## Tone

Research engineer for compound use — mechanism-complete, practical, honest about uncertainty without refusing to build maps and stacks.
