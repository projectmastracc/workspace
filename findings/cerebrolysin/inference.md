## 2. Evidence Mapping with Certainty Labels

### 2.1 Stroke — early neurological recovery (C1)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | Bornstein et al. 2018 meta-analysis (9 DB-PC RCTs; N=1879): Mann–Whitney effect size for NIHSS day 30 (or 21) **MW 0.60**, *P*<0.0001; NNT for clinically relevant early NIHSS change **7.7** (95% CI 5.2–15.0); doses 30–50 mL/day × 10–21 days, start ≤72 h. Includes IPD for CASTA, CARS-1/2, CERE-LYSE, etc. |
| **Observed** | Patel et al. 2025 SR/MA (14 RCTs; N=2884): ΔNIHSS mean difference favoring Cerebrolysin **MD ≈ +1.39** (95% CI 0.53–2.25); primary NIHSS change rated **moderate certainty** (RoB2 + GRADE). Declared no industry funding. |
| **Inferred** | Small–medium early neurological superiority vs placebo as add-on to standard care is real in pooled analyses. |
| **Certainty** | **Probable** (bordering Established for *early NIHSS surrogate*; not Established for hard day-90 disability in unselected AIS — see C2) |
| **Downgrades** | Industry ties on many component trials and Bornstein package; nonparametric MW less familiar to US readers; heterogeneity of rehab/standard care; surrogate (NIHSS) ≠ disability-free survival |
| **Funding/COI** | Bornstein/CARS/CAPTAIN-adjacent authors often EVER Neuro Pharma–linked; Patel 2025 independent declaration is a partial counterweight |

### 2.2 Stroke — day-90 functional outcome overall (C2) and severity subgroup (C3)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | **CASTA** (Heiss 2012; N≈1067 Asia AIS): primary combined global directional test (mRS + BI + NIHSS) **not met** in overall population. Post hoc: NIHSS >12 subgroup trend on NIHSS/mRS (OR ≈1.27, CI lower bounds near 1); day-90 mortality 20.2% placebo vs 10.5% Cerebrolysin in NIHSS >12 (HR ≈1.97 for placebo excess). |
| **Observed** | Bornstein 2018 secondary: full-scale ordinal mRS day 90 in **moderate–severe** subset **MW 0.61** (95% CI 0.52–0.69), *P*=0.0118, **N=314 only**. |
| **Observed** | Patel 2025: mRS 0–2 **RR ≈1.31, NS**; SAE/mortality NS; hemorrhagic transformation RR ≈0.55 (favoring Cerebrolysin in pooled estimate). |
| **Inferred** | Overall unselected AIS day-90 independence is **not reliably demonstrated**. Signal concentrates where baseline deficit is greater — biologically plausible (more room for recovery) but partly post hoc. |
| **Certainty** | C2 (overall day-90 independence): **Unknown** (null primary in largest trial; NS mRS 0–2 in Patel; residual hypotheses only Speculative) |
| **Certainty** | C3 (moderate–severe day-90 benefit): **Speculative** — FINAL (quality gate Option A). Smaller N; subgroup/post hoc; industry MA; no modern severe-only confirmatory RCT. Early NIHSS remains separately **Probable** (C1). |

### 2.3 Stroke — add-on to reperfusion (C4)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | CEREHETIS (Khasanova/Kalinin 2023): add-on to r-tPA — reduced sICH/early deficit signals; **day-90 functional outcome not significantly improved** on secondary reports. |
| **Observed** | Staszewski 2025: adjunct after mechanical thrombectomy (small-core cohort) — improved 90-day functional outcomes reported in that cohort (Transl Stroke Res; modest size, emerging literature). |
| **Certainty** | Early safety/reperfusion-compatibility: **Probable**. Day-90 functional superiority as reperfusion adjunct: **Speculative** (promising, not definitive). |

### 2.4 TBI (C5)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | CAPTAIN II (Muresanu 2020): moderate–severe TBI RCT; multidimensional ensemble superiority; protocol-aligned cycles (e.g., 50 mL early then lower-dose cycles per series). Manufacturer-associated investigators. |
| **Observed** | Vester 2021 prospective MA of CAPTAIN series: **N=185**; primary multidimensional ensemble Day 30 **MW_combined 0.60** (95% CI 0.52–0.66), Day 90 **MW 0.60** (0.52–0.68); SMD ≈0.31–0.34; safety ≈ placebo. I² reported 0% across methods. |
| **Observed** | Jarosz 2023 SR (Brain Sci): doses 10–50 mL; heterogeneous designs; overall supportive narrative on GCS/GOS with quality limits. |
| **Certainty** | Multidimensional recovery benefit in **moderate–severe TBI** with CAPTAIN-style dosing: **Probable** |
| **Downgrades** | Modest total N; industry-linked authorship/statistics (Vester); generalizability outside specialized centers; multidimensional primary may be harder to falsify than a single hard endpoint |

### 2.5 Alzheimer’s disease (C6)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | Gauthier et al. 2015 MA mild–moderate AD RCTs: cognition 4 weeks **SMD −0.40** (−0.66 to −0.13); 6 months SMD −0.37 NS; global clinical change OR **3.32** (4 wk) and **4.98** (6 mo); “global benefit” MW **0.57** at both times; safety ≈ placebo. Authors conclude favorable benefit–risk and that clinicians “should consider” the agent. |
| **Observed** | Wei 2007 earlier MA supportive on CGI. Gavrilova 2021 narrative: multi-target NTF framing; possible enhancement/prolongation of cholinergic drugs. |
| **Observed** | Independent-ish AlzDiscovery Cognitive Vitality: generally good safety; AD metas positive **with quality caveats**; dementia **prevention unknown**. |
| **Certainty** | Short–medium-term CGI/cognition benefit in mild–moderate AD under IV cyclic regimens: **Probable** |
| **Downgrades** | Older trials; IV burden limits blinding integrity in some settings; not disease-modifying proof by modern biomarker standards; not US SOC; effect sizes modest |

| **Certainty** | **Unknown** — no robust primary prevention RCTs. Do not recommend for “anti-aging” or primary prevention. |

### 2.8 Safety (C9)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | Strilciuc et al. 2021 safety MA (12 RCTs; N≈2202 AIS): SAE/mortality **NS vs placebo**; highest dose 50 mL showed **trend** SAE reduction (RR ≈0.6 in high-dose framing); possible advantage in moderate–severe stroke. |
| **Observed** | Patel 2025: SAE/mortality NS; HT signal favorable. Bornstein 2018: safety comparable to placebo. |
| **Conflict note** | Older Cochrane AIS synthesis (Ziganshina line) was more cautious / suggested possible non-fatal SAE excess in some analyses — **material disagreement** with Strilciuc/Patel on SAE direction. Net modern stroke safety MAs lean **neutral-to-slightly favorable**. |
| **Label / monograph** | Contraindications commonly listed: hypersensitivity, epilepsy/seizure predisposition (relative–absolute per label jurisdiction), severe renal impairment. Porcine origin: allergy / religious / cultural considerations. Infusion reactions (dizziness, headache, flushing, nausea) usually mild. |
| **Certainty** | SAE/mortality not increased vs placebo in AIS MAs: **Probable–Established**. Absolute long-term multi-cycle dementia safety less densely mapped but trial AEs generally similar to placebo/donepezil: **Probable**. |

### 2.9 Mechanism (C10)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Observed** | Mixture of low-MW peptides + free amino acids from porcine brain proteolysis. Immunoassay/fragment work (Chen 2007 etc.) detects NTF-like epitopes; preclinical: PI3K/Akt, Shh pathway, anti-excitotoxic, anti-apoptotic, anti-inflammatory, BBB integrity, neurogenesis/plasticity (Rejdak & Neglur 2023; manufacturer monograph synthesis). |
| **Human PD** | Alvarez et al.: serum BDNF increase; donepezil combination augments/prolongs BDNF (2016). TNF-α / IGF-I shifts reported. |
| **Certainty** | Multimodal NTF-mimetic pharmacology as working model: **Probable** (mechanistic + limited human biomarker). **Causal chain from specific peptide → clinical mRS**: **Speculative**. Not a single-entity recombinant NTF drug. |

### 2.10 Dosing (C11)

| Indication (label / trial-aligned) | Daily dose | Timing | Duration |
|------------------------------------|------------|--------|----------|
| Acute ischemic stroke | **20–50 mL** IV (trials often **30–50 mL**) | As soon as possible (≤72 h in meta eligibility) | **10–21 days** |
| TBI | **20–50 mL** (CAPTAIN-style **50 mL** early cycle) | ASAP post-injury | **7–30 days** initial; CAPTAIN used multi-cycle schedules (e.g., 50 mL d1–10 then later 20–30 mL cycles) |
| AD / VaD / cognitive impairment | **10–30 mL** IV typical; some dose-finding up to 30–60 mL in special contexts | Cyclic (e.g., 5 days/week × 4 weeks, repeated; or 4-week cycles with drug-free intervals per trials) | Cycles over months; effects sometimes reported to outlast infusion window |

**Administration (monograph / handbook):**  
- ≤5–10 mL: slow IV injection or IM (label-dependent; IM typically low volume).  
- **10–50 mL: IV infusion only** — dilute to ≥100 mL (saline, Ringer, 5% glucose); infuse **~15–60 min** (often 30–45 min preferred).  
- Do not invent oral or research-chem SQ protocols from IV pharmaceutical data.

**Certainty of trial-aligned IV dosing as the only evidence-based regimen:** **Established** for “what trials used”; efficacy certainty remains indication-specific as above.

## 3. Steelmanned Opposition

### Case against routine use (strongest version)

1. **Failed primary in the largest AIS RCT (CASTA):** A large, double-blind, Asia multicentre trial did not meet its pre-specified global primary. Meta-analytic salvage of early NIHSS and severity subgroups does not erase a null primary in unselected AIS — a pattern classic for “neuroprotectants” that never became US SOC.  
2. **Industry gravity well:** CAPTAIN, CARS, Bornstein, Vester, and much of the positive narrative share manufacturer funding or closely linked statistical groups. Independent Patel 2025 still finds early NIHSS benefit but **not** significant mRS 0–2 — consistent with “real small early effect, soft long-term disability claim.”  
3. **Cochrane-class caution:** VaD Cochrane = insufficient for routine recommendation. AIS Cochrane lineage more pessimistic on clinical benefit / flagged SAE concerns in older analyses. Independent HTA-style summaries often conclude “not ready as standard add-on.”  
4. **Surrogate and multidimensional endpoints:** Early NIHSS and Wei–Lachin multidimensional ensembles can detect modest shifts without proving patient-important day-90 independence or reduced institutionalization.  
5. **Operational burden & opportunity cost:** Daily IV infusion courses compete with rehab time, nursing resources, and proven interventions (thrombectomy systems of care, BP/glucose/swallow protocols, secondary prevention, AChEIs/memantine where indicated).  
6. **Biological murk:** Porcine peptide soup is not a defined active pharmaceutical ingredient with clean PK/PD; batch composition and which peptides drive efficacy remain incompletely specified — a legitimate regulatory and scientific objection.  
7. **US absence of approval** is not mere bureaucracy; it reflects insufficient package for FDA standards of substantial evidence for a defined indication.

### Case for selective use (strongest version)

1. **Replicated early neuro recovery signal** across multiple RCTs and two generations of meta-analysis (Bornstein; Patel independent) with NNT ~8 for early NIHSS-relevant change.  
2. **Safety MAs large and largely neutral** — if benefit is modest, harm does not appear to cancel it in modern stroke pools; high-dose even trends toward fewer SAEs.  
3. **Severity interaction** is clinically sensible: mild stroke recovers well anyway; moderate–severe is where neurorecovery drugs could matter; CASTA mortality split and Bornstein mRS moderate–severe analysis point the same direction.  
4. **TBI CAPTAIN package** used modern multidimensional methods and showed consistent small–medium effects with clean safety — an area with few pharmacologic options beyond supportive care.  
5. **AD meta-analysis** (Gauthier) shows CGI/cognition benefit with acceptable safety; combo with donepezil has mechanistic and clinical rationale for refractory or moderate disease where IV logistics are acceptable.  
6. **Jurisdictions that registered the product** have decades of pharmacovigilance without a catastrophic safety signal emerging as a class recall driver.

**Adjudication:** Opposition wins for **unselected mild AIS as routine US/guideline first-line** and for **DIY/enhancement use**. Selective-use case wins at **Probable** for **early neurorecovery (NIHSS) after AIS**, **moderate–severe TBI**, and **short-term AD symptomatic management under specialist care where legal** — always as **add-on**. Moderate–severe AIS **day-90** enrichment remains **Speculative** only (not Probable day-90 protocol justification).

## 5. What the Research Truly Says (synthesis)

**Substance identity.** Cerebrolysin is a **standardized porcine brain hydrolysate** of low-molecular-weight peptides and amino acids, marketed as a multimodal neurotrophic / neurorecovery agent — **not** recombinant BDNF, **not** an oral nootropic, **not** FDA-approved in the US.

**Stroke.** The most defensible claim is **early improvement in neurological deficit (NIHSS-type)** when 30–50 mL/day IV is started within ~72 h for 10–21 days as add-on to standard care (**Probable**). The largest single RCT (CASTA) failed its overall primary; day-90 functional independence in unselected AIS remains **unproven** (**Unknown**). Moderate–severe day-90 enrichment is **Speculative** only (Bornstein subset N=314 / CASTA post-hoc) — hypothesis-generating, not Probable day-90 proof. Safety for SAE/death is **comparable to placebo** in modern MAs (**Probable**). Compatibility with thrombolysis/thrombectomy is **plausible and early data encouraging** but day-90 functional proof as reperfusion adjunct is **Speculative**.

**TBI.** CAPTAIN II + prospective CAPTAIN meta-analysis support a **small-to-medium multidimensional recovery benefit** after moderate–severe TBI with multi-cycle high-dose-then-maintenance IV regimens (**Probable**), with safety similar to placebo. Independent replication remains the main residual doubt.

**AD.** Meta-analyses support **short-to-medium-term gains on global clinical impression and cognition** in mild–moderate AD with cyclic IV therapy and a favorable benefit–risk in-trial (**Probable**). This is **symptomatic / recovery-style** evidence, not anti-amyloid disease modification. Not US standard of care.

**VaD.** Small positive signals; **Cochrane: insufficient for routine recommendation** — routine use **Speculative**; small short-term benefit may be **Probable** but not Established for routine care.

**Prevention / wellness / DIY enhancement.** **Unknown.** No recommendation.

**MoA.** Best scientific model is **multimodal NTF-mimetic and path cascade modulation** (**Probable** as pharmacology narrative; **Speculative** as precise clinical mechanism).

**Comparators.** Donepezil remains the simpler oral standard for AD cognition; Cerebrolysin is an **IV multi-target alternative or adjunct**, not a proven superior replacement. Citicoline and other “neurotrophics” comparisons are **mostly indirect**.

## 6. Actionable Recommendations (DR)

### 6.1 Recommend **for** (when product is legally available under clinical supervision)

| Scenario | Recommendation | Certainty | Protocol (trial-aligned) | Harm reduction |
|----------|----------------|-----------|--------------------------|----------------|
| **AIS (shared decision; registered markets)** | Offer **as add-on** for **early NIHSS recovery** to full standard stroke care — **not** instead of them. Day-90 unselected = **Unknown**; moderate–severe day-90 enrichment = **Speculative** (do not claim Probable day-90) | **Probable** for early NIHSS only | **30–50 mL IV once daily × 10–21 days**, start ASAP ≤72 h; dilute ≥100 mL; infuse ~30–45 min | Monitor infusion reactions; avoid if epilepsy/severe renal impairment/hypersensitivity per label; continue BP/glucose/DVT/swallow protocols |
| **Moderate–severe TBI** (GCS band akin to CAPTAIN ~6–12) | Offer **as add-on** to neurosurgical/ICU/rehab standard | **Probable** | CAPTAIN-style: e.g. **50 mL/day × ~10 days** early, then scheduled lower-dose cycles (e.g. 20–30 mL) per protocol used in series; total plan multi-week | Same; ICU polypharmacy vigilance; seizure history |
| **Mild–moderate AD** where oral SOC inadequate, declined, or specialist elects multi-modal IV course (registered markets) | Reasonable **specialist option** as cyclic IV; prefer **with** (not replacing without reason) standard dementia care | **Probable** short-term CGI/cognition | **10–30 mL IV** in cycles (e.g. 4 weeks on, intervals off — match local label / Alvarez–Gauthier-era regimens); combination with **donepezil 5–10 mg** is **Probable** for additive BDNF/clinical signal | Caregiver logistics for infusion; standard AD safety (falls, GI on AChEI); porcine product counseling |

| Scenario | Stance | Certainty basis |
|----------|--------|-----------------|

### 6.3 Practical guidance summary (if using pharmaceutical product)

1. **Route:** IV infusion for therapeutic doses (10–50 mL). Low-volume IM only if label allows and dose ≤ label IM max — **not** SQ DIY.  
2. **Acute neurorecovery window matters:** stroke/TBI — start early. Dementia — cyclic, not single infusion.  
3. **Stacking:** With standard stroke/TBI care: yes. With donepezil in AD: **Probable** supportive combo data. With citicoline/“racetams”/research peptides: **Unknown** — do not claim synergy.  
4. **Monitoring:** Clinical neuro status (NIHSS/GCS/mRS or AD scales as appropriate); AE log (headache, dizziness, flushing, agitation, seizure); renal function if risk; stop for hypersensitivity or new seizures. No specific “PCT” — not a hormone.  
5. **Contraindications (typical label):** hypersensitivity to product; epilepsy (often listed); severe renal failure. Porcine origin disclosure.  
6. **Access ethics:** In non-registered countries, do not advise black-market importation; that is outside evidence-based pharmaceutical use.

### 6.4 Certainty → action rule applied

- **Established:** IV dosing ranges used in trials/label; not FDA-approved in US; safety ≈ placebo for SAE/death in AIS MAs (Probable–Established).  
- **Probable:** Early AIS neurorecovery (NIHSS); TBI multidimensional recovery (with caveats); AD short-term CGI/cognition; trial-aligned IV protocols above.  
- **Speculative:** Moderate–severe AIS day-90 mRS enrichment (C3); routine VaD use; definitive reperfusion-adjunct day-90 benefit; disease modification.  
- **Unknown → no recommendation:** Unselected AIS day-90 independence as a proven claim; prevention; DIY SQ; superiority vs citicoline; wellness stacking.

## 10. Bottom Line (one paragraph)

Cerebrolysin is a **pharmaceutical IV multimodal NTF-mimetic** with **Probable** benefits for **early neurological recovery after AIS**, **multidimensional recovery after moderate–severe TBI**, and **short-term global/cognitive improvement in mild–moderate AD**, and a **Probable** safety profile for SAE/mortality near placebo in stroke meta-analyses.
