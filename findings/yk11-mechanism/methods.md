# Methodological Appraisal — YK11 Mechanism, AR Selectivity, Myostatin/Follistatin Claims

**Role:** Methodologist  
**Focus:** Whether cited studies actually support what they claim (design quality, bias, statistics, external validity, applied applicability)  
**Scope:** Molecular mechanism at AR; gene-/tissue-selectivity (SARM claims); myostatin/follistatin relationship  
**Primary sources appraised:** Kanno et al. 2011 (PMID 21372378; DOI 10.1248/bpb.34.318); Kanno et al. 2013 (PMID 23995658; DOI 10.1248/bpb.b13-00231); Yatsu et al. 2018 (PMID 29491216; DOI 10.1248/bpb.b17-00748); Lee et al. 2021 (PMID 33588136; DOI 10.1016/j.bbrc.2021.01.030); Park et al. 2022 (DOI 10.5763/kjsm.2022.40.2.67); Dahleh et al. 2024 (Chem Biol Interact abstract, as listed in intake).  
**Evidence base grade (overall for human performance claims):** **Very Low** (in-vitro + limited rodent; zero controlled human efficacy/safety trials).

---

## 1. Question framing (PICO / equivalent)

| Element | Operational definition for this appraisal |
|--------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Population** | Intended applied context: healthy adult humans seeking anabolic/performance effects (body composition, muscle). Literature populations actually studied: HEK293/293AR (human embryonic kidney, AR-overexpressing), MDA-MB-453 (human breast cancer, endogenous AR), C2C12 (mouse myoblasts), MC3T3-E1 (mouse osteoblasts), septic/exercise rodents (Lee; Dahleh). **No human clinical population.** |
| **Intervention / Exposure** | YK11 (steroidal compound; marketed as SARM / “myostatin inhibitor”), typically 100–500 nM (in vitro) or unknown mg/kg (in vivo abstracts). Comparison ligands: DHT (full AR agonist), hydroxyflutamide / bicalutamide (AR antagonists). |
| **Comparison** | Vehicle/solvent; DHT; DHT±YK11; AR antagonist co-treatment; AR siRNA; anti-follistatin neutralizing antibody. |
| **Outcomes** | (1) AR partial agonism / ARE-luciferase; (2) N/C interaction (mammalian two-hybrid); (3) nuclear translocation; (4) gene-selective endogenous mRNA (FKBP51, FGF18, SARG, HSD11B2); (5) myogenic markers (MyHC, MyoD, Myf5, myogenin); (6) *Fst* mRNA and functional necessity via Ab neutralization; (7) osteoblast proliferation/mineralization/OPG/OC/p-Akt; (8) sepsis mortality/cytokines/myostatin protein (Lee abstract); (9) organ safety under exercise (Dahleh abstract). |

**Claim triad under adjudication**

1. **MoA:** YK11 is a partial AR agonist that activates AR without N/C interaction → gene-selective transcription.  
2. **Selectivity:** YK11 is a SARM with tissue-selective anabolic (muscle/bone) vs androgenic effects.  
3. **Myostatin:** YK11 is a “myostatin inhibitor” via follistatin upregulation (indirect pathway antagonism), not a direct MSTN ligand blocker.

## 2. Design appraisal (per key study)

### 2.1 Kanno et al. 2011 — *Biol Pharm Bull* 34:318–323  
PMID: 21372378 | DOI: 10.1248/bpb.34.318

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Design type** | In vitro mechanistic; reporter assays, mammalian two-hybrid, confocal imaging, qRT-PCR. **Not** a clinical trial; no animals; no tissue-selectivity bioassay (no Hershberger). |
| **Cells** | HEK293 stably overexpressing human AR (293AR); parental 293; MDA-MB-453 (endogenous AR); 293 for YFP-AR translocation and N/C two-hybrid. |
| **Blinding** | Not reported (not applicable to standard cell assays; still relevant for subjective image interpretation of confocal). |
| **Randomization** | Not reported. |
| **Sample size** | Luciferase and RT-PCR: *n* = 3–4 technical/biological replicates (figure legends: mean ± SD, *n* = 3 or 4). No a priori power calculation. |
| **Dose / duration** | DHT 0.01–0.5 µM; YK11 0.1–10 µM (reporter); 0.1 µM DHT / 0.5–10 µM YK11 for gene expression 24 h; confocal 6 h. |
| **Key methods** | ARE-luciferase (PSA ARE tandem); GAL4-AR LBD / VP16-AR NTD two-hybrid for N/C; YFP-AR nuclear localization; endogenous gene RT-PCR (FKBP51, FGF18, SARG, HSD11B2). |
| **Statistics** | Mean ± SD; significance markers present in figures; formal test not detailed beyond standard replicate reporting. |
| **Funding / COI** | Japan Society for the Promotion of Science Grant-in-Aid; Open Research Center (private universities). No industry SARM sponsor. Single laboratory (Toho University group). |
| **GRADE-style quality for MoA claim** | **Low** for molecular AR pharmacology (consistent internal assays; no independent lab replication of full package at time of publication); **Very Low** for clinical SARM claim. |

**What the design can support:** Relative efficacy of YK11 vs DHT on ARE-luciferase (partial agonism ~10–20% of DHT max); blockade of DHT-driven reporter at high YK11; absence of N/C interaction and antagonism of DHT-mediated N/C; nuclear translocation; differential endogenous gene induction in MDA-MB-453.  

**What it cannot support:** Tissue-selective anabolism (prostate vs levator ani vs skeletal muscle); human PK/PD; safety; “SARM” as regulatory/clinical category beyond gene-selectivity in one cancer cell line.

### 2.2 Kanno et al. 2013 — *Biol Pharm Bull* 36:1460–1465  
PMID: 23995658 | DOI: 10.1248/bpb.b13-00231

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Design type** | In vitro mechanistic differentiation study in C2C12 myoblasts. |
| **Cells** | Mouse C2C12; differentiation medium (2% horse serum). |
| **Blinding** | Not reported. |
| **Randomization** | Not reported. |
| **Sample size** | qRT-PCR *n* = 3; immunoblots qualitative/semi-quantitative; no power analysis. |
| **Dose / duration** | YK11 or DHT 500 nM primary; dose-response 1–500 nM; days 2–7 depending on endpoint; FLU 10 µM; anti-Fst Ab for neutralization; AR siRNA. |
| **Key methods** | Immunoblot MyHC, AR; qRT-PCR Myf5, MyoD, myogenin, *Fst*, AR; FLU co-treatment; AR knockdown; anti-follistatin neutralizing antibody on *Myf5*. |
| **Statistics** | Student’s *t*-test; *p* < 0.05; mean ± SD, *n* = 3. **No multiplicity correction** across multiple genes/time points. |
| **Funding** | MEXT Grant-in-Aid for Young Scientists (B); Open Research Center. Same lab as 2011. |
| **GRADE** | **Low / Very Low** for pathway claim in mouse cells; **Very Low** for human muscle anabolism. |

**What the design can support:** YK11 induces myogenic marker mRNA/protein in C2C12 at 100–500 nM; *Fst* mRNA induced by YK11 but not DHT under these conditions; AR dependence (FLU, siRNA); anti-Fst Ab reverses YK11-induced *Myf5* mRNA — i.e., **necessity of extracellular Fst for that specific endpoint in this model**.  

**What it cannot support:** Direct myostatin receptor antagonism; systemic myostatin inhibition; that Fst is the sole or dominant anabolic mechanism in vivo; superiority to DHT for hypertrophy in intact animals/humans; tissue selectivity.

### 2.3 Yatsu et al. 2018 — *Biol Pharm Bull* 41:394–398  
PMID: 29491216 | DOI: 10.1248/bpb.b17-00748

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Design type** | In vitro osteoblast differentiation (MC3T3-E1). |
| **Sample size** | *n* = 3–4; three independent experiments claimed. |
| **Dose / duration** | YK11 0.5 µM; DHT 0.01 µM; HF 1 µM; MTS 96 h; ALP 10 d; mineralization 21 d; OPG/OC mRNA days 4/14; p-Akt 15 min. |
| **Statistics** | One-way ANOVA + Dunnett’s; *p* < 0.05. Better than unpaired *t*-tests alone for multi-group designs. |
| **Funding / COI** | Declared no COI; same Toho University lineage. |
| **GRADE** | **Low** for AR-dependent osteogenic activity in vitro; authors explicitly call for in vivo follow-up. |

Supports: YK11 accelerates MC3T3-E1 proliferation, ALP, mineralization, OPG/OC mRNA via AR (HF-sensitive); rapid p-Akt consistent with non-genomic AR signaling. Does **not** establish bone anabolism in animals/humans or dissociate androgenic risk.

### 2.4 Lee et al. 2021 — *Biochem Biophys Res Commun* 543:1–7  
PMID: 33588136 | DOI: 10.1016/j.bbrc.2021.01.030

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Design type** | Preclinical mouse model of gram-negative bacterial sepsis + YK11 as “myostatin inhibitor”; abstract-level appraisal only (full text paywalled/blocked in this acquisition). |
| **Population** | Septic mice (not healthy resistance-trained adults). |
| **Endpoints (abstract)** | Muscle myostatin protein ↑ with Fst, NF-κB, MRFs, p-FOXO3a, p-Smad2; YK11 reduced cytokines, organ damage markers, mortality. |
| **Dose / route / duration** | **Not retrieved** from full methods. |
| **Blinding / randomization / sample size / stats** | Unknown from abstract. |
| **COI** | Authors declare no COI; non-U.S. government support. |
| **GRADE** | **Very Low** for MoA claims (abstract-only; disease model; framing as “myostatin inhibition” without published binding data). |

**Critical framing issue:** Title/abstract label YK11 as “myostatin inhibitor” while reporting **increased** myostatin protein in septic muscle alongside Fst and other markers — pathway interpretation is ambiguous without full figures, dosing, and controls. Survival/cytokine benefits in sepsis do not validate gym anabolic claims.

### 2.5 Park et al. 2022 — *Korean J Sports Med* 40:67–85  
DOI: 10.5763/kjsm.2022.40.2.67

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Design type** | Narrative review (not systematic; no PRISMA). |
| **Role for evidence** | Secondary synthesis; useful for mapping discourse and caveats (N/C interference when stacking; tendon risk of myostatin pathway; **absence of human trials**). |
| **GRADE as evidence for efficacy** | **Very Low** (opinion + citation of primary in vitro work). |

Park correctly notes YK-11 “blocks myostatin by upregulating its antagonist, follistatin” via AR-mediated effects, and that mechanisms remain incompletely understood. The review’s language sometimes compresses Kanno 2013 into a stronger “myostatin inhibitor” label than the primary paper’s operational findings (Fst induction + Ab rescue of *Myf5*).

### 2.6 Dahleh et al. 2024 (and related safety line)

Rodent studies focusing on organ toxicity (prostate, kidney, liver) and hippocampal oxidative stress under YK11 ± resistance exercise. **Safety-signal literature**, not confirmatory MoA. Design details incomplete from abstract acquisition; relevant for external validity of “safe SARM” marketing claims (they undermine rather than support).

## 3. Bias assessment

Risk codes: **Low / Moderate / High / Unclear**. Domain applies to the claim “YK11 is a gene-selective partial AR agonist / Fst-inducing SARM usable as a myostatin inhibitor.”

| Bias type | Risk | Rationale |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Selection** | High (for applied claims); Low–Moderate (for cell MoA) | Cell lines chosen for AR expression convenience (293AR overexpression; MDA-MB-453 breast cancer; C2C12 immortalized myoblasts). Not representative of adult human skeletal muscle satellite cell physiology or prostate vs muscle in vivo. No random sampling of organisms for tissue selectivity. |
| **Performance** | Moderate–High | Open-label cell culture; investigator knowledge of treatment groups; no reported allocation concealment. Differentiation medium (2% HS) itself drives myogenesis — YK11 effects are additive on a strong background. FBS/csFBS variability uncharacterized. |
| **Detection** | Moderate | qRT-PCR and luciferase are objective; immunoblots often unquantified or single-blot representative images; confocal nuclear translocation qualitative. No pre-registered analysis plans. |
| **Attrition** | Unclear / Low | Cell assays rarely report drop-out of wells; animal sepsis study attrition unknown (abstract). |
| **Reporting** | High | Classic positive-results literature: partial agonism, Fst induction, osteogenesis all “work.” Negative tissue-selectivity or failed in vivo anabolic assays not published by the discovery lab. Lee 2021 full methods/doses not open; commercial literature recycles abstracts. Multiplicity of genes/time points without correction (Kanno 2013 Student’s *t* only). |
| **Publication / single-lab** | High | Core MoA package (2011, 2013, 2018) is **same laboratory lineage** (Kanno/Inouye, Toho University). Limited independent replication of the full N/C + Fst package. Trust downgrade trigger per source-evaluation.md: “Single-lab novelty claim with no external validation.” |
| **Industry / marketing contamination** | High for discourse; Low for Kanno funding | Primary papers MEXT/Open Research Center funded. Marketing and PED forums reframe “Fst mRNA ↑ in C2C12” as “myostatin inhibitor” and invent oral gym doses (5–15 mg/day) with zero PK basis. |
| **Confounding (cell model)** | Moderate | DHT vs YK11 potency differences (YK11 often needs higher nM) confound “gene selectivity” vs concentration/efficacy artifacts. Overexpressed AR (293AR) can distort partial-agonist pharmacology. |

## 4. Statistical adequacy

| Study | Effect sizes & CIs | Multiplicity | Missing data | Test appropriateness | Verdict |
|-------|--------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|---------|
| **Kanno 2011** | Relative luciferase: YK11 max ~10–20% of DHT (figure-level; no CI). Gene mRNA: fold changes graphed as mean ± SD, *n* = 3. **No Cohen’s d, no 95% CI.** | Multiple genes (FKBP51, FGF18, SARG, HSD11B2) and concentrations without family-wise error control. | Not discussed. | Reporter and RT-PCR standard; two-hybrid appropriate for N/C. | **p-value / bar-graph culture.** Partial-agonism magnitude is visible but imprecise. Exploratory. |
| **Kanno 2013** | MRF and *Fst* mRNA: mean ± SD, *n* = 3; * / ** vs solvent; # vs DHT. **No ES, no CI.** | Many endpoints (3 MRFs × doses × days + Fst + MyHC + Ab + FLU + siRNA) with **Student’s *t*-test only** — inflated Type I error. | Not discussed. | *t*-tests for multi-group dose-response are **inappropriate**; ANOVA/post-hoc preferred (used later in Yatsu 2018). | Underpowered for small effects; exploratory, not confirmatory. Anti-Fst Ab effect on *Myf5* is the strongest causal experiment but still *n* = 3. |
| **Yatsu 2018** | Mean ± SD, *n* = 3–4; ANOVA + Dunnett’s — **improved.** Still no ES/CI. p-Akt blot densitometry relative, not absolute. | Multiplicity partially addressed by Dunnett’s. | Not discussed. | Appropriate for multi-group comparisons. | Better stats than 2013; still pilot-scale. |
| **Lee 2021** | Abstract: directional claims (↓ cytokines, ↓ mortality). **No numbers, no CI, no n.** | Unknown. | Unknown. | Unknown. | **Cannot appraise statistical adequacy** without full text. |
| **Park 2022** | No primary data. | N/A | N/A | N/A | Secondary. |

**Cross-cutting statistical failures**

- Demand for effect sizes: **not met** in pivotal MoA papers — magnitude of *Fst* induction vs DHT is described as “significant” but not standardized.  
- Exploratory vs confirmatory: All Kanno/Yatsu work is **exploratory discovery biology**. No pre-registered primary endpoint, no independent confirmatory trial.  
- “No N/C interaction” is supported by two-hybrid null result vs strong DHT positive control — this is a **relative assay finding**, not a biophysical proof of zero interaction at physiological AR concentrations.  
- Small *n* (*n* = 3) is typical for cell papers but **precludes** firm inference about superiority of YK11 over DHT for myogenesis.

## 5. External validity

| Dimension | Match to research question? | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------------|-------|
| **Species / cell type** | Poor for human performance | Mouse C2C12 and MC3T3-E1; human cancer/kidney lines for AR assays. Adult human myofibers and satellite cells untested for Fst induction by YK11. |
| **Dose** | Poor | In vitro 100–500 nM (C2C12); 0.5 µM (osteoblast). **No published human oral bioavailability, free plasma concentration, or muscle exposure** corresponding to marketed 5–15 mg/day. Cannot map nM bath concentrations to oral gym use. |
| **Duration** | Poor | Hours to 21 days in culture; sepsis acute model. Not multi-week training cycles. |
| **Setting** | Poor | Sterile culture / experimental sepsis ≠ resistance-trained eugonadal adults with food, sleep, polypharmacy stacks. |
| **Outcome surrogacy** | Moderate for MoA; Poor for anabolism | ARE-luciferase and *Fst* mRNA are mechanistic surrogates. MyHC in C2C12 ≠ DXA lean mass or 1RM. Sepsis survival ≠ hypertrophy. |
| **Tissue selectivity** | **Not tested** | No Hershberger assay (levator ani vs prostate/seminal vesicle) in the discovery papers. SARM label is **extrapolated** from gene selectivity + SERM analogy, not demonstrated anabolic:androgenic ratio. |
| **Comparator** | Partial | DHT is correct pharmacologic comparator for AR full agonism; missing: other clinical SARMs (enobosarm, LGD-4033), testosterone, vehicle-only training controls in vivo. |

**Established** (**for this literature base**): External validity to human gym use of YK11 as a SARM/myostatin inhibitor is **not demonstrated**.  
**Speculative:** Gene-selective partial agonism observed in MDA-MB-453 *might* translate to differential muscle vs prostate programs in vivo.  
**Unknown:** Human free drug levels at marketed doses; prostate vs muscle efficacy ratio; HPG suppression magnitude.

## 6. Clinical / applied applicability

| Practical context | Translation? | Gap |
|-------------------|--------------|-----|
| Oral “SARM” cycles (community 5–15 mg/day) | **No** validated bridge | No human PK, dose-finding, or body-composition RCT. Any dose recommendation would be **Unknown** → **no recommendation**. |
| Muscle hypertrophy / recomp | Surrogate only (C2C12 MRFs, MyHC) | Differentiation in horse-serum medium ≠ load-induced hypertrophy. DHT also induced MyHC without *Fst* — Fst pathway is **not required** for all AR-driven myogenesis even in the same paper. |
| “Myostatin inhibition” for advanced trainees | **Indirect and unproven in humans** | Mechanism is AR → *Fst* mRNA → presumed TGF-β/myostatin neutralization. No direct ActRIIB competition, no MSTN protein binding, no serum myostatin suppression data in healthy humans. |
| Osteoporosis / bone | In vitro MC3T3-E1 only (Yatsu); rat defect models exist in later abstracts (Wang 2025, not full-appraised) | Far from clinical. |
| Sepsis cachexia | Lee 2021 rodent disease model | Wrong population for performance use; may still inform harm/benefit of pathway in illness. |
| Stacking with other androgens | Theoretically concerning | Kanno 2011: YK11 **prevents DHT-mediated N/C interaction** and can repress DHT-driven ARE-luciferase at high concentration → **potential functional antagonism** of full agonists when co-administered. Park 2022 flags this stacking risk. **Speculative** clinically but mechanistically grounded. |
| Harm reduction (HPG, liver, lipids, tendon) | Class-level SARM/AAS risks + myostatin-pathway tendon concerns | Health Canada and class warnings apply; Dahleh-type organ signals need full-text risk appraisal. Follistatin upregulation → FSH/fertility concerns noted in review literature (**Speculative** for YK11 specifically). |

**DR (actionable guidance) under project rules**

- **Recommend against** treating marketing claims of “myostatin inhibitor SARM with human gym doses” as evidence-based. Certainty that the literature supports such use: **Unknown** → **no dose/cycle recommendation**.  
- **Probable** (cell/rodent only): YK11 engages AR as a partial, gene-context-dependent agonist and can induce *Fst* in C2C12 in an AR-dependent manner.  
- **Harm reduction if exposure occurs despite lack of evidence:** Assume androgenic HPG suppression until proven otherwise; avoid stacking with full AR agonists without understanding N/C interference; monitor liver enzymes, lipids, hematocrit, and reproductive hormones as for other unapproved androgens; **no established PCT protocol for YK11** (Unknown). Tendon loading caution if myostatin pathway is truly perturbed (**Speculative**).  
- Prefer evidence-based anabolic strategies (progressive resistance training, protein adequacy, sleep) over unapproved designer steroids (**Established** for training/nutrition basics; out of scope for YK11 efficacy).

## Summary judgment (methodologist)

1. **Mechanism at AR:** The 2011 package is coherent discovery biology. Partial agonism, nuclear translocation, absent N/C interaction, and gene-selective transcription are **reasonably supported in vitro** (GRADE Low; certainty **Probable**). Authors’ causal link “no N/C → partial agonism” is mechanistic inference, not independently proven.

2. **SARM / AR selectivity:** “SARM” is used as a **label of aspiration** based on gene selectivity and analogy to SERMs/other SARMs that impair N/C interaction. **Tissue** selectivity (muscle/bone vs prostate/HPG) is **not demonstrated** by Hershberger or equivalent in the pivotal papers. Certainty for true tissue-selective SARM status: **Unknown**.

3. **Myostatin / follistatin:** The only rigorous primary experiment is Kanno 2013: AR-dependent *Fst* mRNA induction by YK11 (not DHT) in C2C12, with anti-Fst Ab reversing *Myf5*. That supports **indirect, cell-autocrine/paracrine Fst-mediated contribution** to one differentiation marker. Calling YK11 a “myostatin inhibitor” as a drug class is **conclusion overreach** unless and until systemic MSTN activity, ActRIIB signaling, and human outcomes are measured. Direct receptor antagonism: **unsupported**.

4. **Applied use:** Zero controlled human efficacy/safety data. Community doses lack PK grounding. Per DR rules: **Unknown = no recommendation** for performance dosing. Mechanistic harm-reduction notes (HPG, stack N/C interference, class SARM risks, speculative tendon/FSH issues) are the only actionable layer.

5. **Evidence hierarchy position:** All pivotal MoA evidence sits at **animal/in-vitro / single-lab mechanistic** tier. Downgraded for small *n*, p-value-only reporting, multiplicity, surrogate endpoints, and absence of independent confirmatory programs.
