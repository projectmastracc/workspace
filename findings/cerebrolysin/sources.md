### 2.3 Critical funding pattern (for downstream personas)

> **Nearly all positive landmark RCTs and the most-cited positive stroke/TBI/AD meta-analyses in the Cerebrolysin literature are manufacturer-sponsored or authored by investigators/statisticians with declared EVER ties.**  
> **The clearest independent sources (Cochrane AIS, Cochrane VaD, AHA/ASA guidelines) are neutral-to-cautious and do not underwrite routine use.**  
> **Patel 2025 is the main recent stroke MA with explicit no-funding / no-relationship declarations** — still reliant on an underlying trial base that is heavily industry-funded.

This is a classic **industry-dominated evidence ecosystem**, not isolated single-trial sponsorship.

```
        │         Gauthier 2015 (EVER employee + SAB)
        │         Vester 2021 CAPTAIN PMA
        │
        └─► Marketing layer: cerebrolysin.com PDFs, webinars, interviews
                  (repackage Bornstein, CAPTAIN, Strilciuc safety MA)

Independent / higher-distance sources:
        ├─► Cochrane AIS (Ziganshina) — maps sponsor role; does not endorse routine use
        ├─► Cochrane VaD (Chen/Cui) — small effects; industry-funded primaries; insufficient for routine use
        ├─► AHA/ASA 2019 AIS guidelines — no Cerebrolysin as US standard therapy
        ├─► Patel 2025 — no funding/relationships declared; still synthesizes industry-funded RCTs
        └─► AlzDiscovery Cognitive Vitality — secondary independent synthesis with quality caveats
```

**Evidence telephone flags:**
- Claims that “meta-analysis finally proves efficacy” often cite **Bornstein 2018**, which pools trials EVER funded or assisted and was promoted by the manufacturer.
- Safety “confirmed” messaging often cites **Strilciuc 2021**, formally unfunded but network-adjacent and manufacturer-republished.
- TBI “strong evidence from CAPTAIN” messaging is largely **manufacturer webinar language** built on the CAPTAIN series, not independent multi-sponsor replication.

**Independence gradient (high → low):**  
AHA guidelines ≈ Cochrane reviews > Patel 2025 (declarations clean) > AlzDiscovery > Strilciuc 2021 (declared clean, network-adjacent) > Bornstein/Gauthier/Vester packages > CARS/CASTA/CAPTAIN primaries > product monographs / marketing PDFs.

### 5.1 Narrative vs source-strength mismatch (critical flag)

| Public / commercial narrative | Supporting source cluster | Independent source posture |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| “Meta-analysis proves early stroke recovery benefit” | Bornstein 2018 + CARS package (**Low** independence) | Cochrane AIS: benefit not supported for routine use; CASTA overall primary not met |
| “CAPTAIN proves TBI neurorecovery” | CAPTAIN series + Vester PMA + EVER webinars (**Low**) | Limited independent multi-sponsor replication; not US guideline therapy |
| “Safe as placebo” | Strilciuc 2021 + industry safety messaging | Cochrane AIS has flagged non-fatal SAE signals in some analyses — independent safety synthesis not uniformly “clean” |
| “AD meta-analysis favorable” | Gauthier 2015 (**Low** independence) | Not FDA-approved; not US SOC; foundation reports note quality caveats |
| “VaD useful” | Industry VaD trials | **Cochrane VaD High**: small benefit, insufficient for routine recommendation |

**Flag:** A **high-confidence commercial and regional-clinical narrative** (stroke/TBI neurorecovery standard add-on in parts of EU/Asia) rests disproportionately on **Low-trust (industry-linked) sources**, while **High-trust independent syntheses remain cautious or non-endorsing**. Downstream synthesis must not equate volume of positive EVER-linked papers with independent confirmation.

## 6. Practical rules for other personas

1. **Always dual-cite** industry-linked positive MAs (Bornstein, Gauthier, Vester) with **Patel 2025** and/or **Cochrane** when making stroke/VaD claims.  
2. **Do not use monographs** as efficacy evidence; dosing/contraindications only.  
3. **Name EVER Neuro Pharma** when citing CARS, CASTA, CAPTAIN package, Gauthier, Bornstein (search help), Rejdak COI.  
4. **Patel 2025 COI declaration** is a genuine upgrade for independence of *authorship* — not of the underlying trial funders.  
5. **Unknown funding** should be stated for any paper where full text COI block was inaccessible (partial limitation of this critic pass for some CAPTAIN II funding paragraphs).  
6. **No source in this inventory is “Unreliable” as fabrication** except marketing materials for efficacy; the dominant issue is **dependence and sponsor-controlled evidence generation**, not fraud signals.
