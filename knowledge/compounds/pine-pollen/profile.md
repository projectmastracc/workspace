## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | Pine pollen (*Pinus* spp. pollen grains; common commercial: *P. massoniana*, *P. sylvestris*, *P. ponderosa*, *P. contorta*, mixed Pinus). | **Biggest downsides / sides** | **Allergy / anaphylaxis risk** in atopic or pine-pollen-sensitized (**Established** class risk); GI upset early (**Probable** mild); marketing overclaim vs plant-T math (**Established** mismatch); possible phytosterol dualism (androgen support claims vs β-sitosterol/BPH/antiandrogen narratives) (**Speculative** net human direction); null responders common (**Probable** forum); shelf-life degradation of sterols (**Probable**); quality/species/lot variance (**Established** problem). |

## 2. Chemistry, Classification, Pharmacokinetics

### 2.1 Identity and matrix

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Source** | Male gametophyte pollen of *Pinus* spp. (Pinaceae) |
| **Commercial species** | *P. massoniana* (Masson pine — dominant Chinese + many Western tinctures); *P. sylvestris* (Scotch pine — classic steroid-content papers); *P. ponderosa*, *P. contorta* (North American commercial); mixed blends |
| **Class** | Complex botanical food / traditional Chinese medicinal material (Songhuafen 松花粉); marketed as phytoandrogen / adaptogen |
| **Physical form** | Microscopic grains with tough **exine** (sporopollenin-rich outer wall) → whole grains resist digestion → **cell-wall cracking / breaking** used to improve nutrient access |
| **Routes** | Oral powder (shaken in liquid, food, capsules); oral/sublingual tincture; topical pastes/powders (Chinese skin indications); rare experimental aquaculture feed |

**Not:** synthetic testosterone, SARMs, HCG, clomiphene, or pharmaceutical 5α-reductase inhibitors. Comparative neighbors (tongkat, fadogia, maca, β-sitosterol isolate, saw palmetto, TRT) are section 11.

### 2.2 Macronutrients and micronutrients (typical powder)

Composition varies by species, harvest, processing, and wall-cracking. Order-of-magnitude typical commercial powder (Cheng et al. 2023-type composition reviews):

| Component | Approximate range | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Protein** | ~12–17% | Amino-acid complete-ish plant protein; not a primary gym protein source by dose |
| **Carbohydrate / sugars** | Substantial; species-dependent | Energy substrate; GI load if large spoons |
| **Fiber** | High relative to scoop size | Bulk + gut effects |
| **Fat** | ~3% class | Includes sterols and lipid fraction |
| **B vitamins** | Present (B1, B2, etc. reported) | Nutrition claim support |
| **Minerals** | K, Mg, Ca, Cr and others reported | Not a substitute for targeted mineral repletion when deficient |
| **Flavonoids** | Quercetin, kaempferol, others | Antioxidant narrative (Lee 2009 in vitro) |
| **Polysaccharides** | Present | Immune/anti-fatigue Chinese pharmacology themes |

**Certainty:** Composition as multi-nutrient pollen food — **Established**. Exact % for a given retail lot — **Unknown** without CoA.

### 2.3.1 Plant T dose math — keep prominent (not TRT)

| Powder dose | ≈ T mass at 80 ng/g | vs endogenous male production (~5–7 mg/day) | vs TRT (order) |
|-------------|---------------------|---------------------------------------------|----------------|
| 1 g | 80 ng = 0.00008 mg | ~0.001% of daily production | Negligible |
| 5 g | 400 ng = **0.0004 mg** | Still << 0.01% | Negligible |
| 10 g | 800 ng = **0.0008 mg** | Still trivial | Negligible |
| TRT gel / injectable | — | — | **mg/day systemic** delivery targets |
| Endogenous male production | — | ~5–7 **mg**/day | — |
| Ratio (10 g pollen T vs ~6 mg/day endogenous) | — | **~1/7,500** as *ingested* T mass | — |

Even if androstenedione (~0.6 µg/g) is counted as a weak oral prohormone precursor, multi-gram powder remains in the **microgram total C19 steroid** range before first-pass destruction. Oral unmodified T historically has poor bioavailability without 17α-alkylation or special systems — reinforcing that ng–µg plant T is not a delivery strategy.

**Conclusion (Established):** any observed human “androgenic feel” is **not** explained by simple oral plant-T replacement. Prefer **indirect** mechanisms (section 3, 6). Marketing that equates “contains testosterone” with TRT is a **unit-error myth**.

### 2.4 Processing forms and bioavailability of the matrix

| Form | Processing | Practical implication | Certainty |
|------|------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Whole / raw powder** | Intact exine | Nutrients and sterols partially locked; still used as food | **Probable** lower nutrient accessibility vs cracked |
| **Cracked / broken cell wall powder** | Mechanical/enzymatic wall disruption | Preferred for **nutrition** (protein, minerals, polysaccharides access) | **Probable** improved accessibility; gold-standard human nutrient-balance RCTs sparse |
| **Hydroethanolic tincture** | Alcohol–water extraction of pollen (often *P. massoniana*); concentrated extract claims | Marketed for **phytoandrogen delivery**; sublingual use common; pilot used ~300 mg extract/mL proprietary product | **Speculative** that ethanol extract enriches the “right” actives for T; **Probable** better delivery of lipophilic fraction vs uncracked whole grains |
| **Capsules** | Powder fill | Convenience; dose accuracy depends on fill weight | Practical only |
| **Topical** | Powder/paste on skin (Chinese clinical) | Local wound/dermatitis use — different risk/benefit than oral T claims | **Probable** for local skin indications in that literature |

**Shelf life:** Sterol and brassinosteroid content reported to **degrade with age/storage** (Mushtagh/NDNR-type claims). Buy fresher lots, store cool/dry/dark. **Probable**.

## 4. Clinical Evidence — Full Evaluation

Stratified. Certainty is **claim-level**. Disease/topical Chinese positives do **not** auto-license healthy-user “T boost” claims. Trust tiers: **A** independent primary / high-tier composition; **B** solid secondary or limited primary; **C** high bias (open-label, sponsor, tiny n, bibliometric without RoB); **D** forums/marketing.

### 4.1 Western testosterone / andropause pilot — Wolkodoff et al. (2024/2025)

**Trust tier: C** (borderline D for causal TT claim). Do **not** cite as “clinical proof pine pollen raises T.”

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Design** | Open-label, **n=10** men, age 47–78 (mean ~63); **no placebo, no blinding** |
| **Product** | Proprietary *Pinus massoniana* hydroethanolic tincture (Lost Empire Herbs); ~**300 mg extract/mL** |
| **Dose** | **0.5 mL AM + 0.5 mL PM** = **1 mL/day**, **5 days on / 2 off**, **8 weeks** |
| **TT result** | Mean total T **362.5 → 448.4 ng/dL** (Δ **+85.9**); **p=0.0584** — **not statistically significant** at conventional α=0.05 |
| **Symptoms** | qADAM **23.9 → 31.7**, **p=0.0035** |
| **Safety** | No reported AEs in this tiny sample |
| **Labs missing** | Free T, SHBG, LH, FSH, E2, hematocrit, PSA — **not reported** |
| **Assay** | At-home Elecsys TT immunoassay — method OK class but **home collection / diurnal control** concerns; inferior to morning venous LC-MS/MS for research claims |
| **COI** | Lost Empire provided tincture **and study support**; vendor marketing states they **funded** the study; paper COI section said “None” — **materially incomplete**. Dual vendor relationships (herb + lab kit). |
| **Venue** | *Annals of Clinical and Medical Case Reports* (United Prime) — flagged in predatory-adjacent discussions; receipt→acceptance **14 days** — incompatible with rigorous multi-reviewer endocrinology peer review |
| **Individual-data red flags** | Subject 2: 282 → 703 ng/dL (**+421**) — extreme outlier; mean heavily leverage-dependent. Subject 4: TT **unchanged** (316→316) but qADAM +13 — symptom gain without TT change (expectation/placebo). Several subjects: +12–20 ng/dL (within immunoassay/day-to-day noise). Mid-study labs “repeated… although not tabulated” — selective reporting concern |

**What it can support (weakly):**
- Feasibility of short-term tincture use in older men without obvious acute toxicity in n=10. **Speculative** safety generalization (n too small for rare allergy/anaphylaxis).
- Symptom score movement under open-label conditions. **Probable** that symptoms can improve in an uncontrolled setting; **Speculative** drug-specific fraction after subtracting placebo/P17.
- A **nonsignificant trend** in total T worth **hypothesis generation**, not product claims of “raises T.”
- If any TT rise were real and causal, **mass balance forbids attributing it to absorbed pollen T** — forces indirect mechanism, bias/noise, or other tincture actives (**Unknown**).

**What it does not show:**
- Causal TT elevation (no placebo; p=0.058).
- Free androgen index improvement.
- HPTA stimulation vs random walk / regression to mean / lifestyle co-interventions.
- Young gym population transfer (mean age 63, baseline mid-low 300s).
- Non-proprietary species/extract equivalence.
- Long-term durability or post-cycle stability.

| Claim | Certainty |
|-------|-----------|
| Proprietary tincture 1 mL/day 5/2 × 8 wk raises total T in men | **Speculative** (underpowered trend only) |
| Same regimen improves qADAM symptoms vs true placebo | **Speculative**–**Unknown** (open-label positive ≠ placebo-controlled) |

### 7.2 S1 — Allergy / anaphylaxis (HARD STOP)

**Phenotype:** Rhinitis, conjunctivitis, asthma flare, oral itch, urticaria; rare systemic anaphylaxis in sensitized/atopic/cross-reactive individuals. Oral exposure can still trigger (less common than inhalation but not zero). Dust when scooping powder is an inhalational risk.

**Mechanism (step by step):**
1. Allergen proteins/glycoproteins in pollen matrix recognized by IgE (**P12**).
2. FcεRI cross-linking on mast cells/basophils → Ca²⁺ influx → degranulation.
3. Histamine, leukotrienes, PGD2 → mucosal edema, bronchospasm; systemic cascade if widespread.
4. Cross-reactivity with other pollens possible (Gastaminza-class literature) → reaction despite “never tried pine pollen.”

**Certainty of risk:** **Established** in susceptible; population base rate for oral supplement use **Unknown** precisely.

## 12. Open Questions

1. **Blinded RCT** of standardized *P. massoniana* tincture vs placebo on morning **total T, free T, SHBG, LH, FSH, E2**, qADAM, and sexual function in hypogonadal-range vs eugonadal men — adequately powered (not n=10); pre-registered; independent funding; venous LC-MS/MS.
2. **Human PK** of pollen-derived C19 steroids and brassinosteroids after powder vs tincture vs sublingual.
3. **Species comparison** head-to-head (*massoniana* vs *sylvestris* vs *ponderosa*) on sterol panels and clinical endpoints.
4. **Shelf-life kinetics** of sterols/brassinosteroids under real commercial storage — quantify the degradation claim.
5. **SHBG / free androgen index** as primary — does any symptom benefit track free T?
6. **Women’s endocrine and libido RCTs** — currently anecdote-only.
7. **Net prostate biology** — reconcile Chinese BPH-inhibition pharmacology with Western androgen marketing (tissue, DHT, PSA).
8. **Cracked vs whole** nutrient balance studies in humans (amino acid appearance, mineral AUC).
9. **Allergen characterization** standardized across commercial products; prevalence of sensitization in supplement users.
10. **Body composition / strength** RCTs — expected null, but silence allows marketing fantasies.
11. **Drug interaction** screens (CYP, UGT) for concentrated extracts.
12. **Independent replication** of Wolkodoff without brand COI, with sham tincture control and diurnal-controlled phlebotomy (n≥40–60).
13. Peer-reviewed publication of Mushtagh–Strnad NA-species sterol panel (currently unpublished via NDNR).

**What would *not* change conclusions much:** another open-label brand pilot; more fish sex-reversal or CCl4 papers; bibliometric counts without RoB; unverified n=1 lab screenshots; higher claimed sterol numbers from COI-linked unpub assays without third-party replication and human PK.

## 14. Short Boundary

Pine pollen is a **nutrient-dense pollen food** with a **long Chinese topical/metabolic adjunct history** and a **Western phytoandrogen marketing story that outruns the data**. Plant testosterone is **real and tiny** (~0.0004 mg T per 5 g powder) — **not oral TRT**. One open-label n=10 sponsor pilot is **hypothesis-grade** (NS total T, significant unblinded symptoms, missing free-T/HPTA panel, predatory-adjacent venue) — not a license for “natural TRT” claims. Use cracked powder for food; tincture only as a **time-boxed, allergy-screened, optional-lab** vitality experiment. **Allergy is the hard stop.** For hypogonadism or serious performance pharmacology, this is the wrong primary tool — sleep, body composition, training, and when indicated, **medicine**.

*End of compound profile — pine pollen / Pinus spp. (Research ID 54b301d5).*
