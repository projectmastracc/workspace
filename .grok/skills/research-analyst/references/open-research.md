# Open Research — Transparency & Actionable Reusability

Every `/research` output must be **traceable** and **reusable** for compound contextualization (nootropics, performance, any supplementation).

## Open sourcing requirements

1. **Cite every pivotal claim** — DOI, PMID, or URL  
2. **Label inference** — distinguish direct citation from analyst inference  
3. **Expose funding/COI** — never hide industry sponsorship  
4. **Tag experiential / forum patterns** — multi-source patterns get explicit weighing + concordance; single anecdotes low weight; never Established/Probable from anecdote alone  
5. **Preserve uncertainty** — do not collapse contested evidence into false consensus  
6. **Label literature silence honestly** — silence ≠ proof of non-existence  

## Actionable reusability

Outputs are designed for users to **contextualize and apply** compounds:

| Deliverable | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Executive Card | One-screen verdict + practical note + caveats |
| Compound monograph | Full 15-section framing across applicable lenses |
| Interaction / pathway analysis | Multi-compound protective or stack evaluation |
| Practical Guidance | Evidence-graded dosing, timing, stacks, monitoring |
| evidence-matrix.json | Machine-readable claims, recommendations, anecdotal_patterns, pathway_overlap |
| knowledge/ profiles | Differential updates across runs |

## Recommendation traceability

Each recommendation in Practical Guidance must link to:

- A cited source (trial, review, label, guideline), OR  
- A labeled inference chain ("inferred from mechanism X + animal data Y — **Speculative**"), OR  
- A labeled multi-source anecdotal pattern (**Speculative** ceiling only)

## What "open" does not mean

- Recommending without evidence (**Unknown** = no recommendation)  
- Presenting pure anecdote as Established  
- Erasing consistent forum patterns because RCTs are missing  
- Literature-refusal as the whole answer to stack/protective questions  
- Hiding harm-reduction requirements for performance compounds
