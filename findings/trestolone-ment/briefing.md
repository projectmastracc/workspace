# Trestolone (MENT) — Compound Profile

**Research ID:** 94cde63d  
**Compound:** Trestolone / MENT / 7α-methyl-19-nortestosterone (acetate ester in gym use)  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Class:** Synthetic estrane AAS · 19-nortestosterone derivative · AR agonist · progestogen · experimental male contraceptive / androgen replacement  

**Critical framing — dual-dose paradox:** Clinical MENT Ac implants deliver ~**400–500 µg/implant/day** (Anderson: ~0.4 vs ~0.8 mg/day with 1 vs 2 implants). At that band, the dominant estrogen problem is **under-estrogenization** → lumbar BMD ↓, sexual function incomplete at 1 implant. Forum trestolone acetate is **10–75 mg/day** (entry–advanced), often **~30–100×** the clinical mass delivery. At that band the dominant estrogen problem is **over-aromatization** to 7α-methylestradiol (MeE2) → gyno, water, BP. Same compound, same P3 pathway, **opposite poles**. Do not average these regimes. Every efficacy and side line below is tagged **clinical-low** (implant / ≤~2 mg/day continuous; short IM 1–4 mg) vs **forum-supra** (≥~10 mg/day acetate).

## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | Trestolone (MENT; 7α-methyl-19-nortestosterone); gym form **trestolone acetate** (MENT Ac). Estrane AAS: nandrolone scaffold + **7α-methyl**. Dual **androgen + progestogen**; aromatizes to **7α-methylestradiol (MeE2)** with ER potency ≈ E2. Never marketed. Population Council experimental contraception/HRT (stalled ~2013). UGL injectable acetate for performance. WADA banned. |
| **Primary use cases** | **Performance (forum-supra):** mass, strength, recomp, pumps, libido at acetate **10–75 mg/day** ED (common mid **25–50**). **Clinical-low experimental:** male contraception (implants) + hypogonadal replacement (~0.4–2 mg/day continuous). | **Bottom line** | MENT is a high-affinity AR ligand that does **not** 5α-reduce, binds PR (~50–75% of P4), does **not** bind SHBG, and aromatizes to potent MeE2. Free MENT IV t½ ~**40 min**; MCR ~**1790–2360 L/day** → high free fraction, rapid clearance → acetate **daily** for gym use. Preclinical: ~**10×** T for myotropic/gonadotropin axes vs ~**2×** T for prostate (monkeys). Human implants: sexual function maintained only at **2 implants**; **lumbar BMD fell both arms**; prostate volume/PSA not increased. Suvisaari **4 mg/day IM free MENT → T −74%, LH −70%, FSH −57%**. Gym outcomes at 25–75 mg/day acetate are **forum-only**; reputation split — “libido/recomp king” vs gyno/BP nightmare. Mechanism map is clinically rich at µg doses; phenotype map is anecdotally rich at mg acetate. |
| **Biggest upsides** | **Established:** potent AR agonism; non-5α-reducible tissue-selectivity chemistry; aromatization to bioactive MeE2; profound HPG suppression (contraceptive PD). **Established preclinical:** myotropic:androgenic ~10× T (rat); monkey 10× systemic / 2× prostate. **Probable:** high free fraction; strong gym anabolism/strength/fullness at forum doses; libido high when E managed. |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | **Established (clinical-low):** HPG crash; oligo/azoospermia on-drug; BMD loss when estrogenicity inadequate. **Probable (forum-supra + class):** aggressive gyno/water, BP elevation, HDL↓, Hct↑, acne, hunger, heat/sweat. **Speculative:** MENT-specific prolactin path; MPB reduced vs DHT but residual AR risk; long-term MACE **Unknown**. |
| **Stack role** | High-management 19-nor blast primary or solo when user accepts AI/SERM/BP engineering. Not a set-and-forget TRT analog. Slot: between nandrolone (less estrogenic “feel” for many) and trenbolone (MENT more estrogenic, often less classic tren CNS tax if E controlled). Dual 19-nor (MENT + tren) is an anti-pattern for most. DMAU/related oral norandrogens are contraception-pipeline cousins, not acetate substitutes. |

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Evidence map

| Tier | What exists for MENT | What does **not** exist |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| Systematic review / meta of gym outcomes | No | Hypertrophy RCTs; CV hard outcomes at 25–75 mg/d |

**COI note (Population Council spine):** Agenda is male contraception + androgen replacement — endpoints weighted to sperm, LH/FSH, PSA/prostate volume, BMD, sexual function — **not** 1RM, recomp, or pumps. “Prostate-sparing” framing is real at studied doses but must always be paired with Anderson BMD loss and small n. Not classic pharma sales COI; still indication-biased.

### 4.4 Anderson et al. 2003 — hypogonadal implant RCT (PMID 12788888)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Design** | Parallel RCT, **n=16** hypogonadal men; MENT Ac implants **1 vs 2** (~**400 µg/d each** → ~0.4 vs ~0.8 mg/d) × **24 weeks** |

### Weighing

1. **Clinical-low vs forum-supra are different estrogen environments.** Implants lost bone (too little estrogen effect). Forum blasts report gyno/water/BP (too much MeE2). Same P3, opposite sides of the dose–aromatization curve. **Do not import Anderson BMD loss as proof that gym MENT is “low estrogen.”**
2. **Prostate-sparing is a clinical-low narrative.** Do not treat 50 mg/d acetate as proven prostate-safe.
3. **Highest forum consistency:** suppression, estrogenic burden, BP/water, appetite, strength/fullness, management load (AI/SERM/BP drugs).
4. **Polarization** (love vs hate) tracks E2/BP management skill and individual aromatase/PR sensitivity more than pure fake logs — still heavy selection/survivor bias.
5. **Prolactin counters and “tren lite” branding** are weaker multi-source (split or meme-heavy) than gyno/BP clusters.
6. **Literature silence on recomp ≠ non-existence**; silence on long-term CV **does** mean risk is unquantified — monitor markers, do not deny risk.

### 8.3 Stack engineering (order of operations)

1. **Goal:** bulk (lean into P15 hunger) vs recomp/strength vs experimental HRT-analogue — dose band differs.  
2. **Baseline labs:** CBC, CMP, lipids, total/free T, E2, LH/FSH, PRL, PSA (age), BP log; DEXA if prior low-E or long AAS.  
3. **Structure:** solo MENT vs test base (see dual-E note).  
4. **Start low–mid** for experience; **ED acetate**.  
5. **Estrogen plan before day 1:** AI on hand (anastrozole or exemestane) + SERM on hand (raloxifene preferred by many for gyno). Prefer **reactive or micro-proactive AI**, not nuclear letrozole default.  
6. **BP plan day 1:** home monitor; telmisartan ± tadalafil available if baseline high-normal or rises.  
7. **Hct plan:** donation eligibility if climbing.  
8. **Mid-cycle labs:** symptoms + trends (MeE2 assay caveat); lipids, CBC, BP.  
9. **Adjust nodes:** gyno → SERM ± AI; BP → ARB/cardio/dose; libido flat → check over-AI; sexual weirdness → E2/PRL **before** caber.  
10. **Exit:** SERM PCT ± hCG strategy or cruise/TRT; fertility specialist if conceiving within months.  
11. **Do not** stack conflicting counters (high AI + joint/libido complaints without reducing AI first).

## 12. Open questions

6. **Prostate volume/PSA** at supraphysiologic exposures — does clinical-low sparing survive ~60× implant mass?
