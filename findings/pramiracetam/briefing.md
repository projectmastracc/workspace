## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | Pramiracetam (CI-879; Pramistar; Neupramir; Remen). N-[2-(Diisopropylamino)ethyl]-2-(2-oxopyrrolidin-1-yl)acetamide. Fat-soluble racetam; ATC N06BX16. |
| **Primary use cases** | Cognitive performance under load (study, technical work); memory encoding/recall support; historical post-TBI cognitive rehabilitation interest; elderly mild cognitive support with concurrent training. **Not** a mood elevating stimulant; **not** an Alzheimer’s symptomatic standard. |
| **Bottom line** | Lipophilic Parke-Davis-era racetam whose best-supported molecular handle is **↑ high-affinity choline uptake (HACU / CHT1 capacity)** in hippocampus (**Established** preclinical; **Probable** dominant human cognitive mechanism). Clinical efficacy is **thin and stratified**: tiny DBPC TBI series n=4 positive with off-drug persistence (**Speculative** signal; CNI COI); AD enrichment up to 4 g **negative** (**Probable** null for symptomatic AD — Claus 1991 is the highest-quality clinical paper); elderly open pilot with memory training modestly positive (**Speculative–Probable**). **No modern RCTs in healthy young adults.** Forum phenotype is sharper than clinical literature: “laser focus,” cold logic, study endurance, memory-sponge encoding — and a signature rate of **emotional blunting / zombie affect** vs piracetam/aniracetam. Community range 250–600 mg 1–3×/day **with fat**, choline **titrated both directions** (deficit headache vs excess fog/zombie). Stack role = **cold cognitive drive + cholinergic capacity tool**, not social/mood base. |
| **Biggest upsides** | HACU → ACh synthesis capacity under demand (**Probable**); task-locked focus / reduced distractibility (**Probable** forum phenotype; **Unknown** as healthy RCT endpoint); episodic encoding under active learning (**Probable** forum + preclinical; training synergy pilot); clean linear oral PK, multi-hour coverage (**Established**); TBI cognitive interest signal despite tiny n (**Speculative** clinical). |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Emotional blunting / anhedonia / flattened affect (**Probable** forum phenotype; mechanism **Speculative** ACh-network imbalance); headache/brain fog from choline-pool mismatch (**Probable**); stim-vs-sedate biphasic split (**Probable** variance); tolerance over continuous weeks (**Probable** forum); powder burn/taste (**Established** practical); rare loud mood-crash reports (**Speculative** causality); AD non-response at high dose (**Probable** null in that design). |
| **Stack role** | **Primary:** cold focus / memory-work sessions when emotional range is expendable. **Secondary:** racetam layer under fat + choline donor PRN; aniracetam for affect offset; caffeine only after solo mapping. **Not:** first-line anxiety, depression, social warmth, or creative-flow agent. |

### 2.4 Negative binding / what it is *not*

Preclinical neurochemistry (Pugsley & Shih 1983; Pavlík et al. 1987): **no** stimulant-like direct DA/5-HT receptor agonist or reuptake-inhibitor profile; no classic monoamine “upper” signature (**Established–Probable** negative binding). Memory effects can be **suppressed by adrenal steroids** in animal work (**Probable** animal; **Speculative** human cortisol-threshold).

**Phenotype implication:** Expecting Adderall-like DA drive mis-maps the compound. Expecting pure piracetam “clarity without edge” under-rates blunting risk. Cold logic without euphoria is the architectural prediction of P1 + negative monoamine binding.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Evidence hierarchy overview

| Tier | Status for pramiracetam |
|------|-------------------------|
| Systematic review / meta (pram-specific) | No modern high-quality pram-only meta. Class coverage in Malykh & Sadaie 2010. |
| PK / controlled PK | **Chang 1985**, **Auteri 1992** — solid (**T1** for PK claims). |
| Efficacy RCT healthy young “nootropic” | **None** of modern adequate size. |
| Efficacy disease / elderly | Small AD negative (Claus **T2**); tiny TBI positive (McLean **T5 / COI**); elderly open pilot (De Vreese **T2-minus**). |
| Preclinical | HACU, NOS, EEG, recognition memory, scopolamine models — pathway map only. |

**Joint logical product:** AD null ∧ TBI tiny positive ∧ no healthy RCT → efficacy prior for healthy nootropic use = **weak clinical, not zero mechanism**. Cannot sell as “clinically proven study drug.” Cannot dismiss as “clinically proven inert.” Correct posture: **under-determined efficacy + over-determined forum phenotype + mid-strength HACU story**.

### 4.2 Pharmacokinetics / tolerance

**Chang et al. 1985 — *J Clin Pharmacol***  
- Design: Oral PK within clinical tolerance; normal male volunteers; single 400–1600 mg.  
- Result: Linear PK; t½ 4.5–6.5 h; Tmax 2–3 h; dose-independent CL/Vd; no significant sides at tested single doses.  
- Limits: Single-dose; healthy males; 1980s analytics; not efficacy; no chronic PK.  
- COI: Parke-Davis development — acceptable for PK numbers.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** human PK skeleton.

**Auteri et al. 1992**  
- Design: 600 mg solution vs tablet, n=11, wide age range.  
- Result: t½ ~4.3–4.7 h; formulation mainly changes absorption rate; inter-subject t½ variability high (range ~2–8 h).  
- **Certainty:** **Established** formulation/PK confirmation; variance explains BID vs TID user split and some “nothing” reports from coverage gaps.

### 4.3 Traumatic brain injury / anoxia — cognitive

**McLean et al. 1991 — *Brain Injury***  
- Design: DBPC; **n=4** young males with TBI/anoxia cognitive deficits; long course (~18 mo context in secondary); dose on order of **~400 mg** schedule.  
- Result: Improved memory/cognition; benefits reportedly **persisted ~1 month after discontinuation** (interesting for plasticity vs pure symptomatic cover).  
- Limits: **Catastrophically small n** — CIs can span null to large benefit even with blinding; historical diagnostics; practice effects.  
- **COI (material):** Co-author Elkan Gamzu / Cambridge Neuroscience (CNI) held TBI/stroke/ECT development rights. Orphan ECT path later withdrawn. McLean sits inside commercial pivot narrative, not modern independent TBI program. Secondary sources (ERABI-type tables, Malykh, marketing) recycle the same four subjects.  
- **Certainty of population effect:** **Speculative** (signal of interest only).  
- **Certainty something real happened in those subjects:** **Probable** within-trial, still fragile.  
- **Briefing rule:** Never convert to standardized effect size for study stacks. Historical interest; pathway-compatible hypothesis for severe acquired brain injury cognition; **not** Established treatment.

### 4.4 Alzheimer’s disease / dementia

**Claus et al. 1991 — *Neurology***  
- Design: 2-phase placebo-controlled **enrichment** AD; n=10; doses **up to 4000 mg**; FDG-PET in 2.  
- Result: 8/10 “best dose” in finding phase; **only 2/10** replicated confirmation; authors: **unlikely** clinically meaningful symptomatic AD benefit; PET “no definite change.”  
- Why negative enrichment matters: Enrichment is biased toward false positives when positive — a **failed confirmation** is therefore somewhat more informative against AD efficacy (still n=10).  
- Limits: Tiny; old AD criteria; high-dose ≠ community nootropic dose; batteries ≠ “laser focus.”  
- **Certainty:** **Probable** that high-dose pram is **not** useful symptomatic AD Rx. **Not** proof of universal cognitive inertness in other populations. Highest-quality clinical paper in corpus and it is **negative**.

**Branconnier et al. 1983 — *Psychopharmacol Bull***  
- Preliminary AD exploratory — thin bulletin format; superseded by Claus. **Unknown / historical only.** Do not cite as efficacy.

**Implication:** Pramiracetam is **not** validated AD pharmacotherapy. High-dose failure argues against “just push grams like piracetam” for degenerative dementia (**Probable** practical takeaway). Marketing that soft-pedals or omits Claus is claim-inflating.

### 4.5 Elderly / memory training

**De Vreese et al. 1996 — *Arch Gerontol Geriatr***  
- Design: Open 4-arm pilot; elderly non-demented memory complainers; n=35 total (MT 10 / DT 8 / DTMT 10 / CTR 7); Italian Rx environment.  
- Result: Objective memory ranking **DTMT > DT > MT > CTR** (drug + training best; drug alone > training alone). Metamemory mixed.  
- Limits: **Open-label**; small cells; training confounds; no DBPC replication; wrong age for student nootropic.  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative–Probable** for “drug + active learning synergy” hypothesis; weak as monotherapy claim. Aligns with forum “memory sponge while studying.”

### 4.6 Review synthesis

**Malykh & Sadaie 2010 — *Drugs***  
- Places pramiracetam in **Subgroup-1** racetams (piracetam, oxiracetam, aniracetam, pramiracetam, phenylpiracetam).  
- Notes incomplete class MOA (“enigma” language is honest); mentions TBI cognitive-deficit **reports** (not meta-proof).  
- **Use:** Class framing and gap listing (**T4**). Do not convert “reportedly improved TBI deficits” into large-effect Established efficacy.

### Forum structural biases (do not launder away)

Self-selection (strong effects post more); expectancy/placebo from “laser” lore; confounded stacks (fat, caffeine, GPC, other racetams, exam weeks); dose/form chaos (powder burn, no fat, RC purity); vocabulary contagion (“laser,” “zombie,” “choline headache”); severity salience for rare mood crash; almost never blinded N-of-1. → **Phenomenology Probable; causal rates Speculative-to-Probable; never Established from forum alone. Never invent % responders.**

### Weighing

1. **Forum is the main human PD dataset for healthy users.** Clinical literature will not capture laser-vs-zombie tradeoff — it barely tested healthy young adults and did not instrument affect deeply.  
2. **Emotional blunting is the differentiator** vs piracetam (milder, bulkier) and aniracetam (more anxiolytic/positive affect reports). First-class risk in protocol design, not footnote. **Benefit and side may be the same spectrum** for some users (affective narrowing → perceived productivity) — **Speculative** but methodologically important.  
3. **Choline reports are bidirectional:** deficiency headache **and** over-choline dulling. Titration, not fixed high Alpha-GPC for all. “Always stack choline” creates many P15 casualties.  
4. **Stim/sedate split** → first-week test doses single-agent, daytime only, logged for sleep and affect — not stacked with caffeine day one.  
5. **Null responders are real.** Cost-per-responder is rational use; 1–2 week structured trial with predefined stop rules beats months of sunk cost.  
6. **Literature silence ≠ absence** for blunting, laser focus, tolerance.  
7. **Severity outliers (depression crash)** get asymmetric stop rules even if base rate is low / causality Speculative.

### 7.1 Headache / brain fog (choline-pool mismatch)

1. **Phenotype**  
   Frontal/band headache, pressure, mental dullness, “can’t push through,” sometimes neck tension. Onset hours after dose or day 1–3 of introduction/dose jump. Classic “racetam headache.” Distinct from migraine with aura for most; migraineurs may be sensitive.

2. **Mechanism**  
   - **Primary (P14 under P1):** ↑ HACU increases choline demand for ACh synthesis. If free choline / membrane PC supply lags → relative ACh synthesis stress → fog, headache, irritability.  
   - **Secondary:** rebound vascular tone; dehydration/caffeine confound; sleep debt.  
   - **Opposite error (critical):** excess Alpha-GPC/CDP → cholinergic muddiness, depression-adjacent fog (**P15 edge**). “More choline” is **not** monotonic.  
   - Mechanism of supply–demand mismatch: **Probable** practical model; **Speculative** as proven human ACh-turnover biochemistry.

### 8.2 Order of operations (engineering sequence)

1. **Baseline week:** Sleep, protein, dietary choline (eggs), no multi-racetam chaos.  
2. **Pram solo intro:** 300 mg morning with fat-containing meal × 2–3 days. Caps only.  
3. **Observe four axes:** (A) headache/fog, (B) blunting, (C) stim vs sedate, (D) sleep latency.  
4. **Titrate dose:** 300 → 600 mg total/day split BID before chasing stacks. Community ceiling often 600–1200 mg/day.  
5. **Choline last-mile:** Only if A-axis positive for **deficit**. Start Alpha-GPC ~150–300 mg **or** CDP 250 mg — not both mega-doses.  
6. **Emotion color:** If B-axis blunting at useful cognitive dose → add aniracetam or cut dose/frequency **before** adding more choline.  
7. **Arousal fine-tune:** Caffeine low-dose for sedation phenotype; remove caffeine/late doses for insomnia phenotype.  
8. **Cycle:** Default 4–5 days/week or hard study blocks to manage tolerance (7.7) and blunting (7.2).  
9. **Protection-optional layer:** Only if user demands nNOS/redox theater — dietary antioxidants ± NAC; label **Speculative**.

### 8.3 Choline excess vs deficit (central conflict)

| State | Signals | Move |
|-------|---------|------|
| **Deficit** | Pressure headache, empty fog, irritability, better after eggs/GPC | Add choline donor low dose |
| **Excess** | Heavy head, apathy, zombie, depressed-adjacent, muddy thinking | Cut GPC/CDP; keep or lower pram |
| **Sweet spot** | Clear focus, mild dryness of emotion tolerable, no headache | Freeze doses; don’t “optimize” weekly |

**Rule:** Choline is a **titration**, not a moral mandate.

### 8.4 Example assembled stacks (not prescriptions)

**A. Minimal cognitive (responder, no headache)**  
- Pramiracetam 300 mg AM + fat  
- Optional 300 mg early afternoon  
- No standing choline

**B. Deficit-headache phenotype**  
- Pram 300 mg BID with meals  
- CDP-choline 250 mg AM **or** Alpha-GPC 150–300 mg

**C. Blunting at effective dose**  
- Pram 300 mg AM only (drop PM)  
- Aniracetam 750 mg with fat AM or midday  
- Choline PRN only  
- Mandatory off days  

**D. Stim-insomnia phenotype**  
- Pram 300 mg with breakfast only

**E. Speculative redox-anxious user (optional)**

## 12. Open questions

Data that would most change this map or counters:

1. **Healthy young adult DBPC crossover** — pramiracetam vs placebo on attention, episodic memory, ecological study task, and validated affect/anhedonia scales at 300–600 mg BID × 2–4 weeks (n≥40–60 ideal).  
2. **Human target engagement** — cholinergic PET/SPECT or fMRI valuation-task under pram to test P15/P16; CSF/microdialysis HACU proxies.  
3. **Choline co-admin 2×2 RCT** — pram × choline → adjudicate headache/fog causality and optimal donor/dose.  
4. **Food-effect PK** — high-fat vs fasted crossover AUC; steady-state BID; women (Chang was all male); renal impairment cohort.  
5. **CSF or TMS proxies** of cortical excitability / NO-cGMP at oral therapeutic doses (tests P4 human relevance); ASL MRI CBF dose-ranging.  
6. **Dose–response 200–1200 mg** with co-primary focus + blunting.  
7. **Head-to-head** pram vs coluracetam vs oxiracetam vs aniracetam on blunting vs focus tradeoff.  
8. **McLean-style TBI replication** n≥50 modern outcomes — upgrade or kill injury niche.  
9. **Long-term safety** — 6–12 month controlled exposure with mood + cognitive endpoints.  
10. **PREP / AMPA** modern binding panels — kill or elevate P8/P9.  
11. **Genetics of response** — CHT1/CHRNA variants vs laser vs zombie split.  
12. **Tolerance biology** — CHT1 trafficking vs expectancy vs choline drift.

Until these exist, honesty metric is **precision about uncertainty**, not refusal to map pathways or stacks.
