## Class
Investigational oral prodrug of LL-340001; CNS-directed selective **TRβ** agonist (CNS-TR thyromimetic). Autobahn Therapeutics. CAS 2156649-32-8. **Not approved.**

## Core thesis
Deliver brain-biased TRβ agonism (bioenergetics, mitochondrial biogenesis, neuroplasticity) with less peripheral TR liability than therapeutic-dose **T3**. Parent T3 augmentation literature is the multi-source reason the MoA is pursued — not proof of drug efficacy.

## Clinical status
- Ph1 n=48: SAD 0.0075–0.15 mg; MAD 0.0028–0.0056 mg ×14 d; generally well tolerated; mood alteration n=2 at 0.0056 mg; TSH dip only at ~54× planned Ph2 top
- AMPLIFY-BD open-label n=21: HAMD-17 −16.8 W6, 75% response, 50% remission (company Jun 2026) — **Speculative** efficacy (open-label discount)
- AMPLIFY MDD placebo-controlled pending Q3 2026
- FDA Fast Track adjunctive bipolar depression (May 2026)

## Certainty ceilings
- Identity/chemistry: Established
- Ph1 safety at studied doses: Probable–Established (sponsor data)
- Drug-specific antidepressant efficacy: Speculative until R/DB/PC peer-reviewed
- T3 class psych augmentation: Probable (heterogeneous)
