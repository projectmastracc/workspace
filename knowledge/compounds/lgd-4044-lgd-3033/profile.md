## Bottom line

- **LGD-4033:** oral SARM, t½ 24–36 h, dose-dependent LBM↑ and T/SHBG/HDL↓ at 0.1–2 mg; gym doses 5–10 mg; DILI cases at high unregulated use.  
- **LGD-3303:** strong muscle/bone, prostate partial agonist plateau in rats; no human RCT; forum “dry/strong” claims Speculative.  
- Product fraud common (Van Wagoner ~52% contain labeled SARM class).
