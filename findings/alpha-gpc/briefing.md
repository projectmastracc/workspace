## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | Alpha-GPC (α-GPC); L-α-glycerylphosphorylcholine; choline alfoscerate (INN); glycerophosphocholine (GPC). Class: choline-containing phospholipid precursor / cholinergic precursor. Historical pharma brands: Gliatilin®, Delecit®. |
| **Primary use cases** | **Neuro:** cognitive support in MCI/AD/post-stroke (clinical 1200 mg); nootropic focus and racetam choline support (150–600 mg). **Performance:** acute power/force preworkout (~600 mg). **Nutrition:** highly bioavailable choline donor (~40–41% choline by weight). |
| **Bottom line** | Alpha-GPC is a deacylated phosphatidylcholine fragment that delivers free choline efficiently after oral intake, supporting acetylcholine (ACh) synthesis and membrane phospholipid salvage. Disease-population RCTs at **1200 mg/day** show modest cognitive benefit in mild–moderate AD and add-on benefit with donepezil (ASCOMALVA). Sports RCTs at **~600 mg** show small acute force signals with thin replication. Forum use clusters at **150–600 mg** for focus, racetam support, and preworkout. Practical risks: cholinergic sides (headache, GI, insomnia), a vivid but subset-frequency depression/anhedonia signal (DA–ACh balance hypothesis), and a large Korean observational **stroke association** in older *prescribed* users—confounded by indication, mechanistically bridged by TMAO, neither dismissible nor transferable as proven causation to young intermittent supplementers. Not a stimulant, not an AChE inhibitor, not a body-composition drug. |
| **Biggest upsides** | Efficient oral choline donation vs bitartrate (**Established** PK). AD symptomatic signal at 1200 mg (**Probable** disease-population). Donepezil + Alpha-GPC synergy (**Probable**, ASCOMALVA program). Small acute strength/force at ~600 mg (**Probable**, limited RCTs, low precision). Better GI than free choline salts for many (**Probable** forum + form chemistry). Racetam headache prevention (**Speculative**, literature-silent, high forum consistency). |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Headache, GI, insomnia/overstimulation (**Probable** clinical + forum). Depression/anhedonia with continuous high dose (**Speculative** forum-dominant, pathway-plausible). Bell-shaped response / brain fog at excess (**Speculative–Probable**). Stroke/CVD association in older Rx users (**Probable** association; **Speculative** causal fraction; **Unknown–Speculative** absolute risk for young intermittent users). Mild hypotension at high choline loads (**Probable** UL pharmacology). |
| **Stack role** | **Choline backbone** for racetam stacks; **acute preworkout** ergogenic at 300–600 mg elemental; **clinical precursor** at 1200 mg in dementia care (± AChE-I under clinician). Not a standalone “smart drug” with stimulant-class effect sizes in healthy young adults. Prefer lower doses + cycling if mood-sensitive; prefer CDP-choline if chronic neuroprotection narrative and lower free-choline spike preferred. |

**Label trap (read first):** Many retail products are **50% Alpha-GPC** (silica-stabilized). A “600 mg” scoop of 50% material = **~300 mg elemental Alpha-GPC** ≈ **~120–125 mg choline equivalent**. Always dose by **elemental Alpha-GPC**, not product weight. **Established** practical error.

## 2. Chemistry, Classification, Pharmacokinetics

### 2.1 Identity and chemistry

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| **IUPAC / common** | L-α-glycerylphosphorylcholine; choline alfoscerate |
| **Formula / MW** | C₈H₂₀NO₆P; ~257.2 g/mol |
| **Choline mass fraction** | ~40–41% (1000 mg elemental Alpha-GPC ≈ 400–410 mg free choline equivalent) |
| **Structure** | Choline esterified to *sn*-glycero-3-phosphate (fully deacylated PC fragment; no fatty acyl chains) |
| **Class** | Phospholipid intermediate / cholinergic precursor / nootropic + sports ergogenic claim |
| **Sources** | Enzymatic or chemical hydrolysis of soy/sunflower phosphatidylcholine; natural food GPC fraction (~16–19% of dietary choline forms in some surveys) |
| **Routes** | Oral (powder, tablet, softgel, capsule); IM pharmaceutical in some countries; IV historically in research |

**Solubility / stability:** Highly water-soluble hygroscopic solid; commercial forms often silica-adsorbed. Store dry, sealed.

**Choline-equivalent math (practical):**

| Elemental Alpha-GPC | ≈ Choline equivalent | Notes |
|---------------------|----------------------|-------|
| 150 mg | ~60–62 mg | Common racetam-support start |
| 300 mg | ~120–125 mg | Common focus / low stack dose |
| 600 mg | ~240–245 mg | Sports force literature band |
| 1200 mg | ~480–490 mg | Clinical dementia trial dose (≈ adult choline AI from this source alone) |

IOM adult choline AI: 425–550 mg/day. IOM UL: **3500 mg/day choline** (hypotension, fishy odor, GI). UL-type sides can appear below mass-UL in sensitive users via cholinergic PD, not only total choline mass.

**50% product conversion examples:**

| Product weight (50% AGPC) | Elemental Alpha-GPC | ≈ Choline |
|---------------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| 300 mg powder | ~150 mg | ~60 mg |
| 600 mg powder | ~300 mg | ~120 mg |
| 1200 mg powder | ~600 mg | ~240 mg |
| 2400 mg powder | ~1200 mg | ~480 mg |

Health Canada 2024: 1 mg AGPC ≈ 0.4 mg choline. **Established** regulatory equivalence framing.

### 2.2 Absorption, distribution, metabolism

**Oral free choline rise (human):**
- Peruca et al. 1991: 1200 mg oral → plasma free choline ~10.1 → Cmax ~13.6 μM at ~2 h; significant AUC ↑ vs placebo. **Established** (small older PK).
- Min et al. 2019 bioequivalence (1200 mg as 3×400 mg tablet vs soft capsule, healthy, fasting): Cmax ~0.37–0.38 μg/mL choline-related analyte, Tmax ~3.5–3.9 h, AUC₀–∞ ~3.3–3.4 μg·h/mL; well tolerated. **Established** formulation PK.
- Oral Tmax typically **~1–4 h** depending on formulation/assay; oral peaks **substantially lower than IM**. **Established**.

**IM (pharmaceutical context):**
- Gatti et al. 1992: Alpha-GPC 1000 mg IM → free choline Cmax 28.2 ± 3.1 μmol/L vs citicoline 1000 mg IM 14.0 ± 1.2 μmol/L (*p*<0.01); AUC₀–₄h roughly double. Normalized to choline content, difference NS — superior raw delivery mainly reflects higher choline mass fraction. Free choline t½ post-IM ~0.5–6.2 h. **Established**. Do **not** import IM effect magnitude into oral nootropic claims.

**Circulating species:** Main circulating species after oral intake is **free choline**; intact Alpha-GPC largely not detected in plasma (Abbiati 1993 rat ADME; Health Canada summary). **Probable** for humans by triangulation.

**Metabolic fate:**
1. Gut/tissue cleavage → free choline + glycerol-1-phosphate (G-1-P).
2. Choline → (a) high-affinity neuronal uptake (CHT1) → ChAT → ACh; (b) betaine (methyl donation / one-carbon); (c) Kennedy pathway reincorporation into PC.
3. G-1-P → phospholipid salvage pool after phosphorylation/acylation.

**CNS penetration:** Crosses BBB primarily as choline and related metabolites. Animal models: ↑ hippocampal/cortical ACh (Govoni, Lopez, Missale, Traini review lines). Human brain ACh direct measurement post-oral AGPC sparse. **Probable** CNS choline delivery; **Speculative** magnitude of synaptic ACh rise in healthy young adults at 150–600 mg.

**Food effect:** Sparse dedicated food-effect RCTs. Many trials fasted. Practice: with or without food; empty stomach may worsen GI. **Unknown** formal food-effect AUC; **Probable** practice benefit of food for GI.

**Bell-shaped dose-response (preclinical):** PKC translocation and scopolamine models show inverted-U patterns; excess ACh hypothesized to engage autoreceptors and desensitization (Govoni 1993; Missale 1992). Maps to forum “too much choline = fog.” **Speculative–Probable** for human phenotype; **Established** as preclinical pattern.

### 4.2 Cognitive impairment / dementia / AD

#### Moreno (De Jesus Moreno) 2003 — *Clin Ther* (primary monotherapy efficacy anchor)
- **Design:** Multicenter DB placebo RCT; mild–moderate AD; oral choline alfoscerate **1200 mg/day** × **180 days**.
- **Measured:** ADAS-Cog, MMSE, GDS, CGI.
- **Direction:** Significant improvements favoring active on cognitive and global measures.
- **Limits:** Era methods; Italian medical-product context; limited modern multi-country independent replication; **AD population only**.
- **Grade:** **Moderate**.
- **Certainty for AD-symptom improvement at 1200 mg:** **Probable**.  
- **Transfer to healthy young “IQ boost”:** **Speculative** (high indirectness — disease-state cholinergic deficit may amplify precursor benefit).

#### Parnetti et al. 1993
- Multicenter DB RCT, suspected SDAT; Alpha-GPC 1200 mg/day vs ALCAR 1500 mg/day × 6 months.
- Both arms improved some measures; Alpha-GPC sometimes favored on select endpoints; good tolerability (mild insomnia/gastralgia/restlessness rare).
- **No placebo arm** — cannot isolate absolute drug effect.
- **Grade:** Low–Moderate. **Certainty:** **Probable** tolerability; **Speculative–Probable** comparative cognitive movement.

#### Ban et al. 1991
- Open n≈817 elderly dementia/cognitive decline, 1200 mg/day × 6 months: clinical improvement reported; AEs mild (agitation, heartburn, nausea, headache, insomnia, orthostatic symptoms at low rates).
- **Grade:** Very Low efficacy / Low AE descriptive. Efficacy **Speculative**; AE class **Probable** *in that population*.

#### ASCOMALVA program (Amenta 2012, 2014; Carotenuto 2017; Traini 2020)
- RCT: **donepezil + choline alphoscerate** vs **donepezil + placebo** in AD **with cerebrovascular injury**.
- Interim ~12 mo and ~2 yr: combination improved MMSE, ADAS-cog, IADL, NPI vs donepezil alone (BADL mixed early); lower BPSD/caregiver distress.
- **Interpretation:** Precursor + AChE-I synergy (P16) — **not** pure Alpha-GPC monotherapy in healthy young adults.
- **Grade:** **Moderate** for add-on in that phenotype; Very Low–Low as evidence for healthy solo use.
- **Certainty:** **Probable** add-on benefit in studied population; **Established** direction of pharmacological synergy; **Speculative** that healthy users “need” chronic 1200 mg.
- Duong 2024-type commentary questions long-term polypharmacy confirmation needs.

#### Post-stroke / multi-infarct recovery (Sangiorgi 1994; Di Perri/Frattola/Abbati 1991 era)
- Often IM 1000 mg/day vs citicoline/oxiracetam; mental recovery scale improvements.
- Older European methods; variable rigor; route often ≠ oral supplement.
- **Certainty:** **Speculative–Probable** historical recovery signal.

#### Reviews
- Traini 2013; Kansakar 2023; Biggio 2025: Alpha-GPC (and citicoline) still positioned among choline phospholipids that often failed when plain choline/PC were tested in AD. **Probable** relative ranking within old precursor class — secondary evidence only.

### 4.4 Safety / epidemiology / CV

#### Lee et al. 2021 — *JAMA Network Open* (PMID 34817582) — fair weighing

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Design | Korean NHIS retrospective cohort, age ≥50, n≈12M total, α-GPC users n≈108,877 **prescribed** |
| Key results (matched) | Total stroke aHR **1.43** (95% CI **1.41–1.46**); ischemic ~1.34; hemorrhagic ~1.37; **dose-response** with cumulative exposure |
| Strengths | Enormous n; national claims; dose-response; both ischemic + hemorrhagic elevated; peer-reviewed major journal; public-funder epidemiology (not Italian product-company trial) |
| Critical limit | **Confounding by indication**: users typically older with cognitive/cerebrovascular **reasons for prescription** — residual confounding remains after matching. Sicker patients stay on drug longer → dose-response can track severity, not only drug. |
| Directness to young OTC | **Highly indirect** (age, indication, Rx vs supplement, chronic cumulative exposure vs intermittent 150–600 mg) |
| TMAO | Cohort does **not** measure TMAO. Choline→TMAO pathway is a **plausibility bridge**, not demonstrated mediation in Lee. |

**Certainty labels (discipline):**

| Statement | Label |
|-----------|-------|
| Rx α-GPC associated with higher stroke incidence in Korean ≥50 | **Probable** (association) |
| α-GPC causes stroke via TMAO in that cohort | **Speculative** |
| Same relative risk applies to healthy young intermittent users | **Speculative–Unknown** |
| Chronic high-dose use in older/vascular-risk patients warrants shared decision-making + CV risk-factor control | **Probable** practical stance |

**Must not say:** “Alpha-GPC has been proven to cause stroke.” / “Your 300 mg preworkout raises stroke risk 43%.”  
**Must not say:** Ignore the signal because of residual confounding (absence of proven causation ≠ proof of safety).

#### TMAO pathway (general choline literature)
- Gut flora: choline → TMA → hepatic FMO3 → TMAO; TMAO linked observationally to atherosclerosis/stroke (Wang, Tang, Zhu lines).
- Alpha-GPC is highly bioavailable choline → **hypothesized** higher TMAO risk than some bound forms; form-specific human TMAO RCTs for Alpha-GPC are sparse.
- Animal α-GPC/choline atherogenic models: **Speculative** for human causality.
- **Certainty:** **Established** that choline can feed TMAO pathway; **Speculative** quantitative extra risk of supplemental Alpha-GPC vs diet in young healthy users.

### 4.5 Sleep / short tolerability

- Ferini-Strambi 1991: 1200 mg oral × 7 days — no major sleep-parameter effect (small). Does **not** disprove evening insomnia anecdotes. **Unknown–Speculative** null.
- Canal 1991: 10 days 1200 mg — good tolerance. **Probable** short-term tolerance at clinical dose.

### 4.6 What clinical data do **not** show well

1. Large modern multi-country RCTs in **healthy young adults** for general “IQ boost.”
2. Robust GRADE-high meta-analyses for AD **disease modification**.
3. Cognitive enhancement effect sizes in eugeric young populations comparable to stimulants.
4. Adequately powered multi-site sports meta on force/power with consistent secondary outcomes (jump, hypertrophy, body comp).
5. RCT of Alpha-GPC **mood** endpoints in healthy adults (depression signal untested in trials).
6. Form-specific head-to-head TMAO AUC RCTs (Alpha-GPC vs PC vs bitartrate) with microbiome control.
7. Optimal cycling vs continuous protocols for receptor homeostasis.
8. Food-effect PK completeness for common softgel vs 50% powder.

## 6. Desired Effects — Mechanism Depth

### 6.1 Cognitive support in AD / MCI / vascular cognitive impairment

- **Phenotype:** Better ADAS-Cog/MMSE/global clinical impression vs placebo or as add-on to donepezil; functional and behavioral scores in combo trials.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 → P2 → P4/P5; P3; P16 (combo); P12/P13 speculative long-horizon.
- **Mechanism chain:** Oral Alpha-GPC → free choline + G-1-P → elevated plasma choline → neuronal uptake via CHT1 → ChAT synthesizes ACh → nAChR/mAChR signaling supports attention and memory circuits compromised in AD; glycerophosphate arm supports membrane PC salvage; with donepezil, less ACh breakdown + more substrate = higher synaptic ACh tone.
- **Clinical:** Moreno 2003; Parnetti 1993; ASCOMALVA; Ban 1991; post-stroke era trials. **Probable** in indicated populations at **1200 mg/day**.
- **Forum:** Less relevant (patients ≠ Reddit healthy users).
- **Practical:** Clinical band 1200 mg/day split morning + midday; avoid late evening. Not a DIY AD treatment substitute for standard care. **Probable** trial-aligned dosing.

### 6.2 Subjective focus / mental clarity (healthy / nootropic users)

- **Phenotype:** Mild “on,” cleaner attention, less mental fatigue — without classic caffeine jitters for many.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1 → P2 → P4/P5/P19; excess → P6.
- **Mechanism chain:** Modest ACh substrate increase may facilitate cortical nAChR (α4β2/α7) tone and muscarinic cognition pathways; α7 and α4β2 modulate DA/NE in attention networks (**Speculative** magnitude from oral GPC in healthy brains). Effect larger under demand (P2b) than at idle replete baseline.
- **Clinical:** Not well demonstrated in modern healthy-young RCTs. **Speculative–Probable** mild effect at best.
- **Forum:** Moderate–high positive reports at 300–600 mg morning. Confounds common.
- **Practical:** 300–600 mg elemental morning; start 150–300 mg if sensitive. Check 50% labels. **Speculative** efficacy.

### 6.3 Racetam / noopept “choline support” (headache prevention, synergy)

- **Phenotype:** Reduced “racetam headache”; subjective synergy with piracetam, oxiracetam, aniracetam, phenylpiracetam, noopept.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1–P2, P17 (substrate for hypothesized ↑ ACh demand under racetam use); P6 if overdone.
- **Mechanism chain:** Racetams hypothesized to increase ACh utilization or membrane dynamics → relative choline demand → headache/fog when depleted; Alpha-GPC repletes free choline efficiently. **Literature-silent as controlled RCT**; high forum consistency.
- **Clinical:** Silent. **Speculative**.
- **Forum:** High consistency classic lore (150–300 mg with racetam).
- **Practical:** 150–300 mg elemental with racetam dose; do not automatically escalate to 1200 mg. If headache persists, decide **deficit vs excess** branch (too little vs too much choline) before escalating — may need to cut racetam, not add more GPC. **Speculative**.

### 6.4 Acute power / force (preworkout)

- **Phenotype:** Small increase in peak force (bench, IMTP); mind-muscle connection reports.
- **Pathway IDs:** P8 (GH acute); P9 (NMJ/recruitment hyp.); P4 (arousal); P9b (attention).
- **Mechanism chain:** (1) Acute GH facilitation via cholinergic tone on GHRH/somatotroph axis — lab-plausible, training-adaptation unproven. (2) Hypothesized NMJ ACh availability / central drive improving voluntary force expression — under-specified (healthy NMJ rarely substrate-limited). (3) Mild central arousal via nAChR.
- **Clinical:** Bellar 2015; Ziegenfuss 2008 abstract; Marcus 2017 weaker at ≤500 mg. **Probable** small force effect at **~600 mg elemental**.
- **Forum:** Moderate; aligns with data direction.
- **Practical:** 300–600 mg elemental **30–60 min pre**; prefer **600 mg elemental** if chasing force endpoint (if product is 50%, that is ~1200 mg product weight). Not a substitute for progressive overload/creatine. **Probable** at 600 mg for force; **Speculative** for jump/hypertrophy.

### 6.5 Acute GH pulse

- **Phenotype:** Lab GH AUC/peak increase; not proven lean mass from Alpha-GPC alone.
- **Pathway IDs:** P8.
- **Mechanism chain:** Cholinergic stimulation facilitates GH release (classic neuroendocrinology); Alpha-GPC may amplify pulse acutely.
- **Clinical:** Ziegenfuss-type abstract and related acute literature. **Probable** acute lab; **Speculative** body-comp relevance. Do not propagate secondary “44-fold” style claims without primary-table verification.
- **Forum:** Over-claimed as “GH booster.”
- **Practical:** Do not stack expectations like a GHRP/GHRH secretagogue. **Speculative** for physique outcomes.

### 6.6 Membrane / neuroprotective narrative (post-ischemia, chronic brain health)

- **Phenotype:** Recovery of cognitive scores post-stroke; long-term “brain membrane support” marketing.
- **Pathway IDs:** P3, P1, P20; P13 speculative.
- **Mechanism chain:** G-1-P + choline feed PC resynthesis → membrane repair after ischemic injury; ACh support for network recovery.
- **Clinical:** 1990s European post-stroke work; modern standards limited. **Speculative–Probable** older evidence.
- **Forum:** CDP-choline often preferred for this narrative.
- **Practical:** Disease-context dosing 1000–1200 mg (sometimes IM historically). Healthy-user “neuroprotection” claim **Speculative**.

### 6.7 Choline nutrition (meeting AI)

- **Phenotype:** Contribute to choline AI; support methyl metabolism via betaine path.
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P11, P3b.
- **Clinical/regulatory:** Health Canada recognizes AGPC as choline source (0.4 mg choline per 1 mg AGPC). **Established** as choline donor math.
- **Practical:** Efficient but expensive vs eggs/bitartrate for pure nutrition; use when CNS delivery / low GI preferred. Pregnancy: general choline importance is **Established** nutritionally; Alpha-GPC-specific pregnancy RCTs not the basis of care — food-first and obstetric guidance. **Probable** as choline source; **Unknown** Alpha-GPC-specific perinatal outcomes.

### 8.1 Order of operations (engineering sequence)

1. **Define use-case band** (racetam 150–300 / focus 300–600 / preworkout 300–600 / clinical 1200 medical).
2. **Audit total cholinergic load** before adding — diet choline, other caps, huperzine, AChE-I, racetams, nicotine (P2/P16/P17 pile-up prevention).
3. **Confirm elemental dose** from label % (50% trap).
4. **Timing skeleton** — default AM or preworkout; never default evening (P15); with food if GI history.
5. **Titrate lowest effective** — start 150–300 mg even if goal is 600 mg force; watch 3–7 days for headache, mood, sleep, orthostasis.
6. **Add performance co-stack only after AGPC alone tolerated** — caffeine/theanine common; creatine/beta-alanine orthogonal.
7. **Racetam pairing** — 150–300 mg concurrent or AM; headache → deficit vs excess branch before escalating.
8. **Chronic risk layer (parallel)** — if use > few weeks continuous or age/risk ↑: minimize cumulative mg·days, prefer intermittent, CV factor control, consider PC baseline. Do **not** treat fish oil + baby aspirin as license for unrestricted chronic 1200 mg.
9. **Mood/fog tripwire → stop path** — anhedonia or paradoxical fog: cut/stop first (P6/P7), do **not** add more choline.
10. **Exit** — simple stop usually sufficient (short free-choline half-life); residual mood: sleep, exercise, rule out primary depression.

## 12. Open Questions

1. **Does supplemental (non-Rx, young healthy) Alpha-GPC raise stroke risk?** Needs age-stratified prospective data with indication control and cumulative dose — would most change §7.7 weighting.
2. **Form-specific human TMAO AUC:** Alpha-GPC vs PC vs bitartrate vs mixed diet under standardized microbiome profiling.
3. **Adequately powered multi-site sports RCT/meta** on force, power, jump, and training adaptations at 300 vs 600 vs 1200 mg elemental.
4. **RCT mood endpoints** in healthy adults (depression/anhedonia signal confirmation or refutation; dose-response).
5. **Optimal cycling vs continuous** for nAChR/mAChR homeostasis and subjective tolerance.
6. **Healthy-young cognition** modern multi-domain RCT with biomarkers (choline, optional imaging).
7. **Food-effect PK** on Cmax/AUC for common softgel vs 50% powder.
8. **Genetic modifiers:** PEMT, FMO3, CHRNA variants predicting responders vs mood-crashers.
9. **Long-term ASCOMALVA-class replication** outside original networks for polypharmacy value.
10. **Whether counters for TMAO** (fiber, specific polyphenols) change outcomes in Alpha-GPC users specifically.
