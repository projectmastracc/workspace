# Example: Literature-silent + strong anecdote — Cerebrolysin skin / appearance claims (calibration)

**Why this is a quality target:** Explicit test of the rule **literature silence ≠ “effect does not exist.”** Patterned on community reports of skin quality / “anti-ageing appearance” with cerebrolysin while controlled aesthetic trials are largely absent.

> This is a **structural golden example**, not a full live monograph. Full runs should expand all 15 sections.

---

## Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound / class** | Cerebrolysin — porcine brain-derived peptide mixture (CNS peptide / neuro) |
| **Claim under review** | Improves skin quality / youthful appearance |
| **Overall certainty (claim)** | **Speculative** (anecdote-forward; literature largely silent on dermatologic endpoints) |
| **Verdict** | Controlled aesthetic evidence is largely absent; consistent multi-source user reports of skin/appearance changes exist and should be weighed with caution — not denied, not treated as proven. |
| **Key practical note** | **No evidence-graded dermatologic protocol** can be recommended (**Unknown** for skin indication). Neuro use is a separate evidence question. |
| **Top caveats** | Non-sterile injectable risks; regulatory status varies; batch/source quality; confounded by concurrent stacks. |

---

## 5. Human Evidence (claim-specific)

Controlled human trials of cerebrolysin focus on stroke, TBI, dementia-related cognition — **not** validated dermatologic or cosmetic endpoints in high-quality literature sampled for this claim (**literature-silent** for skin youth as a primary outcome).

State clearly: absence of trials is **not** a positive demonstration of no effect.

## 7. Subjective / Experiential Profile

| Theme | Consistency | Concordance |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Improved skin texture / “glow” / less aged appearance during or after courses | Moderate–high in niche peptide/nootropic communities | **literature-silent** |
| Concurrent cognitive / mood reports | Variable | separate claim; do not conflate |
| Attribution confounded by GH peptides, retinoids, diet, training | Common | lowers causal confidence |

**Weighing (mandatory pattern):**

> Controlled literature is largely **silent** on cerebrolysin for skin quality or anti-ageing appearance. Consistent multi-source forum and log reports describe improvements in skin texture/appearance during courses for some users (**Speculative**; concordance: **literature-silent**). These reports are worth noting with appropriate caution (placebo, concurrent compounds, selection bias, non-blinded self-report). They do **not** establish efficacy and do **not** support a dermatologic dosing protocol. Conversely, it is incorrect to assert that skin effects “do not exist” solely because RCTs for this endpoint are missing.

## 8. Practical Guidance

| Topic | Guidance | Certainty |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Skin / anti-ageing appearance protocol | **Do not recommend** a protocol | **Unknown** |
| Speculative note only | Some users report skin changes; treat as unverified experiential signal | **Speculative** |
| Injectable peptide harm reduction | Sterility, sourcing, medical contraindications remain relevant if used for any reason | **Probable** (general injectable risk) |

## 9. Safety (abbrev.)

Allergic potential, source/quality risk, injection safety — discuss regardless of skin claim. Regulatory status is not “supplement aisle” in many jurisdictions.

## 15. FAQ

| Claim | Verdict | Certainty |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| “Cerebrolysin has no skin effects because there are no papers” | Overreach — silence ≠ disproof | **Unknown** (effect); claim of non-existence **not Established** |
| “Forums prove anti-ageing efficacy” | Overreach — anecdote ceiling | **Speculative** at best |

### Calibration note for reviewers

**FAIL** if briefing says the effect does not exist solely due to missing papers.  
**FAIL** if briefing recommends a skin protocol as Probable/Established from forums alone.  
**PASS** if both silence and consistent anecdotes are stated with Speculative ceiling and concordance `literature-silent`.
