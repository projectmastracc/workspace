# Masteron (Drostanolone) — Full Compound Profile

**Research ID:** f6b3d435  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Class:** Synthetic DHT-derived anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS); 2α-methyl-dihydrotestosterone esters  
**Status:** Historically marketed for advanced breast cancer (Drolban, Masteril, Masteron); largely withdrawn from legitimate markets; underground performance use continues; US Schedule III / similar controlled status in many jurisdictions; WADA prohibited  

---

## 1. Executive Card

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Compound** | **Drostanolone** (INN; also dromostanolone): 2α-methyl-5α-androstan-17β-ol-3-one (2α-methyl-DHT). Clinical ester: **drostanolone propionate** (Masteron / Masteril / Drolban). Underground: propionate and **enanthate**. Class: non-aromatizing, non-17α-alkylated injectable DHT derivative. |
| **Primary use cases** | **Historical medical:** palliative androgen therapy for advanced androgen-responsive breast cancer in postmenopausal women. **Performance:** contest-prep / cutting “hardness,” dry look, mild anabolic support stacked under a testosterone (or other) base; aesthetic “polish” rather than mass agent. Not a nootropic. |
| **Bottom line** | Masteron is a **moderate, DHT-class AR agonist** engineered so it resists 3α-HSD inactivation in muscle better than plain DHT, giving a usable anabolic:androgenic animal ratio (~1:3–1:4 androgenic:anabolic in classic rodent tables — i.e., relatively more anabolic than raw DHT). It **does not aromatize**, does **not** reduce further via 5α-reductase, and is **not** 17α-alkylated (hepatotoxicity risk low vs orals). Recreational value is almost entirely **phenotype on already lean tissue** (hardness, grain, reduced “watery” look) plus mild tissue accretion — not a bulking workhorse. Human performance RCTs are **absent**. Medical evidence is 1960s–70s breast-cancer series with objective regression in a minority of women and clear virilization liability. Biggest practical liabilities: **androgenic skin/hair** (acne, MPB acceleration if predisposed), **class CV lipid/BP/Hct effects**, full **HPTA suppression**, and the fact that “anti-estrogen” reputation is **not** AI-class pharmacology for gym gyno prevention. |
| **Biggest upsides** | Non-aromatizing dry look (**Established** chemistry; **Probable** aesthetic phenotype); modest anabolism with relatively “clean” liver profile vs 17α-alkyl orals (**Probable**); historical antitumor utility as androgen therapy (**Established** historical use, superseded clinically); low water retention / no classic estrogenic bloating (**Established** non-aromatization). |
| **Biggest downsides / sides** | Androgenic alopecia & acne if genetically susceptible (**Probable**); HPTA shutdown (**Established** class); atherogenic lipid shift & BP/Hct class effects (**Probable** as AAS); weak pure-mass ROI vs cost/pins (**Forum Probable**); virilization in women (**Established** medical use); underground product quality risk (**Probable**). |
| **Stack role** | **Finishing / hardening adjunct** under a testosterone base (or other primary anabolic), typically last 6–10 weeks of a cut or prep. Poor standalone mass agent. Not a substitute for diet-driven leanness. |

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure & classification

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Parent | Dihydrotestosterone (5α-androstan-17β-ol-3-one) |
| Key modification | **2α-methyl** group — reduces susceptibility to 3α-HSD in skeletal muscle (plain DHT is rapidly inactivated there → poor myotrophic effect) |
| Esters | **Propionate** (historical pharmaceutical; short); **enanthate** (UG common; longer) |
| Formula (propionate) | C₂₃H₃₆O₃; MW ≈ 360.5 g/mol |
| Aromatization | **None** (already 5α-reduced; no Δ4 substrate for aromatase) |
| 5α-reductase | Not a substrate (already 5α-reduced); finasteride does **not** convert it to a weaker metabolite the way it affects nandrolone→DHN |
| 17α-alkylation | **No** → oral bioavailability near zero; injectable only; low classic alkyl hepatotoxicity |
| Related molecules | Methasterone (17α-methyl-drostanolone, oral “Superdrol” — different toxicity); oxymetholone (2-hydroxymethylene-17α-methyl); mesterolone (1α-methyl-DHT, Proviron — high SHBG binder, weak anabolic) |

### Pharmacokinetics

| Parameter | Propionate | Enanthate (UG) |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| Route | IM oil only (oral BA ~0–2%) | IM oil |
| Release | Short ester; multi-day depot | Longer ester; weekly-ish |
| Reported t½ (propionate IM) | ~**2 days** (literature/summary PK; ester-limited) | Longer (class analogy to other enanthates; human drostanolone-E PK sparse) |
| Active form | Drostanolone after ester cleavage by esterases | Same |
| Metabolism | Hepatic phase I/II class AAS pathways; urine metabolites for anti-doping |
| CNS penetration | Androgens cross BBB; specific Masteron CNS PK **Unknown** |
| Food | N/A (injectable) |

**Certainty:** Chemistry **Established**. Propionate half-life ~2 d **Probable** (secondary sources / AAS PK literature; sparse modern PK studies). Enanthate frequency guidance is **Speculative** community practice.

### Historical medical dosing (context only)

Breast-cancer templates listed drostanolone propionate **100 mg IM 1–3×/week** (order of ~100–300+ mg/week), with virilization still common at those exposures. Performance doses often overlap or exceed this range.

## 4. Clinical evidence (full evaluation)

### 4.1 Medical — advanced breast cancer (primary human dataset)

| Source class | Finding | Limits | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|--------|-----------|
| Cooperative Breast Cancer Group / 1960s series (Thomas, Goldenberg, others) | Objective responses reported with 2α-methyl-DHT propionate in advanced breast cancer; roughly in the ballpark of other androgen therapies of the era | Pre-modern staging, pre-tamoxifen standard of care; heterogeneous definition of “response” | Historical **Established** that androgen therapy can induce remissions in a subset |
| Helman et al. era comparative reports | One series: drostanolone overall response ~**30.6%** vs ~25.5% other hormone modalities in their grouping (oophorectomy/nandrolone/estrogens comparisons reported nearby) | Small groups, era methods | **Probable** historical efficacy order-of-magnitude |
| Drug labeling / monographs | Palliation of androgen-responsive recurrent mammary cancer in selected postmenopausal windows | Obsolete vs SERMs, AIs, CDK4/6, ADCs | **Established** former indication |
| Virilization | Acne, hirsutism, voice change, clitoromegaly at medical doses (e.g. high-frequency 100 mg injections) | Expected AR agonism in women | **Established** |

**Mechanism of antitumor effect (modern framing):** Not primarily “aromatase inhibition like anastrozole.” More coherent models: **AR agonism in breast** altering growth programs; **AR–ERα crosstalk** (AR can antagonize certain ERα transcriptional programs in ER+ models); reduction of estrogen-driven signaling environment. Older claims that Masteron is a potent aromatase inhibitor are **overstated / Speculative** for gym use and poorly supported relative to true AIs.

**What clinical data do not show:** Superiority to modern endocrine therapy; performance enhancement in athletes; “safe” long-term cosmetic use in men; Masteron-only lipid/BP isolation RCTs.

### 4.2 Performance / body composition

| Evidence | Finding | Certainty |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Human RCTs of drostanolone for muscle/strength | **None** modern high-quality | — |
| Class AAS RCTs (testosterone dose-response, Bhasin etc.) | Supraphysiologic AR agonism builds LBM/strength | **Established** class; Masteron contribution **Speculative** magnitude |
| Observational AAS cohorts (HAARLEM etc.) | Mixed compounds; Masteron not isolated | Class CV **Established**–**Probable** |

### 4.3 Safety labs (class-extrapolated)

Bond 2022 / HAARLEM-type data apply to **injectable AAS generally**: BP ↑, HDL ↓, LDL/ApoB ↑, Hct ↑, LV mass ↑ on cycle — often partially reversible after short cycles; **lifetime exposure** associates with plaque/strain. Masteron as a non-oral injectable is expected to be **less hepatotoxic** than stanozolol/oxandrolone, but **not CV-neutral**.

### 4.4 Binding / preclinical

- AR agonist (definition).  
- Animal androgenic:anabolic ratios historically ~**1:3–1:4** (androgenic:anabolic) for drostanolone — better muscle retention of activity than plain DHT.  
- Saartok 1984: detailed AR/SHBG RBAs for many AAS; **mesterolone** dominates SHBG binding — do not conflate Masteron with Proviron’s SHBG story without specific RBA.

**Certainty for “Masteron raises free T by crushing SHBG like Proviron”:** **Speculative** / often overstated in forums.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Muscle hardness, dryness, visual “quality”

1. **Phenotype:** Tighter skin-over-muscle look, less subcutaneous “blur,” improved separation when lean.  
2. **Pathways:** P1 (AR myocyte), P3/P16 (no E2 water), mild P18.  
3. **Chain:** AR-driven contractile protein maintenance + absence of aromatization water + possible glycogen/skin changes (**Speculative**) → optical hardness.  
4. **Clinical:** No RCTs.  
5. **Forum:** Core use-case; strongest when BF already low.  
6. **Practice:** 300–500 mg/wk typical male performance; 6–10 weeks; **Certainty: Speculative** efficacy, **high** consensus.  
7. **Limit:** Will not create hardness through fat; diet and peak week still dominate.

### 6.2 Mild lean mass / strength support

1. **Phenotype:** Modest LBM retention or slow gain; strength density more than scale weight.  
2. **Pathways:** P1, P2.  
3. **Clinical:** Extrapolated from class AR agonism (**Probable** direction, **Unknown** effect size for Masteron-alone).  
4. **Forum:** “Not a bulker.”  
5. **Certainty:** **Probable** mild anabolism; **Speculative** magnitude vs equal-dollar test or primo.

### 6.3 “Anti-estrogenic” / gyno risk reduction (performance claim)

1. **Phenotype users want:** Less bloating, less gyno flare on cycle.  
2. **Real chain:** Masteron adds **non-aromatizing androgen** → raises androgen tone without adding E2. It does **not** reliably block aromatase like anastrozole or occupy ER like tamoxifen.  
3. **Breast cancer history** proves tissue anti-estrogenic *context* in women with tumors — not that 400 mg/wk Masteron is a male gyno protocol.  
4. **Certainty:** Dry look **Probable**; AI replacement **Speculative** and often **false security**. High-test cycles still need E2 management by labs.

### 6.4 Libido (variable)

- DHT-class androgens often support libido via AR (**Probable** class).  
- If Masteron is run **without** adequate systemic T/E2, some report libido drop (low E2 or suppression).  
- **Certainty:** **Speculative** individual.

## 7. Side effects — mechanism depth + counters

### 7.1 Androgenic alopecia (male-pattern hair loss)

1. **Phenotype:** Accelerated temple/crown thinning in genetically susceptible men; can be rapid on DHT-derivatives.  
2. **Mechanism:** P11 — scalp follicle AR agonism → miniaturization cycle (same axis as DHT). 2α-methyl does not remove androgenic scalp activity. Finasteride **does not** metabolically “disarm” Masteron the way it reduces T→DHT (Masteron already DHT-backbone).  
3. **Pathway → counter**

| Pathway node | Counter | How | Clinical | Forum | Practice | Certainty |
|--------------|---------|-----|----------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Scalp AR | Dose minimization / avoid if aggressive MPB history | Less ligand | — | Strong | Best prevention | **Probable** |
| Follicle miniaturization | Topical minoxidil 5% | Prolongs anagen | **Established** MPB | Common | Continuous use | **Established** for MPB |
| DHT path (limited for Masteron) | Finasteride/dutasteride | Blocks T→DHT only | **Established** for T-driven MPB | Mixed on Masteron | May help residual T-DHT from test base, not Masteron itself | **Speculative** for Masteron-driven loss |
| Cosmetic | Topical anti-androgens (e.g. experimental) | Local AR | Mixed | Niche | — | **Speculative** |
| Damage done | Accept or stop androgen | — | — | — | Early shedding decision window | **Probable** |

### 7.2 Acne / oily skin

1. **Phenotype:** Face/back/chest acne; oiliness.  
2. **Mechanism:** P12 sebum AR.  
3. **Counters:** Hygiene; benzoyl peroxide / topical retinoid (**Established** acne care); dose cut; short isotretinoin only severe under derm (**Probable** efficacy, real sides).  
4. **Certainty:** Side **Probable**; standard acne care **Established**.

### 7.3 HPTA suppression / hypogonadism risk

1. **Phenotype:** Low LH/FSH/endo-T on drug; post-cycle crash if no plan.  
2. **Mechanism:** P5.  
3. **Counters:** Always run **testosterone base** for male performance cycles (**Probable** practice); PCT with SERM ± hCG if coming off (**Speculative** protocol details; recovery individual); TRT if ASIH persists. Fertility: sperm banking / hCG strategies clinician-guided.  
4. **Certainty:** Suppression **Established**.

### 7.4 Dyslipidemia (↓HDL, ↑LDL/ApoB)

1. **Phenotype:** Crushed HDL on labs; ApoB creep.  
2. **Mechanism:** P7 hepatic lipase / class AAS. Injectables &lt; orals but not free pass.  
3. **Counters**

| Node | Counter | Certainty |
|------|---------|-----------|
| ApoB/LDL | Omega-3 2–3 g EPA+DHA; citrus bergamot; berberine; **statin/ezetimibe** if indicated | Omega-3 **Probable** TG; statin **Established** ApoB |
| HDL | Do not chase with niacin for outcomes | Bond-class: niacin non-benefit **Established** |
| Duration | Limit blast length; recheck off-cycle | **Probable** |

### 7.5 Blood pressure / erythrocytosis / CV load

1. **Phenotype:** Higher home BP; high Hct; long-term plaque risk is **career AAS**, not Masteron-unique.  
2. **Mechanism:** P8–P10 class.  
3. **Counters:** Home BP log + correct cuff; telmisartan or other ARB if elevated (**Probable**); nebivolol if HR/BP residual; phlebotomy if Hct high (**Probable**); zone-2 cardio; no smoking; don’t stack unnecessary orals.  
4. **Certainty:** Class CV harm **Probable**–**Established**; Masteron-specific magnitude **Unknown**.

### 7.6 Prostate / LUTS

1. **Mechanism:** P13 AR.  
2. **Counters:** Age-appropriate PSA/monitoring; avoid if known prostate cancer; α-blockers for LUTS clinician-guided.  
3. **Certainty:** Risk **Speculative**–**Probable** with dose/age; not proven Masteron &gt; other androgens.

### 7.7 Low estrogen symptoms (when stacked wrong)

1. **Phenotype:** Joint pain, low mood, ED, dry skin — especially Masteron + high AI + low BF.  
2. **Mechanism:** P3 + aggressive AI → E2 floor.  
3. **Counters:** **Labs for E2**; reduce/stop AI; ensure adequate test base aromatization; joint care. Masteron is not the AI — don’t double-suppress estrogen.  
4. **Certainty:** **Probable** when E2 low.

### 7.8 Injection-site / oil reactions

1. **Phenotype:** PIP with propionate more than long esters (volume/frequency).  
2. **Counters:** Rotate sites, warm oil, slow inject, sterile technique; switch ester.  
3. **Certainty:** **Probable**.

### 7.9 Virilization (female users)

1. **Phenotype:** Voice deepening (may be irreversible), hirsutism, clitoromegaly, cycle disruption.  
2. **Mechanism:** P1 systemic AR. Medical doses already virilizing.  
3. **Counters:** Avoid for aesthetic use if voice risk unacceptable; immediate cessation at first voice change does not always reverse.  
4. **Certainty:** **Established** medical experience.

### 7.10 Hepatotoxicity

1. **Phenotype:** Usually minimal for injectable non-alkylated.  
2. **Mechanism:** Not 17α-alkyl.  
3. **Counters:** Avoid stacking hepatotoxic orals/alcohol; interpret AST/ALT with training (use GGT).  
4. **Certainty:** Low hepatotox **Probable** relative to orals.

### 7.11 Mood / sleep

1. **Phenotype:** Usually milder than tren/19-nors; some irritability or insomnia at high dose.  
2. **Mechanism:** P17 **Speculative**.  
3. **Counters:** Sleep hygiene, dose sanity, avoid stim stacking.  
4. **Certainty:** **Speculative**.

| Domain | What | When |

### Contraindications / high-caution

- Prostate cancer or unexplained high PSA  
- Pregnancy (androgen)  
- Uncontrolled HTN, unstable CAD, high baseline Hct polycythemia  
- Aggressive androgenetic alopecia if hair priority #1

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
