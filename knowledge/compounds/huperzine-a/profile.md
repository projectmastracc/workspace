# Huperzine A — Knowledge Profile

**Slug:** huperzine-a  
**Class:** Reversible competitive AChE inhibitor (Lycopodium alkaloid from *Huperzia serrata*); weak NMDA antagonist at μM; multi-pathway neuroprotectant (mostly preclinical).  
**Lenses:** Neuropharmacology primary; supplement secondary; pre-workout focus tertiary.

## Bottom line
Potent, highly AChE-selective (~900× vs BuChE), BBB-penetrant alkaloid. β half-life model-dependent (~5–12 h). Modest AD cognitive signals (China trials; Rafii 2011: 200 μg bid primary fail, 400 μg bid secondary ADAS-Cog +2.27 at 11 wk). Healthy enhancement weak/null (Morasch 2015). Strongest healthy phenomenology: vivid/lucid dreams. Classic cholinergic sides. Purified μg dosing + cycling culture.

## Key PK / potency
- IC50 AChE ~82 nM; Ki ~24.9 nM
- Oral 0.4 mg: Tmax ~58 min, Cmax ~2.59 ng/mL, β t½ ~12 h (Li 2007); alternate model ~4.8 h (Qian 1995)
- NMDA MK-801 IC50 ~65–82 μM — unlikely primary at supplement plasma

## Practical (healthy exploration)
- Start 50 μg morning; typical 50–200 μg/day; cycle (e.g. 3–5 days/week)
- Avoid other AChEIs, pregnancy, seizure/bradycardia/asthma/ulcer/urinary obstruction without medical context
- Do not auto-stack high Alpha-GPC
