# L-DOPA / Mucuna pruriens — Knowledge Profile

**Slug:** l-dopa-mucuna-pruriens  
**Source briefing:** findings/l-dopa-mucuna-pruriens/briefing.md  
**Updated:** 2026-07-31

## One-liner
Oral L-DOPA precursor (pharma or *Mucuna* seed); central DA after AADC; real acute effects, unreliable OTC labeling, poor fit for daily chronic nootropic use.

## Key pathways
- LAT1 absorption/BBB; peripheral vs central AADC; COMT/MAO
- D1/D2 motor + reward; TIDA → ↓PRL → HPG support
- Homeostatic downregulation (tolerance/crash)
- Mucuna matrix/THIQ effects hypothesized vs pure LD

## Evidence anchors
- Katzenschlager 2004; Cilia 2017/2018 PD
- Shukla/Ahmad fertility open 5 g/day powder
- Soumyanath 2018; Cohen 2022 product HPLC
- RIVM 2024 caution

## Practical ceiling
- Dose by **mg L-DOPA**, not mg Mucuna
- Intermittent use preferred for healthy users
- Never stack with Rx LD/MAOIs unsupervised
