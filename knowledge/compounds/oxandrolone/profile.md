**Research ID:** anavar6a  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Class:** Oral 17α-alkylated DHT-derivative AAS (2-oxa-DHT)  
**Status:** Historically FDA-approved (Oxandrin) as adjunctive anabolic for weight restoration / corticosteroid catabolism / osteoporotic bone pain; US NDA withdrawn 2023 for safety/effectiveness; Schedule III; WADA prohibited; widespread underground performance use

## 2. Chemistry, classification, PK

### Structure and receptor pharmacology

- **IUPAC / structure:** 17β-hydroxy-17α-methyl-2-oxa-5α-androstan-3-one. **2-oxa** substitution in the A-ring (lactone/oxa analog of DHT) + **17α-methyl** for oral resistance to first-pass 17β-HSD inactivation. **Established.**
- **Class:** Synthetic androgen / anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS); DHT derivative already 5α-reduced.
- **AR:** Full agonist at androgen receptor. **Established.**
- **Not a substrate for aromatase** — no conversion to estradiol. **Established** structural. Explains low estrogenic water / classic E2 gyno risk *from this ligand*. Does **not** mean zero gyno risk in all contexts (endogenous axis, stacks, mislabel — see §7.11).
- **Not a substrate for 5α-reductase** — already 5α-reduced DHT scaffold; no local amplification to a more potent skin/scalp metabolite via 5αR. **Established** enzymology. Does **not** mean non-androgenic: intrinsic AR activity still drives sebum, AGA-susceptible hair loss, and virilization. Finasteride/dutasteride **cannot** neutralize oxandrolone itself. **Established.**
- **Progestogenic activity:** Negligible / none reported clinically. **Probable** (contrast 19-nors).
- **2-oxa / resistance to 3α-HSD:** Class explanation for retained anabolic activity in muscle despite DHT-like backbone (plain DHT is rapidly inactivated in muscle by 3α-HSD; 2-oxa structure impairs that inactivation). **Probable** chemical rationale; quantitative human muscle 3α-HSD resistance **Speculative.**
- **Anabolic:androgenic ratio (animal assays):** Often cited ~**3–13:1** vs testosterone ~1:1 (rodent levator ani vs ventral prostate). **Probable** directional selectivity; **Speculative** quantitative translation to human performance doses.
- **SHBG:** Clinical use **suppresses hepatic SHBG production** (Grunfeld; T+oxandrolone case patterns). Binding tables often list oxandrolone RBA ~0 — primary mechanism is **↓ synthesis**, not competitive displacement. **Established** direction of SHBG↓; mechanism **Probable.**

### Pharmacokinetics

| Parameter | Value | Certainty |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Route | Oral tablets (2.5 mg, 10 mg historical strengths) | **Established** |
| Bioavailability (F) | ~**97%** | **Established** |
| Tmax | ~1 h | **Established** |
| Protein binding | ~**94–97%** | **Established** |
| Distribution t½ | ~0.5 h (biphasic) | **Established** label-class |
| Elimination t½ | **Adults ~9.4–10.4 h** (label younger volunteers mean **10.4 h**); **elderly 13.3 h** | **Established** |
| Metabolism | Partial sulfation → 17-epioxandrolone; 16α/16β-hydroxy metabolites; lactone hydrolysis product; **notable renal emphasis** vs heavily hepatic AAS | **Established** metabolite map |
| Excretion | Urine major; **~28% unchanged** (some reports ~36% free oxandrolone within 72 h + ~8% 17-epi); feces ~3% | **Established–Probable** |
| Dose–plasma vs effect | Label: correlation between plasma level and therapeutic effect **not defined** | **Unknown** for gym endpoints |

**Practical PK notes**

- Short t½ → **split dosing** (BID) for steadier levels is **Probable** PK logic; once-daily still common in community practice.
- Elderly: longer t½ + greater sensitivity to fluid retention and transaminase rise → label preferred lower geriatric dose (**5 mg BID**).
- Renal-predominant clearance does **not** eliminate hepatic first-pass / 17α-alkyl risk; oral alkylation still hits liver sinusoids and lipid synthesis machinery (hepatic lipase pathway).
- Detection: long urinary metabolite window relative to short clinical t½ (anti-doping relevance).

### Legal / product status

- US: NDA 013718 (Oxandrin) **approval withdrawn June 28, 2023**; determination withdrawn for **safety or effectiveness** (Federal Register Sep 2023). 1984 Endocrinologic and Metabolic Drugs Advisory Committee had unanimously found **no evidence of efficacy** for original marketed indications; labeling carried peliosis, hepatic tumors, lipid/atherosclerosis, and other class warnings. **Established** regulatory fact. Does **not** erase positive RCTs in burns/HIV/body composition — it reflects labeled-indication and modern benefit–risk bar.
- Schedule III controlled substance (US); underground labs dominate current performance supply → **identity/dose uncertainty** is structural (**Probable** market hazard; **Unknown** for any given tablet without analytics).

## 4. Clinical evidence — full evaluation

### Stratification overview

| Tier | Oxandrolone-specific content |
|------|------------------------------|
| Regulatory / label (Tier A) | Oxandrin FDA PI (doses, PK, warfarin, boxed hepatic/lipid warnings); 2023 withdrawal for safety/effectiveness |
| Systematic reviews / metas | Burns (Kopel 2022; Real 2014; multi-LOS/healing metas); Turner Cochrane Mohamed 2019; de Mello Gindri 2025 multi-condition SR (abstract-level this run) |
| Landmark RCTs | Demling & Orgill 2000 burns; Grunfeld 2006 HIV dose-ranging; Schroeder 2004 older men durability; Mavros 2015 older women + PRT; Porro 2012 pediatric burns 5-yr |
| Mechanistic human | Sheffield-Moore 1999 FSR/AR mRNA (young men) |
| Observational performance-adjacent | Greboggy 2024 RTP oxandrolone lipid/endocrine time course |
| Preclinical / class | A:A ratios; 17α-alkyl hepatology; oral AAS lipid literature (Hartgens) |

**Design note:** Oxandrolone has **multiple human RCTs** in medical populations and short-term healthy men — denser clinical spine than most underground AAS. Performance-dose RCTs in trained athletes remain sparse; forum + medical transfer + Greboggy fill that gap with bias.

**Translation rule (methods):** Effect sizes from burn/HIV are **upper-bound biased** for healthy recomp. Older-adult 20 mg data (Schroeder) are the **closest clinical prior** for male performance dosing — still age-mismatched. Acute FSR **+44%** is mechanism, not a weekly hypertrophy guarantee.

### Landmark studies — measures, effects, limits

**FDA Oxandrin label (Savient; 2005 labeling package)** — **Established** for PK/interactions/warnings  
- **Historical indications:** adjunctive weight gain after surgery/chronic infection/severe trauma; failure to maintain weight; offset corticosteroid protein catabolism; osteoporotic bone pain relief.  
- **Adult dose:** **2.5–20 mg/day** in 2–4 divided doses; courses **2–4 weeks** usually adequate, intermittent.  
- **Pediatric (label):** total daily ≤0.1 mg/kg or ≤0.045 mg/lb; intermittent.  
- **Geriatric:** **5 mg BID**; greater fluid/transaminase sensitivity.  
- **PK:** t½ **10.4 h** younger; **13.3 h** elderly.  
- **Warfarin multidose study:** oxandrolone 5 or 10 mg BID × 15 subjects on warfarin → S-warfarin t½ **26 → 48 h**, AUC **4.55 → 12.08 ng·h/mL**; mean warfarin dose **6.13 → 1.13 mg/day** (~**80–85% reduction**) to hold INR ~1.5; microscopic hematuria 9/15, gingival bleeding 1/15.  
- **Boxed-class warnings:** peliosis hepatis; liver cell tumors (benign and fatal malignant reported); blood lipid changes associated with atherosclerosis risk (↓HDL, sometimes ↑LDL).  
- **Limits:** Original indication efficacy disputed since 1984 adcom; not performance-labeled (“not shown to enhance athletic ability”).

**FDA withdrawal (2023)** — **Established** regulatory event  
Manufacturer non-marketing (2019) → FDA concluded problems sufficiently serious for removal; formal determination withdrawn for **safety or effectiveness**. Cites 1984 unanimous “no evidence of efficacy” for then-labeled indications + extensive safety warnings. **Does not erase** positive RCTs in burns/HIV/body composition.

**Demling & Orgill 2000 (*J Crit Care*)** — RCT burns 40–70% TBSA  
- Oxandrolone **20 mg/day** (n=11) vs placebo (n=9) from day 2–3 post-burn.  
- **Net weight loss:** **3 ± 1.9 vs 8 ± 3.1 kg**.  
- **Net daily N loss (d7–28):** **4 ± 1.9 vs 13 ± 4 g**.  
- **Donor-site healing:** **9 ± 2 vs 13 ± 3 days**.  
- No major liver dysfunction attributed.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** anticatabolic + wound-healing effect in severe burns at ~20 mg/day. Limits: small n; ICU nutrition context ≠ gym cut. External validity to eugonadal trainees: **poor** for magnitude.

**Porro et al. 2012 (*J Am Coll Surg*)** — Pediatric severe burns, 5-year outcomes  
- Oxandrolone **0.1 mg/kg BID** × 12 months vs placebo; ± exercise.  
- ↓ REE, ↓ rate-pressure product, ↑ IGF-1 year 1.  
- Ox + exercise: ↑ LBM and strength vs controls.  
- ↑ BMC (ages **7–18**); improved height percentile.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** long-horizon pediatric burn recovery signals. Limits: not adult gym.

**Kopel et al. 2022 / Real et al. 2014 / burn metas**  
- Convergence: shorter LOS, faster donor-site healing, less weight/protein loss.  
- **Certainty:** **Established–Probable** pooled clinical utility in burns.

**Grunfeld et al. 2006 (*JAIDS*)** — RCT HIV-associated weight loss, men n=262  
| Arm | Weight Δ (kg) | BCM Δ (kg) | Notes |
|-----|---------------|------------|-------|
| Placebo | +1.1 ± 2.7 | +0.45 ± 1.7 | Placebo gain nonzero → concurrent care |
| 20 mg | +1.8 ± 3.9 | +0.91 ± 2.2 | Not clearly > placebo on key endpoints |
| 40 mg | +2.8 ± 3.3 | +1.5 ± 2.5 | Weight > placebo; BCM > placebo |
| 80 mg | +2.3 ± 2.9 | +1.8 ± 1.8 | BCM > placebo; weight not cleanly > 40 mg |

- **Suppressed SHBG, LH, FSH, total and free testosterone** at studied doses.  
- ↑ transaminases, ↑ LDL, ↓ HDL.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** dose-related BCM/weight in HIV wasting; **Established** endocrine + lipid + LFT package at **20–80 mg** (dose band overlapping performance practice). Best multi-arm AE prior for gym risk framing. Limits: wasting population; no supervised RT factorial.

**Sheffield-Moore et al. 1999 (*JCEM*)** — Young healthy men, 5 days  
- Oxandrolone **15 mg/day** × 5 days (n=6, before–after).  
- Muscle protein **FSR +44%**; fractional breakdown unchanged; net balance more positive; improved intracellular AA reutilization.  
- **AR mRNA ↑** in skeletal muscle; IGF-I mRNA unchanged.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** acute human anabolic mechanism. Limits: tiny n; 5 days; not trained hypertrophy trial. Do **not** scale +44% FSR to +44% size.

**Schroeder et al. 2004 (*J Appl Physiol*)** — Older men 60–87 y, n=32 — **best male 20 mg translation bridge**  
- **20 mg/day** × 12 weeks vs placebo.  
- **LBM +3.0 ± 1.5 kg**; total body water +2.9 kg; **proximal thigh muscle area +12.4 ± 8.4 cm²**.  
- 1-RM strength ↑ (leg press ~6.7%, chest press ~9.3%, etc.).  
- **Fat −1.9 ± 1.0 kg** (trunk −1.3 kg).  
- **Week 24 (12 wk off):** LBM and strength increments **no longer different from baseline**; fat loss **partly sustained** (−1.5 kg total, −1.0 kg trunk).  
- **Certainty:** **Established** on-treatment body composition/strength; **Established** that anabolic gains were not durable without continued androgen/stimulus. Critical for performance expectation management. TBW≈LBM numerically → some fluid coupling; MRI CSA still supports real muscle area change (**Probable** mixed tissue + fluid).

**Mavros et al. 2015 (*Med Sci Sports Exerc*)** — Older women + high-intensity PRT  
- **10 mg/day** × 12 weeks + PRT 3×/wk vs PRT + placebo.  
- LBM **+2.6 kg** (95% CI 1.0–4.2) beyond PRT; fat **−1 kg** beyond PRT.  
- Strength, power, chair rise, balance, walk: **time effects only — no oxandrolone advantage** over PRT alone.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** composition; **Established** null on function beyond training. LBM ≠ automatic performance.

**Mohamed et al. 2019 Cochrane (CD010736)** — Turner syndrome  
- Oxandrolone + GH vs GH alone: final adult height **+2.7 cm** (MD; 95% CI ~1.3–4.1; 5 trials, n≈270; **moderate** quality of evidence).  
- AE RR ~1.81 (0.83–3.96; **low** QoE) — mainly virilization signals.  
- Freriks 2012 long-term follow-up: height gain maintained; minority mild persistent subjective virilization / lower voice; historical preference **0.03 mg/kg/d** over 0.06.  
- **Certainty:** **Established** height adjunct; **Probable** residual virilization risk even at pediatric microdoses. Adult female gym height endpoint irrelevant; AE biology **directional** warning.

**Balagopal et al. 2006** — DMD small-n: enhances myosin synthesis / gene expression. **Probable** limited mechanism support.

**de Mello Gindri et al. 2025** — SR multi-condition (PROSPERO CRD42024539483; ~24 studies, n≈1905). Burns/HIV/older-women directional support; low reported AE incidence in **clinical** settings. Abstract-level access this run — **Probable** as overview; do not treat pooled AE rates as Established.

**Greboggy et al. 2024 (*RSD*)** — Observational RTP men, oxandrolone users (n=11) vs controls (n=11); T1 pre, T2 cessation (mean cycle **7.4 wk**), T3 ~3 mo off. Self-report doses **0.04–0.97 mg/kg**.  
- **HDL median ~24 mg/dL at T2** (ref >40) → recover **~49 mg/dL at T3**.  
- TG higher at T3 (177 vs ref <175). Total T reduced signals at T2/T3.  
- Authors: association ≠ definitive causality; small n; self-selected.  
- **Certainty:** **Probable** real-world lipid crash magnitude in self-directed oxandrolone use (triangulated with label + Grunfeld + class); recovery over months **Probable**. Highest external validity among lipid sources for gym male oral use despite weak design.

**Hartgens et al. 2004** — Multi-AAS stacks: profound HDL↓ ApoA1↓ ApoB↑; incomplete recovery 6 wk off; nandrolone alone milder. **Established** that orals/stacks drive atherogenic profile more than mild injectables alone. Not oxandrolone monotherapy isolation.

### What clinical data do **NOT** show

- No large modern RCTs of oxandrolone in resistance-trained eugonadal athletes for hypertrophy/strength at common gym doses with hard CV endpoints.  
- No proof that “Anavar-only” cycles spare HPTA (Grunfeld contradicts “non-suppressive” folklore at 20–80 mg).  
- No proof fat loss durability generalizes without training/diet (Schroeder: fat partly held; LBM did not).  
- No head-to-head gold-standard athlete trial vs Winstrol / Primo / Masteron / low-dose test for recomp.  
- 2023 withdrawal does **not** mean burns/HIV RCTs were fraudulent; it means labeled benefit–risk failed modern bar.  
- Mild enzyme rises in short clinical courses ≠ clearance of rare peliosis/tumor risk with chronic/high oral alkyl use.  
- Hard ASCVD endpoints (MI, stroke) from intermittent oxandrolone monotherapy: **Unknown**.

### Multi-source convergent patterns (usable) vs single-thread (discard)

**Convergent (Tier C multi-forum + clinical triangulation):** dry recomp look; men 20–50 mg 6–8 wk / women 5–10 mg culture; lipid crash; real suppression; UGL distrust; DHT-like hair/skin in susceptibles; voice stop-rules for women.

**Single-thread / marketing (Tier D — Speculative max or discard):** “+15 lb dry Anavar-only bulk” without bloodwork; vendor purity screenshots; X one-offs; claims of zero suppression or liver-safe oral.

### Known forum failure modes

1. **Survivorship** — satisfied users post more.  
2. **Stack confounding** — “Anavar cycle” often includes test, other orals, fat burners, or misidentified tablets.  
3. **Product identity** — without analytics, forum dose–response is not oxandrolone dose–response.  
4. **“Mild” brand bias** — under-reporting of lipids/suppression until labs forced.  
5. **PCT mythology** — heterogeneous SERM protocols; no controlled performance-user PCT RCTs in this inventory.

### Weighing

Forum density for **dry recomp look, strength, and lipid carnage** is high and **concordant** with clinical lipids/endocrine and non-aromatizing chemistry. The “mild” brand is best read as **relative to wet orals and high-androgen injectables**, not as a free pass. Women’s discourse correctly prioritizes **voice and clitoral changes** as stop-criteria (partly irreversible). UGL identity noise means some “Anavar sides” are other orals and some “non-responders” were underdosed product. Never convert forum frequency to incidence %.

## 6. Desired effects — mechanism depth

### 6.1 Lean body mass / muscle protein synthesis

- **Phenotype:** Measurable LBM and myofiber protein accrual; “quality” muscle with less watery fullness.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P2, P3, P6, P25.  
- **Mechanism chain:** Oral oxandrolone → high AR occupancy in muscle → ARE-driven MPS genes; short-term human data show **FSR +44%** with **unchanged breakdown** and better intracellular AA reuse; **AR mRNA upregulation** may amplify sensitivity; 2-oxa structure hypothesized to resist 3α-HSD inactivation that normally blunts DHT in muscle. Anticatabolic tone helps in deficit.  
- **Clinical:** Sheffield-Moore **Established** acute; Schroeder **+3.0 kg LBM** / +12.4 cm² thigh **Established**; Mavros **+2.6 kg** women **Established**; Grunfeld BCM dose-related **Established**.  
- **Forum:** High consistency dry gains.  
- **Practical:** Expect **retention** only if training/protein continue (Schroeder durability lesson — anabolic gains largely gone by 12 wk off). Certainty of anabolic effect: **Established** clinical; gym magnitude dose-dependent **Probable**.

### 6.2 Strength

- **Phenotype:** 1-RM and working-set load increases over 6–12 weeks.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P1, P2 ± CNS P18.  
- **Mechanism:** Myocellular force capacity from protein accrual + possible neural AR contribution.  
- **Clinical:** Schroeder strength % gains **Established** older men; Mavros **no additive** strength beyond PRT in older women — training status matters.  
- **Forum:** High. Certainty: **Probable** trained users at 20–50 mg.

### 6.3 Anticatabolic (cuts, burns, corticosteroids)

- **Phenotype:** Less LBM loss under caloric deficit or hypermetabolic stress; historical steroid-catabolism offset.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P25, P1, P2, P16.  
- **Mechanism:** AR-mediated maintenance of synthesis when cortisol/hypermetabolism would drive net negative balance; burns: less N excretion, preserved mass.  
- **Clinical:** Demling N loss **4 vs 13 g/day** **Established**; label corticosteroid indication historical.  
- **Forum:** Cut-protection reputation high. Certainty: **Established** severe catabolic states; **Probable** dieted athletes (magnitude unknown).

### 6.4 Fat mass reduction / recomp

- **Phenotype:** Simultaneous lean gain or hold + fat loss; trunk fat drop; “hard” look.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P27, P1, P4; not P29 as primary.  
- **Mechanism:** Adipocyte AR signaling + androgen-driven body composition shift + energy balance; non-aromatizing profile avoids estrogenic water that masks definition. Burns REE actually **decreased** — do not treat ox as a thermogenic fat-burner.  
- **Clinical:** Schroeder fat **−1.9 kg** (partly durable); Mavros **−1 kg** beyond PRT.  
- **Forum:** Signature recomp claim. Certainty: **Established** clinical composition; **Probable** gym recomp when diet tight.

### 6.5 Dry look, vascularity, low estrogenic water

- **Phenotype:** Harder appearance, visible vascularity, less puff than test/Dbol.  
- **Pathway IDs:** P4, P14, P27.  
- **Mechanism:** No aromatization to E2 → no E2-mediated extracellular water from the compound; fat loss over muscle.  
- **Support:** Structure **Established**; cosmetic **Probable** forum.  
- **Note:** “Dry” ≠ safe lipids or zero androgenic fluid from other mechanisms (P14 mild edema possible).

### 6.6 Wound healing / donor site (medical)

- **Pathway:** P16, P1, P25. Demling donor **9 vs 13 days** **Established**. Gym injury-recovery transfer **Speculative**.

### 6.7 Bone mineral / height (specific populations)

- **Pathway:** P15. Porro BMC; Turner **+2.7 cm** **Established** those settings. Adult gym BMD benefit **Unknown**.

### 6.8 Oral convenience / short clearable half-life

- **Phenotype:** No injection; ability to stop and clear relatively fast vs long esters.  
- **PK:** t½ ~10 h **Established**. Does not equal “clears from anti-doping quickly” (metabolites persist).

## 7. Side effects — phenotype, mechanism, pathway→counter tables

Dose reduction and **hard stop** are first-line for most nodes. Counters lower residual risk; they do **not** create safety. Speculative counters (NAC/TUDCA/bergamot) are pathway-matched and labeled — not RCT-proven shields for AAS orals.

### 7.1 Hepatotoxicity (17α-alkyl class) — S1 / P9

1. **Phenotype:** Asymptomatic ALT/AST/GGT/ALP rise; cholestatic pattern possible; rare jaundice; **rare** peliosis hepatis (blood-filled cysts → hemorrhage/failure); **rare** hepatic adenomas/HCC with prolonged high-dose oral AAS.  
2. **Mechanism:** 17α-methylation enables oral bioavailability but stresses hepatic sinusoidal/canalicular handling (P9). First-pass exposure high despite renal metabolite clearance. Label boxed warnings class-shared with other 17α-alkyls.

**Test base (men):** At performance doses, plan for HPTA suppression (**Established** at ≥20 mg in Grunfeld). Exogenous testosterone maintains androgenicity and sexual function on-cycle; PCT or blast-cruise planning off-cycle. Solo “var-only” short courses still suppress variably — do not market as non-suppressive.

**Women notes:** Therapeutic index for virilization is narrow. Product authenticity critical (overdose → irreversible voice). 5α-reductase inhibitors do not neutralize oxandrolone. Prioritize stop criteria over completing a calendar.

**Nutrition/training:** Protein adequate for FSR advantage; progressive resistance required for functional transfer (Mavros lesson). Deficit for recomp; do not expect Schroeder-like fat loss without energy control. Gains require training/protein co-drive (**Established** co-dependence).

### Contraindications / strong avoid (label-aligned)

- Known/suspected **prostate or male breast carcinoma**  
- **Breast cancer in women with hypercalcemia**  
- **Pregnancy** (Category X — fetal masculinization; embryotoxicity in animals at high multiples)  
- **Nephrosis** (nephrotic phase)  
- **Hypercalcemia**  
- Active serious hepatic disease; caution severe cardiac/renal disease (edema)  
- Unwillingness to stop at virilization (women)  
- Concurrent warfarin without specialist-level INR infrastructure

## 11. Comparative context

| Comparator | Similarity | Oxandrolone advantage | Oxandrolone disadvantage | Practical read |
|------------|------------|----------------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| **Winstrol (stanozolol)** | Oral (or injectable) DHT-ish dry look; cutting | Often described as slightly less joint-harsh / less “crispy” androgen feel for some; denser human RCT medical data | Winstrol also brutal on lipids/HDL; ox still crashes HDL hard | Both dry orals; neither “safe”; lipids comparable class problem |
| **Primobolan (metenolone)** | Dry recomp reputation; milder androgen feel | Ox is oral convenient; primo injectable (enanthate/acetate) avoids 17α-alkyl if injectable | Injectable primo skips first-pass alkyl liver hit; lipids often friendlier than oral ox | Primo preferred if avoiding oral liver/lipid spike; ox preferred if needle-free short block |
| **Masteron (drostanolone)** | Dry hardness, DHT-derived | Ox oral; masteron injectable | Masteron no 17α-alkyl (injectable); different side nuance (androgenicity, hair) | Hardness compounds; masteron for injectable dry stack; ox for oral |
| **Low-dose testosterone** | LBM/strength foundation | Ox less water/E2 if used alone; oral | Test is physiologic hormone; manageable E2; injectable or cream; better long-term base | Men: **test base + optional ox** > ox solo for most goals |
| **Turinabol (chlorodehydromethyltestosterone)** | Oral “dry” mild reputation | Ox has far denser clinical RCT package | Both 17α-alkyl; both lipid/liver class risks | Similar niche; ox better studied medically |
| **Dianabol / Anadrol** | Oral mass agents | Ox drier, less estrogenic water, often milder enzymes relatively | Far less mass than Dbol/Adrol; still full lipid/HPTA liabilities | Different role; do not treat ox as “safe Dbol” |

**Summary comparative stance:** Oxandrolone is the **best-documented “mild oral”** in clinical literature, but “mild” is relative — mainly vs wet orals and extreme androgen injectables for *feel*, not vs injectable non-alkylated dry compounds for *lipid/hepatic burden*. For lipid/hepatic burden, injectable non-alkylated dry compounds (primo, masteron, low-dose test) are structurally cleaner. For pure oral convenience and burn/HIV-style anticatabolic data, oxandrolone is the reference molecule.

**“Mild oral” myth — final cut:**  
- Relative mildness of androgenic *subjective feel* and *enzyme spikes* vs Dbol/Anadrol: **Probable**.  
- Mild on **HDL and HPTA**: **false** — **Established** crash and suppression at studied doses.  
- Liver-safe: **false** — class boxed risks remain; relative ranking only.  
- Safe women’s oral: **false** — virilization risk **Established**; only risk-reduced bands exist.

## 14. Boundary paragraph

This document is a mechanism-complete research briefing for informed compound users and clinicians who already accept risk tradeoffs — not a prescription, not medical advice, and not an assurance of safety at any dose. Oxandrolone has **real anabolic and anticatabolic efficacy** in controlled human studies (FSR +44%; LBM +~3 kg older men; burns N-balance and healing; Turner +2.7 cm with GH) and **real endocrine, lipid, hepatic-class, and virilization liabilities** (HPTA suppression at ≥20 mg; HDL often into the 20s; 17α-alkyl boxed risks; warfarin 80–85% potentiation). US branded approval withdrawal (2023) and Schedule III / WADA status do not erase those data either way. Individual response, product identity, stacking, and genetics dominate outcomes; counters reduce but do not eliminate residual absolute risk. Decisions belong to the user and their qualified clinician with labs in hand.
